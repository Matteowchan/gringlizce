Grammar: Past Ability (could / was able to)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Past Ability?

Geçmişte neyi yapabildiğini ya da yapamadığını anlatmak istediğinde, present'teki can / can't yetmez — zamanı geçmişe taşıman gerekir. Bunun için üç yapı kullanırız: could, couldn't ve was / were able to. Bak, aynı cümle geçmişte nasıl değişiyor: "I can swim." (şimdi) → "When I was five, I could swim." (geçmişte).

  • When I was young, I could run very fast.Gençken çok hızlı koşabilirdim.
  • She couldn't drive last year.Geçen yıl araba kullanamıyordu.
  • The exam was hard, but he was able to pass it.Sınav zordu, ama onu geçmeyi başardı.
  • After hours of work, the doctors were able to save the man.Saatlerce çalıştıktan sonra doktorlar adamı kurtarmayı başardı.

Bu bölümde üç yapıyı öğreneceğiz: could (genel geçmiş yeterlilik), couldn't (olumsuz) ve was / were able to (özellikle tek seferlik bir başarı için). Then we will learn the important difference between them. Adım adım gidelim.

1. could: Genel Geçmiş Yeterlilik (general past ability)

could, geçmişte genel olarak yapabildiğin şeyleri anlatır — bir dönem boyunca sahip olduğun bir yetenek. Türkçedeki "-ebilirdim / -abilirdim" ekine denk gelir. En güzel yanı: özne ne olursa olsun şekli değişmez ve ardından her zaman yalın fiil gelir (fiile -s ya da -ed eklenmez).

Öznecould + yalın fiilAnlam
I / YouI could swimyüzebilirdim
He / She / ItShe could singşarkı söyleyebilirdi
We / TheyThey could readokuyabilirlerdi
  • My grandfather could speak four languages.Büyükbabam dört dil konuşabilirdi.
  • When she was six, she could ride a bike.Altı yaşındayken bisiklete binebiliyordu.
Kolay hatırlatma
  • could herkes için aynıdır: I could, she could, they could — asla "coulds" olmaz.
  • Fiil hep yalındır: could swim ✓ — could to swim ✗ — could swam ✗.

2. couldn't: Olumsuz (negative — could not)

Geçmişte bir şeyi yapamadığını söylemek için couldn't (= could not) kullanırız. Türkçedeki "-emedim / -amadım" ekine denk gelir. Yine özne değişse de şekli aynıdır ve fiil yalın kalır. Hem genel yetersizlik hem de tek bir olaydaki başarısızlık için kullanılabilir.

Yapı
  • özne + couldn't + yalın fiil...
  • I + couldn't + sleep = I couldn't sleep (uyuyamadım)
  • Resmî yazıda could not, günlük dilde couldn't — ikisi de aynıdır.
  • The baby couldn't walk yet.Bebek henüz yürüyemiyordu.
  • It was too dark, so we couldn't see the road.Çok karanlıktı, bu yüzden yolu göremedik.
  • He could not find his keys this morning.Bu sabah anahtarlarını bulamadı.
Dikkat
  • couldn't zaten olumsuzdur: didn't couldn't ✗ — couldn't ✓.
  • Fiil yalın kalır: couldn't find ✓ — couldn't found ✗.

3. was / were able to (structure)

be able to, "yapabilmek / -meyi başarmak" demektir. Geçmiş için be fiilini geçmişe çeviririz: was (I / he / she / it) ve were (you / we / they). Ardından her zaman to + yalın fiil gelir. Dikkat: burada özneye göre was mı were mi seçmen gerekir — could'un aksine şekil değişir.

Öznewas / were able to + fiilAnlam
I / He / She / ItHe was able to finishbitirebildi / bitirmeyi başardı
You / We / TheyWe were able to escapekaçmayı başardık
  • After many tries, she was able to open the door.Birçok denemeden sonra kapıyı açmayı başardı.
  • The players trained hard and were able to win the cup.Oyuncular sıkı çalıştı ve kupayı kazanmayı başardı.
Nasıl seçilir — 2 adım
  • 1. Özne tekil (I/he/she/it) ise → was able to.
  • 2. Özne çoğul (you/we/they) ise → were able to.
  • Ardından hep to + yalın fiil: was able to run ✓ — was able run ✗.

4. could mı, was able to mu? (the key difference)

Bu ünitenin en önemli kuralı budur, dikkatle oku. İki yapı çoğu zaman aynı görünse de tek bir durumda ayrışır: geçmişte tek bir kez başarılan bir eylem. Böyle bir başarı için could kullanılmaz; was / were able to gerekir.

DurumKullanÖrnek
Genel yetenek (bir dönem boyunca)could / was able toI could swim as a child.
Tek seferlik başarı (belirli bir an)was/were able to ✓ (could ✗)The race was hard, but I was able to win.
Olumsuz (her iki durumda)couldn't = wasn't able toI couldn't win the race.
Altın kural
  • Genel yetenek → could ya da was able to (ikisi de olur).
  • Tek seferlik başarı → sadece was/were able to: "The test was hard, but she was able to pass." ✓ — "...she could pass." ✗.
  • Olumsuzda ise fark kalkar: couldn't her durumda kullanılabilir.

5. Sorular ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru sorarken Could özneden önce gelir: "Could you swim when you were five?" — yardımcı fiile (did) gerek yoktur. was/were able to ile soru için be fiilini öne alırız: "Were you able to finish?"

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Could you ride a bike?Yes, I could.No, I couldn't.
Were they able to come?Yes, they were.No, they weren't.
  • Could your sister read before school?Kız kardeşin okuldan önce okuyabiliyor muydu?
  • Were you able to fix the car in the end?Sonunda arabayı tamir edebildin mi?
Karıştırma
  • Soruda "did" yoktur: "Could you swim?" ✓ — "Did you could swim?" ✗.
  • be able to sorusunda be fiili öne gelir: "Were you able to...?" ✓ — "Did you able to...?" ✗.

6. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların geçmiş yeterlilik yapılarında en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
When I was five, I could to swim.When I was five, I could swim.could'dan sonra "to" gelmez, yalın fiil gelir.
She could sang very well.She could sing very well.could + yalın fiil (sang değil sing).
The test was hard, but I could pass it....but I was able to pass it.Tek seferlik başarı için was able to, could değil.
They was able to win the match.They were able to win the match.Çoğul özne (they) → were able to.
I didn't could find my phone.I couldn't find my phone.couldn't zaten olumsuz; "didn't" eklenmez.
Did you could ride a bike?Could you ride a bike?Soruda "did" yok; Could öne gelir.

7. Uygulama: Eski Günler (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about what they could and couldn't do in the past. Koyu yazılan yapılara dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — When you were a child, could you swim?Çocukken yüzebiliyor muydun?
  • — Yes, I could swim very well, but I couldn't ride a bike.Evet, çok iyi yüzebiliyordum ama bisiklete binemiyordum.
  • — Really? So how did you learn?Gerçekten mi? Peki nasıl öğrendin?
  • — One summer I practised every day, and finally I was able to ride to school alone.Bir yaz her gün pratik yaptım ve sonunda okula tek başıma gitmeyi başardım.
  • — Nice! My sister and I couldn't cook at all back then.Güzel! Kız kardeşimle ben o zamanlar hiç yemek yapamıyorduk.
  • — But last week you were able to make a whole dinner! People change.Ama geçen hafta koca bir akşam yemeği yapabildin! İnsanlar değişiyor.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda "yüzebiliyordum" için hangi yapı kullanıldı — genel yetenek olduğu için? (Cevap: could)
  • "finally I was able to ride to school" — burada neden could değil de was able to var? (Cevap: tek seferlik bir başarı)
  • "were able to make a whole dinner" cümlesindeki özne çoğul olsaydı hangi kelime gelirdi? (Cevap: zaten were; tekilde was olurdu)

Reading: A New Start at a New School

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • The text is about a hard time in someone's life. Which words show ability or difficulty? (Metin birinin zor bir dönemini anlatıyor. Hangi kelimeler yeterlilik ya da zorluk gösteriyor?)
  • Look at the title. Do you think the story ends happily or sadly? (Başlığa bak. Sence hikâye mutlu mu yoksa üzücü mü bitiyor?)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. could, couldn't, was/were able to yapılarına dikkat et.

Words to spot (Metinde geçen, Speaking bölümünden tanıdığın kelimeler): village · grow · shy · nervous · audience · difficult · save · practise · could · couldn't · was able to

Yeni bir okulda yeni bir başlangıç: çalışıp başarmak.
A New Start at a New School

Two years ago, my friend Deniz moved from a small village to a big city and started a new school. Life in the village was simple. She could ride her bike everywhere, she could grow tomatoes in the garden, and she could fix almost anything on her old bike. But she couldn't speak much English, and she couldn't play any instrument. For a village girl, the new school was strange and difficult.

In the village, Deniz was never bored. Every day after school she could play football with her cousins in the fields. She could swim in the small river in summer, and she could climb the tall tree behind her house. Her mother could cook wonderful village food, and her father could tell funny old stories. At the weekend, the whole family could sit together and sing. For Deniz, life in the village was easy and full of friends.

Moving day was not easy. Deniz and her family packed everything into a small van. She said goodbye to her cousins and her old bike, and she couldn't stop crying. The city was big, loud and grey. On her first morning at the new school, she stood at the gate and couldn't move. There were so many new faces. She wanted to be brave, but she felt very small and alone.

In the first weeks, Deniz couldn't make any friends. She wanted to talk to her classmates, but she was always too shy and nervous to say much. She couldn't sleep at night because she worried about school. Her brother said, "Be patient. You are clever, and soon you will find good friends."

The lessons were also very hard for her. In the village school the classes were small, but here there were thirty students in one room. She couldn't understand the fast English lessons, and she couldn't follow the science teacher. At lunch time everyone sat with their friends, but Deniz sat alone. She missed her cousins, her bike and her garden. Some nights she wanted to go back home to the village.

Then her brother had an idea. He gave Deniz his old guitar and said, "Music is easy to share. You don't need many words to make friends." At first Deniz couldn't play even one song. Her fingers hurt and the notes sounded wrong. But she was patient. Every evening she practised in her room for one hour. Slowly, week by week, she got better, and soon she could play two or three easy songs.

The week before the talent show, Deniz was very nervous. She couldn't sleep well, and she couldn't eat much at breakfast. "What if I forget the song? What if the audience laughs?" she thought. Her brother said, "You practised hard. You are ready. Just be calm and enjoy it." Deniz took a deep breath. She knew she couldn't be perfect, but she wanted to try her best.

One Friday, the school had a talent show. Deniz loved music, so she decided to play the guitar on the stage. The audience was big and the song was difficult, but this time she stayed calm. After three difficult minutes, she was able to play the whole song, and everyone clapped. She was so happy that she couldn't stop smiling.

After the last note, a girl named Elif came to talk to her. "That was great! Could you teach me?" she asked. Deniz was so surprised that at first she couldn't answer. Then she smiled and said yes. That evening, for the first time, Deniz walked home with a new friend. She couldn't wait to tell her brother the good news.

After the show, Deniz made friends fast. In a short time she was able to speak English with the other students, and after a few months she was able to save enough money for a new guitar. She still couldn't play difficult songs well at first, but she practised every weekend until she could.

Life at the new school got better and better. Deniz joined the music club, and she also joined the school volleyball team. Her new teammates were kind and clever, and they could help her with her homework. On Saturdays the club met to play music together, and Deniz was never shy on the stage again. She could laugh, talk and play with her friends for hours.

Today Deniz says, "In the village I could do many things, but at the new school I learned that I was able to do much more." She never gave up, and that is why everything changed.

Deniz's story teaches us something simple. When you move to a new place, the first days can be difficult, and you may feel shy or nervous. You may even think, "I can't do this." But if you are patient and you practise a little every day, one day you will be able to do it too. Deniz couldn't speak English or play the guitar before, but now she can do both, and she has many good friends.

Write and submit your paragraph; your teacher will review it. (Paragrafını yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

Geçmiş Yeterlilik Yazını Canlandırır (Richer Writing)

Kısa, kopuk cümleler bir yazıyı zayıf gösterir. What makes a "past" story interesting is past ability: neyi yapabildiğini, neyi yapamadığını ve sonunda neyi başardığını anlatırsın. Bir de bağlaçları (and, but, so, because, when...) eklersen, yazın gerçek bir hikâyeye dönüşür.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Past ability + bağlaç ile ✓
I was a child. I couldn't swim. I practised. I learned. Now I swim well. When I was a child, I couldn't swim, but I practised every summer until I was able to swim well, and now I love the sea.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 geçmiş yeterlilik yapısı kullan (could / couldn't / was/were able to).
  • Ayrıca en az 3 bağlaç kullan: and, but, so, because, when, after, before, until.

Task (Görev)

Write about what you could and couldn't do when you were younger, and one thing you finally managed to do. (Küçükken neleri yapabildiğini, neleri yapamadığını ve sonunda başardığın bir şeyi anlat.) Use past ability.

NELER OLMALI (İçerik)

  • two or three things you could do as a child (could ile)
  • one or two things you couldn't do then (couldn't ile)
  • one thing you finally managed to do (was/were able to ile — tek seferlik başarı)
  • one detail for each: why it was easy, hard, or important
  • en az 3 geçmiş yeterlilik yapısı: could, couldn't, was/were able to
  • en az 3 bağlaç: and, but, so, because, when, until
  • Speaking bölümünden en az 4 kelime (village, practise, nervous, patient, clever, difficult...)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR

"When I was younger, I could ..." "At first I couldn't ..." "After a lot of practice, I was able to ..."

Your Paragraph (100-130 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~125 words) When I was younger, I could do many small things, but I couldn't do the big ones. In our village I could climb trees, and I could swim in the river because the water was warm in summer. However, I couldn't ride a bike, and I couldn't speak any English at all.

At school English was very difficult, so I was often nervous. But my teacher was patient, and I practised every evening until I was able to read simple stories.

The best day was when my father brought home an old bike. At first I fell many times and couldn't balance, but after two weeks I was able to ride to school alone. That day I learned that hard work always helps.
