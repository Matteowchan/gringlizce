Final Sınavı: Ünite 1-38 (Büyük Sınav)

Read the rules, then start! (Kuralları oku, sonra başla!) Bu sınav bütün Junior A1'i kapsar.

Sınav Hakkında

Bu büyük sınav, Junior A1'in 38 ünitesinin tamamını ölçer: selamlaşma ve be (am/is/are); aile ve iyelik sıfatları; a/an ve çoğullar; there is/are; can/can't; Present Continuous (şu an olan); Present Simple ve likes; some/any; zamirler; this/that/these/those ve emir kipi; was/were; yer edatları; saat; Wh- soruları; ve birçok eğlenceli konu — animals, my town, jobs, feelings, my house, transport, my body, playground, shopping, garden, birthday, zoo, beach, school, sports. Konuşma (speaking) yoktur. Sınav tek bir sayaçla ilerler; süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Suggested time: 15 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 15 dakika.) Read the little dialogue, then answer. (Kısa diyaloğu oku, sonra cevapla.)

Bu bölümde ses yoktur. Aşağıdaki kısa diyaloğu dikkatlice oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the dialogue below, then answer the questions.)

Lucy and Max at the Park

Lucy: Hi Max! What are you doing?

Max: Hi Lucy! I am playing with my new ball. It is red and yellow.

Lucy: Wow, it is nice! Can you play football?

Max: Yes, I can. I play football every Saturday with my dad. Can you play?

Lucy: No, I can't play football, but I can ride my bike. Look, this is my bike!

Max: It's blue! I like it. Where is your dog today?

Lucy: My dog Toby is at home. He is sleeping. He is very lazy!

Max: Ha ha! Are you hungry?

Lucy: Yes, a little. I have two apples in my bag. Here is one for you.

Max: Thank you, Lucy! You are a good friend.

Junior A1 Final · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Text 1 geçmişi (dün/geçen hafta), Text 2 şu anı anlatır. (Text 1 is about the past, Text 2 is about now.)

Text 1 · Emma's Day at the Zoo

Last Saturday, Emma went to the zoo with her family. It was a sunny day, and Emma was very happy. She wore her favourite red hat.

First, they saw the monkeys. The monkeys were funny — they jumped and played in the trees, and Emma laughed a lot. Then they saw a big elephant. It was grey and it had very big ears. Emma was a little scared, but her dad said, "Don't worry, elephants are friendly."

For lunch, they ate sandwiches, and Emma drank orange juice. After lunch, they saw the lions. The lions were sleeping in the sun, and they didn't move at all.

At the end of the day, Emma was tired but very happy. Her favourite animals were the monkeys. "I want to come again!" she said.

Junior A1 Final · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Note: My Weekend (30-50 words)

Write a short note to your friend about last weekend. Tell them: what you did, where you went, and one thing you can do. (Arkadaşına geçen hafta sonunla ilgili kısa bir not yaz: ne yaptın, nereye gittin ve yapabildiğin bir şey.)

  • Use Past Simple: went, played, ate, saw... (geçen hafta sonunu geçmiş zamanla anlat)
  • Use can: I can ride a bike / swim... (bir yeteneğini yaz)
  • 30-50 words. (30-50 kelime)
NOT YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Sam," / "Dear friend,"
  • Gövde: hafta sonu ne yaptın (went, played...) + bir yeteneğin (I can...).
  • Kapanış: "See you soon!" + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My Favourite Animal (60-90 words)

Write a paragraph about your favourite animal (real or imaginary): what it is, what it looks like (colour, big or small), what it can do, and why you like it. (En sevdiğin hayvanı anlatan bir paragraf yaz: ne olduğu, nasıl göründüğü — renk, büyük/küçük —, neler yapabildiği ve onu neden sevdiğin.)

  • Use is / are and colours: It is big and grey... (nasıl göründüğünü anlat)
  • Use can: It can run / jump / fly... (neler yapabildiğini yaz)
  • Use because to say why you like it. (neden sevdiğini "because" ile yaz)
  • 60-90 words. (60-90 kelime)
