Grammar: Present Simple (I / you / we / they)

Rutinlerini, alışkanlıklarını ve zevklerini anlat: I play, we go, they like. (Talk about your routines and habits.)

Selam, ben yine Gri. Bu ünitede Present Simple (Geniş Zaman) ile çalışıyoruz: her gün yaptıkların, arkadaşlarınla rutinlerin, spor ve sosyal medya alışkanlıkların. I, you, we, they ile fiil sade kalır: I post, we train, they chat. Hadi başlayalım.

Dinleme (Listening)

Kısa bir video dinle ve izle. (Listen and watch a short video.)

Video çok yakında burada olacak!

Present Simple Nedir? (What is Present Simple?)

Her gün tekrar eden işleri, alışkanlıkları, rutinleri ve genel gerçekleri anlatmak için Present Simple kullanırsın. Genç hayatında bu zamanı çok kullanacaksın:

  • "I go to school by bus."Okula otobüsle giderim. (okul rutini)
  • "My friends and I study together."Arkadaşlarımla birlikte ders çalışırız. (arkadaşlar)
  • "We post videos on weekends."Hafta sonları video paylaşırız. (sosyal medya)
  • "They play basketball after school."Okuldan sonra basketbol oynarlar. (spor)
  • "You watch a lot of series."Çok fazla dizi izlersin. (hobi)

Bu ünitede sadece I, you, we, they ile çalışıyoruz. Bu kişilerle fiil çoğu zaman olduğu gibi (sade) kalır:

  • "I play." · "You play." · "We play." · "They play."Oynarım · Oynarsın · Oynarız · Oynarlar.
Dikkat
  • he / she / it (o) biraz farklıdır — fiile -s ekler ve onu Ünite 4'te öğreneceğiz. Şimdilik yalnızca I, you, we, they.

1. Fiil Sade Kalır (The Verb Stays the Same)

I, you, we ya da they ile fiile hiçbir şey eklemezsin. Fiil hep sade (yalın) kalır:

KişiFiilÖrnekTürkçesi
IstudyI study every evening.Her akşam ders çalışırım.
youplayYou play football.Futbol oynarsın.
wechatWe chat online.Online sohbet ederiz.
theytrainThey train on Mondays.Pazartesileri antrenman yaparlar.
Kolay yol
  • I / you / we / they → fiile hiç -s eklemezsin. Fiil sade kalır: play, go, like, watch, post.
  • Bu ünitede sık kullanacağın rutin ifadeleri: every day, on weekends, after school, in the evening.

2. "yapmam / yapmayız" Demek: don't (Negative)

Bir şeyi yapmadığını söylemek için fiilden önce don't (do not) koyarsın. Fiil yine sade kalır:

OlumluOlumsuzÖrnekTürkçesi
I likeI don't likeI don't like horror films.Korku filmi sevmem.
We studyWe don't studyWe don't study on Sundays.Pazarları ders çalışmayız.
They useThey don't useThey don't use that app.O uygulamayı kullanmazlar.
Not
  • don't = do not. don't'tan sonra fiil sade kalır: "I don't like" ✓ — "I don't likes" ✗.

3. Soru Sormak: Do ...? (Questions)

Soru sormak için cümlenin başına Do koyarsın. Türkçedeki "mi / mısın" ekinin işini bu yapar:

  • You follow her. → Do you follow her?Onu takip edersin. → Onu takip eder misin?
  • They play online. → Do they play online?Online oynarlar. → Online oynarlar mı?

Kısa cevaplar da kolaydır:

SoruEvetHayır
Do you play video games?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Do they train every week?Yes, they do.No, they don't.
Do we have practice today?Yes, we do.No, we don't.
Dikkat
  • Do'dan sonra fiil sade kalır: "Do you follow...?" ✓ — "Do you follows...?" ✗.

4. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I likes music.I like music.I ile fiil sade kalır, -s yok.
We plays online.We play online.we → play (sade).
I no use that app.I don't use that app.Olumsuz için "don't" kullan.
You follow me?Do you follow me?Soru: başa "Do" koy.
They don't studies.They don't study.don't'tan sonra fiil sade.

5. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Talk)

İki genç okul rutinleri ve hobileri hakkında konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Do you play any sports after school?Okuldan sonra spor yapar mısın?
  • — Yes, I do. My friends and I train for basketball, and we practise twice a week.Evet. Arkadaşlarımla basketbol için antrenman yaparız ve haftada iki kez çalışırız.
  • — Nice. I don't play team sports, but I run every morning.Güzel. Takım sporu yapmam ama her sabah koşarım.
  • — Cool! Do you post your runs online?Harika! Koşularını internette paylaşır mısın?
  • — Sometimes I share a photo, but I don't use social media a lot.Bazen bir fotoğraf paylaşırım ama sosyal medyayı çok kullanmam.
  • — Same here. We study together on weekends, so we meet at the library.Ben de öyle. Hafta sonları birlikte ders çalışırız, o yüzden kütüphanede buluşuruz.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane soru var? (Cevap: 2)
  • "I don't use social media a lot." ne demek? (Cevap: Sosyal medyayı çok kullanmam.)

Alıştırma (Practice)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

study (v)
to learn a subject for school (ders çalışmak)
"We study together after school."
hang out (phr. v)
to spend free time with friends (takılmak, vakit geçirmek)
"I hang out with my friends."
post (v)
to share a photo or a video online (paylaşmak, gönderi atmak)
"They post videos every week."
follow (v)
to see someone's posts on an app (takip etmek)
"I follow my favourite team."
chat (v)
to talk with people by messages (yazışmak, sohbet etmek)
"We chat online in the evening."
practise (v)

"They practise the guitar."
train (v)

"We train twice a week."

"You watch a lot of series."

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Talk about your routines, your friends, and your hobbies. Write 5 sentences. (Rutinlerin, arkadaşların ve hobilerin hakkında 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Fiili sade kullan: I study, we hang out, they post (studies / posts değil).
  • Bir rutinini, arkadaşlarınla ne yaptığını ve sevmediğin bir şeyi (I don't...) anlat.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"Every day I ..." "My friends and I ..." "On weekends we ..." "I don't ..." "After school I ..."
Sample Answer 1. Every day I go to school by bus, and I study in the evening.
2. My friends and I hang out after school.
3. On weekends we play basketball and we train together.
4. I follow my favourite teams online, but I don't post a lot.
5. I don't like horror films, but I watch series with my friends.

Reading: My Friends and My Week

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

study · hang out · post · follow · chat · practise · train · watch · weekend · every day

İpucu
  • Önce hızlıca oku — her kelimeyi anlaman gerekmez.
  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
My Friends and My Week

Hi! I am Derin and I am fifteen. My friends and I go to the same school, and we study together in the evening. We don't like exams, but we help each other, so the week is easier.

My school is not far from my home. I get up at seven o'clock, and I have breakfast with my family. Then I walk to the bus stop with my friend Mila. We are in the same class, and we sit together. Our teachers are nice, and the lessons are fun.

I like English and art, but I don't like maths a lot. In the break, my friends and I eat a sandwich and we talk. We laugh a lot at school. After the last lesson, we say goodbye and we go home.

Mila is my best friend at school. She is funny and kind, and we sit next to each other. We share our lunch, and we help each other in class. After school, we walk home together, and we talk about our day. I am happy because we are good friends.

I live with my mum, my dad, and my brother. We eat dinner together every evening, and we talk about our day. My parents work a lot, but on weekends we cook and clean the house together. They are kind, and they help me with my homework when I need it. My brother and I don't argue much, and we share our games.

Our home is small but warm. My mum and dad are kind, and they cook great food. My brother is thirteen, and his name is Ali. In the evening, we play video games together. Sometimes we want different TV shows, but we are good friends.

On some weekends, my grandma and grandpa visit us. They are very kind, and they bring nice food. We sit in the kitchen, and we drink tea together. They love my stories about school, and they ask many questions. We are a happy family, and I love these calm days.

We have a small cat at home. Her name is Pamuk, and she is white and soft. My brother and I feed her every day, and we give her fresh water. At night, she is often on my bed. I love her a lot.

I like food a lot. For breakfast, I eat eggs and bread. At school, my friends and I buy fruit and water. In the evening, we all eat dinner together, and my favourite meal is pasta. On weekends, my mum and I make a cake, and we eat it with tea.

After school, we hang out in the park. My friend Kaan and I train for basketball twice a week, and we practise a lot because we want to win our next match. We don't stop when we are tired, and we always give our best.

I am not very tall, but I am fast. Kaan is my best friend on the team, and we play together. Our coach is kind, and we listen to him. We wear blue shirts in every match. When we win, we are so happy, and we eat pizza together.

In the evening, I chat with my classmates online. We follow the same teams, and we post short videos on weekends. I don't use social media a lot, but I check my messages every day. My friends and I also plan our weekend on a group chat.

I have many hobbies. I play the guitar, and I sing simple songs. My friends and I like the same music, and we share new songs online. On rainy days, we stay at home, and we watch films. I don't like horror films, but I love funny films.

My friends are really nice. On Saturdays we meet at the library, and we do our homework together. We don't play games all day. Do you have good friends like this? I am lucky because my friends and I like the same things. We watch films, we listen to music, and we laugh a lot.

On Saturday, I clean my room in the morning. Then my friends and I meet in the park or at the library. We ride our bikes, and we play basketball. In the afternoon, I do my homework, so I am free in the evening. I like my calm weekends.

I want to be a good student. English is important for me, and I practise every day. My friends and I help each other with new words. We read short stories in class, and we write simple sentences. Little by little, we get better every week. I am proud of my friends and my family.

I don't watch much TV. I like books and I read every night. My brother and I read comics too, and we talk about them. Today I am happy because we don't have homework tonight!

Writing: My Week with Friends

Rutinlerini ve arkadaşlarınla neler yaptığını anlat ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Write about your routines and friends; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI study in the evening and I read every night.
butamaI like sports but I don't watch much TV.
becauseçünküWe practise a lot because we want to win.
sobu yüzdenMy friends live near me, so we hang out every day.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but, because ya da so.

Task (Görev)

Write about your week with your friends. (Arkadaşlarınla geçen haftan hakkında yaz.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • a daily routine (her gün yaptığın bir şey)
  • what you do with your friends (arkadaşlarınla ne yaptığın)
  • a sport or a hobby you do (yaptığın bir spor ya da hobi)
  • one thing you don't do (yapmadığın bir şey — I don't...)
  • how you feel today (bugün nasıl hissettiğin)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Hello! Every day I ..." "My friends and I ..." "On weekends we ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) Hello! Every day I go to school by bus, and I study in the evening. After school, my friends and I hang out in the park. We play basketball and we train twice a week because we want to win. I follow my favourite teams online, but I don't post a lot. Today I am happy because we don't have homework!
