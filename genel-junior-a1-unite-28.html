Grammar: Present Continuous (am / is / are + -ing)

Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra oyunu oyna! (Read the rules, then play the game.)

Present Continuous Nedir? (What is Present Continuous?)

Şu anda, tam bu dakikada olan şeyleri anlatmak için Present Continuous (Şimdiki Zaman) kullanırız. Oyun alanında herkes bir şey yapıyor:

  • "The children are playing."Çocuklar oynuyor. (şimdi)
  • "He is climbing the ladder."Merdivene tırmanıyor.
  • "She is swinging."Salıncakta sallanıyor.
  • "They are running."Koşuyorlar.
  • "I am jumping."Zıplıyorum.

Kalıp çok kolay: am / is / are + fiil + -ing. Türkçedeki "-yor" ekinin işini bu yapar!

Dikkat!
  • I ile → am. he / she / it ile → is. you / we / they ile → are.
  • Bir işaret ara: now (şimdi), Look! (Bak!). Bunlar Present Continuous'un habercisidir.

1. am / is / are + fiil-ing (be + -ing)

Önce doğru yardımcı fiili (am / is / are) seç, sonra fiile -ing ekle:

KişibeÖrnekTürkçesi
IamI am jumping.Zıplıyorum.
he / sheisShe is sliding.Kayıyor.
youareYou are climbing.Tırmanıyorsun.
weareWe are playing.Oynuyoruz.
theyareThey are running.Koşuyorlar.
Kolay yol
  • Kısaca: I'm playing · She's sliding · They're running. (am/is/are'in kısası.)
  • Yardımcı fiili unutma! "She is playing" ✓ (She playing ✗).

2. Olumsuz: not (Negative)

Bir şeyin olmadığını söylemek için am / is / are'den sonra not koyarız:

OlumluOlumsuzÖrnekTürkçesi
I am jumpingI am not jumpingI am not jumping.Zıplamıyorum.
He is runningHe isn't runningHe isn't running.Koşmuyor.
They are slidingThey aren't slidingThey aren't sliding.Kaymıyorlar.
Not
  • isn't = is not, aren't = are not. Fiil yine -ing kalır: "He isn't running" ✓.

3. Soru: Is/Are ...? & What ... doing? (Questions)

Soru sormak için Is ya da Are'i cümlenin başına alırız:

  • You are climbing. → Are you climbing?Tırmanıyorsun. → Tırmanıyor musun?
  • She is swinging. → Is she swinging?Sallanıyor. → Sallanıyor mu?

Ne yapıyor? diye sormak için What ... doing? kullanırız:

SoruCevapTürkçesi
What are you doing?I am running.Ne yapıyorsun? — Koşuyorum.
What is he doing?He is climbing.O ne yapıyor? — Tırmanıyor.
Are they playing?Yes, they are.Oynuyorlar mı? — Evet.
Dikkat!
  • Kısa cevap: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't. / Yes, they are.

4. -ing Nasıl Yazılır? (-ing Spelling)

Çoğu fiile sadece -ing ekleriz. Ama iki küçük kural daha var:

KuralFiil-ing hâli
Normal: +ingplay, jump, climbplaying, jumping, climbing
Sonda e varsa: e düşerslide, ride, makesliding, riding, making
Kısa fiil: son harf çift olurrun, swim, sitrunning, swimming, sitting
Not
  • run → running (n çift olur), slide → sliding (e düşer), swing → swinging (normal).

5. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Talk)

İki çocuk oyun alanında konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What are you doing?Ne yapıyorsun?
  • — I am climbing the ladder! Are you coming?Merdivene tırmanıyorum! Geliyor musun?
  • — No, I am not. I am swinging. Look at Mavi!Hayır. Salıncakta sallanıyorum. Mavi'ye bak!
  • — Mavi is sliding down the big slide. She is laughing.Mavi büyük kaydıraktan kayıyor. Gülüyor.
  • — The boys are running too. They are playing a game.Çocuklar da koşuyor. Bir oyun oynuyorlar.
  • — Come on! We are all playing together now!Hadi! Hepimiz şimdi birlikte oynuyoruz!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç çocuk tırmanıyor? (Cevap: 1)
  • "She is sliding." ne demek? (Cevap: Kayıyor.)

Speaking: Playground Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan! (Learn the words, then use them.)

play (v)
to have fun with a game (oynamak)
"The children are playing."
run (v)
to move very fast with your legs (koşmak)
"They are running."
jump (v)
to push up from the ground with your legs (zıplamak, atlamak)
"I am jumping high."
climb (v)
to go up something using hands and feet (tırmanmak)
"He is climbing the ladder."
slide (v)
to move down smoothly (kaymak)
"She is sliding down."
swing (v)
to move up and down on a seat on ropes (salıncakta sallanmak)
"We are swinging."
playground (n)
a place with swings and slides (oyun alanı, oyun parkı)
"The playground is fun."
ladder (n)
steps you climb up (merdiven)
"I am climbing the ladder."
fast (adv)
very quickly (hızlı, hızlıca)
Zıttı: slow (yavaş)
"They are running fast."
now (adv)
at this moment (şimdi, şu an)
"We are playing now."
Oyun alanındaki arkadaşlar: uğur böceği, kelebek ve çiçek.

Your Turn! (Senin Sıran)

Talk about what the children are doing at the playground. Write 5 sentences. (Oyun alanında çocuklar ne yapıyor, anlat. 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Present Continuous kullan: am / is / are + fiil-ing (is playing, are running).
  • En az bir is ve bir are cümlesi kur, bir de kendin için I am ... de.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"I am ..." "He is ..." "She is ..." "They are ..." "We are ..."
Sample Answer 1. I am at the playground with my friends.
2. The children are playing and they are laughing.
3. He is climbing the ladder now.
4. She is sliding down the big slide.
5. We are running fast and we are jumping!

Reading: Fun at the Playground

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

play · run · jump · climb · slide · swing · playground · fast

İpucu

  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
Fun at the Playground

It is a sunny day. It is Saturday, so the children are not at school today. Ela and her friends are at the playground. They are having a lot of fun. Everyone is doing something!

The playground is next to a green park. There are tall trees and soft grass. There are swings, a big slide, a ladder, and a sandbox. Many families are here today. The mothers and fathers are sitting on the benches. They are watching the children and talking.

Look at the swings! Ela is swinging. She is going up and down. Her friend Can is next to her. He is not swinging now. He is climbing the ladder. Can wants to go on the big slide.

Under the slide, a small girl named Zeynep is drawing with chalk. She is making a big yellow sun on the ground. Next to her, a boy is eating an apple. He is watching his friends and smiling. Everyone is busy and happy in the warm sun.

Two boys, Deniz and Toprak, are running. They are playing a game of tag. They are running very fast. A little dog is running with them too. The dog is barking and jumping. It is happy.

The little dog runs after the two boys. It is a brown dog with a red collar. It is barking loudly: woof, woof! Then it jumps up and down. The boys laugh and run faster. The dog is playing the game too, and it is very happy.

Near the tree, some girls are jumping. They are counting: one, two, three! Mavi is not jumping. She is sliding down the big slide, and she is laughing.

The girls near the tree are having fun with their game. They jump and they clap their hands. One, two, three, jump! But Mavi does not want to jump today. She likes the big slide more. She is sliding down again and again, and she is laughing.

On the other side of the playground, there is an ice cream man. Some children are buying ice cream from him. A baby is sitting in a pushchair and clapping her small hands. The birds are singing in the tall trees. Their mothers wave and smile at them from the benches. It is a perfect day to play outside, and nobody wants to go home yet.

"Come here!" Ela says. "We are playing a new game." Now all the children are playing together. The sun is going down, and it is time to go home. The children are tired, but they are smiling. "Tomorrow we are coming again!" they say.

It is a beautiful sunny day, with no rain and no wind. The children are playing, running, and laughing together. Nobody is sad, and nobody is angry. When the sun goes down, everyone is a little tired. "What a fun day!" says Ela. "Let's come back tomorrow!"

Writing: At the Playground

Oyun alanında çocuklar ne yapıyor, yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about the playground; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveThey are running and they are laughing.
butamaI am swinging but he is climbing.
becauseçünküI am happy because we are playing.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about a playground. What are the children doing now? (Bir oyun alanında çocuklar şu anda ne yapıyor, yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • where the children are (nerede olduğu: at the playground)
  • what they are doing (are playing, are running)
  • one thing with is (he/she is climbing / sliding)
  • what you are doing (I am ...)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"The children are ..." "He is ..." "She is ..." "I am ..."

Sample Answer (~40 words) It is a sunny day and the children are at the playground. They are playing together. My friend is climbing the ladder, and a girl is sliding down the slide. Two boys are running fast because they are playing a game. I am swinging!
