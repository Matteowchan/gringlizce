"favourite" Nedir? (What is a "favourite"?)

Bir şeyi çok ama çok seversen, o senin "favourite"indir — yani en sevdiğin. Bir renk, bir hayvan ya da bir yiyecek olabilir. Mesela:

  • "My favourite colour is blue."En sevdiğim renk mavi. (renk)
  • "My favourite animal is a cat."En sevdiğim hayvan kedi. (hayvan)
  • "My favourite food is pizza."En sevdiğim yemek pizza. (yiyecek)

Bir şeyden hoşlanırsan "like" (severim) deriz. Hoşlanmazsan "don't like" (sevmem):

  • "I like ice cream."Dondurmayı severim.
  • "I don't like onions."Soğanı sevmem.
  • "She likes dogs."O köpekleri sever.

Şimdi bu güzel yapıları birlikte adım adım öğreneceğiz!

1. "My favourite ... is ..." (En sevdiğim ...)

En sevdiğin bir şeyi söylemek çok kolay. Şu kalıbı kullan: My favourite + şey + is + cevap.

KalıpÖrnekTürkçesi
My favourite colour is ...My favourite colour is green.En sevdiğim renk yeşil.
My favourite animal is ...My favourite animal is a dog.En sevdiğim hayvan köpek.
My favourite food is ...My favourite food is pizza.En sevdiğim yemek pizza.
My favourite toy is ...My favourite toy is a car.En sevdiğim oyuncak araba.
Dikkat!
  • Tek bir şey için is kullan: My favourite colour is red.
  • Birden çok şey için are kullan: My favourite animals are cats and dogs.
  • is'i unutma! "My favourite colour blue." ✗ → "My favourite colour is blue." ✓

2. "I like ..." (Severim)

Sevdiğin şeyleri söylemek için like kullanırız. I / you / we / they ile like; ama he / she / it ile sonuna -s ekleriz: likes.

KişiFiilÖrnekTürkçesi
I / YoulikeI like apples.Elmaları severim.
We / TheylikeWe like cats.Kedileri severiz.
He / ShelikesShe likes ice cream.O dondurmayı sever.
ItlikesMy dog likes balls.Köpeğim topları sever.
Kolay yol
  • I / you / we / they → like.
  • he / she / it (tek kişi/şey) → likes (sonuna küçük bir s).
  • Genel bir şeyi severken çoğul kullan: I like cats. (bütün kediler)

3. "Sevmem" Demek: don't like (Negative)

Bir şeyi sevmediğini söylemek için don't like kullanırız. He / she / it için doesn't like deriz.

KişiOlumsuzÖrnekTürkçesi
I / You / We / Theydon't likeI don't like onions.Soğanı sevmem.
He / She / Itdoesn't likeShe doesn't like spiders.O örümcekleri sevmez.
Not
  • doesn't'ten sonra fiile -s ekleme: "She doesn't likes." ✗ → "She doesn't like." ✓

4. Soru Sormak (Questions)

Favori bir şeyi sormak için What is your favourite ...? deriz. Bir şeyi sevip sevmediğini sormak için Do you like ...? kullanırız.

  • What is your favourite colour? → My favourite colour is red.En sevdiğin renk ne? → En sevdiğim renk kırmızı.
  • Do you like pizza? → Yes, I do!Pizzayı sever misin? → Evet, severim!

Kısa cevaplar çok kolaydır:

SoruEvetHayır
Do you like cats?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does he like football?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Dikkat!
  • he / she / it için soruda Does kullan: "Does she like dogs?"
  • Cevapta "like" tekrar etme: "Yes, I like." ✗ → "Yes, I do." ✓

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
My favourite colour blue.My favourite colour is blue."is" atlanamaz.
I likes apples.I like apples.I → like (s takılmaz).
She like cats.She likes cats.he / she / it → likes.
I no like onions.I don't like onions.Olumsuz: don't like.
Do you like pizza? Yes, I like.Yes, I do.Kısa cevap: Yes, I do.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Talk)

İki çocuk favori şeylerinden bahsediyor. Koyu yazılan favourite ve like kelimelerine dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Hi! What is your favourite colour?Selam! En sevdiğin renk ne?
  • — My favourite colour is purple. And you?En sevdiğim renk mor. Ya sen?
  • — I like blue. My favourite colour is blue!Ben maviyi severim. En sevdiğim renk mavi!
  • — Do you like animals?Hayvanları sever misin?
  • — Yes, I do! My favourite animal is a dog.Evet, severim! En sevdiğim hayvan köpek.
  • — I like dogs too. My favourite food is pizza. Do you like pizza?Ben de köpekleri severim. En sevdiğim yemek pizza. Pizzayı sever misin?
  • — Yes, I do! Pizza is my favourite food too!Evet, severim! Pizza benim de en sevdiğim yemek!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane soru var? (Cevap: 3)
  • Bir arkadaş "dog" için "likes" mi "like" mi der? (Cevap: I like dogs)

Let's Play! (Alıştırma)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Speaking: My Favourite Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan! (Learn the words, then use them.)

favourite (adj)
the one you like best (en sevdiğin)
"Blue is my favourite colour."
colour (n)
red, blue, green, yellow... (renk)
"What is your favourite colour?"
animal (n)
a cat, a dog, a bird... (hayvan)
"My favourite animal is a cat."
food (n)
things we eat (yiyecek, yemek)
"Pizza is my favourite food."
fruit (n)
apples, bananas, oranges... (meyve)
"I like fruit. Bananas are nice!"
like (v)
to think something is nice (sevmek, hoşlanmak)
Zıttı: don't like (sevmemek)
"I like ice cream!"
pizza (n)
food with cheese and tomatoes (pizza)
"My favourite food is pizza."
dog (n)
an animal that says "woof" (köpek)
"My dog is small and brown."
ice cream (n)
a cold, sweet food (dondurma)
"My sister likes ice cream."
best (adj)
number one, the top (en iyi)
"Green is the best colour!"
Bahçedeki arkadaşlar: uğur böceği, kelebek ve çiçek.

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

favourite · colour · animal · food · fruit · like · pizza · dog · best

İpucu

  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
My Favourite Things

Hello! My name is Emir. I am eight years old. These are my favourite things. I want to tell you all about them!

I live in a small house with my mum, my dad and my sister Ada. We are a happy family. I have brown eyes and short hair. I am a friendly boy. I go to school in the morning, and I come home in the afternoon. Our house has a small garden with green plants, and I am a good boy at home.

My favourite colour is green. Green is the colour of grass and trees. My bag is green, and my pencil is green too. My favourite colour is not red. Red is nice, but green is the best!

Green is everywhere! The trees are green and the grass in my garden is green. My favourite t-shirt is green too. My mum says, "Green is a good colour, Emir!" I look for green things every day. Boncuk has a green ball too, and I kick it in the garden. Green makes me happy.

My favourite animal is a dog. My dog's name is Boncuk. He is small and brown. He is very funny. I like dogs, and I like cats too. I don't like spiders. Spiders are not my favourite!

Boncuk is a good dog. He is small, brown and very funny. Every morning, he runs to me and we play in the garden. He can run and he can jump. He likes his ball a lot. At night, Boncuk sleeps in his little bed. I love my dog very much.

My favourite food is pizza. I like pizza with cheese and tomatoes. I like apples and bananas too. I don't like onions. My sister Ada likes ice cream. Ice cream is her favourite food. She likes chocolate ice cream.

On Sunday, my family eats pizza together. Mum makes a big pizza with cheese and tomatoes. It is hot and yummy. I eat two pieces! I drink water or milk. After lunch, we have fruit, and I like apples and bananas. My sister Ada eats her chocolate ice cream. We sit at the table and we talk. Pizza night is the best!

My favourite toy is a red car. It is fast and small. My favourite day is Saturday because I am not at school. On Saturday, I play with my friends. My best friend is Can. He likes football, but I like drawing.

I love Saturday! I get up and I play with my friends in the park. Can and I are best friends. Can likes football, and he plays with his ball. I like drawing, and I draw cars, dogs and trees. We have fun together. In the evening, I read a book with Ada, and then I go to bed happy. Saturday is a happy day for me.

What are your favourite things? What is your favourite colour? Tell me!

Writing: My Favourite Things

Favori şeylerini yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about your favourites; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI like cats and I like dogs.
butamaI like pizza but I don't like onions.
becauseçünküGreen is my favourite because it is nice.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your favourite things. (Favori şeylerin hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • your favourite colour (en sevdiğin renk)
  • your favourite animal (en sevdiğin hayvan)
  • your favourite food (en sevdiğin yiyecek)
  • one thing you like and one thing you don't like (sevdiğin ve sevmediğin bir şey)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"My favourite colour is ..." "My favourite animal is ..." "I like ... but I don't like ..."
