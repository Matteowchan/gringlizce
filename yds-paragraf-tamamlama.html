Paragraph CompletionParagraf Tamamlama

Bu soru tipinde tek bir kelimeyi değil, paragrafın mantık örgüsünü tamamlayan cümleyi ararsın.

In this question type you look not for a single word but for the sentence that completes the paragraph's web of logic.

Paragraf tamamlama, akademik bir paragraftaki boşluğa hem anlamca hem de bağlaç/gönderim açısından oturan tam cümleyi seçmektir. Boşluğun konumu (baş, orta, son) hangi tür cümlenin gerektiğini doğrudan belirler.

Paragraph completion means choosing the full sentence that fits the gap in an academic paragraph both in meaning and in terms of connectors/reference. The position of the gap (beginning, middle, end) directly determines what type of sentence is needed.

Topic Summary
  • Gap at the start → topic/introductory sentence (the following sentences expand on it).
  • Gap in the middle → bridge sentence (two-way fit with what comes before + after).
  • Gap at the end → conclusion/summary sentence (therefore, thus, in short).
  • Match the connector/reference: what do however, this, such, these point to?
  • The longest option = not the correct one; watch out for a distractor that's on-topic but breaks the flow.

Paragraf tamamlama sorusunda 4-5 cümlelik akademik bir paragrafın bir cümlesi çıkarılmış ve yerine ---- işareti konmuştur. Altta beş şık vardır; her biri tam bir cümledir (özne + yüklem, yaklaşık 10-18 kelime). Tek kelime, bağlaç ya da yarım ifade şık olmaz. Senin görevin, boşluğa hem anlamca (coherence) hem de bağlantı öğeleriyle (cohesion) tam oturan cümleyi bulmaktır.

Doğru cümle üç şeyi aynı anda sağlar: paragrafın konu bütünlüğünü korur, komşu cümlelerle anlam akışını sürdürür ve however, therefore, this, such, these gibi bağlaç ile gönderim öğelerini doğru bağlar.

Boşluğun konumu, aradığın cümlenin türünü belirler:

  • Başta ise: paragrafı açan konu cümlesi. Sonraki cümleler onu örneklendirir ya da açıklar.
  • Ortada ise: bir önceki ile bir sonraki cümleyi birbirine bağlayan köprü cümlesi.
  • Sonda ise: paragrafı toparlayan sonuç veya özet cümlesi.

Bu yüzden ilk bakışın boşluğa değil, boşluğun nerede olduğuna olmalı: konum, sana daha okumadan cümlenin işlevini söyler.

In a paragraph completion question, one sentence has been removed from a 4-5 sentence academic paragraph and replaced with a ---- mark. There are five options below; each is a complete sentence (subject + predicate, about 10-18 words). A single word, a connector or a half expression is never an option. Your task is to find the sentence that fits the gap perfectly both in meaning (coherence) and in connecting elements (cohesion).

The correct sentence does three things at once: it preserves the paragraph's topical unity, maintains the flow of meaning with the neighbouring sentences, and correctly links the however, therefore, this, such, these-type connector and reference elements.

The position of the gap determines the type of sentence you're looking for:

  • If at the start: the topic sentence that opens the paragraph. The following sentences illustrate or explain it.
  • If in the middle: the bridge sentence that links the previous and the following sentence.
  • If at the end: the conclusion or summary sentence that wraps up the paragraph.

That's why your first glance should be not at the gap but at where the gap is: the position tells you the sentence's function before you even read.

Paragraf tamamlamada acele, en büyük hata kaynağıdır. Şu sırayı bozma:

  1. Önce boşluksuz paragrafı oku, konuyu yakala. Boşluğu geçici olarak yok say. Paragraf ne anlatıyor, ana fikir ne, ton nasıl (tanım mı, karşıtlık mı, sebep-sonuç mu)? Konuyu bir cümleyle içinden özetleyebilmelisin.
  2. Boşluğun konumundan cümle türünü belirle. Baştaysa konu cümlesi, ortadaysa köprü, sondaysa sonuç ararsın. Bu, beş şıktan en az ikisini genellikle daha okurken eler.
  3. Komşu cümlelerle bağ kur. Boşluktan hemen önceki ve sonraki cümleye bak. Sonraki cümle However ile başlıyorsa boşluk bir karşıtlık kurmalı; This / These / Such ile başlıyorsa boşlukta o sözcüğün işaret ettiği somut bir kavram geçmeli.
  4. Şıkları akış + dilbilgisi ile ele. Doğru şık zaman uyumunu, gönderimi ve mantığı korur. Konuyla ilgili görünen ama yeni bir konu açan, yanlış bağlaç ilişkisi kuran ya da zaman/özne uyumsuzluğu taşıyan şıkları çıkar.
Altın kural: Seçtiğin cümleyi boşluğa koyup paragrafı baştan sona bir daha oku. Akış hiç takılmadan, doğal biçimde ilerliyorsa cevabın odur.

In paragraph completion, haste is the biggest source of error. Don't break this order:

  1. First read the paragraph without the gap and grasp the topic. Ignore the gap for now. What is the paragraph about, what is the main idea, what is the tone (a definition, a contrast, cause-and-effect)? You should be able to summarize the topic to yourself in one sentence.
  2. Determine the sentence type from the gap's position. If it's at the start you look for a topic sentence, if in the middle a bridge, if at the end a conclusion. This usually eliminates at least two of the five options while you're still reading.
  3. Connect with the neighbouring sentences. Look at the sentence right before and right after the gap. If the following sentence starts with However, the gap must set up a contrast; if it starts with This / These / Such, then a concrete concept that this word points to must appear in the gap.
  4. Eliminate the options with flow + grammar. The correct option preserves tense agreement, reference and logic. Discard the options that look on-topic but open a new subject, set up a wrong connector relationship, or carry a tense/subject mismatch.
Golden rule: Put your chosen sentence in the gap and read the paragraph again from start to finish. If the flow moves along naturally without any snag, that is your answer.

Doğru cümlenin işlevi, boşluğun paragraftaki yerine göre neredeyse sabittir. Bu tabloyu içselleştirirsen çeldiricilerin yarısını daha okumadan elersin.

The function of the correct sentence is almost fixed according to the gap's place in the paragraph. If you internalize this table, you eliminate half of the distractors before even reading.

PositionExpected sentenceClue
At the startTopic / introductory sentence: sets up the general framework of the paragraph.The following sentences support this idea with examples, data or explanation. There is a move from general to specific.
In the middleBridge sentence: links the previous idea to the next one, provides the transition.Two-way fit is essential. The this, these, such or however, therefore in the following sentence points to the gap.
At the endConclusion / summary sentence: wraps up the paragraph or draws an inference.Often begins with Therefore, Thus, As a result, In short. It brings no new information; it closes the existing idea.

Note: A gap at the start must give the main idea; a very narrow sentence focused on a single detail cannot be an introduction. A gap at the end cannot open a new discussion; if it does, it's wrong.

Örnek 1 (baş boşluk).

---- For instance, coral reefs, which support a quarter of all marine species, are highly sensitive to even minor increases in water temperature. When the surrounding water grows too warm, corals expel the algae living in their tissues and gradually turn white, a process known as bleaching. Repeated bleaching events, now occurring more frequently, can leave entire reef systems lifeless within a few decades.

  1. Coral reefs are among the most colourful ecosystems that tourists travel great distances to see.
  2. Marine ecosystems are remarkably vulnerable to the temperature changes driven by global warming.
  3. Scientists have recently developed new techniques to grow corals in controlled laboratories.
  4. Ocean currents distribute nutrients that allow diverse marine species to flourish worldwide.
  5. Overfishing remains the single greatest threat to the survival of tropical fish populations.
Doğru: B. Boşluk başta olduğu için konu cümlesi gerekir. Paragrafın geri kalanı mercan resiflerinin ısı artışına duyarlılığını (ağarma) anlatıyor; yani örnek, "deniz ekosistemlerinin sıcaklık değişimine kırılganlığı" fikrini destekliyor. For instance ("örneğin") ifadesi, boşluğun bir genel iddia olması gerektiğini kanıtlar; mercan resifi bu iddianın örneğidir. B tam bu genel çerçeveyi kurar. A ve C konuyla ilgili ama ısı/kırılganlık akışını başlatmaz; D ve E (akıntı, aşırı avlanma) paragrafın hiç değinmediği yan konulardır.

Örnek 2 (orta boşluk).

For most of history, maps were drawn by hand and reflected the limited knowledge of their makers. ---- These digital tools can now update themselves in real time, integrating traffic data, satellite imagery and user reports within seconds. As a result, the modern traveller rarely encounters the blank or mistaken spaces that once filled older charts.

  1. Ancient sailors relied on the stars far more than on any drawn representation of the coast.
  2. The invention of the printing press made identical copies of a single map widely available.
  3. Today, however, mapmaking has been transformed by computers and constantly updated databases.
  4. Many early cartographers deliberately left unexplored regions decorated with imaginary creatures.
  5. Reading a paper map still requires a skill that fewer young people seem to possess.
Doğru: C. Boşluk ortada; bir köprü cümlesi gerekiyor. Önceki cümle geçmişteki elle çizim ve sınırlı bilgiden söz ediyor. Sonraki cümle ise These digital tools ("bu dijital araçlar") diyor; demek ki boşlukta dijital araçlardan/bilgisayarlardan ilk kez söz edilmeli ki These bir şeye işaret edebilsin. C hem however ile geçmiş-bugün karşıtlığını kurar hem de "computers and databases" ile sonraki cümledeki gönderime zemin hazırlar. B (matbaa) ve A (yıldızlar) geçmişte kalır, sonraki "digital tools" ifadesini karşılamaz; D ve E akışı ileri taşımaz.

Örnek 3 (son boşluk).

Sleep researchers once assumed that the brain simply shut down during rest. Recent studies, however, reveal intense activity: during deep sleep the brain replays the day's events and strengthens newly formed memories. It also clears out metabolic waste that accumulates while we are awake. ----

  1. Therefore, far from being a passive state, sleep is an active period essential to mental health.
  2. Many adults nevertheless continue to sleep fewer hours than doctors typically recommend.
  3. The amount of sleep a person needs changes considerably across different stages of life.
  4. Caffeine consumed late in the day can significantly delay the onset of deep sleep.
  5. Dreams have fascinated philosophers and scientists alike for thousands of years.
Doğru: A. Boşluk sonda; sonuç/özet cümlesi gerekir. Paragraf boyunca uykunun aslında aktif olduğu kanıtlanıyor (anı pekiştirme, atık temizliği). A cümlesi Therefore ile bu kanıtları toparlar ve "uyku pasif değil, aktif ve zorunlu bir süreçtir" çıkarımını yapar; paragrafın açılışındaki "beyin kapanır" yanılgısını da tam kapatır. B, C, D uykuyla ilgilidir ama yeni konular açar (uyku süresi, kafein) ve sonuç işlevi görmez; E ise tümüyle konu dışıdır.

Example 1 (gap at the start).

---- For instance, coral reefs, which support a quarter of all marine species, are highly sensitive to even minor increases in water temperature. When the surrounding water grows too warm, corals expel the algae living in their tissues and gradually turn white, a process known as bleaching. Repeated bleaching events, now occurring more frequently, can leave entire reef systems lifeless within a few decades.

  1. Coral reefs are among the most colourful ecosystems that tourists travel great distances to see.
  2. Marine ecosystems are remarkably vulnerable to the temperature changes driven by global warming.
  3. Scientists have recently developed new techniques to grow corals in controlled laboratories.
  4. Ocean currents distribute nutrients that allow diverse marine species to flourish worldwide.
  5. Overfishing remains the single greatest threat to the survival of tropical fish populations.
Correct: B. Because the gap is at the start, a topic sentence is needed. The rest of the paragraph describes coral reefs' sensitivity to temperature rise (bleaching); that is, the example supports the idea of "the vulnerability of marine ecosystems to temperature change". The phrase For instance proves that the gap must be a general claim; the coral reef is an example of this claim. B sets up exactly this general framework. A and C are on-topic but don't start the temperature/vulnerability flow; D and E (currents, overfishing) are side topics the paragraph never touches.

Example 2 (gap in the middle).

For most of history, maps were drawn by hand and reflected the limited knowledge of their makers. ---- These digital tools can now update themselves in real time, integrating traffic data, satellite imagery and user reports within seconds. As a result, the modern traveller rarely encounters the blank or mistaken spaces that once filled older charts.

  1. Ancient sailors relied on the stars far more than on any drawn representation of the coast.
  2. The invention of the printing press made identical copies of a single map widely available.
  3. Today, however, mapmaking has been transformed by computers and constantly updated databases.
  4. Many early cartographers deliberately left unexplored regions decorated with imaginary creatures.
  5. Reading a paper map still requires a skill that fewer young people seem to possess.
Correct: C. The gap is in the middle; a bridge sentence is needed. The previous sentence talks about hand-drawing in the past and the limited knowledge of the time. The following sentence, meanwhile, says These digital tools; so the gap must mention digital tools/computers for the first time, so that These can point to something. C both sets up the past-present contrast with however and, with "computers and databases", prepares the ground for the reference in the following sentence. B (printing press) and A (stars) stay in the past and don't match the later "digital tools" phrase; D and E don't carry the flow forward.

Example 3 (gap at the end).

Sleep researchers once assumed that the brain simply shut down during rest. Recent studies, however, reveal intense activity: during deep sleep the brain replays the day's events and strengthens newly formed memories. It also clears out metabolic waste that accumulates while we are awake. ----

  1. Therefore, far from being a passive state, sleep is an active period essential to mental health.
  2. Many adults nevertheless continue to sleep fewer hours than doctors typically recommend.
  3. The amount of sleep a person needs changes considerably across different stages of life.
  4. Caffeine consumed late in the day can significantly delay the onset of deep sleep.
  5. Dreams have fascinated philosophers and scientists alike for thousands of years.
Correct: A. The gap is at the end; a conclusion/summary sentence is needed. Throughout the paragraph it is proven that sleep is actually active (memory consolidation, waste clearance). Sentence A gathers this evidence with Therefore and draws the inference that "sleep is not passive but an active and essential process"; it also fully closes the "the brain shuts down" misconception from the paragraph's opening. B, C, D are related to sleep but open new topics (sleep duration, caffeine) and don't serve as a conclusion; E is entirely off-topic.

Paragrafı okumadan boşluğa atlamak. Doğrudan şıklara dalıp boşluğa "kulağa hoş gelen" cümleyi koymak en yaygın hatadır. Konuyu bir bütün olarak kavramadan hiçbir şık güvenilir değildir.

Sadece boşluğun bitişiğindeki cümleye bakmak. Özellikle orta boşlukta doğru cümle iki yönlü uyum ister. Yalnızca önceki cümleyle uyumlu görünen bir şık, sonraki cümleyle (örneğin bir This / These gönderimiyle) çelişebilir.

Konuyla ilgili ama akışı bozan şıkkı seçmek. Çeldiriciler çoğu zaman paragrafın genel temasıyla alakalıdır; bu yüzden "tanıdık" gelir. Oysa doğru cümle temayla değil, o cümlenin bulunduğu yerdeki mantık akışıyla uyumlu olandır. "İlgili" ile "yerine oturan" aynı şey değildir.

Yanlış bağlaç ve zaman ilişkisi. However beklenen yerde sebep-sonuç (therefore) kuran, ya da paragraf geçmiş zamandayken ani bir geniş zamana kayan şık yanlıştır. Ayrıca en uzun şıkkı otomatik doğru sanma; uzunluk bir ipucu değildir.

Jumping to the gap without reading the paragraph. Diving straight into the options and putting the "nice-sounding" sentence in the gap is the most common mistake. Without grasping the topic as a whole, no option is reliable.

Looking only at the sentence adjacent to the gap. Especially with a gap in the middle, the correct sentence requires a two-way fit. An option that looks compatible only with the previous sentence may contradict the following one (for example a This / These reference).

Choosing an option that's on-topic but breaks the flow. Distractors are often related to the paragraph's general theme; that's why they feel "familiar". But the correct sentence is the one compatible not with the theme but with the logical flow at the place where that sentence sits. "Related" and "fitting into place" are not the same thing.

Wrong connector and tense relationship. An option that sets up cause-and-effect (therefore) where However is expected, or that suddenly shifts to the present tense while the paragraph is in the past, is wrong. Also, don't assume the longest option is automatically correct; length is not a clue.

Cevabı işaretlemeden önce şu beş maddeyi zihninden geçir:

  1. Konuyu yakaladım mı? Paragrafın ana fikrini bir cümleyle özetleyebiliyorum.
  2. Konumu tespit ettim mi? Boşluk baş (konu), orta (köprü) ya da son (sonuç) mu, buna göre cümle türünü belirledim.
  3. Gönderimleri eşledim mi? Komşu cümlelerdeki this, these, such, however, therefore öğelerinin neyi işaret ettiğini boşluktaki cümle karşılıyor.
  4. Akışı bozan çeldiricileri eledim mi? Konuyla ilgili ama yeni konu açan, zaman/bağlaç uyumsuz şıkları çıkardım.
  5. Son okuma testini yaptım mı? Seçtiğim cümleyi yerine koyup paragrafı baştan sona okudum; hiçbir yerde takılmadan akıyor.
Unutma: Paragraf tamamlama bir kelime bilgisi değil, bir mantık örgüsü testidir. Doğru cümle, paragrafın düşünce zincirindeki eksik halkayı hiç boşluk yokmuş gibi tamamlar.

Before marking the answer, run through these five items in your mind:

  1. Did I grasp the topic? I can summarize the paragraph's main idea in one sentence.
  2. Did I identify the position? Is the gap at the start (topic), middle (bridge) or end (conclusion)—I determined the sentence type accordingly.
  3. Did I match the references? The sentence in the gap satisfies what the this, these, such, however, therefore elements in the neighbouring sentences point to.
  4. Did I eliminate the flow-breaking distractors? I discarded the options that are on-topic but open a new subject or have a tense/connector mismatch.
  5. Did I do the final reading test? I put my chosen sentence in place and read the paragraph from start to finish; it flows without snagging anywhere.
Remember: Paragraph completion is not a test of vocabulary but of a web of logic. The correct sentence completes the missing link in the paragraph's chain of thought as if there had never been a gap.
