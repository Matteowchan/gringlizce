Grammar: The Past Continuous

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Past Continuous?

Geçen ünitede Past Simple ile biten olayları anlattın: "I watched a film." Now we need a tense for a different idea: an action that was in progress at a moment in the past — o an sürüyor, henüz bitmemiş. Buna Past Continuous denir. Bir filmi düşün: Past Simple fotoğraftır (olay oldu, bitti), Past Continuous ise videodur (olay o an akıyordu).

  • At 8 o'clock last night, I was watching a film.Dün gece saat 8'de film izliyordum.
  • This time yesterday, we were driving to Ankara.Dün bu saatlerde Ankara'ya gidiyorduk.
  • The sun was shining and the birds were singing.Güneş parlıyordu ve kuşlar ötüyordu.

Bu bölümde önce was / were + fiil-ing yapısını kuracağız. Then we will use while and when, and we will compare Past Continuous with Past Simple — iki zamanın birlikte kullanıldığı kesinti (interruption) kalıbını göreceğiz. Adım adım gidelim.

1. Yapı (was / were + verb-ing)

Past Continuous iki parçadan oluşur: be fiilinin geçmişi (was / were) + ana fiilin -ing hâli. The subject decides was or were — özne was mı were mi seçer.

Öznebe (geçmiş)ÖrnekTürkçesi
I / he / she / itwas + -ingShe was doing her homework.Ödevini yapıyordu.
you / we / theywere + -ingThey were playing outside.Dışarıda oynuyorlardı.
TürYapıÖrnek
Olumluözne + was/were + fiil-ingI was reading a book.
Olumsuzözne + wasn't/weren't + fiil-ingI wasn't reading a book.
SoruWas/Were + özne + fiil-ing?Were you reading a book?
-ing yazımı
  • Çoğu fiil: + ing — play → playing, read → reading.
  • -e ile biten: e düşer + ing — make → making, have → having.
  • Kısa ünlü + tek sessiz: sessizi çiftle + ing — run → running, sit → sitting.

2. Zaman İfadeleri (When Do We Use It?)

Past Continuous, geçmişte belirli bir anda devam eden bir eylemi anlatır. These time markers point to that exact moment:

İfadeAnlamıÖrnek
at 8 o'clocksaat 8'deAt 8 o'clock, I was having dinner.
this time yesterdaydün bu saatlerdeThis time yesterday, we were flying.
all morning / all daybütün sabah / günIt was raining all morning.
while-irkenWhile she was cooking...

3. while ve when (The Two Key Words)

Bu ünitenin kalbi while ve when. İkisi de olayları birbirine bağlar ama farklı fiillerle çalışır. Öğren:

BağlaçYanına gelen zamanÖrnek
while
(-irken)		genelde Past Continuous (süren eylem)While I was walking, I met a friend.
when
(-diğinde)		genelde Past Simple (kısa, ani olay)I met a friend when I arrived.
İki süren eylem
  • Aynı anda süren iki eylem varsa ikisi de Past Continuous olur, genelde while ile bağlanır: "While he was cooking, I was cleaning."
  • Kural değil eğilim: while uzun/süren eylemi, when kısa/ani olayı sever.

4. Kesinti Kalıbı (Past Continuous vs Past Simple)

En sık kullanılan yapı budur. A long action was in progress (Past Continuous) when a short action interrupted it (Past Simple). Uzun eylem arka plandır; kısa eylem onu keser.

  • I was sleeping when the phone rang.Telefon çaldığında uyuyordum. (uyku sürüyordu → telefon kesti)
  • While we were watching the film, the lights went out.Filmi izlerken ışıklar gitti.
  • She was cooking dinner when her guests arrived.Misafirleri geldiğinde akşam yemeği yapıyordu.
Altın kural
  • Uzun / süren eylem = Past Continuous (was/were + -ing). Kısa / kesen eylem = Past Simple.
  • when genelde Past Simple'ın yanında, while genelde Past Continuous'un yanındadır.
  • İki eylem sırayla olduysa (biri bitti, sonra öteki başladı) ikisi de Past Simple olur: "I finished my homework and went outside."

5. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların Past Continuous'ta en sık yaptığı hatalar. Kendi cümlelerini bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
They was playing.They were playing.you/we/they → were.
I was play football.I was playing football.was/were'den sonra fiil -ing alır.
When it rained, I was walk.When it rained, I was walking.süren eylem = was + -ing.
While I was cook, the bell rang.While I was cooking, the bell rang.while + Past Continuous.
I was knowing the answer.I knew the answer.know, want, like gibi fiiller -ing almaz.
Küçük not
  • Durum fiilleri (know, like, want, have = sahip olmak) genelde Past Continuous'ta kullanılmaz; onlar Past Simple ister.

6. Uygulama: Bir Kaza Anı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. A teacher asks a student about a small accident in the playground. Koyu yazılan Past Continuous ve Past Simple yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — What were you doing at break time?Teneffüste ne yapıyordun?
  • — I was riding my bike in the playground. It was raining, so I was riding slowly.Oyun bahçesinde bisikletimi sürüyordum. Yağmur yağıyordu, o yüzden yavaş sürüyordum.
  • — And what happened?Peki ne oldu?
  • — While I was riding near the trees, a dog ran in front of my bike.Ağaçların yanında sürerken bir köpek bisikletimin önüne fırladı.
  • — Were you looking at your friends?Arkadaşlarına mı bakıyordun?
  • — No, I wasn't. I stopped quickly when I saw the dog.Hayır, bakmıyordum. Köpeği görünce hızlıca durdum.
Kendini test et
  • Çocuk kaza anında ne yapıyordu? (Cevap: was riding his bike — arka plan eylemi.)
  • Köpeği görünce olan kısa olaylar hangi zamandadır? (Cevap: ran / stopped — Past Simple.)
  • "While I was riding" — neden while + Past Continuous kullanıldı? (Bölüm 3'e bak.)

Reading: A Scary Night

Read the text, then answer the questions. (Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • What do you do when you hear a strange noise at night? (Gece tuhaf bir ses duyduğunda ne yaparsın?)
  • The story describes actions in progress. Which tense will you see a lot? (Metin süren eylemleri anlatıyor. Hangi zamanı çok göreceksin?)
  • Look at the title. Do you think the night stays scary, or does it end well? Guess! (Başlığa bak. Gece hep korkulu mu kalır, yoksa iyi mi biter? Tahmin et!)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. was/were + -ing yapılarına ve while / when bağlaçlarına dikkat et.

Words to spot (Metinde geçen, Speaking bölümünden tanıdığın kelimeler): midnight · shadow · thunder · torch · suddenly · frightened · blowing · howling · creaking · shaking · hiding · whispering

Fırtınalı bir gece: elektrik kesildi, el feneri karanlığı aydınlattı.
A Scary Night

It was a cold night in October, and the wind was blowing hard outside our house. My name is Deniz, and I am eleven years old. That night, my parents were away at my aunt's house, so my big sister Ela and I were staying at home alone with our old dog, Boncuk. We were not really afraid — not yet.

At nine o'clock, we were watching a film in the living room. Ela was eating popcorn, and I was drinking hot milk. Outside, the rain was falling harder and harder, and thunder was rumbling far away. Boncuk was sleeping quietly under the table. Everything felt warm and safe.

Then, at ten o'clock, something strange happened. While we were laughing at the film, the lights suddenly went out. The whole house was dark, and the television stopped. "Don't worry," Ela said, but her voice was a little shaky. We couldn't see anything, so we sat very still and listened.

The storm was getting louder. The wind was howling around the windows, and the old stairs were creaking. Boncuk woke up and started to growl. My heart was beating fast, because I heard a strange noise from the kitchen. Tap, tap, tap. Something was moving in the dark.

"What was that?" I whispered. Ela took my hand. "Stay here," she said. She found a torch in the drawer, and its light was shining on the walls. We were both shaking a little, but we didn't run. Slowly, we walked to the kitchen door. The tapping was still going. Tap, tap, tap.

Ela opened the door quickly and pointed the torch inside. For a moment, we saw a big black shadow near the window. I nearly screamed! But then we looked again — and we laughed. It was only a tree branch. The wind was blowing it against the glass, and it was making the tapping sound. There was no monster at all.

We felt much better after that, but the night was not over. While we were walking back to the sofa, we heard another sound — a soft cry near the front door. This time, Boncuk ran to the door and wagged his tail. Ela opened it carefully. On the step, a small wet cat was hiding under the doormat. It was cold and frightened, and it was crying for help.

"Poor little thing," Ela said. We brought the cat inside, and I gave it some warm milk. Before long, it stopped crying and fell asleep on a soft towel. The storm was still going outside, but inside our house it was warm again. We were not scared anymore.

At midnight, the lights came back on. We were so happy! When our parents came home, we told them the whole story. They were surprised, and they were proud of us too, because we stayed calm and helped the little cat.

That night was scary at first, but it became one of my favourite memories. We kept the cat, and we called her Storm. Now, when the wind is howling outside, I am not afraid. I just look at Storm sleeping on the sofa, and I smile.

Bağlaçlar Hikâyeyi Akıtır (Connectors)

Bir hikâye, alt alta dizilmiş cümleler değildir. What makes a story flow is connectors (bağlaçlar): olayları birbirine bağlar, sırayı ve ilişkiyi gösterirler. Bu ünitede özellikle when ve while işine yarayacak — çünkü süren bir eylemi (Past Continuous) kesen bir olaya (Past Simple) bağlarlar.

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparWe laughed and talked all night.
butamaZıtlık gösterirIt was cold but sunny.
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirIt was raining, so we stayed in.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI was happy because my friends came.
when-diğindeKısa/ani olayI was cooking when the bell rang.
while-irkenSüren eylemWhile we were eating, music played.
after-den sonraSonraki olayAfter the party, we went home.
before-den önceÖnceki olayWe ate before the film started.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
It was my birthday. We were sitting in the garden. It started to rain. We went inside. We were happy. It was my birthday, and we were sitting in the garden when it started to rain. So we went inside, but we were still happy.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan; en az biri when ya da while olsun.
  • when / while / after / before cümlenin başında olduğunda genellikle virgül gelir: "While we were eating, ..."
