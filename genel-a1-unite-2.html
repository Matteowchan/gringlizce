Grammar: have / has + Possessive Adjectives + Plurals

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden "have / has"?

Ünite 1'de kim olduğunu söylemeyi öğrendin. Bu ünitede neyin olduğunu söylemeyi öğreneceksin. İngilizcede sahiplik anlatmanın en yaygın yolu "have / has" fiilidir — Türkçedeki "var" anlamını verir. Ekibini, aileni ve eşyalarını tanıtırken hep bu fiili kullanacaksın:

  • "I have a new laptop."Yeni bir dizüstü bilgisayarım var. (eşya)
  • "She has two monitors."Onun iki ekranı var. (eşya)
  • "We have a big project."Büyük bir projemiz var. (iş)
  • "Our team has six members."Ekibimizin altı üyesi var. (ekip)
  • "He has two brothers."Onun iki erkek kardeşi var. (aile)

Türkçede "Laptopum var." deriz — sahip olan kişi eklerle anlaşılır. İngilizcede ise özne + have/has kalıbı kullanılır ve özneye göre have / has şeklinde değişir. Bu bölümde bu fiilin olumlu, olumsuz ve soru hâllerini, iyelik sıfatlarını (my, your, his...) ve isimlerin çoğul yapılışını işleyeceğiz.

a / an tekrarı (Ünite 1'den)
  • "have/has"ten sonra tekil bir isim geliyorsa a/an unutma: "I have a printer.", "She has an assistant."
  • Sessiz harf sesiyle başlayan kelimede a (a laptop, a task), sesli harf sesiyle başlayanda an (an office, an email).

1. Olumlu Cümle (have / has)

Kural tektir: I / you / we / they → have, he / she / it → has. Cümle kurgusu: Özne + have/has + sahip olunan şey.

Özne + fiilÖrnekTürkçesi
I haveI have a new task.Yeni bir görevim var.
You haveYou have an email from Mark.Mark'tan bir e-postan var.
He hasHe has fifteen years of experience.Onun on beş yıl deneyimi var.
She hasShe has two monitors.Onun iki ekranı var.
It hasThe office has a big kitchen.Ofisin büyük bir mutfağı var.
We haveWe have a new project.Yeni bir projemiz var.
They haveThey have a deadline on Friday.Cuma günü teslim tarihleri var.
Dikkat
  • "Our team", "the company" gibi tekil grup isimleri "it" gibi davranır: "Our team has five members."
  • have/has'in kısaltması yoktur: "am → 'm" gibi bir kısa form beklemeyin. "I've a car." ✗ → "I have a car." ✓ Cümlede have/has her zaman açık yazılır ve söylenir.
  • Dikkat: "He's..." gördüğünde bu 's = is demektir (He's a manager = He is a manager), sahiplik anlatmaz. Sahiplik için "He has a car." dersin.

2. Olumsuz Cümle (don't have / doesn't have)

Olumsuz yapmak için yardımcı fiil do/does + not kullanılır: do not have → don't have, does not have → doesn't have. Türkçedeki "yok" anlamını verir. Olumsuzda ana fiil her zaman yalın hâldedir: have (has değil).

Tam formKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi
I / You / We / They do not havedon't haveI don't have a printer.Yazıcım yok.
He / She / It does not havedoesn't haveShe doesn't have a meeting today.Onun bugün toplantısı yok.
Dikkat
  • Olumsuzda da özne kuralı işler: he/she/it → doesn't, diğerleri → don't: "Our team doesn't have a designer."
  • doesn't'tan sonra has gelmez, fiil yalın kalır: "She doesn't has a car." ✗ → "She doesn't have a car." ✓ Aynı şekilde: "I don't has..." ✗ → "I don't have..." ✓
  • Mini not: don't/doesn't yapısını (geniş zamanın olumsuzu) Ünite 3-4'te derinlemesine öğreneceksin; şimdilik "yok" demek için bu iki kalıbı ezberle.

3. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru yapmak için başa yardımcı fiil Do/Does gelir, özneden sonra fiil yalın hâlde kalır: You have a laptop. → Do you have a laptop? — he/she/it öznelerinde Does kullanılır: Does she have a big desk?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Do you have a laptop?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she have a big desk?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Does the team have a manager?Yes, it does.No, it doesn't.
Do we have a meeting at ten?Yes, we do.No, we don't.
Do they have a new project?Yes, they do.No, they don't.
Önemli kural: soruda ve olumsuzda yardımcı fiil do/does
  • Soru have/has ile değil, do/does ile kurulur: "Has she a laptop?" ✗ → "Does she have a laptop?" ✓
  • Does'tan sonra fiil yalın kalır: "Does she has a laptop?" ✗ → "Does she have a laptop?" ✓
  • Kısa cevapta da do/does kullanılır, have/has değil: "Yes, I have." ✗ → "Yes, I do." ✓ · "No, she hasn't." ✗ → "No, she doesn't." ✓
  • Soru kelimesiyle de aynı sıra korunur: "What do you have in your bag?", "How many members does the team have?"

4. İyelik Sıfatları (Possessive Adjectives)

"have / has" bir şeye sahip olduğunu söyler; iyelik sıfatları ise o şeyin kime ait olduğunu gösterir: my (benim), your (senin) gibi. Her zaman bir ismin önünde kullanılırlar:

Zamirİyelik sıfatıÖrnekTürkçesi
Imy (benim)This is my desk.Bu benim masam.
youyour (senin / sizin)Your task is easy.Senin görevin kolay.
hehis (onun — erkek)His office is next to the kitchen.Onun ofisi mutfağın yanında.
sheher (onun — kadın)Her laptop is new.Onun dizüstü bilgisayarı yeni.
itits (onun — cansız)The company is big. Its name is Baytek.Şirket büyük. Adı Baytek.
weour (bizim)Our manager is very experienced.Bizim yöneticimiz çok deneyimli.
theytheir (onların)Their department is upstairs.Onların departmanı üst katta.
its ≠ it's — en klasik tuzak
  • its = "onun" (iyelik sıfatı): "The laptop is old, but its screen is good."
  • it's = "it is" veya "it has" kısaltması: "It's a software company." (= It is)
  • İyelik sıfatından sonra the / a gelmez: "my the manager" ✗ → "my manager" ✓

5. Çoğullar (Plurals)

"Ekibimizin altı üyesi var" demek için isimlerin çoğulunu bilmen gerekir. Çoğu isim -s alır ama önemli istisnalar vardır:

KuralÖrneklerTürkçesi
Çoğu isim: + -slaptop → laptops · desk → desks · task → tasks · customer → customersdizüstüler · masalar · görevler · müşteriler
-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o ile bitenler: + -esbox → boxes · boss → bosses · watch → watcheskutular · patronlar · saatler
Sessiz harf + y: y → -iescompany → companies · city → cities · family → familiesşirketler · şehirler · aileler
Düzensizler (ezberle!)person → people · man → men · woman → women · child → childreninsanlar · adamlar · kadınlar · çocuklar
Dikkat
  • En sık hata: "two persons" ✗ → "two people" ✓ — "person" kelimesinin çoğulu "people"dır.
  • "day" gibi sesli harf + y ile bitenler normal -s alır: day → days (daies ✗).
  • Çoğul isimler "are" ile kullanılır: "The monitors are new."

7. Uygulama: Ekip Turu (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Yeni stajyer Emre, ilk gününde ofisi geziyor. Diyalog bu bölümde öğrendiğin yapıların tamamını kullanıyor — koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Good morning! I'm Emre, the new intern.Günaydın! Ben Emre, yeni stajyer.
  • — Welcome, Emre! I'm Selin. This is our office. Our team has six members.Hoş geldin Emre! Ben Selin. Burası bizim ofisimiz. Ekibimizin altı üyesi var.
  • — It's very big! Does the team have a manager?Çok büyükmüş! Ekibin bir yöneticisi var mı?
  • — Yes, it does. Mr. Demir is our manager. His office is next to the meeting room.Evet, var. Yöneticimiz Demir Bey. Onun ofisi toplantı odasının yanında.
  • — I see. And who is she?Anladım. Peki o kim?
  • — That's Zeynep, our assistant. She has two monitors because she works with many documents.O Zeynep, bizim asistanımız. Birçok belgeyle çalıştığı için iki ekranı var.
  • — Do you have a desk for me?Benim için bir masanız var mı?
  • — Yes, we do. Your desk is near the window, and your laptop is new. But we don't have a printer here — the printer is in Zeynep's room.Evet, var. Masan pencerenin yanında ve dizüstü bilgisayarın yeni. Ama burada yazıcımız yok — yazıcı Zeynep'in odasında.
  • — Great! Thank you. My first day is very exciting!Harika! Teşekkürler. İlk günüm çok heyecanlı!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane have / has yapısı var, bulabilir misin? (Cevap: 5 — has, Does...have, has, Do...have, don't have)
  • "Our team has six members." cümlesindeki iyelik sıfatı hangisi? (Cevap: Our)
  • Selin neden "Yes, it does." diyor da "Yes, it has." demiyor? (Bölüm 3'teki kurala bak.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them in your own sentences. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

team (n)
a group of people who work together (ekip)
Syn: group (grup) · Ant: individual (birey)
"Our team has six members."
member (n)
a person in a team or group (üye)
Syn: participant (katılımcı) · Ant: outsider (dışarıdan biri)
"Ali is a member of our team."
project (n)
a planned piece of work (proje)
Syn: plan (plan) · Ant: routine (rutin)
"We have a new project."
task (n)
a piece of work you must do (görev)
Syn: job (iş) · Ant: break (mola)
"My first task is easy."
customer (n)
a person who buys from a company (müşteri)
Syn: client (müşteri) · Ant: seller (satıcı)
"The customer is a big bank."
assistant (n)
a person who helps at work (asistan)
Syn: helper (yardımcı) · Ant: boss (patron)
"Zeynep is our assistant."
laptop (n)
a small computer you can carry (dizüstü bilgisayar)
Syn: computer (bilgisayar) · Ant: desktop (masaüstü)
"Her laptop is new and fast."
printer (n)
a machine that puts documents on paper (yazıcı)
Syn: device (cihaz) · Ant: screen (ekran — kağıt yerine ekranda gösterir)
"The printer is in Zeynep's room."
document (n)
a paper or file with information (belge)
Syn: file (dosya)
"This document is for the meeting."
deadline (n)
the last day to finish a task (son teslim tarihi)
Syn: due date (bitiş tarihi)
"The deadline is on Friday."
experienced (adj)
with a lot of practice at work (deneyimli)
Syn: skilled (yetenekli) · Ant: new (yeni)
"Mr. Demir is very experienced."
helpful (adj)
ready to help people (yardımsever)
Syn: kind (nazik) · Ant: unhelpful (yardımcı olmayan)
"Our assistant is very helpful."

Selin's Team

Selin is a designer at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. She is not new now, and she is very happy because her team is great. Her team has six members, and they are all friendly and helpful.

Baytek is in a tall building in the city center of Istanbul. The office is on the fifth floor. It is big and bright, and it has many big windows. From the windows, the team can see the city and the sea. The team room has six desks and a long meeting table. Every desk has a computer and a chair. There is a small kitchen too. The kitchen has a coffee machine, a fridge, and a big table. The coffee is free, and it is very good. Selin likes her office because it is clean, warm, and quiet. Every morning, she takes the lift to the fifth floor, and she says "Good morning" to her team.

Mr. Demir is the manager. He is not young, but he is very experienced — he has fifteen years of experience. His office is next to the meeting room, and his door is always open.

Mr. Demir is a kind manager. He is at the office every day at nine o'clock. In the morning, he reads his emails, and he drinks a cup of black coffee. He has many meetings, but he is never angry. He helps the young engineers with their tasks, and he answers their questions. Selin likes her manager because he is calm and fair. He knows the names of all the customers. "A good team has a good manager, and a good manager has a good team," he always says. On Fridays, he brings cakes for everyone.

Ali and Merve are engineers. They have a new project, so they are very busy this week. The deadline is on Friday, and it is an important project because the customer is a big bank.

Ali and Merve are good friends and good engineers. Their desks are next to each other, near the window. Ali has a big monitor, a black keyboard, and many pens in a cup. Merve has a small plant on her desk. Its leaves are green, and it is her favourite thing. They work on the new project every day. The project is difficult, but it is interesting. Sometimes they stay late because the deadline is close. But they are not tired, because they like their work and they help each other. "Two engineers are better than one," Ali says.

Zeynep is the team assistant. She is very helpful. She has two monitors on her desk because she works with many documents. Her desk is near the printer, so everyone visits her!

Zeynep is always busy, but she is always happy. Her desk is big, and it has two monitors, a phone, a printer, and many documents. People come to her desk all day. "Zeynep, where is my file?" they ask. "Zeynep, do you have a pen?" She knows the answer every time, and she has an answer for every question. She is the heart of the team. At three o'clock, she makes tea for everyone, and all the people say "Thank you, Zeynep." She is not only an assistant; she is a good friend to all the team.

Mark is from London. He talks to customers every day because his English is perfect. He has a small notebook, and the customers' names are all in it.

Mark is a friendly man from England. His desk is near the door, so he is the first person you see. He has a phone, a laptop, and his small notebook. On the phone, he talks to customers in English every day. His voice is calm and clear, and his words are polite. The customers like Mark very much. In his notebook, he has all the customers' names and their phone numbers. After work, he studies Turkish at home, because he wants to speak with his team in Turkish too. His Turkish is not perfect now, but it is better every week.

Every Monday morning, the team has a meeting in the meeting room. The room has a big table and ten chairs. Mr. Demir sits at the top of the table. Everyone talks about their tasks for the week. Ali and Merve talk about the new project. Zeynep writes the notes, and Mark talks about the customers. The meeting is short, only thirty minutes, but it is important. After the meeting, everyone knows the plan for the week.

And Selin? She has a new laptop, and it is very fast. But she doesn't have a printer, so her documents are on Zeynep's desk. That is not a problem, because Zeynep is her good friend.

Selin has a good day every day. Her laptop is fast, her chair is comfortable, and her desk is near a big window. In the morning, she makes tea and she opens her emails. Then she works on her designs. She has many colours on her screen, and she loves her job. When she has a problem, she asks Zeynep or Mr. Demir, and they always help her.

A normal day at Baytek is calm and happy. The team starts work at nine and finishes at six. At lunch time, some people go to a café near the office, and other people eat in the kitchen. In the afternoon, the office is quiet because everyone is busy. Soft music plays from Mark's computer. At six o'clock, the team says "Goodbye" and "See you tomorrow." Selin walks to the bus stop with Zeynep, because they are good friends and neighbours.

The team is small, but it is strong. Everyone helps everyone. When Ali has a problem, Merve helps him. When Selin has a question, Zeynep has the answer. When a customer is difficult, Mark is calm and kind. Mr. Demir is proud of his team, because they work well together. "We are a family here," he says. "A good team is not only about work; it is about people." The team likes these words very much.

At one o'clock, the team eats lunch together in the small kitchen. They talk about their projects, their families, and their weekends. On Monday, they talk about football and films. On Friday, they are happy because the weekend is near. Selin is not alone now. She has a team, and her team is like a small family. "I am very lucky," she thinks. "I have a great team, a good manager, and a job that I love. Every day at Baytek is a good day."

Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (From Sentences to a Text)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. Bir yazıyı cümle yığınından ayıran şey bağlaçlardır (connectors): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A1 seviyesinde beş bağlaç öğreniyoruz — bu beşi, yazacağın her metinde işine yarayacak:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparOur team is small and friendly.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI have a laptop, but I don't have a printer.
orveyaSeçenek sunarIs your desk upstairs or downstairs?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirWe have a deadline, so we are busy.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI like my team because my colleagues are helpful.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
We are a team. We are six people. We have a project. We are busy. Our manager is nice. We are a team of six people, and we are busy because we have a new project. Our manager is very nice, so we are happy.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirlerini bağla.
  • "so" ve "but" öncesinde genellikle virgül kullanılır: "We have a deadline, so we are busy."

  • Kapanış: "Best regards," · "Kind regards," · "See you soon,"

Task (Görev)

You have a new colleague. Write an email to him/her. Describe your team. (Yeni bir iş arkadaşın var. Ona bir e-posta yaz; ekibini anlat.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • the people in your team and their jobs (ekipteki kişiler ve görevleri)
  • what your team has — use have / has (ekibin nelere sahip olduğu)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Dear ... , / Best regards,)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: team, member, project, customer, assistant, laptop, deadline, experienced, helpful...)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"Our team has ... members, and ..." "Our manager is ... He/She has ..." "I have ..., but I don't have ..."

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~150 words) Dear Ayşe,

Welcome to Baytek! I am Selin, and I am a designer on your new team. Here is some information about our team.

Our team has six members, and everyone is very friendly. Our manager is Mr. Demir. He has fifteen years of experience, so he is a great teacher. Ali and Merve are our engineers. They have a new project, and its deadline is on Friday, so they are a little busy this week.

Zeynep is our assistant. She is very helpful, and she has two monitors on her desk. Mark talks to our customers every day because his English is perfect.

Your desk is next to my desk. You have a new laptop, but you don't have a printer — Zeynep's printer is for everyone!

See you on Monday.

Best regards,
Selin
