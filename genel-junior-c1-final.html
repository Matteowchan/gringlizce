C1 Final — Tüm Üniteler (1-19) (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final, C1 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: ileri zaman ve görünüş; ileri modal anlam ve modal perfect'ler (needn't have, must have); devrik yapılar ve devrik koşullar (Not only..., Had I known...); ileri ve karışık koşullar (but for, were I to); vurgu yapıları (cleft & emphatic); ileri ortaç cümlecikleri (having been done); isimleştirme (nominalisation); ileri edilgen ve rapor yapıları (is believed to have...); eksiltme ve yerine koyma (ellipsis & substitution); ileri ilgi cümlecikleri (few of whom); ileri ödün ve zıtlık (much as, whereas); karmaşık ad öbekleri; akademik ihtiyat (hedging); bağdaşıklık ve söylem belirleyicileri; emir kipi ve kalıp ifadeler (the subjunctive); eşdizim ve deyimsel doğruluk; kayıt ve üslup denetimi. Beş bölümde dil bilgisi, İngilizce kullanımı, okuma, dinleme ve yazma test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

C1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/7

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) İki metin: Text 1 aynı anda birçok iş yapma (multitasking) üzerine, Text 2 şehirlerde doğaya yer açma üzerinedir. (Two texts: doing many things at once, and making room for nature in cities.)

Text 1 · Two Things at Once

Lots of students are proud of doing their homework while watching videos, chatting to friends and listening to music, all at the same time. They call it multitasking, and they are convinced it saves time. Yet scientists who study the brain say that this is mostly an illusion. What really happens is that the brain jumps quickly from one task to another, and every single jump has a tiny cost.

On its own, a single jump is far too small to notice. The trouble is that these little costs add up. Over an afternoon of homework, they can slow you down and cause silly mistakes that you would never normally make. Interestingly, the students who are most certain that they are brilliant at multitasking are often the ones who do worst when they are actually tested.

Scientists warn that constantly switching between tasks may even make it harder to concentrate for long periods later on. So the next time you feel clever for juggling five things at once, remember that your brain would almost certainly prefer to do one thing well.

Text 2 · Bringing Nature Back to the City

For a long time, the people who planned cities thought of nature as a kind of decoration. Trees and flowers were something you added at the very end, once all the important buildings had been finished. Today, that idea is changing fast. A new way of thinking, often called green infrastructure, says that plants are not just pretty — they are useful, doing jobs that would otherwise need expensive machines.

Trees, for example, are now valued because they can soak up rainwater that would otherwise flood the streets. Rows of plants can cool a city down on very hot days, so people use less electricity for air conditioning. Wetlands, which were once drained and forgotten, are being brought back because they clean dirty water and soften the effect of floods.

Some people warn that these projects are sometimes chosen to look good rather than to do real good, and that nobody remembers to look after them once the excitement has faded. Even so, the big change is hard to argue with. Nature, once seen as the opposite of the city, is now treated as one of its greatest treasures.

C1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/7

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 13 min, Task 2: 22 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 13 dk, Görev 2: 22 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Formal Email: A Camp That Was Not as Described (130-170 words)

Your family paid for a week at a summer science camp, but it was very different from what the website described. Write a formal email to the camp organisers. Explain what was promised and what actually happened, describe how it made you feel, and state clearly what action you would like them to take. (Ailen bir haftalık yaz bilim kampı için ücret ödedi, ama kamp internet sitesinde anlatılandan çok farklıydı. Kamp yöneticilerine resmi bir e-posta yaz: ne vaat edildiğini ve gerçekte ne olduğunu açıkla, bunun seni nasıl hissettirdiğini anlat ve ne yapmalarını istediğini net şekilde belirt.)

  • Use a formal opening and closing: "Dear Sir / Madam," ... "Yours faithfully," / "Yours sincerely,". (resmi açılış ve kapanış)
  • Use at least one passive / reporting structure: "We were promised that...", "It was stated that the camp would...". (edilgen / rapor yapısı)
  • Include reported speech: "The website claimed that...". (dolaylı anlatım)
  • Make a firm but polite request with would / could: "I would therefore be grateful if you could...". (kesin ama kibar rica)
  • 130-170 words, formal register throughout. (130-170 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Dear Sir / Madam," + yazma sebebin (I am writing to complain about / regarding...).
  • Gövde: vaat edilen (passive + reported speech) → gerçekte olan → nasıl hissettirdiği → beklediğin işlem (would/could + refund/repeat).
  • Kapanış: "I look forward to your prompt response." + "Yours faithfully," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Argument Essay: Should Children Be Given Homework Every Day? (200-260 words)

Some people believe that children should be given homework every single day, while others think young people need much more free time to play, rest and be with their families. Discuss both views and give your own opinion, supporting it with clear reasons and examples. (Bazıları çocuklara her gün ödev verilmesi gerektiğine inanırken, bazıları gençlerin oyun oynamak, dinlenmek ve aileleriyle vakit geçirmek için çok daha fazla serbest zamana ihtiyacı olduğunu düşünüyor. Her iki görüşü tartış ve kendi görüşünü net gerekçeler ve örneklerle destekle.)

  • Discuss both views before giving your own opinion. (kendi görüşünden önce iki görüşü de tartış)
  • Use at least three concession/contrast linkers: whereas, despite, nevertheless, although, however. (en az üç zıtlık bağlacı)
  • Include one conditional (Third or Mixed): "If teachers had set less homework, we would...". (bir koşul cümlesi)
  • Use passive and reporting structures (it is argued that..., is believed to be...). (edilgen ve rapor yapıları)
  • 200-260 words, organised into clear paragraphs. (200-260 kelime)
