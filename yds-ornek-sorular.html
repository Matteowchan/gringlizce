Örnek 1Zor

(I) Forgetting, far from being a simple failure of the mind, may in fact turn out to be useful. (II) If we remembered every last detail we ever encountered, our thoughts would soon be overwhelmed. (III) By letting go of trivial information, the brain keeps whatever truly matters within easy reach. (IV) The tallest trees on the Earth can grow to well over a hundred metres in total height. (V) In this sense, a healthy memory depends quite as much on forgetting as on remembering.

(I) Plants may seem quite still, but they are in fact constantly responding to the world around them. (II) Many of them slowly turn their leaves and stems so as to face the brightest available light. (III) The sailors of antiquity navigated the open seas by carefully observing the positions of the stars. (IV) This steady movement allows the plants to capture as much of the sunlight as they possibly can. (V) Some flowers even follow the sun right across the sky from early morning until the evening.

Örnek 3Kolay

(I) A dog experiences the world through its nose to a degree that humans can barely imagine. (II) Its sense of smell is many thousands of times more sensitive than our own could ever be. (III) Most domestic cats prefer to spend the greater part of the day quietly asleep. (IV) This is why dogs are trained to detect everything from hidden explosives to certain diseases. (V) For a dog, a single sniff can reveal who has passed by and just how long ago.

Örnek 4Orta
Öğrenciler proje için gereken kaynakları kendileri bulmakta zorlandıkça, öğretmen onlara kütüphanedeki dijital veri tabanlarını nasıl kullanacaklarını adım adım göstermeye karar verdi.
ASince the students found the necessary resources for the project without any difficulty, the teacher saw no reason to explain the library's digital databases. The teacher then moved briskly on to the next topic.
BThe teacher refused to show the students how to use the library's digital databases, even though they struggled to find resources for the project.
CBecause the teacher showed the students how to use the digital databases, they no longer needed any resources for their project.
DAs the students struggled to find the resources they needed for the project themselves, the teacher decided to show them step by step how to use the library's digital databases.Doğru ✓
EThe students easily found the resources for their project, so the teacher only briefly mentioned the library's digital databases.
Çözümü göster

Çözüm: 'kaynak bulmakta zorlandıkça öğretmen adım adım göstermeye karar verdi' yapısını doğru veren D doğrudur.

Yanlış şıklar: A öğrencilerin 'zorlanmadığını' söyler; B öğretmenin 'reddettiğini' söyler; C neden-sonucu ters kurar; E 'kolayca buldu, kısaca değindi' der.

Örnek 5Kolay
Uçak kalkışından hemen önce şiddetli bir fırtına başladığı için tüm seferler ertelendi ve yolcular saatlerce terminalde beklemek zorunda kaldı.
ABecause a violent storm began just before the plane's departure, all flights were postponed and the passengers had to wait in the terminal for hours.Doğru ✓
BAlthough a violent storm began just before the plane's departure, all flights took off on time and no passenger had to wait.
CA violent storm began just after all the flights had landed, so the passengers left the terminal without any delay.
DThe flights were postponed because the passengers refused to board, even though the weather before departure was calm.
EIf a violent storm had begun before the plane's departure, all flights would have been postponed and the passengers delayed. Fortunately, the skies stayed perfectly clear that evening.
Çözümü göster

Çözüm: 'fırtına başladığı için seferler ertelendi, yolcular saatlerce bekledi' neden-sonucunu doğru veren A doğrudur.

Yanlış şıklar: B 'zamanında kalktı, kimse beklemedi' der; C fırtınayı 'iniş sonrasına' koyar; D 'yolcular reddetti/hava sakindi' der; E gerçek dışı koşul yapısına kaydırır.

Örnek 6Kolay
Küçük kızın haftalarca süren tedavisi başarıyla tamamlandığında, hastanedeki bütün hemşireler onun sağlığına kavuşmasına içtenlikle sevindi.
ABefore the little girl's treatment had even begun, the nurses at the hospital were already certain that she would never recover.
BThe nurses at the hospital were disappointed that the little girl's long treatment had failed to restore her health.
CIf the little girl's treatment had been completed successfully, all the nurses at the hospital would have been delighted at her recovery. They finished the whole assignment well ahead of schedule.
DWhen the little girl's weeks-long treatment was successfully completed, all the nurses at the hospital were sincerely delighted at her recovery.Doğru ✓
EThe little girl recovered so quickly that her treatment was completed in a single day, to the surprise of all the nurses.
Çözümü göster

Çözüm: 'haftalarca süren tedavi başarıyla tamamlanınca hemşireler içtenlikle sevindi' yapısını doğru veren D doğrudur.

Yanlış şıklar: A 'tedavi başlamadan/iyileşmez' der; B 'başarısız oldu, hayal kırıklığı' der; C gerçek dışı koşul kurar; E 'tek günde iyileşti' der.

Örnek 7Zor

We often assume that having more choices will always make us happier. Up to a certain point, this is quite true. Beyond that point, however, the whole picture changes. Faced with dozens of nearly identical options, shoppers can feel anxious, delay deciding, or regret whatever they finally pick. ----

AMore options, therefore, will always and everywhere lead to still greater satisfaction.
BShoppers, as it happens, prefer to have as many separate choices as could possibly exist.
CHaving noticeably fewer choices is well known to make almost all people deeply unhappy.
DRegret, in real life, essentially never follows from having simply made a decision.
EAn abundance of choice, in short, can become a burden rather than a welcome gift.Doğru ✓

Örnek 8Orta

Failure is usually treated as something to be avoided at almost any cost. In many creative fields, however, it plays a surprisingly useful role. ---- Each unsuccessful attempt reveals what does not work and slowly narrows the search for what does. Seen this way, a whole string of failures can be the necessary path to a real breakthrough.

AGenuinely successful people, it is often claimed, have in truth never failed at anything.
BCarefully avoiding all failure is, in the end, the surest route to truly original work.
CCreative work, when you look at it closely, involves no trial and error whatever.
DMistakes often provide exactly the kind of information that plain success simply cannot.Doğru ✓
EOne single failure is, as a general rule, quite enough to bring any career to an end.

Çözüm: Her başarısız denemenin işe yaramayanı gösterip aramayı daralttığı anlatılır; boşluk hataların başarının veremediği bilgiyi sağladığını söyleyen D ile bağlanır.

Yanlış şıklar: A başarılıların hiç başarısız olmadığını, B başarısızlıktan kaçınmanın özgün işe götürdüğünü, C yaratıcı işte deneme-yanılma olmadığını, E tek hatanın kariyeri bitirdiğini söyler.
