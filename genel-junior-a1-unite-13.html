Grammar: in · on · under

Eşyalar nerede? The cat is on the box. The ball is under the chair. (Say where things are.)

Nerede? in / on / under (Prepositions of Place)

Bir şeyin nerede olduğunu söylemek için küçük kelimeler kullanırız. Önce örneklere bak:

  • The toy is in the bag.Oyuncak çantanın içinde. (in = içinde)
  • The cat is on the box.Kedi kutunun üstünde. (on = üstünde)
  • The ball is under the chair.Top sandalyenin altında. (under = altında)
Kolay yol
  • in = içinde · on = üstünde · under = altında.
  • Kalıp: nesne + is/are + yer kelimesi + the + yer.
  • "The book is on the table." (Kitap masanın üstünde.)

1. in / on / under (İçinde / Üstünde / Altında)

Bu üç kelime çok kolay. Resme bakar gibi düşün: içinde mi, üstünde mi, altında mı?

KelimeAnlamıÖrnek
iniçindeThe toy is in the box.
onüstündeThe book is on the table.
underaltındaThe ball is under the bed.
Kolay yol
  • Kutunun içinde → in. Masanın üstünde → on.
  • Sandalyenin altında → under.

2. next to / behind (Yanında / Arkasında)

İki kelime daha! next to = yanında (yan yana), behind = arkasında (arkada saklı):

KelimeAnlamıÖrnek
next toyanındaThe bed is next to the window.
behindarkasındaThe chair is behind the door.
Kolay yol
  • next to iki kelimedir: "next to the bed" (yatağın yanında).
  • behind = arkasında: "behind the door" (kapının arkasında).

3. is / are ile kullan (Özet Tablo)

Yer kelimesinden önce is ya da are gelir. Tek şey → is, çok şey → are:

is (tek şey)are (çok şey)
The cat is on the bed.The toys are in the box.
The ball is under the chair.The books are on the table.
Kolay yol
  • Tek nesne → is. Çoğul nesne (-s) → are.
  • "The toys is in the box" ✗ → "The toys are in the box" ✓.

4. Olumsuz (is not / are not)

"Değil" demek için not ekleriz: is not = isn't, are not = aren't:

OlumluOlumsuz
The dog is under the table.The dog isn't on the bed.
The shoes are in the box.The shoes aren't under the bed.
Kolay yol
  • is + not → isn't. are + not → aren't.
  • "The ball isn't in the box." (Top kutunun içinde değil.)

5. Where is ...? / Where are ...? (Soru + Cevap)

"Nerede?" diye sormak için Where is (tek) ya da Where are (çok) kullanırız:

SoruCevap
Where is the cat?It is on the bed.
Where are the toys?They are in the box.
Altın kural
  • Yer kelimeleri: in (içinde), on (üstünde), under (altında), next to (yanında), behind (arkasında). Tek şey → is, çok şey → are. Soru: Where is / Where are en başa gelir.

6. In My Room (Mini Diyalog)

İki arkadaş odadaki eşyaların yerini konuşuyor. Koyu kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Where is the cat? — It is on the bed.— Kedi nerede? — Yatağın üstünde.
  • — Where are your toys? — They are in the box.— Oyuncakların nerede? — Kutunun içinde.
  • — Where is the ball? — It is under the chair.— Top nerede? — Sandalyenin altında.
  • — The chair is behind the door, and the bed is next to the window.— Sandalye kapının arkasında, yatak da pencerenin yanında.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "The cat is in the bed" ✗ → "The cat is on the bed" ✓ (üstünde).
  • "The toys is in the box" ✗ → "The toys are in the box" ✓ (çoğul).
  • "The bed is next the window" ✗ → "The bed is next to the window" ✓.

Speaking: My Words

Yer bildiren kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendin kullan! (Learn the words, then use them.)

Kelimeler (Prepositions & Nesneler)

Bazı kelimeler yeri gösterir (in, on, under, next to, behind), bazıları odadaki nesnelerdir (box, table, bed, chair, door). Örnekle kullan!

on (preposition)
yer sözcüğü (üstünde)

"The ball is under the chair."

"The bed is next to the window."
behind (preposition)
yer sözcüğü (arkasında)

box (noun)

Reading: My Room

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

in (içinde) · on (üstünde) · under (altında) · next to (yanında) · behind (arkasında)

İpucu
  • Metin bir çocuğun odasını anlatıyor. Her eşya için "nerede?" diye sor.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruların cevaplarını metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
My Room

Hello! I am Emma. This is my room. My room is small but nice. My bed is next to the window. My cat is on the bed. She is happy!

My room has one big window. The window is very nice. In the morning, the sun comes in. My curtains are blue and yellow. I open my curtains every day. My room has white walls. I have some posters of animals on my wall. I love cats and dogs very much. My room is not big, but it is cosy and warm. I am very happy in my room.

There is a big box under my bed. My toys are in the box. My red ball is on the floor. My teddy bear is next to my pillow.

There is a table in my room. My books are on the table. My school bag is under the table. My pencils are in the bag. There is a shelf on the wall. My games are on the shelf, and my hat is next to the games.

There is a soft rug on the floor. The rug is green and round. My rug is very soft and warm. I have a clock on the wall too. The clock is round and white. There is a small plant next to my window. The plant is green and pretty. I water my plant every morning. My room has many nice colours, and it always makes me smile.

My chair is behind the door. My blue jacket is on the chair. My shoes are under the chair — they are not on the bed! My lamp is on the table too. There is a picture on the wall, and the picture is behind my bed.

Every morning, I look out of my window. I can see the garden. There are flowers and green trees outside. The sky is blue and the sun is bright. Some birds sing in the trees. Sometimes a little butterfly comes near my window. My window is my favourite thing in my room. I love to look outside and dream about my day.

My best friend is Lily. She comes to my room every Saturday. We read my books and draw together. Lily likes my room very much. We sit on the floor and play games. We laugh and have fun together for many hours. My room is my favourite place in the whole house!

At night, I turn off my lamp and go to sleep. My cat sleeps on the bed with me. In the morning, I make my bed and clean my room. I put my toys back in the box and my books on the table. I am a tidy girl, and I keep my room neat and clean every single day.

Where is my dog Max? He is not in my room! He is in the garden. My room is clean and tidy. I love my room! Where are your toys? Is your bed next to the window too?

Writing: My Room

Odandaki eşyalar nerede? Anlat! (Write about your room.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveMy bed is next to the window and my cat is on the bed.
butamaMy room is small, but it is nice.
becauseçünküI am happy because my room is tidy.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your room. (Odan hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • on / in (The cat is on the bed. My toys are in the box.)
  • under / next to / behind (The ball is under the chair.)
  • is / are doğru (tek şey → is, çok şey → are)
  • one negative / olumsuz ("My shoes aren't on the bed.")

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"My bed is next to ..." "The cat is on ..." "My toys are in ..." "The ball is under ..."

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~45 words) This is my room. My bed is next to the window and my cat is on the bed. My toys are in the box under the bed. My books are on the table, but my shoes aren't on the bed. My chair is behind the door!
