Ünite 14 · Wh- Questions · Bölüm 1/6

Grammar: Wh- Questions (What / Where / Who / When / Why / How)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Bilgi İstemek: Wh- Soruları

Şimdiye kadar çoğunlukla Yes/No soruları sordun: "Are you busy?", "Do you work here?" — cevabı "evet" ya da "hayır". Ama iş hayatında çoğu zaman "evet/hayır" yetmez; bilgi istersin: kim, nerede, ne zaman, neden, nasıl? İşte bunun için Wh- soruları vardır:

  • Who is your manager?Yöneticin kim? (kişi)
  • Where is the meeting room?Toplantı odası nerede? (yer)
  • When do we start?Ne zaman başlıyoruz? (zaman)
  • What do you do?Ne iş yaparsın? (şey / meslek)
  • How do I open this file?Bu dosyayı nasıl açarım? (yöntem)

Bu bölümde altı temel soru kelimesini, bunları be (is/are) ve do/does ile nasıl kuracağını ve doğru kelime sırasını öğreneceksin. İyi soru sormak, yeni bir işte öğrenmenin ilk adımıdır.

1. Altı Soru Kelimesi (The Question Words)

Her soru kelimesi farklı bir bilgi ister. İş yerinden örneklerle:

Soru kelimesiNe sorar?ÖrnekTürkçesi
Whatşey / eylemWhat is your job?İşin ne?
WhereyerWhere is the printer?Yazıcı nerede?
WhokişiWho is the new manager?Yeni yönetici kim?
WhenzamanWhen is the meeting?Toplantı ne zaman?
WhysebepWhy do you like your job?İşini neden seviyorsun?
Howyöntem / durumHow do you go to work?İşe nasıl gidersin?
Küçük not
  • Who = kim (kişi), What = ne (şey). Karıştırma: "Who is your boss?" (kişi) ≠ "What is your job?" (şey).
  • How tek başına "nasıl" demektir ama başka kelimelerle de birleşir: How many (kaç tane), How much (ne kadar), How old (kaç yaşında).

2. be ile Soru: is / are

Cümlede ana fiil yoksa — sadece bir durum, yer, isim ya da sıfat varsa — soruyu be (is/are) ile kurarsın. Kural çok basit: Wh- + is/are + özne? Yani is/are özneden ÖNCE gelir:

Düz cümleSoruTürkçesi
The meeting is at ten.When is the meeting?Toplantı ne zaman?
The office is on floor 3.Where is the office?Ofis nerede?
Your manager is Mr. Demir.Who is your manager?Yöneticin kim?
The customers are here.Where are the customers?Müşteriler nerede?
Dikkat
  • Tekil özne → is: "Where is the file?" Çoğul özne → are: "Where are the files?"
  • "How are you?" ve "What is your name?" de bu kalıptır — zaten biliyorsun.

3. do / does ile Soru

Cümlede bir eylem fiili varsa (work, start, go, open, use...), soruyu geniş zamanda do/does ile kurarsın. Kalıp: Wh- + do/does + özne + yalın fiil?

ÖzneYardımcıÖrnekTürkçesi
I / you / we / theydoWhere do you work?Nerede çalışırsın?
he / she / itdoesWhere does she work?O nerede çalışır?
the meetingdoesWhat time does the meeting start?Toplantı saat kaçta başlar?
theydoWhy do they use this program?Bu programı neden kullanırlar?
En kritik kural
  • does'tan sonra fiil YALIN kalır, -s ALMAZ: "Where does she work?" ✓ / "Where does she works?" ✗
  • he/she/it → does; diğer hepsi → do.
  • "What do you do?" = "Ne iş yaparsın?" — ilk "do" yardımcı, ikinci "do" ana fiildir.

4. Genişletilmiş Soru Kelimeleri (More Question Words)

Altı temel kelimeden birkaç yeni soru kelimesi türetilir. İş yerinde çok işine yarayacaklar:

Soru kelimesiNe sorar?Örnek
How manysayı (sayılabilen)How many people work here?
How muchmiktar / fiyatHow much is a coffee in the canteen?
What timesaatWhat time do you finish work?
Whichseçenek (hangi)Which department do you work in?
WhosekiminWhose desk is this?
Dikkat
  • How many + çoğul isim: "How many meetings?" · How much + sayılamayan: "How much time?"
  • What time = "saat kaçta"; When'den daha kesin bir zaman sorar.

5. Doğru Kelime Sırası (Word Order)

Wh- sorularının tek bir altın kuralı vardır. Sıra hep aynıdır:

Wh- kelimesi + (is/are ya da do/does) + özne + fiil?

Wh-YardımcıÖzneFiil
Wheredoyouwork?
Whendoesthe meetingstart?
Whoisyour manager— (fiil yok)
HowdoIopen the file?
Dikkat
  • Yardımcı (is/are/do/does) her zaman ÖZNEDEN ÖNCE gelir. Düz cümle sırası ("You work where?") yanlıştır.
  • be sorularında ayrı bir fiil yoktur; is/are'ın kendisi yeter: "Where is the office?"

6. Sorulara Cevap Vermek (Answering)

Wh- sorularının cevabı "evet/hayır" değildir — istenen bilgidir. Genellikle kısa bir cevap yeterlidir:

SoruKısa cevapTam cevap
Where do you work?At Baytek.I work at Baytek.
Who is your manager?Mr. Demir.Mr. Demir is my manager.
What time does it start?At nine.It starts at nine.
How many people are here?Six.Six people are here.
Pratik not
  • İş yerinde çoğu zaman kısa cevap ("At nine.", "On floor 3.") kibar ve yeterlidir.
  • Yer için at / in / on: at Baytek, in Istanbul, on floor 3.

7. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Wh- sorularında Türkçe konuşanların en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi sorularını yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
Where you work?Where do you work?Eylem fiilinde do/does gerekir.
Where does she works?Where does she work?does'tan sonra fiil yalın kalır.
What time the meeting starts?What time does the meeting start?Soru için does gerekir; sıra değişir.
Who your manager is?Who is your manager?be sorusunda is özneden önce gelir.
How many people works here?How many people work here?people çoğuldur → work.
What is your job do?What is your job?Tek yardımcı yeter; is ve do birlikte olmaz.

8. Uygulama: İlk Gün Soruları (Model Dialogue)

Selin bugün Baytek'te ilk günü. Mentoru Mark'a sorular soruyor. Wh- sorularına ve kelime sırasına dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Where is my desk?Masam nerede?
  • — It's next to the window.Pencerenin yanında.
  • — Who is our manager?Yöneticimiz kim?
  • — Mr. Demir. He's very kind.Mr. Demir. Çok kibar biri.
  • — What time does the workday start?İş günü saat kaçta başlar?
  • — It starts at nine and finishes at six.Dokuzda başlar, altıda biter.
  • — How many people are in our team?Ekibimizde kaç kişi var?
  • — We are six. You are number seven!Altı kişiyiz. Sen yedincisin!
  • — What do I do first?İlk olarak ne yaparım?
  • — You read the customer file.Müşteri dosyasını okursun.
  • — Why do I read it now?Neden şimdi okuyorum?
  • — Because the meeting is on Friday.Çünkü toplantı cuma günü.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç farklı Wh- kelimesi var? (Cevap: 6 — Where, Who, What time, How many, What, Why)
  • "What time does the workday start?" sorusunda neden "does" var? (Bölüm 3'e bak.)
  • "How many people are...?" neden "are"? (people çoğul → be sorusunda are.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them to ask your own questions. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi sorularını sormak için kullan.)

ask (v)
to say a question to someone (sormak)
Syn: question (soru sormak) · Ant: answer (cevaplamak)
"Where do I ask for help?"
answer (v)
to give a reply to a question (cevaplamak)
Syn: reply (yanıtlamak) · Ant: ask (sormak)
"Who answers the phone in your team?"
manager (n)
the person who leads a team (yönetici)
Syn: boss (patron) · Ant: employee (çalışan)
"Who is your manager?"
colleague (n)
a person you work with (iş arkadaşı)
Syn: co-worker (mesai arkadaşı) · Ant: customer (müşteri)
"Where does your colleague sit?"
meeting (n)
a time when people talk about work (toplantı)
Syn: talk (görüşme) · Ant: break (mola)
"What time is the meeting?"
department (n)
one part of a company (departman, bölüm)
Syn: team (ekip) · Ant: company (şirket — bütünü)
"Which department do you work in?"
task (n)
a piece of work to do (görev)
Syn: job (iş) · Ant: rest (dinlenme)
"What is my first task?"
schedule (n)
a plan of times for work (program, çizelge)
Syn: plan (plan) · Ant: free time (boş zaman)
"When is the meeting on the schedule?"
deadline (n)
the last day to finish work (son teslim tarihi)
Syn: due date (bitiş günü) · Ant: start date (başlangıç günü)
"When is the deadline for the report?"
canteen (n)
a place at work to eat lunch (yemekhane)
Syn: cafeteria (kafeterya) · Ant: office (ofis)
"Where is the canteen?"
floor (n)
one level of a building (kat)
Syn: level (seviye, kat) · Ant: —
"Which floor is your office on?"
project (n)
a big piece of work with a goal (proje)
Syn: work (iş) · Ant: break (mola)
"Who is the manager of this project?"
İlk gün ofiste: yeni çalışan Wh- sorularıyla ekibi ve iş yerini tanıyor.

Selin's First Day Questions

Selin is a new engineer at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. Today is her first day, and she has many questions. Selin is a little nervous, but she is not shy. Her mentor, Mark, is very friendly, so she is not afraid to ask.

Selin is twenty-four years old. She lives in Istanbul with her family. She likes computers, and she likes her new job. Baytek is a big company. It has many people and many rooms. The building is tall and modern. Selin comes to work early. It is eight o'clock in the morning, and the office is quiet.

Selin has a small notebook and a pen. She writes her questions in the notebook. 'Good morning, Selin. Welcome to Baytek!' Mark says. 'I am your mentor. I help you today.' Selin smiles. 'Thank you, Mark. I have many questions,' she says. 'That is good,' Mark answers. 'Please ask me. Questions are not a problem.'

'Where is my desk?' Selin asks. 'It is next to the window, near the coffee machine,' Mark says. 'And where is the meeting room?' 'It is on the third floor.'

Selin looks at her desk. It is a nice desk. There is a new computer, a lamp, and a small plant on it. 'Is the computer fast?' Selin asks. 'Yes, it is very fast,' Mark says. The window is big, and the room is bright. Selin is happy, because the coffee machine is very near. She likes coffee in the morning.

'How do I turn on the computer?' Selin asks. 'You press this button here,' Mark says. 'What is my email address?' 'It is on this paper. Your password is here too.' Selin writes the information in her notebook. She wants to remember everything on her first day.

'Where is the printer?' Selin asks. 'It is near the door, on the left,' Mark says. 'How do I use it?' 'It is easy. You press the green button, and the paper comes out.' Selin looks at the office. There are many desks, some chairs, and a big clock on the wall. It is a good place to work.

Selin wants to know about the team. 'Who is our manager?' she asks. 'Mr. Demir is our manager. He is a kind person,' Mark answers. 'How many people work in our team?' 'We are six people. You are number seven!'

'Where does Mr. Demir sit?' Selin asks. 'His office is on the third floor, next to the meeting room,' Mark says. 'When do I meet him?' 'You meet him this afternoon. He is in a meeting now.' Selin is a little nervous about the manager, but she is excited too.

Now Selin meets her colleagues. 'This is Elif. She is a very good engineer,' Mark says. 'Hello, Elif. Nice to meet you,' Selin says. 'Nice to meet you too. Welcome to the team!' Elif is friendly and kind. 'Who is that man near the door?' Selin asks. 'That is Can. He works in the design department. He is a nice person too.'

At ten o'clock, Selin asks about the daily schedule. 'What time does the workday start?' 'It starts at nine and finishes at six,' Mark says. 'When do we have lunch?' 'At half past twelve, in the canteen.'

'Where is the canteen?' Selin asks. 'It is on the first floor. The food is good, and it is not expensive,' Mark says. 'How much is a coffee in the canteen?' 'Coffee is free for everybody!' Selin is very happy. She likes free coffee, and she likes the canteen.

'When do we have a break?' Selin asks. 'We have a short break at eleven, and one more break at three,' Mark says. 'What do people do in the break?' 'They drink coffee, they talk, and some people go for a walk.' Selin thinks the workday is nice. It is not too long, and there are good breaks.

Selin is curious about her first task. 'What do I do first?' she asks. 'You read the customer file. Why? Because our meeting with the customer is on Friday.' 'How do I open the file?' 'Elif helps you with the computer.'

Selin opens the customer file. It is a long file with many pages. 'Why is this file important?' she asks. 'Because the customer is a big company. We work with them every week,' Mark says. 'What is the name of the customer?' 'It is Delta Bank. They want a new program.'

'How do I ask questions about the file?' Selin asks. 'You ask me, or you ask Elif. We are here for you,' Mark says. Selin reads the file for two hours. She writes some notes in her notebook. When she has a question, she asks Elif, and Elif always helps her. Selin is not afraid, because everybody is kind.

At half past twelve, Selin and Elif go to the canteen. 'What food is good here?' Selin asks. 'The soup is very good, and the salad is fresh,' Elif says. They eat lunch and they talk about work. 'How long do you work at Baytek?' Selin asks. 'I work here for three years. It is a good company,' Elif answers. Selin is happy, because she has a new friend on her first day.

After lunch, the phone on Selin's desk rings. 'Who answers the phone?' Selin asks. 'You can answer it. It is easy,' Elif says. Selin picks up the phone. 'Hello, this is Baytek. How can I help you?' she says. It is a customer with a small question. Selin is polite and calm, and the customer is happy. 'That is very good, Selin!' Elif says with a smile.

In the afternoon, Selin meets Mr. Demir. 'Hello, Selin. Welcome to our team,' he says. 'Thank you very much,' Selin answers. 'Do you have any questions?' Mr. Demir asks. 'Yes. When is the customer meeting?' 'It is on Friday, at ten o'clock. You come with me and Elif,' he says. Selin is happy, because now she is ready for the meeting.

At the end of the day, Mark smiles. 'You ask good questions, Selin. Good questions are the first step at a new job. Welcome to Baytek!'

Selin goes home at six o'clock. She is tired, but she is very happy. On the bus, she reads her notebook. It is full of questions and answers. Now she knows her desk, her team, her manager, and her first task. Tomorrow is a new day, and Selin has new questions. A good question is always the first step.

What time does the workday start, because I want to arrive on time? When do we have lunch, and where is the canteen? How many people are in our team?

I also have a question about my first task. What do I do on my first morning, and who helps me with the computer? Why do we use this program?

These are all my questions for now. See you on Monday!

Best wishes,
Selin

Unit Check (Ünite Kontrolü)

Mixed questions from the whole unit, then check what you can do. (Ünitenin tamamından karışık sorular; sonra neler yapabildiğini işaretle.)

Mixed Quiz (Karışık Test)

14 questions: grammar + vocabulary + reading. (14 soru: gramer + kelime + okuma.) Her soru anında değerlendirilir.

Can-Do Check (Yapabiliyorum Listesi)

Tick what you can do now. Honest answers help your teacher. (Şu an yapabildiklerini işaretle. Dürüst cevaplar öğretmenine yol gösterir.)

İşaretlemediğin madde varsa ilgili bölüme dönüp tekrar çalış — soldaki menüden her bölüme geri dönebilirsin.

Üniteyi Tamamladın! (Unit Complete!)

Tebrikler! Artık iş yerinde bilgi istemek için Wh- soruları kurabiliyorsun. Kim (Who), nerede (Where), ne zaman (When), neden (Why), nasıl (How) ve ne (What) soruları soruyor; be (is/are) ve do/does yardımcılarını doğru seçiyor; How many ve What time gibi kalıpları kullanıyorsun. İyi soru sormak, yeni bir işte öğrenmenin ilk adımıdır — ve sen bu adımı attın.

Bir sonraki üniteye geçmeden önce Speaking ve Writing görevlerini gönderdiğinden emin ol; öğretmenin sorularını değerlendirecek.
