Grammar: Future Plans (be going to + verb)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Gelecek Planları — "be going to"

Şimdiye kadar bugünü anlattın: "I work at Baytek.", "We have a meeting every Monday." Peki yarın, bu akşam, gelecek hafta ya da gelecek ay ne yapacaksın? Planlarını ve niyetlerini anlatmak için İngilizcede en sık kullanılan yapı be going to'dur:

  • "I am going to send the report tomorrow."Raporu yarın göndereceğim. (plan)
  • "We are going to meet the client next week."Gelecek hafta müşteriyle görüşeceğiz. (plan)
  • "She is going to present the design on Friday."Cuma günü tasarımı sunacak. (plan)
  • "They aren't going to change the price."Fiyatı değiştirmeyecekler. (olumsuz)
  • "Are you going to join the meeting?"Toplantıya katılacak mısın? (soru)

Kısacası: bir şeyi yapmayı planlıyorsan ya da niyetin varsa, be going to kullanırsın. Bu, iş hayatının plan dilidir — toplantıları, projeleri ve gelecek adımları anlatmanın anahtarı. Bu bölümde yapıyı (am/is/are + going to + fiil), olumsuzu, soruyu ve gelecek zaman ifadelerini öğreneceksin.

1. Temel Yapı: be going to + fiil (Structure)

Yapı üç parçadan oluşur: özneye uygun am / is / are, sonra going to, sonra YALIN fiil. Değişen tek şey baştaki "be" fiilidir — özneye göre seçilir:

Öznebe going toÖrnekTürkçesi
Iam going toI am going to send the report.Raporu göndereceğim.
He / She / It (Selin, the team)is going toShe is going to meet the client.Müşteriyle görüşecek.
You / We / Theyare going toWe are going to launch the app.Uygulamayı piyasaya süreceğiz.

Fiil hep yalın kalır — özne ne olursa olsun -ed almaz, -s almaz:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓
She is going to meets the client.She is going to meet the client.
I am going to sent the email.I am going to send the email.
Kısaltmalar (Contractions)
  • Konuşmada ve arkadaşa yazışmada "be" kısaltılır: I'm going to, She's going to, We're going to, They're going to.
  • Örnek: "I'm going to finish the plan today." = "I am going to finish the plan today."
  • "going to" günlük konuşmada "gonna" diye okunur ama yazıda daima "going to" yazılır.

2. Olumsuz Cümle (Negative)

Olumsuz yapmak için be fiilinden sonra not eklersin: am/is/are + not + going to + fiil. Fiil yine yalın kalır:

OlumluOlumsuzTürkçesi
I am going to work this weekend.I am not going to work this weekend.Bu hafta sonu çalışmayacağım.
She is going to call the supplier.She isn't going to call the supplier.Tedarikçiyi aramayacak.
They are going to sign today.They aren't going to sign today.Bugün imzalamayacaklar.
Kısa yollar
  • is not = isn't · are not = aren't. "am not"un kısaltması yoktur: "I'm not going to..."
  • "not"u be ile going to arasına koy: "She is not going to come."
  • Fiil olumsuzda da yalın: "They aren't going to go." ✓ — "goes/went" değil.

3. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru için be fiili başa gelir: Am / Is / Are + özne + going to + fiil? You are going to join. → Are you going to join?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Are you going to join the call?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is she going to present it?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Are they going to sign it?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Dikkat
  • Kısa cevapta "going to + fiil" TEKRAR EDİLMEZ, sadece be kalır: "Yes, I am." (❌ "Yes, I am going to join.")
  • Wh- sorularında sıra aynıdır: "What are you going to do?", "When is she going to start?", "Where are they going to meet?"
  • Soru kelimesi (What/When/Where) + be + özne + going to + fiil.

4. Ne Zaman "going to"? — Zaman İfadeleri (When & Time Markers)

"be going to" geleceğe bakar: planlarını ve niyetlerini anlatır. Cümleyi genelde bir gelecek zaman ifadesi tamamlar:

Gelecek zaman ifadesiAnlamıÖrnek
tomorrowyarınI am going to call him tomorrow.
tonight / this afternoonbu akşam / bu öğleden sonraWe are going to meet this afternoon.
next week / month / yeargelecek hafta / ay / yılShe is going to start next week.
in two weeks / sooniki hafta içinde / yakındaThey are going to travel in two weeks.
on Mondaypazartesi günüWe are going to sign it on Monday.
Plan mı, rutin mi?
  • Rutin / her zaman için geniş zaman: "Every day I check my emails." (her gün — alışkanlık)
  • Plan / niyet için going to: "Tomorrow I am going to check the new system." (yarın — plan)
  • "going to" yesterday / last week gibi geçmiş ifadelerle KULLANILMAZ — o ifadeler geçmişi gösterir.

5. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

"be going to"da Türkçe konuşanların en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I am going to sent the email.I am going to send the email.going to'dan sonra fiil yalın kalır.
She going to call the client.She is going to call the client."be" (is) atlanmaz.
We is going to start soon.We are going to start soon.Özne uyumu: we ile "are".
He is going to meets us.He is going to meet us.Fiil yalın: "meet", "meets" değil.
— Are you going to help? — Yes, I going to.— Are you going to help? — Yes, I am.Kısa cevapta sadece "be" kalır.
I am going to work yesterday.I am going to work tomorrow.going to gelecektir; "yesterday" ile kullanılmaz.

6. Uygulama: Haftanın Planları (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Aşağıdaki diyalog yapıyı, olumsuzu ve soruları birlikte kullanıyor — koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Hi Deniz! You look busy today. What are you going to do this week?Selam Deniz! Bugün meşgul görünüyorsun. Bu hafta ne yapacaksın?
  • — Hi Mark! We are going to start a big new project. I am going to prepare the first designs.Selam Mark! Büyük yeni bir projeye başlıyoruz. İlk tasarımları hazırlayacağım.
  • — That sounds great! Are you going to work alone?Kulağa harika geliyor! Yalnız mı çalışacaksın?
  • — No, I'm not. Elif is going to help me, and we are going to finish the plan by Friday.Hayır. Elif bana yardım edecek ve planı cuma gününe kadar bitireceğiz.
  • — Is the customer going to visit the office?Müşteri ofisi ziyaret edecek mi?
  • — No, they aren't. We are going to meet online on Thursday.Hayır. Perşembe günü online görüşeceğiz.
  • — When is Mr. Demir going to present the project?Mr. Demir projeyi ne zaman sunacak?
  • — He is going to present it next Monday. After that, we are going to hire two new designers.Gelecek pazartesi sunacak. Ondan sonra iki yeni tasarımcı işe alacağız.
  • — Are you going to travel for this project?Bu proje için seyahat edecek misin?
  • — Yes, I am! In two weeks, I am going to fly to Berlin to sign the contract. I'm not going to travel alone, so I'm not nervous.Evet! İki hafta içinde sözleşmeyi imzalamak için Berlin'e uçacağım. Yalnız gitmeyeceğim, o yüzden gergin değilim.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane "going to" ile kurulmuş soru var? (Cevap: 5)
  • "I'm not going to travel alone" cümlesinde olumsuzluk nasıl yapılmış? (Cevap: am + not + going to)
  • Mark "Are you going to work alone?" diye soruyor; kısa cevabı ne? (Cevap: "No, I'm not.")

İş Planlarının Dili (Why This Matters)

"be going to" iş hayatında en çok işine yarayacak yapılardan biridir: bir sonraki adımını, toplantı planını, proje takvimini ve niyetlerini bununla anlatırsın. Artık bugünü, alışkanlıklarını ve şimdi olanı anlatabiliyordun; şimdi geleceğe de bakabiliyorsun. Bu ünitedeki am/is/are seçimini, olumsuzu ve soruyu sağlamlaştır — bir sonraki üniteye en güçlü halinle gir.

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the verbs, then use them with 'be going to' in your own sentences. (Fiilleri öğren, sonra 'be going to' ile kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

send (v)
to make something go to someone (göndermek)
Syn: post (yollamak) · Ant: receive (almak)
"I am going to send the report tomorrow."
meet (v)
to see and talk with someone (buluşmak, görüşmek)
Syn: see (görüşmek) · Ant: miss (kaçırmak)
"We are going to meet the client on Monday."
prepare (v)
to get something ready (hazırlamak)
Syn: get ready (hazırlamak) · Ant: forget (unutmak)
"She is going to prepare the slides."
organize (v)
to plan and arrange something (düzenlemek)
Syn: arrange (ayarlamak) · Ant: cancel (iptal etmek)
"They are going to organize a team meeting."
launch (v)
to start selling a new product (piyasaya sürmek)
Syn: release (çıkarmak) · Ant: cancel (iptal etmek)
"We are going to launch the app in June."
hire (v)
to give someone a job (işe almak)
Syn: employ (istihdam etmek) · Ant: fire (işten çıkarmak)
"The manager is going to hire two designers."
present (v)
to show and explain something (sunmak)
Syn: show (göstermek) · Ant: hide (saklamak)
"I am going to present the plan on Friday."
visit (v)
to go and see a place or person (ziyaret etmek)
Syn: go to see (görmeye gitmek) · Ant: leave (ayrılmak)
"We are going to visit the Berlin office."
sign (v)
to write your name on a document (imzalamak)
Syn: approve (onaylamak) · Ant: reject (reddetmek)
"They are going to sign the contract."
discuss (v)
to talk about something with people (tartışmak, görüşmek)
Syn: talk about (konuşmak) · Ant: ignore (görmezden gelmek)
"We are going to discuss the budget."
review (v)
to look at something again carefully (gözden geçirmek)
Syn: check (kontrol etmek) · Ant: skip (atlamak)
"She is going to review the design."
finish (v)
to complete something (bitirmek)
Syn: complete (tamamlamak) · Ant: start (başlamak)
"I am going to finish the project this week."

Baytek's Plans for the New Project

Selin is a young designer. She works at Baytek. Baytek is a small company in Istanbul. The company makes apps and websites for other companies. Selin likes her job very much. She works with a small but friendly team. Every morning, she comes to the office at nine o'clock. She sits at a big desk near the window. Baytek has an exciting month ahead. Next week, Selin's team is going to start a big new project for a customer from Germany, and everyone is busy with the plans.

This is not a normal month. It is a very busy month. The new customer is from Germany, and the customer wants a new app. The app is big and important. Everyone at Baytek is excited. The team has many jobs to do this month. They have meetings, plans, designs, and a trip. Selin looks at her calendar on the wall. There are many stars on it. Every star is an important day. 'This month is going to be great,' Selin thinks with a smile.

The German customer has a big shop. The shop sells shoes and bags. Now the customer wants an app for phones. People are going to use the app to buy shoes online. The app is going to be easy and fast. It is going to have nice pictures and clear buttons. Selin is going to design the first screens. She is going to make the app beautiful. The customer is going to like it. This is a big job, but Selin is not afraid. She is ready to work.

Baytek has a good team. Mr. Demir is the manager. He is calm, and he helps everyone. Mark is a designer, and he is Selin's best friend at work. Elif works in sales, and she talks to customers. Selin is happy in this team, and this month they are going to do great work together.

This week, Selin is going to prepare the first designs. She is going to work with Mark, and they are going to finish the plan by Friday. On Thursday, the team is going to meet the customer online and discuss the details.

Selin likes to work with Mark. Mark is a good designer too. He is kind, and he works fast. Selin and Mark are going to share the work this week. Selin is going to draw the screens, and Mark is going to choose the colors. Every afternoon, they are going to sit together and check the designs. They are going to talk about small problems and fix them. On Wednesday, they are going to show the first designs to Mr. Demir. He is going to give them good ideas.

Thursday is an important day. The team is not going to travel this week. They are going to use a video call. Selin is going to open her laptop at two o'clock. Mark, Mr. Demir, and the customer are going to join the call. On the screen, they are going to see the customer from Germany. They are going to say hello and talk about the app. Selin is going to show the first designs on the screen. The customer is going to ask questions, and the team is going to answer them.

Next week is going to be very important. On Monday, the manager, Mr. Demir, is going to present the project to the whole company. After that, the team is going to hire two new designers because the project is big. Selin is going to help the new designers in their first week.

The two new designers are going to help the team a lot. The project is big, so the team needs more people. The new designers are going to learn fast. Selin is going to be their friend and helper. She is going to show them the office and the computers. She is going to explain the project step by step. She is going to answer all their questions. 'Don't worry,' Selin is going to say. 'We are a good team, and we are going to help each other every day.'

Monday is going to be a big day for Mr. Demir. He is going to stand in front of the whole company. He is going to use a big screen and show the plan. He is going to talk about the new app and the German customer. All the workers are going to listen and clap. Selin is going to sit in the front and watch. She is going to be proud of her team. After the presentation, everyone is going to have coffee together and talk about the project.

In two weeks, Selin and Elif are going to travel to Berlin. They are going to visit the customer's office and sign the contract. Selin is very happy because this is her first business trip. She isn't going to travel alone, so she isn't nervous.

Berlin is a big city in Germany. Selin is going to fly there with Elif. Elif works in the sales team. She is friendly, and she speaks good English. On the plane, they are going to read about the customer. At the airport in Berlin, a driver is going to wait for them. At the office, they are going to meet the customer's team. They are going to have a long meeting and then sign the contract. In the evening, Selin is going to take photos and send them to her family.

At the end of the month, the team is going to launch the first part of the project. They are not going to celebrate yet, but they are going to review the results together. 'We have a lot of work,' Selin says, 'and we are going to do it well!'

Selin is going to be very busy this month. She is going to work hard every day, and some days are going to be long. But Selin is not going to be sad, because she loves her job and her team. At the end of the month, the team is going to take a short break. After that, they are going to start the next part of the project. Selin is going to learn many new things, and she is going to be a better designer. 'This is a big month,' she says, 'and I am ready for it!'

  • Açılış: "Hi ...," · "Hello ...," · "Dear ...,"
  • Kapanış: "Best wishes," · "See you soon," · "Take care,"

Task (Görev)

Write an email to a friend about your plans for the new project (or for next month at work). (Bir arkadaşına yeni proje / gelecek ay için planlarını anlatan bir e-posta yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • at least 6 'be going to' sentences (en az 6 "be going to" cümlesi)
  • at least 1 negative sentence with am/is/are not going to (en az 1 olumsuz cümle)
  • at least 1 question with 'going to' (en az 1 soru: Are you going to...?)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: send, meet, prepare, launch, hire, present, sign...)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Hi ... / Best wishes,)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"Next month we are going to start ..." "First, I am going to ..." "Are you going to visit ...?"

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~160 words) Hi Ali,

How are you? I have great news about work! Next month we are going to start a big new project at Baytek, and I am very excited.

First, I am going to prepare the first designs with Mark, and we are going to finish the plan by Friday. On Thursday, we are going to meet the customer online and discuss the details. Our manager is going to present the project to the whole company next week, so everybody is busy now.

In two weeks, Elif and I are going to travel to Berlin. We are going to visit the customer's office and sign the contract. I am a little nervous, but I am not going to travel alone, so I feel better.

We are not going to celebrate yet because the project is big. Are you going to visit Istanbul soon? Let's have lunch!

Best wishes,
Selin

Listening

Videoyu izle, ardından dinleme görevlerini tamamla (eşleştirme, çoktan seçmeli, boşluk, tablo, sıralama).

Unit Check (Ünite Kontrolü)

Mixed questions from the whole unit, then check what you can do. (Ünitenin tamamından karışık sorular; sonra neler yapabildiğini işaretle.)

Mixed Quiz (Karışık Test)

14 questions: grammar + vocabulary + reading. (14 soru: gramer + kelime + okuma.) Her soru anında değerlendirilir.

Can-Do Check (Yapabiliyorum Listesi)

Tick what you can do now. Honest answers help your teacher. (Şu an yapabildiklerini işaretle. Dürüst cevaplar öğretmenine yol gösterir.)

İşaretlemediğin madde varsa ilgili bölüme dönüp tekrar çalış — soldaki menüden her bölüme geri dönebilirsin.

Üniteyi Bitirdin! (Unit Complete!)

Tebrikler! Genel İngilizce A1 Ünite 18'i tamamladın. Artık gelecek planlarını "be going to" ile anlatabiliyorsun: bir sonraki toplantını, proje adımlarını, iş gezilerini ve niyetlerini İngilizce söyleyebilirsin. am/is/are seçimi, olumsuz ve soru yapıları elinde — bu, iş hayatının plan dilidir.

Bir sonraki üniteyle çalışmaya devam et; her ünite İngilizceni biraz daha güçlendiriyor. (Keep going — the next unit is waiting for you.)
