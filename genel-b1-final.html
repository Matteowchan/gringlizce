Final Sınavı: Tüm Üniteler (1-19) (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final, B1 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: Present Perfect karşıtlıkları (been/gone, for/since, Past Simple ile fark); Present Perfect Continuous; Past Perfect; gelecek zaman biçimleri (will / going to / present continuous / present simple); Second Conditional; edilgen çatı (Passive); dolaylı anlatım (Reported Speech); dolaylı sorular ve emirler; olasılık modalları (may/might/could); zorunluluk modalları (must/have to/mustn't/don't have to); gerund ve infinitive; ilgi cümlecikleri (who/which/whose/where, defining/non-defining); used to; soru biçimleri (dolaylı sorular, özne-nesne soruları, question tags); bağlaçlar (although/despite/because of/so...that); belirteçler ve niceleyiciler (determiners & quantifiers); artikellerin ileri kullanımı (articles); phrasal verbs; sıfat ve zarflar (adjectives & adverbs). Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

B1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Text 1 bir mühendisin geçmiş hikâyesini, Text 2 bir şehir projesinin geleceğini anlatır. (Text 1 tells an engineer's past story; Text 2 is about a city's future plan.)

Text 1 · The Long Road to the Lab

Deniz had never thought about becoming an engineer until the summer she turned seventeen. Her uncle, who had worked at a wind farm for twenty years, invited her to spend two weeks at the site. By the end of the first week, she had already decided what she wanted to study. "You have exactly the right kind of mind for this," her uncle told her, and she has remembered those words ever since.

Getting into university was not easy. Deniz applied three times before she was accepted, and during those years she had been working in a small electronics shop to save money. The shop was owned by a retired teacher who taught her how to repair old radios. Deniz says that this experience was more useful than many of her later classes, because it taught her to stay calm whenever something went wrong.

Since she graduated, Deniz has been designing batteries for electric buses. Last year one of her designs was chosen for a large city project, and thousands of people now travel on buses that contain her work. She told a journalist that she still could not quite believe it. "If I had given up after my second rejection," she said, "none of this would have happened."

Text 2 · A Plan for the Old Harbour

The city council has just announced an ambitious plan for the old harbour, which has been closed to the public for almost thirty years. If the plan is approved next month, work will begin in the spring, and the whole area is going to be transformed into a park and a science museum.

Supporters say that the harbour could become the most popular place in the city. "If we don't act now, the old buildings will fall down completely," said one council member. Under the plan, the largest warehouse, which was built in 1890, is going to be repaired rather than knocked down, because many people believe it should be protected. A new bridge will connect the harbour to the city centre, so visitors will be able to walk there in ten minutes.

Not everyone agrees. Some local shop owners are worried that the building work will drive their customers away for two or three years. Others argue that the money would be better spent on schools. A study which was published last week suggested that the project might cost twice as much as the council expects.

The council has promised that local people will be asked for their opinions before any final decision is made. "If residents were against the idea," the mayor said, "we would not go ahead." The first public meeting is going to be held next Tuesday.

B1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 12 min, Task 2: 23 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 12 dk, Görev 2: 23 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Semi-formal Email: A Problem with a Course (100-130 words)

You booked a weekend photography course at a local studio, but you have just received a message saying the course has been moved to a different date. Write a semi-formal email to the studio manager. Explain the problem, say how the change affects you, and politely ask what can be done. (Yerel bir stüdyoda hafta sonu fotoğrafçılık kursuna kayıt oldun, ama kursun başka bir tarihe alındığına dair bir mesaj aldın. Stüdyo yöneticisine yarı-resmi bir e-posta yaz: sorunu açıkla, bu değişikliğin seni nasıl etkilediğini söyle ve kibarca ne yapılabileceğini sor.)

  • Use a semi-formal opening and closing: "Dear Mr / Ms ...," ... "Yours sincerely," / "Best regards,". (yarı-resmi açılış ve kapanış)
  • Use at least one passive: "I was told that...", "the course has been moved...". (en az bir edilgen yapı)
  • Use reported speech: "The message said that...". (dolaylı anlatım)
  • Make a polite request with would / could: "I would be grateful if you could...". (kibar rica)
  • 100-130 words. (100-130 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Dear Ms ...," + yazma sebebin (I am writing about / regarding...).
  • Gövde: sorun (passive + reported speech) → nasıl etkiledi → kibar rica (would/could).
  • Kapanış: "I look forward to hearing from you." + "Yours sincerely," / "Best regards," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Opinion Paragraph: Work and Study (170-220 words)

Some people believe that students should have a part-time job while they study, while others think they should focus only on their studies. What is your opinion? Write a paragraph giving your view with clear reasons and examples. (Bazıları öğrencilerin okurken yarı zamanlı bir işte çalışması gerektiğini, bazıları ise yalnızca derslerine odaklanmaları gerektiğini düşünür. Senin görüşün ne? Görüşünü net gerekçeler ve örneklerle bir paragrafta yaz.)

  • State your opinion clearly and support it. (görüşünü net belirt ve gerekçelendir)
  • Use at least two linkers: although, despite, because of, so...that. (en az iki bağlaç)
  • Include one Second Conditional sentence: "If students had a job, they would...". (bir Second Conditional cümlesi)
  • Use Present Perfect and modals (should, could, might). (Present Perfect ve modallar)
  • 170-220 words. (170-220 kelime)
