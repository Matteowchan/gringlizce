Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-8 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-8'de öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: Present Perfect ve Past Simple farkı (for/since, been/gone); Present Perfect Continuous; Past Perfect; gelecek yapıları (will / going to / Present Continuous); Second Conditional; Passive Voice; Reported Speech ve aktarılan sorular/emirler. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Konular okul, arkadaşlar, oyunlar, hayvanlar ve aile etrafında döner. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · The Class That Started a Garden

Two years ago, nobody in Class 5B had ever grown a single vegetable. Today, the school has a big green garden, and it is looked after by the children themselves. The idea came from a ten-year-old boy called Emir, who loved plants and wanted to grow tomatoes at school.

At first, the teachers weren't sure. They thought the garden would get muddy and messy, and they worried that the children would forget their normal lessons. However, Emir had already drawn a clear plan. He showed his teacher how the garden could be used for science, maths and art lessons. After he had explained everything, the teachers finally said yes.

The first months were hard. The young plants were eaten by insects, and one week the whole garden was flooded by heavy rain. Emir and his friends have solved most of these problems since then. New tools were bought with money from a school cake sale, and a kind farmer taught the children how to keep the soil healthy.

Today the children pick fresh vegetables every Friday. Teachers say that pupils who work in the garden eat more vegetables at home, not fewer. Some shy children, who never spoke in class before, now happily show visitors around the garden. The vegetables are shared with families and the school kitchen.

Emir believes the future will be even greener. 'If every school had a garden like ours,' he says, 'children would love nature much more. A tiny seed is not just a plant — it is a big lesson.'

Text 2 · The Zoo Trip We Almost Missed

Last spring, our class won a quiz competition, and the prize was a day trip to the city zoo. We had wanted to go there for years, so everyone was excited. But one week before the trip, we almost lost our chance.

The problem was money. The school had promised to pay for the bus, but the company suddenly asked for more. Our teacher, Miss Deniz, told us that the trip might be cancelled. We were very sad, because we had already chosen which animals we wanted to see.

Then my friend Ayla had a clever idea. 'If we made a small sale,' she said, 'we could sell cakes and old toys and get the money ourselves.' At first some children laughed, but soon the whole class was baking. The sale was held in the school playground on a sunny Saturday, and lots of parents came.

To everyone's surprise, we got enough money in a single afternoon. The extra coins were given to the animal shelter as a gift. A few days later, the bus arrived, and we finally travelled to the zoo. The lions were very loud, but the baby elephant was everyone's favourite.

On the way home, we talked about the sale. Miss Deniz smiled and said, 'You have learned something more important than any lesson. When a group works together, almost nothing is impossible.'

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 12 min, Task 2: 23 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 12 dk, Görev 2: 23 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: Some Good News (100-130 words)

Recently something good happened to you: you passed an important test, joined a school club, won a game, or learned a new skill like riding a bike. Write an email to a friend and share your news. (Yakın zamanda güzel bir şey oldu: önemli bir sınavı geçtin, bir okul kulübüne katıldın, bir oyun kazandın ya da bisiklete binmek gibi yeni bir beceri öğrendin. Bir arkadaşına e-posta yazıp haberini paylaş.)

  • Use Present Perfect for your news ("I have just passed..."). (haber için Present Perfect)
  • Use Past Simple for the details — when, where, how. (ayrıntılar için Past Simple)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: because, so, but, and, after. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 100-130 words. (100-130 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Ayla," + haber cümlesi ("I have some great news! I have just...").
  • Gövde: ne oldu, nasıl oldu? — Present Perfect (haber) + Past Simple (ayrıntı) + because/so.
  • Kapanış: "Let's meet soon and celebrate!" / "Take care," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: Playing Outside or Video Games? (140-170 words)

Some children think that playing outside is better than playing video games. Others disagree. What is your opinion? Give reasons and examples. (Bazı çocuklar dışarıda oynamanın video oyunu oynamaktan daha iyi olduğunu düşünür; bazıları katılmaz. Senin görüşün ne? Sebep ve örnek ver.)

  • State your opinion clearly at the start. (görüşünü baştan net belirt)
  • Use a Second Conditional sentence ("If children played outside more, ..."). (bir Second Conditional cümlesi)
  • Use at least 4 connectors: however, because, so, although, for example. (en az 4 bağlaç)
  • 140-170 words. (140-170 kelime)
