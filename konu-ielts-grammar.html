IELTS Grammar AccuracyIELTS Dilbilgisi Doğruluğu — Bandı Tavanlayan 7 Hata

IELTS Writing'de orta bandda takılmanın sebebi çoğu zaman süslü kelime eksikliği değil, cümlelerin ayakta durmamasıdır. Bu rehber, Türk öğrencilerde tekrar eden yedi dilbilgisi hatasını — fragment, comma splice, özne-fiil uyumu, çoğul -s, article, zaman kipi — kural + çözümlü alıştırma + cevap anahtarıyla adım adım kapatır.

The reason you stall at a middle band in IELTS Writing is usually not a shortage of fancy vocabulary but sentences that do not stand up. This guide closes, step by step, the seven grammar mistakes that recur in Turkish students — fragments, comma splices, subject-verb agreement, plural -s, articles, verb tense — with rule + worked exercise + answer key.

1. Neden orta bandda takılırsın: dil, taktik değil

IELTS Writing'de orta bandlarda (5.5–6.0) takılmanın sebebi genellikle süslü kelime eksikliği değildir — cümlelerin ayakta durmamasıdır. Değerlendirici bir metni okurken üç soru sorar: Bu cümle tam mı? Özne ile fiil uyuyor mu? İki cümle doğru mu birleştirilmiş? Bu üç soruya "evet" gelmiyorsa, ne kadar iyi kelime kullanırsan kullan Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA) puanın — ve dolayısıyla genel band — tavanlanır.

"Süslü kelime band yükseltmez; ayakta duran cümle yükseltir. Önce cümleyi kırılmaz yap, sonra süsle.

Bu bandda geçerli tek strateji

Karmaşık cümle kurmaya çalışıp cümleyi bozmak yerine doğru basit cümle yaz. Band 6.0 tanımı zaten şunu söyler: basit cümleler çoğunlukla doğru, karmaşık cümle denemeleri hatalı olabilir. Yani karmaşığı denemek zorunda değilsin; basiti hatasız yazmak zorundasın.

1. Why you stall at a middle band: language, not tactics

The reason for stalling at the middle bands (5.5–6.0) in IELTS Writing is usually not a lack of fancy vocabulary — it is that the sentences do not stand up. As the examiner reads a text, they ask three questions: Is this sentence complete? Do the subject and verb agree? Are the two clauses joined correctly? If the answer to these three is not "yes", then however good your vocabulary is, your Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA) score — and therefore your overall band — is capped.

"Fancy vocabulary does not raise your band; a sentence that stands up does. First make the sentence unbreakable, then decorate it.

The only strategy that works at this band

Instead of trying to build a complex sentence and breaking it, write a correct simple sentence. The band 6.0 definition already says: simple sentences mostly correct, attempts at complex sentences may be faulty. So you do not have to attempt the complex; you have to write the simple without error.

2. Why do these mistakes recur in Turkish students?

These mistakes are not carelessness but language transfer (L1 transfer): a Turkish rule is carried over into English. Knowing the cause makes the mistake easier to spot.

Why it matters

Knowing that a mistake has its root in Turkish lets you catch it in advance as you write. These are not random but systematic errors — and because they are systematic, they can be closed systematically.

In TurkishIn EnglishThe resulting error
There is no articlea, an, the are obligatoryPollution is serious problem.
A comma joins two clausesA comma cannot join two full sentencesExercise is good, it reduces stress.
No plural ending after a numberPlural follows a numberShe read five book.
There is no third-person -s-s is obligatory in the present simpleHe go to school.
You can say "informations", "advices"information, advice are uncountableHe gave me three advices.
A sentence can start with "because"Because cannot form a sentence on its ownBecause the traffic is heavy.
"Although" and "but" come togetherAlthough and but do not come togetherAlthough she was tired, but she finished.

3. Yedi hata türü — kural + çözümlü örnek

Her hata türü için önce kural, sonra alıştırma ve cevap anahtarı var. Cevaba bakmadan önce kendin dene; sonra karşılaştır. (Kaynak: Gri IELTS Kamp — Gramer Doğruluğu kitapçığı, 113 alıştırmadan seçme.)

3.1 · Fragment (eksik cümle)

Kural: Bir cümlede üç şey olmalı: özne, çekimli fiil, tam düşünce. Because, although, while, which, who ile başlayan parça tek başına cümle olamaz; -ing ile başlayan parça çekimli fiil sayılmaz. Fragment en çok noktanın erken konmasıyla oluşur.

Tam cümle mi, fragment mı? Fragment ise düzelt:

İfadeKarar + düzeltme
Because many students work part-time.Fragment → Because many students work part-time, they have little time to study.
The government should invest in public transport.Tam cümle ✓
Which is the main reason for the problem.Fragment → This is the main reason for the problem.
Although technology makes life easier.Fragment → Although technology makes life easier, it also creates new problems.
Studying every night without a break.Fragment → Studying every night without a break is exhausting.
While some people prefer to work from home.Fragment → While some people prefer to work from home, others prefer an office.

3.2 · Run-on ve Comma Splice (en sık hata)

Kural: İki tam cümleyi virgül tek başına birleştiremez (comma splice); hiçbir işaret koymamak da yanlıştır (run-on). Dört çözüm: (1) nokta, (2) noktalı virgül, (3) virgül + and/but/so/because, (4) birini yan cümleye çevir. Dikkat: however, therefore, moreover birer zarftır, bağlaç değil — önlerine virgül yetmez, nokta/noktalı virgül gerekir.

HatalıDoğru
Online learning is convenient, it saves travel time.Online learning is convenient because it saves travel time.
The city is crowded, however the air quality is good.The city is crowded. However, the air quality is good.
Many students work part-time they need the money.Many students work part-time because they need the money.
Exercise is important, therefore schools should provide more sport.Exercise is important; therefore, schools should provide more sport.
Some people prefer books, others prefer films.Some people prefer books, while others prefer films.

3.3 · Özne–Fiil Uyumu

Kural: Geniş zamanda üçüncü tekil şahıs fiile -s alır. Araya giren öbek fiili etkilemez: fiil, öbeğin son ismine değil asıl özneye uyar. Each, every, everyone, nobody ve sayılamaz isimler tekildir.

Parantezdeki fiilin doğru biçimi:

CümleCevap
The number of cars in the city ___ (rise) every year.rises — "the number of" tekildir
Every student in these three classes ___ (have) a laptop.has — "every" tekil
The information provided by the researchers ___ (be) reliable.is — "information" sayılamaz/tekil
Technology, together with globalisation, ___ (change) the job market.changes — asıl özne "technology"
Neither of the two solutions ___ (seem) practical.seems — "neither of" tekil
One of the main causes of obesity ___ (be) poor diet.is — asıl özne "one"

3.4 · Çoğul -s ve Sayılabilirlik

Kural: Sayıdan sonra çoğul gelir (three books). Şu isimler asla -s almaz: information, advice, research, equipment, furniture, knowledge, progress, evidence, news, homework, traffic, money. Bunlarla a piece of / a great deal of kullanılır. Many sayılabilir, much sayılamaz ile.

HatalıDoğru
The teacher gave us three advices.The teacher gave us three pieces of advice.
Many researches show that sleep affects memory.A great deal of research shows that sleep affects memory.
We need more informations about the course.We need more information about the course.
The company bought new equipments.The company bought new equipment.
The news are not encouraging.The news is not encouraging.
The traffics in Istanbul are terrible.The traffic in Istanbul is terrible.

3.5 · Article (a, an, the ve sıfır)

Kural: İlk kez bahsediyorsan a/an, ikinci kez the. Tekil sayılabilir isim asla yalnız kalmaz. Genel konuşuyorsan çoğul/sayılamaz isim article almaz (Students need support.). Benzersiz şeyler the alır (the government, the internet). Ülke/şehir isimleri genelde article almaz. Article hatası anlamı bozmaz ama Band 6'ya çıkmayı engeller.

BoşlukCevap
___ education is a basic human right.∅ (genel) — Education...
She wants to become ___ engineer.an
___ government should invest more in transport.The (benzersiz)
The article I read yesterday was convincing. / I read ___ interesting article.1. The (2. bahis) · 2. an (ilk bahis)
Istanbul is ___ largest city in Turkey.the (superlatif)
___ internet has changed the way we work.The
___ Turkey has a young population.∅ (ülke ismi)

3.6 · Zaman Kipi Tutarlılığı

Kural: Task 2 görüş ve genel gerçek → present simple. Task 1 geçmiş tarihli veri → past simple; tarihsiz veri/süreç → present simple, sıklıkla edilgen. Speaking Part 2 hikâye → past simple. En sık hata: bir paragrafta geçmişe kayıp sonra bugüne dönmek.

HatalıDoğru
The graph shows that sales increase sharply between 2010 and 2015....sales increased sharply between 2010 and 2015. (tarihli → geçmiş)
Last summer I go to Antalya and I stayed there.Last summer I went to Antalya and stayed there.
Since 2015, the number of users is increasing.Since 2015, the number of users has been increasing.
When I was a child, I am very shy.When I was a child, I was very shy.
If governments will act now, the problem can be solved.If governments act now, the problem can be solved. (1. koşul)
Yesterday I have finished my essay.Yesterday I finished my essay. (belirli geçmiş)

3.7 · Karma Düzeltme (hata türü söylenmez)

Gerçek yazıda hangi hatayı aradığını kimse söylemez. Her cümlede tek hata var; bul, düzelt, türünü tanı.

HatalıDoğru + tür
Many student prefer to study alone.Many students... — çoğul -s
The government should provides free healthcare....should provide... — modal + yalın fiil
Pollution is serious problem in modern cities....is a serious problem... — article
Although she was tired, but she finished the report.Although she was tired, she finished... — although+but birlikte olmaz
I am agree with this opinion.I agree with this opinion. — agree fiildir
Despite of the rain, the event continued.Despite the rain... — despite + isim (of yok)
I have visited London three years ago.I visited London three years ago. — ago → past simple
There is many reasons why students drop out.There are many reasons... — özne-fiil uyumu

3. The seven mistake types — rule + worked example

For each mistake type there is first a rule, then an exercise and an answer key. Try it yourself before looking at the answer; then compare. (Source: Gri IELTS Camp — Grammatical Accuracy booklet, a selection from 113 exercises.)

3.1 · Fragment (incomplete sentence)

Rule: A sentence must have three things: a subject, a finite verb, a complete thought. A fragment beginning with because, although, while, which, who cannot be a sentence on its own; a fragment beginning with -ing does not count as a finite verb. Fragments most often come from placing a full stop too early.

Full sentence or fragment? If a fragment, fix it:

StatementDecision + fix
Because many students work part-time.Fragment → Because many students work part-time, they have little time to study.
The government should invest in public transport.Full sentence ✓
Which is the main reason for the problem.Fragment → This is the main reason for the problem.
Although technology makes life easier.Fragment → Although technology makes life easier, it also creates new problems.
Studying every night without a break.Fragment → Studying every night without a break is exhausting.
While some people prefer to work from home.Fragment → While some people prefer to work from home, others prefer an office.

3.2 · Run-on and Comma Splice (the most common mistake)

Rule: A comma on its own cannot join two full sentences (comma splice); placing no mark at all is also wrong (run-on). Four fixes: (1) a full stop, (2) a semicolon, (3) a comma + and/but/so/because, (4) turn one into a subordinate clause. Note: however, therefore, moreover are adverbs, not conjunctions — a comma in front of them is not enough, you need a full stop/semicolon.

IncorrectCorrect
Online learning is convenient, it saves travel time.Online learning is convenient because it saves travel time.
The city is crowded, however the air quality is good.The city is crowded. However, the air quality is good.
Many students work part-time they need the money.Many students work part-time because they need the money.
Exercise is important, therefore schools should provide more sport.Exercise is important; therefore, schools should provide more sport.
Some people prefer books, others prefer films.Some people prefer books, while others prefer films.

3.3 · Subject–Verb Agreement

Rule: In the present simple, the third-person singular verb takes -s. An intervening phrase does not affect the verb: the verb agrees with the real subject, not the last noun in the phrase. Each, every, everyone, nobody and uncountable nouns are singular.

The correct form of the verb in brackets:

SentenceAnswer
The number of cars in the city ___ (rise) every year.rises — "the number of" is singular
Every student in these three classes ___ (have) a laptop.has — "every" is singular
The information provided by the researchers ___ (be) reliable.is — "information" is uncountable/singular
Technology, together with globalisation, ___ (change) the job market.changes — the real subject is "technology"
Neither of the two solutions ___ (seem) practical.seems — "neither of" is singular
One of the main causes of obesity ___ (be) poor diet.is — the real subject is "one"

3.4 · Plural -s and Countability

Rule: A plural follows a number (three books). The following nouns never take -s: information, advice, research, equipment, furniture, knowledge, progress, evidence, news, homework, traffic, money. With these, use a piece of / a great deal of. Many is used with countable nouns, much with uncountable.

IncorrectCorrect
The teacher gave us three advices.The teacher gave us three pieces of advice.
Many researches show that sleep affects memory.A great deal of research shows that sleep affects memory.
We need more informations about the course.We need more information about the course.
The company bought new equipments.The company bought new equipment.
The news are not encouraging.The news is not encouraging.
The traffics in Istanbul are terrible.The traffic in Istanbul is terrible.

3.5 · Articles (a, an, the and zero)

Rule: If you are mentioning it for the first time, use a/an; the second time, the. A singular countable noun never stands alone. If you are speaking generally, a plural/uncountable noun takes no article (Students need support.). Unique things take the (the government, the internet). Names of countries/cities usually take no article. An article error does not distort meaning but it prevents you from reaching Band 6.

GapAnswer
___ education is a basic human right.∅ (general) — Education...
She wants to become ___ engineer.an
___ government should invest more in transport.The (unique)
The article I read yesterday was convincing. / I read ___ interesting article.1. The (second mention) · 2. an (first mention)
Istanbul is ___ largest city in Turkey.the (superlative)
___ internet has changed the way we work.The
___ Turkey has a young population.∅ (country name)

3.6 · Verb Tense Consistency

Rule: Task 2 opinions and general truths → present simple. Task 1 dated past data → past simple; undated data/process → present simple, often passive. A Speaking Part 2 story → past simple. The most common mistake: slipping into the past in one paragraph and then returning to the present.

IncorrectCorrect
The graph shows that sales increase sharply between 2010 and 2015....sales increased sharply between 2010 and 2015. (dated → past)
Last summer I go to Antalya and I stayed there.Last summer I went to Antalya and stayed there.
Since 2015, the number of users is increasing.Since 2015, the number of users has been increasing.
When I was a child, I am very shy.When I was a child, I was very shy.
If governments will act now, the problem can be solved.If governments act now, the problem can be solved. (first conditional)
Yesterday I have finished my essay.Yesterday I finished my essay. (definite past)

3.7 · Mixed Correction (the error type is not given)

In real writing, no one tells you which mistake to look for. There is one error in each sentence; find it, fix it, name its type.

IncorrectCorrect + type
Many student prefer to study alone.Many students... — plural -s
The government should provides free healthcare....should provide... — modal + bare verb
Pollution is serious problem in modern cities....is a serious problem... — article
Although she was tired, but she finished the report.Although she was tired, she finished... — although+but don't go together
I am agree with this opinion.I agree with this opinion. — agree is a verb
Despite of the rain, the event continued.Despite the rain... — despite + noun (no of)
I have visited London three years ago.I visited London three years ago. — ago → past simple
There is many reasons why students drop out.There are many reasons... — subject-verb agreement

4. Paragraf temizleme (çözümlü)

Gerçek yazıda hata tek başına durmaz, paragrafın içinde saklanır. Yöntem: paragrafı dört kez oku, her okuyuşta tek hata türünü ara. Aynı anda hepsini aramak işe yaramaz — her tur tek bir avı kovalar:

  1. Birinci tur: yalnızca çoğul -s ara — sayıdan sonra ve genel çoğullarda.
  2. İkinci tur: yalnızca üçüncü tekil -s ara — geniş zaman fiillerinde.
  3. Üçüncü tur: yalnızca virgül ara — comma splice ve run-on avı.
  4. Dördüncü tur: yalnızca article ara — eksik ya da fazla a/an/the.

Hatalı paragraf (Task 2 — sevdiğin dersi okumak)

Many student believe that they should study subject they enjoy. In my opinion this is correct, because a person who enjoy his lesson will work harder. For example my cousin studied engineering, he did not like it and he left the university after two year. This show that interest is very important. Although he was a hardworking person, but he could not continue.

Temiz hali (düzeltmeler altı çizili)

Many students believe that they should study subjects they enjoy. In my opinion this is correct, because a person who enjoys his lessons will work harder. For example, my cousin studied engineering; he did not like it and he left university after two years. This shows that interest is very important. Although he was a hardworking person, he could not continue.

Yakalanan hatalar: çoğul -s (student→students, subject→subjects, year→years), üçüncü tekil -s (enjoy→enjoys, show→shows), comma splice (engineering, he→engineering; he), gereksiz article (the university→university), although+but (son cümlede but atıldı).

4. Paragraph cleaning (worked)

In real writing, an error does not stand alone; it hides inside a paragraph. Method: read the paragraph four times, and on each pass hunt for one error type. Looking for them all at once does not work — each pass chases a single quarry:

  1. First pass: look only for plural -s — after numbers and in general plurals.
  2. Second pass: look only for third-person -s — on present-simple verbs.
  3. Third pass: look only for commas — hunting comma splices and run-ons.
  4. Fourth pass: look only for articles — a missing or extra a/an/the.

Faulty paragraph (Task 2 — studying a subject you love)

Many student believe that they should study subject they enjoy. In my opinion this is correct, because a person who enjoy his lesson will work harder. For example my cousin studied engineering, he did not like it and he left the university after two year. This show that interest is very important. Although he was a hardworking person, but he could not continue.

Clean version (corrections underlined)

Many students believe that they should study subjects they enjoy. In my opinion this is correct, because a person who enjoys his lessons will work harder. For example, my cousin studied engineering; he did not like it and he left university after two years. This shows that interest is very important. Although he was a hardworking person, he could not continue.

Errors caught: plural -s (student→students, subject→subjects, year→years), third-person -s (enjoy→enjoys, show→shows), comma splice (engineering, he→engineering; he), unnecessary article (the university→university), although+but (but dropped in the last sentence).

5. Kendi metnini tarama (yazdıktan sonra 5 dakika)

Listeyi sırayla değil, tek maddeyi arayarak uygula: metni baştan sona okuyup sadece virgülleri ara, sonra baştan sona okuyup sadece -s eklerini ara.

  • Her cümlede özne ve çekimli fiil var mı?
  • Because, although, which ile başlayan tek başına cümle bıraktım mı?
  • İki tam cümleyi sadece virgülle birleştirdim mi? However, therefore önüne nokta koydum mu?
  • Üçüncü tekil şahıs fiillerinde -s var mı? Uzun özne öbeğinde fiil asıl özneye mi uyuyor?
  • Sayıdan sonra çoğul -s koydum mu? information, advice, research, equipment kelimelerine -s eklemedim mi?
  • Tekil sayılabilir isim yalnız mı kaldı, article aldı mı? Genel anlamda gereksiz the ekledim mi?
  • Task 2'de present simple'da kaldım mı? Task 1'de tarih varsa geçmiş zaman kullandım mı?
  • I am agree yazdım mı? Although ile but'ı aynı cümlede kullandım mı?

En çok puan getiren üç madde

  • Comma splice. İki tam cümleyi virgülle birleştirmeyi bırakmak tek başına GRA'yı yükseltir.
  • Üçüncü tekil -s. En kolay düzeltilen, en sık yapılan hata.
  • Fragment. Because ile başlayan tek başına cümle bırakmamak.

5. Scanning your own text (5 minutes after writing)

Apply the list not all at once but one item at a time: read the text from start to finish looking only for commas, then read it again from start to finish looking only for -s endings.

  • Does every sentence have a subject and a finite verb?
  • Did I leave a stand-alone sentence starting with because, although, which?
  • Did I join two full sentences with only a comma? Did I put a full stop before however, therefore?
  • Is there an -s on third-person singular verbs? In a long subject phrase, does the verb agree with the real subject?
  • Did I put a plural -s after numbers? Did I avoid adding -s to information, advice, research, equipment?
  • Is a singular countable noun left alone, or did it take an article? Did I add an unnecessary the in a general sense?
  • Did I stay in present simple in Task 2? In Task 1, did I use the past tense where there is a date?
  • Did I write I am agree? Did I use although and but in the same sentence?

The three highest-scoring items

  • Comma splice. Just stopping joining two full sentences with a comma raises your GRA on its own.
  • Third-person -s. The easiest to fix, the most frequently made mistake.
  • Fragment. Not leaving a stand-alone sentence starting with because.
