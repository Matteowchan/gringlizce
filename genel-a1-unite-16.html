Ünite 16 · Past Simple Irregular · Bölüm 1/7

Grammar: Past Simple Irregular (went / had / made / didn't / Did...?)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Geçmişte Ne Yaptın? — Bu Kez Düzensiz Fiiller

Geçmişte ne yaptığını anlatmak için Past Simple kullanılır. Düzenli fiillerde bu kolaydı: fiilin sonuna -ed ekliyordun (worked, called, started). Ama İngilizcenin en sık kullanılan fiilleri bu kurala uymaz — bunlar düzensiz fiillerdir (irregular verbs) ve tamamen değişirler: go → went, have → had, make → made. Bu ünitenin tamamı bu düzensiz fiillere ayrılmıştır:

  • "I went to a meeting yesterday."Dün bir toplantıya gittim. (go → went)
  • "We had lunch with the client."Müşteriyle öğle yemeği yedik. (have → had)
  • "She made a new design."Yeni bir tasarım yaptı. (make → made)
  • "They didn't come to the office."Ofise gelmediler. (olumsuz)
  • "Did you see my email?"E-postamı gördün mü? (soru)

Kısacası: düzenli fiiller -ed alır, düzensiz fiillerin ise ezberlenmesi gereken özel bir geçmiş hali vardır. İyi haber: olumsuz (didn't) ve soru (Did) yapıları her iki grupta da aynıdır. Bu bölümde en sık düzensiz fiilleri, didn't ile olumsuzu ve Did ile soruyu öğreneceksin.

1. Hızlı Hatırlatma: Düzenli Fiiller (-ed) (Quick Recap)

Düzensizlere geçmeden önce düzenli fiilleri kısaca hatırlayalım. Çoğu fiilin geçmiş hali basittir: sonuna -ed eklenir ve özneye göre DEĞİŞMEZ. Bu ünitede bunlar arka planda; asıl odak düzensiz fiiller:

FiilGeçmiş (V2)ÖrnekTürkçesi
workworkedI worked late yesterday.Dün geç saate kadar çalıştım.
callcalledShe called the customer this morning.Bu sabah müşteriyi aradı.
startstartedThe meeting started late.Toplantı geç başladı.
Aklında kalsın
  • Düzenli fiil = fiil + -ed (worked, called, started).
  • Düzensiz fiil = tamamen değişir, ezberlenir (go → went, not "goed").
  • En sık hata: düzensiz fiile -ed eklemek. "He goed" ✗ → "He went" ✓

2. Düzensiz Fiiller — Bu Ünitenin Kalbi (Irregular Verbs)

İngilizcenin en sık kullanılan fiilleri kurala uymaz: -ed almazlar, tamamen değişirler. Kötü haber: bunları ezberlemen gerekir. İyi haber: A1'de sadece bu 14 tanesi yeter — ve hepsi iş hayatının en sık fiilleri:

FiilGeçmiş (V2)ÖrnekTürkçesi
gowentI went to a meeting at ten.Onda bir toplantıya gittim.
havehadWe had lunch with the client.Müşteriyle öğle yemeği yedik.
makemadeShe made a new design.Yeni bir tasarım yaptı.
meetmetI met the new manager yesterday.Dün yeni yöneticiyle tanıştım.
comecameHe came to the office early.Ofise erken geldi.
seesawI saw your email this morning.E-postanı bu sabah gördüm.
sendsentThey sent the report on Friday.Raporu cuma günü gönderdiler.
writewroteShe wrote three emails.Üç e-posta yazdı.
taketookI took a taxi to the airport.Havalimanına taksiyle gittim.
getgotHe got his first salary last week.Geçen hafta ilk maaşını aldı.
givegaveThe manager gave me the contract.Yönetici bana sözleşmeyi verdi.
saysaid"Good job," she said."İyi iş," dedi.
dodidWe did a lot of work yesterday.Dün çok iş yaptık.
buyboughtThey bought new computers.Yeni bilgisayarlar aldılar.
Ezber taktiği
  • Düzensiz fiili tek başına değil, bir cümle içinde ezberle: "went" değil, "I went to a meeting."
  • Bu 14 fiili her gün 2 dakika tekrar et — bir haftada oturur.
  • "say" fiilinin geçmişi "said" yazılır ama "sed" diye okunur.

3. Olumsuz Cümle: didn't (Negative)

Olumsuz için tek yardımcı yeter: didn't (did not). Kural çok nettir: didn't + YALIN fiil. Fiil asla -ed almaz, asla V2 olmaz — çünkü geçmiş anlamını "didn't" zaten taşıyor:

OlumluOlumsuzTürkçesi
I finished the report.I didn't finish the report.Raporu bitirmedim.
She called the customer.She didn't call the customer.Müşteriyi aramadı.
They went to the meeting.They didn't go to the meeting.Toplantıya gitmediler.
He sent the email.He didn't send the email.E-postayı göndermedi.
En kritik kural
  • "I didn't finished the report." ✗ → "I didn't finish the report." ✓ — didn't'ten sonra fiil YALIN kalır.
  • Düzensiz fiilde de aynı: "She didn't went" ✗ → "She didn't go" ✓
  • didn't her özne için aynıdır: I didn't, she didn't, they didn't — değişmez.

4. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar: Did ...? (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru için cümlenin başına Did gelir ve fiil yine yalın kalır: You called the customer. → Did you call the customer?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Did you call the customer?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did she finish the design?Yes, she did.No, she didn't.
Did they sign the contract?Yes, they did.No, they didn't.
Did the meeting start late?Yes, it did.No, it didn't.
Dikkat
  • Kısa cevapta ana fiil TEKRAR EDİLMEZ: "Yes, I called." yerine "Yes, I did."
  • Soru kelimesiyle sıra aynı kalır: "What did you do yesterday?", "Where did she go?", "When did they arrive?"
  • Sık zaman ifadeleri: yesterday (dün), last week/month/year (geçen hafta/ay/yıl), two days ago (iki gün önce), on Monday (pazartesi günü).

5. was/were mi, Past Simple mı? (State or Action?)

Bu ünitenin en önemli ayrımı: geçmişten bahsederken hangisini seçeceksin? Cevap basit — cümlede eylem var mı, yok mu?

  • "The meeting was long."Toplantı uzundu. (durum — fiil yok, sıfat var)
  • "The meeting started late."Toplantı geç başladı. (eylem — "start" fiili var)
  • "I was at the office. I worked until seven."Ofisteydim. Yediye kadar çalıştım. (önce durum, sonra eylem)

Soru sorarken de aynı mantık geçerlidir — Did mi, Was/Were mü?

Sorudaki şeyKullanÖrnek
Durum (sıfat, yer, meslek — ana fiil yok)Was / WereWas the meeting long? · Were you at the office?
Eylem (bir fiil var: start, call, go...)Did + yalın fiilDid the meeting start late? · Did you call him?
Pratik test
  • Cümlede be dışında bir fiil görüyorsan (start, work, go, send...) → did/didn't.
  • Sadece sıfat/yer/isim varsa (long, at the office, a manager) → was/were.
  • İkisi asla birlikte kullanılmaz: "Did you were busy?" ✗ → "Were you busy?" ✓

6. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Past Simple'da Türkçe konuşanların en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I didn't went to the meeting.I didn't go to the meeting.didn't'ten sonra fiil yalın kalır.
Did you sent the email?Did you send the email?Did'den sonra da fiil yalın kalır.
He goed home early.He went home early.go düzensizdir: went. "-ed" almaz.
I did finished the report.I finished the report.Olumlu cümlede "did" kullanılmaz; fiil zaten V2 olur.
Yesterday I finish the report.Yesterday I finished the report.Geçmiş zaman ifadesi (yesterday) varsa fiil V2 olmalı.
She maked a design.She made a design.make düzensizdir: made.
Did you were at the office?Were you at the office?Durum sorusunda Was/Were kullanılır; Did ile birleşmez.

7. Uygulama: Dünkü İş Günü (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Aşağıdaki diyalog düzenli fiilleri, düzensiz fiilleri, didn't'i ve Did sorularını birlikte kullanıyor — koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Hi Deniz! I didn't see you yesterday. Where were you?Selam Deniz! Dün seni görmedim. Neredeydin?
  • — I was at the Ankara office. I visited our new customer there.Ankara ofisindeydim. Oradaki yeni müşterimizi ziyaret ettim.
  • — Oh! Did you travel by plane?Ee! Uçakla mı gittin?
  • — No, I didn't. I went by train. It was comfortable.Hayır. Trenle gittim. Rahattı.
  • — Did the meeting go well?Toplantı iyi geçti mi?
  • — Yes, it did. We agreed on the price and they signed the contract!Evet, iyi geçti. Fiyatta anlaştık ve sözleşmeyi imzaladılar!
  • — That's great news! What did you do after the meeting?Harika haber! Toplantıdan sonra ne yaptın?
  • — We celebrated at a restaurant, and I came back late at night.Bir restoranda kutladık ve gece geç saatte döndüm.
  • — You worked a lot yesterday. Did you finish your report too?Dün çok çalışmışsın. Raporunu da bitirdin mi?
  • — No, I didn't finish it — I was very tired. But I started it on the train!Hayır, bitirmedim — çok yorgundum. Ama trende başladım!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane "did" ile kurulmuş soru var? (Cevap: 4 — "What did you do" dahil)
  • "went" ve "came" hangi fiillerin geçmiş halleri? (Cevap: go ve come)
  • Deniz neden "I didn't finish it" diyor da "I didn't finished it" demiyor? (Bölüm 3'teki kurala bak.)

Düzensiz Fiilleri Nasıl Oturtursun? (What's Next?)

Düzensiz fiiller ezber ister ama korkma — günlük İngilizcenin büyük kısmı sadece birkaç düzine düzensiz fiille döner ve bu ünitedeki went, had, made, saw, took, came, got gibi fiiller tam olarak onlar. Taktik basit: her fiili tek başına değil bir cümle içinde ezberle ("I went to a meeting"), her gün 2-3 dakika tekrar et ve yazarken didn't/Did'den sonra fiilin daima YALIN kaldığını hatırla. Bir hafta içinde bu fiiller kendiliğinden gelir. Sıradaki ünitelerde daha fazla düzensiz fiil ve geçmiş anlatı seni bekliyor.

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the verbs with their past forms, then use them in your own sentences. (Fiilleri geçmiş halleriyle öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

go / went (v)
to move to another place (gitmek)
Syn: travel (seyahat etmek) · Ant: stay (kalmak)
"I went to a meeting in Ankara."
have / had (v)
to eat, hold, or experience something (sahip olmak; yemek/yaşamak)
Syn: hold (tutmak) · Ant: lack (yoksun olmak)
"We had lunch with the client."
make / made (v)
to create or produce something (yapmak, üretmek)
Syn: create (yaratmak) · Ant: break (bozmak)
"She made a new design."
see / saw (v)
to notice with your eyes (görmek)
Syn: notice (fark etmek) · Ant: miss (gözden kaçırmak)
"I saw your email this morning."
take / took (v)
to carry, or to use transport (almak; -e binmek)
Syn: catch (yakalamak — otobüs/tren) · Ant: give (vermek)
"He took a taxi to the airport."
come / came (v)
to arrive at a place (gelmek)
Syn: arrive (varmak) · Ant: leave (ayrılmak)
"She came to the office early."
get / got (v)
to receive or obtain something (almak, elde etmek)
Syn: receive (almak) · Ant: lose (kaybetmek)
"We got the contract last week."
give / gave (v)
to hand something to someone (vermek)
Syn: hand (uzatmak) · Ant: take (almak)
"The manager gave me the report."
meet / met (v)
to see and talk with someone (tanışmak, buluşmak)
Syn: see (görüşmek) · Ant: miss (kaçırmak)
"I met the new client yesterday."
send / sent (v)
to make something go to someone (göndermek)
Syn: post (yollamak) · Ant: receive (almak)
"She sent three emails on Friday."
write / wrote (v)
to put words on paper or a screen (yazmak)
Syn: type (klavyeyle yazmak) · Ant: erase (silmek)
"He wrote a long report."
buy / bought (v)
to get something for money (satın almak)
Syn: purchase (satın almak) · Ant: sell / sold (satmak)
"They bought new computers."
Toplantı sonrası: sözleşme imzalandı, el sıkışılıyor — dün ne olduğunu Past Simple ile anlatıyorlar.

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

  • Think about YOUR last week at work or school. What did you do? (Kendi geçen haftanı düşün. Neler yaptın?)
  • What is a busy week like for you? (Senin için yoğun bir hafta nasıl geçer?)
  • Look at the picture below. What does it show? (Aşağıdaki görsele bak. Ne anlatıyor olabilir?)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin — konuyu yakala.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar her zaman metnin içindedir.
  • Bu metin bir geçmiş anlatısıdır ve düzensiz fiillerle doludur: V2 hallerine dikkat et (went, had, made, saw, took...).

Verbs to spot (Metinde geçen düzensiz geçmiş formlar — gözün bunları arasın): came · had · gave · made · sent · saw · went · took · met · said · bought · got · left · spent

Selin'in geçen haftası: yoğun günler, imzalanan sözleşme ve hafta sonu ailesiyle zaman — hepsi düzensiz fiillerle anlatılıyor.
Selin's Busy Week

Selin is thirty years old. She lives in Istanbul with her family. She works at a company. The company's name is Baytek. Baytek makes computer programs and websites. Many people work there. Selin is a designer. She makes designs for the screen. She chooses the colours and the pictures. She likes her job very much. Selin works with a small team. There are four people in her team. Their names are Selin, Aylin, Emre, and Deniz. Deniz is the manager. He is a kind boss. The team is friendly, and they help each other every day. Selin goes to the office by bus. The office is on a big street in the city centre. She starts work at nine o'clock in the morning. She has lunch at one o'clock. Usually, she goes home at six o'clock. This is a normal week for Selin. But last week was not a normal week. Last week was very special and very busy.

Last week was very busy for Selin at Baytek. On Monday, she came to the office early and had a long meeting with her team. The manager gave her an important job: a new design for a big customer.

The meeting on Monday was long, but it was interesting. All the team sat around a big table. Deniz, the manager, talked about a new project. The project was for a big customer. The customer's company was very famous. The customer wanted a new design for a website. It was an important job for Baytek. Deniz looked at Selin and smiled. 'Selin, this job is for you,' he said. Selin was happy, but she was also a little scared. The job was very big, and the time was short. She had only a few days. Selin wrote some notes in her book. Then she looked at Deniz and said, 'Yes, I can do it.' After the meeting, Selin made a cup of coffee. She sat at her desk. She was quiet, and she started to think about the new design. Ideas came into her head, and she began to draw.

On Tuesday, Selin made the first design. She worked on it all day and sent it to the customer in the evening. On Wednesday morning, she saw the customer's answer in her email. They liked the design, but they wanted two small changes.

Selin read the customer's email again on Wednesday morning. The customer wanted two small changes. The first change was about the colour. The customer did not like the red colour. They wanted a blue colour. The second change was about the words. The customer wanted bigger words on the page. Selin thought the changes were easy. She opened her computer and started to work. First, she changed the red colour to blue. Then she made the words a little bigger. The new design looked very nice and clean. At lunch time, Selin showed the design to her friend Aylin. Aylin looked at the screen and smiled. 'This is beautiful, Selin,' she said. 'The customer will love it.' In the afternoon, Selin sent the new design to the customer. Then she waited for their answer. She felt happy and calm. Her hard work was good work.

On Wednesday evening, Selin got ready for the big meeting. The meeting with the customer was on Thursday. Selin wanted to be ready. She looked at all her notes. She practised her words at home. She stood in front of the mirror and talked about the design. Her cat watched her from the sofa. 'Do not be scared,' Selin said to herself. 'The design is good.' Then she chose her clothes for the meeting. She went to bed early because she was tired.

On Thursday, Selin went to a meeting with the customer, Mr. Blake. She took the train to the city centre because the traffic was terrible. At the meeting, she met Mr. Blake and his team. She was a little nervous, but she said everything clearly. In the end, Mr. Blake said, 'This is excellent work,' and he bought the design!

After the meeting, Selin was very happy. She walked out of the building with a big smile. She took the train back to the office again. On the train, she called her manager. 'Deniz! Mr. Blake bought the design!' she said. Deniz was very glad. 'Well done, Selin! That is great news,' he said. When Selin came back to the office, all the team clapped their hands. They were very happy for her. Selin's face was a little red, but she smiled. That evening, she went home late. She was tired, but she felt great. She ate a light dinner and called her mother. Then she went to bed early. It was a long day, but it was a very good day.

On Friday, the team had good news. They got the contract, so everyone was very happy. Selin didn't work late that day. She left the office at five and went home. At the weekend, she didn't think about work. She spent time with her family and had a big dinner with her friends.

On Saturday, Selin got up late. She did not set an alarm clock. She made a big breakfast. She ate eggs, bread, cheese, and olives. She drank tea in the garden. The sun was warm, and the birds sang. Selin felt happy and relaxed. In the afternoon, she went to the park with her sister. They walked and talked for two hours. They ate ice cream near the lake. In the evening, Selin cooked dinner for her family. Her mother and father came to her house. They ate together and laughed a lot. On Sunday, Selin met her old friends. They went to a small café near the sea. They drank coffee and ate cake. They talked about many things. Selin did not open her computer all weekend. She did not read her work emails. She just rested and had fun with the people she loves.

'Last week was hard, but I learned a lot,' Selin says. 'I didn't know this customer before, and now we are partners. It was a great week!'

Now Selin and Mr. Blake work together often. They send emails to each other every week. Baytek has a new customer, and Selin has a new friend. Sometimes, a hard week is the best week. Selin knows this now. Every morning, she goes to the office with a smile on her face. She is not scared of big jobs any more. She is ready for the next one. And she always remembers her busy, happy week.

Geçmişi Anlatan Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (Writing a Past Narrative)

Geçmiş anlatısı, cümleleri zamana göre dizip bağlaçlarla bağlamaktır. A1'de öğrendiğin beş bağlaç geçmiş anlatıda da aynen çalışır — bu kez Past Simple cümleleri bağlıyorlar:

orveyaSeçenek sunarDid you go by bus or by train?

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı anlatı ✓
I started my job. I was nervous. My colleagues helped me. I learned a lot. I am happy. I started my job last month and I was very nervous. But my colleagues helped me, so I learned a lot. Now I am happy because they are my friends.
Geçmiş anlatının iki motoru
  • Zaman ifadeleri anlatıyı sıralar: On my first day... In the first week... Last Friday... After the meeting...
  • was/were + Past Simple karışımı anlatıyı canlandırır: önce durumu ver (I was nervous), sonra eylemi anlat (but I asked questions).

Bu kez ekibe değil, bir arkadaşına yazıyorsun — yapı aynıdır ama ton samimidir:

  • Açılış: "Hi ...," · "Hello ...," · "Dear ...,"

Task (Görev)

Write an email to a friend about a busy week at your job. (Bir arkadaşına işteki yoğun bir haftanı anlatan bir e-posta yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • at least 6 Past Simple verbs, at least 4 irregular (en az 6 geçmiş zaman fiili, en az 4'ü düzensiz: went, had, made, saw, took, came, got...)
  • at least 1 sentence with didn't (en az 1 didn't'li cümle)
  • 1-2 sentences with was/were for feelings or situations (durum/duygu için 1-2 was/were cümlesi)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 verbs from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 fiil: went, had, made, saw, took, came, got, gave, met, sent...)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Hi ... / Best wishes,)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"Last week was really busy at work ..." "On Monday, I went ..., and then ..." "We had good news on Friday, so ..."

Sample Answer (~160 words) Hi Ali,

How are you? Last week was very busy at work, so I have a lot of news!

On Monday, I came to the office early and had a long meeting with my team. My manager gave me a big design job for an important customer. On Tuesday, I made the first design and sent it in the evening. On Wednesday, I saw the customer's answer — they liked it, but they wanted two small changes.

On Thursday, I took the train to the city and met the customer, Mr. Blake. I was a little nervous, but the meeting went well and he bought the design! On Friday, we got the contract, so everyone was very happy.

I didn't work at the weekend. I spent time with my family and had a big dinner with friends. It was hard, but I learned a lot!

Visit me soon.
