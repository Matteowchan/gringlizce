Grammar: Nouns & Quantities

Kaç tane? Ne kadar? Sayıları ve miktarları söyle! (How many? How much? Say numbers and amounts.)

Merhaba! Ben Gri. Bugün şeyleri sayacağız! Sayabildiklerimiz için çoğul yaparız: one apple → three apples. Ama su ve süt gibi şeyler sayılmaz: some water, some milk. Az mı çok mu? How many books? ve How much water? Hazır mısın?

Sayabilir miyim? (Two Ideas)

Bazı şeyleri sayabiliriz (one, two, three) ve çoğul yaparız: apples, books. Bazı şeyleri sayamayız (su, süt) — bunlar hep tekildir: water, milk. Aynı sepet, iki tür isim:

  • There are three apples and two books.Üç elma ve iki kitap var. (sayabilirim = çoğul)
  • There is some water and some milk.Biraz su ve biraz süt var. (sayamam = çoğul yok)
  • How many apples? How much water?Kaç elma? Ne kadar su?
Kolay yol
  • Sayabiliyorsan (1, 2, 3) → çoğul yap: apples, books.
  • Sayamıyorsan (su, süt, pirinç) → çoğul yok: water, milk, rice.

1. Çoğullar: -s / -es / Düzensiz (Plurals)

Bir şeyden birden fazla varsa çoğul yaparız. Çoğu isme sona -s ekleriz; bazılarına -es; birkaç isim ise düzensizdir:

KuralTekil → ÇoğulÖrnek
çoğu isim +sapple → applesthree apples
-s, -x, -ch, -sh sonrası +esbox → boxestwo boxes, three buses
düzensiz (ezberle)child → childrentwo children, three men
Kolay yol
  • Çoğu isim: sona -s ekle. apples, books, dogs.
  • box, bus, watch, dish gibi bitişler → -es: boxes, buses.
  • Düzensiz: child → children, man → men, foot → feet.

2. Sayılabilir / Sayılamaz (Countable / Uncountable)

Bazı isimleri sayabiliriz (one, two, three) — bunlara countable deriz. Bazılarını sayamayız (su, süt) — bunlara uncountable deriz:

Sayılabilir (countable)Sayılamaz (uncountable)
an apple, two appleswater (çoğulu yok)
a book, five booksmilk, rice, bread
a cat, three catsmoney, sugar
Kolay yol
  • Countable: sayı ile kullan → three apples. Çoğulu var.
  • Uncountable: sayı yok, çoğul yok → some water (❌ two waters).

3. some / any (Biraz / Hiç)

some = biraz (olumlu cümlelerde). any = hiç (olumsuz cümlelerde ve sorularda). İkisi hem sayılabilir hem sayılamaz isimlerle kullanılır:

KelimeNe zaman?Örnek
someolumlu (+)I have some water. There are some apples.
anyolumsuz (−)There isn't any milk.
anysoru (?)Do you have any books?
Kolay yol
  • Olumlu cümle → some: I have some juice.
  • Olumsuz ya da soru → any: There isn't any milk. Any books?

4. much / many / a lot of (Ne kadar / Kaç tane)

Miktar sormak için: many sayılabilirle, much sayılamazla. a lot of ise ikisiyle de kullanılır ve "çok" demektir:

KelimeKullanımÖrnek
manysayılabilirHow many books?
muchsayılamazHow much water?
a lot ofikisi de (çok)I have a lot of toys. There is a lot of water.
Kolay yol
  • Sayabiliyorsan → many: many apples, many books.
  • Sayamıyorsan → much: much water, much milk.
  • "Çok" demek için ikisinde de → a lot of.

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Doğru ✓ / Yanlış ✗)

İsimleri ve miktarları karıştırmak kolaydır. Doğru biçimi seç:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓
three applethree apples
many watermuch water
some milkssome milk
Altın kural
  • Sayı + isim → çoğul (-s). Sayılamaz isim → çoğul yok, much kullan.

6. In the Kitchen (Mini Diyalog)

İki çocuk mutfaktaki şeyleri konuşuyor. Koyu kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — How many apples are there? — There are three apples.— Kaç elma var? — Üç elma var.
  • — Is there any milk? — No, there isn't any milk.— Hiç süt var mı? — Hayır, hiç süt yok.
  • — How much water do you want? — Just some water, please.— Ne kadar su istersin? — Sadece biraz su, lütfen.
  • — I have a lot of cookies! — And I have two boxes of juice.— Çok kurabiyem var! — Bende de iki kutu meyve suyu var.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "three apple" ✗ → "three apples" ✓ (sayı + çoğul).
  • "many water" ✗ → "much water" ✓ (sayılamaz → much).
  • "some milks" ✗ → "some milk" ✓ (sayılamaz → çoğul yok).

Let's Play! (Alıştırma)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Speaking: My Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan! (Learn the words, then use them.)

İsimler ve Miktarlar (Nouns & Quantities)

Her kelimenin bir işi var: bazıları isim (apple, water), bazıları miktar (some, many). Örnekle kullan!

apple / apples (n)
apple → apples (elma) — sayılabilir
"There are three apples."
box / boxes (n)
box → boxes (kutu)
Dikkat: -x sonrası -es (box → boxes).
"I have two boxes of toys."
child / children (n)
child → children (çocuk) — düzensiz
Dikkat: düzensiz çoğul (child → children).
"Two children are playing."
water (n)
(su) — sayılamaz
Dikkat: çoğulu yok (❌ waters).
"I have some water."
milk (n)
(süt) — sayılamaz
"There isn't any milk."
some (quantity)
(biraz) → olumlu cümlede
Arkadaşları: some apples, some water.
"There are some cookies."
any (quantity)
(hiç) → olumsuz / soru
Arkadaşları: not any, any books?
"Do you have any juice?"
many (quantity)
(kaç / çok) → sayılabilir
"How many books do you have?"
much (quantity)
(ne kadar) → sayılamaz

"I have a lot of toys."
Bahçedeki arkadaşlar: uğur böceği, kelebek ve çiçek.

Your Turn! (Senin Sıran)

Write 5 sentences: say how many things you have and how much. (5 cümle yaz: kaç tane / ne kadar şeyin var?)

Kurallar
  • Sayabildiklerini çoğul yap → three apples, two books.
  • Sayamadıklarınla → some water, some milk.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"There are three ..." "I have some ..." "There isn't any ..." "How many ...?" "I have a lot of ..."
Sample Answer 1. There are three apples on the table.
2. I have two books and five toys.
3. I have some water in my bottle.
4. There isn't any milk in the fridge.
5. How many crayons do you have?

Reading: A Picnic in the Park

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okurken sayıları ve miktar kelimelerini bul. Bunlar sana kaç tane / ne kadar olduğunu söyler:

three apples · two sandwiches (sayılabilir) · some water · any milk · a lot of grapes (miktar)

İpucu
  • Metin bir pikniği anlatıyor — sepette çok şey var. Sayılara dikkat et!
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruların cevaplarını metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A Picnic in the Park

Hi! I am Ada. Today we are going to the park for a picnic. Look in my basket! There are three apples and two big sandwiches. There are also some cookies — I think there are six cookies!

I love picnics with my family. In my bag there are two red apples and one green apple. I have a small yellow blanket and a big blue plate. My mum makes nice sandwiches with cheese and tomato. There is some soft bread too. Everyone is hungry and happy today!

I have some water and some orange juice. But there isn't any milk. Emir wants milk, so he is a little sad. "How many bananas do we have?" asks Emir. "We have two bananas," I say.

Emir likes fruit very much. He counts the bananas again. 'One, two, we have two bananas!' he says. There isn't any milk, so he drinks orange juice. There is a lot of water in a big bottle. We are not thirsty now. Emir smiles and he is happy again.

A picnic needs a lot of nice food, and we have so much today. There is a lot of bread and there is some yellow cheese. There are many sandwiches on the big plate. We have some sweet grapes and some fresh oranges. There isn't any milk and there isn't much salad, but we don't mind. There is a lot of cold water for everyone. My family loves to eat outside on a warm day. How much food do you take on a picnic?

The park is big and green. There are a lot of children here. Two boys play with a ball. The children run and jump. I can see three dogs and one small cat. The cat is funny!

The park is my favourite place. There are many tall trees and a lot of green grass. Some children fly a kite and some children play football. I can see three dogs and one small cat near the trees. The dogs run fast and the cat sits under a bench. It is a busy, happy park!

Mum brings a lot of grapes and some bread. There isn't much salad, but that is okay. We have a lot of food for a happy picnic! How many things are in your picnic basket?

Mum opens the big basket slowly. There are a lot of grapes and some sweet oranges. There isn't much salad, but we have a lot of other food. I take some grapes and Emir takes a cookie. We share everything and we are not hungry now. A picnic with family is always fun!

We sit on a big blanket. It is warm and sunny. Emir has four red boxes. "How much juice is there?" he asks. "There is a lot of juice!" I say. We eat and we laugh. What a happy day!

Writing: In My Bag

Çantanda neler var, kaç tane? Anlat! (What is in your bag? How many?)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveThere are three books and two pens.
butamaI have some water but there isn't any juice.
becauseçünküI am happy because I have a lot of toys.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about the things in your school bag. (Okul çantandaki şeyleri anlat.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one plural noun (çoğul isim — three books / two pens)
  • one uncountable with some (sayılamaz — some water)
  • one negative with any (olumsuz — There isn't any ...)
  • a question with How many / How much

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"There are ..." "I have some ..." "There isn't any ..." "How many ...?"

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~45 words) Look in my school bag! There are three books and two pens. I have some water and a lot of stickers. But there isn't any juice, so I am a little sad. I like my red pencil box. How many crayons do you have? I have six crayons!
