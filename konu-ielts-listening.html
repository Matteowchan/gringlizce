IELTS ListeningIELTS Listening — Bölümler, Soru Tipleri, Stratejiler

IELTS Listening tek dinlemede çözülen, hız ve dikkat isteyen bir bölümdür. Bu rehber sınavın dört bölümünü, on soru tipini, ses akışındaki tuzakları ve yazım-format kurallarını örneklerle açar; band 6 ile band 8 arasındaki farkı dinleme davranışı düzeyinde gösterir.

IELTS Listening is a section solved on a single listen, demanding speed and attention. This guide opens up the four sections of the exam, the ten question types, the traps in the audio flow and the spelling-and-format rules with examples; it shows the difference between band 6 and band 8 at the level of listening behaviour.

1. Genel Yapı: 4 Bölüm, 40 Soru, ~30 Dakika

IELTS Listening tüm adaylar için aynıdır: Academic ve General Training modülleri bu bölümde birebir aynı testi çözer. Ses kaydı yaklaşık 30 dakika sürer ve yalnızca bir kez çalınır. Kağıt-tabanlı sınavda kaydın sonunda cevapları cevap kağıdına aktarmak için 10 dakika transfer süresi verilir; bilgisayar-tabanlı sınavda ise transfer süresi yoktur, onun yerine yalnızca 2 dakika kontrol süresi vardır çünkü cevapları zaten ekrana yazarsınız.

Test dört bölüme (section / part) ayrılır ve her bölümde 10 soru bulunur; toplam 40 soru. Zorluk bölümden bölüme kademeli olarak artar: 1. bölüm en kolay, 4. bölüm en zordur. Her bölümden önce soruları okumanız için kısa bir boşluk, çoğunda bölümün ortasında ya da sonunda ikinci bir okuma boşluğu verilir.

1. Overall Structure: 4 Sections, 40 Questions, ~30 Minutes

IELTS Listening is the same for all candidates: the Academic and General Training modules take the identical test in this section. The recording lasts about 30 minutes and plays only once. In the paper-based exam you are given 10 minutes of transfer time at the end to copy your answers onto the answer sheet; in the computer-based exam there is no transfer time, only 2 minutes of checking time instead, because you have already typed your answers on screen.

The test is divided into four sections (parts) with 10 questions each; 40 questions in total. The difficulty rises gradually from section to section: Section 1 is the easiest, Section 4 the hardest. Before each section there is a short gap for you to read the questions, and in most there is a second reading gap in the middle or at the end.

2. Section 1 — Günlük Diyalog ve Form Doldurma

İlk bölüm iki kişi arasında günlük, işlevsel bir konuşmadır: bir müşteri ile resepsiyonist, bir kiracı ile emlakçı, bir müşteri ile çağrı merkezi görevlisi. Amaç bilgi alışverişidir — isim, adres, tarih, telefon, fiyat, tercih. Dil sadedir ama bilgi yoğunluğu yüksektir: sayılar, harf harf söylenen (spelled out) isimler ve rakamlar peş peşe gelir.

Section 1 neredeyse her zaman form / note completion ile başlar. Kolaylığı yanıltıcıdır: puanınızın en kolay 10 sorusu buradadır, dolayısıyla hedef %100 doğrudur. Öncelikli zorluk anlamak değil, doğru yazmak ve sözlü bir "düzeltmeyi" (correction) kaçırmamaktır.

Mini transkript + soru

— Could I take your surname, please?
— Yes, it's Harrington. H-A-double R-I-N-G-T-O-N.
— Thank you. And a contact number?
— It's oh-seven-nine-double-four, one-two-three... sorry, one-two-eight, oh-six-five.

Soru: Surname: ________  |  Phone: 0794 41 2 ___ 065

Cevap: Harrington / 8. Konuşmacı "three" dedi, sonra "sorry, one-two-eight" diyerek düzeltti. Son söylenen sayı doğru cevaptır; ilk duyduğunuzu yazıp geçerseniz tuzağa düşersiniz. "double R" = RR, "oh" = 0.

  • Yap: Alfabeyi ve 13/30, 40/14 gibi karışan sayı çiftlerini akıcı tanıyana kadar çalış. Türk adaylar için en sık kayıp buradadır.
  • Yap: "Sorry", "actually", "I mean", "let me change that" duyduğunda kalemini kaldır — düzeltme geliyor.
  • Yapma: Boşluğun yanındaki örnek cevabı (genelde bir tane verilir) atlama; formatın nasıl olması gerektiğini o gösterir.

2. Section 1 — Everyday Dialogue and Form-Filling

The first section is an everyday, functional conversation between two people: a customer and a receptionist, a tenant and an estate agent, a customer and a call-centre operator. The aim is an exchange of information — name, address, date, phone, price, preference. The language is simple but the information density is high: numbers, names spelled out and figures come one after another.

Section 1 almost always begins with form / note completion. Its ease is deceptive: the easiest 10 questions of your score are here, so the target is 100% correct. The main challenge is not understanding but writing correctly and not missing a spoken "correction".

Mini transcript + question

— Could I take your surname, please?
— Yes, it's Harrington. H-A-double R-I-N-G-T-O-N.
— Thank you. And a contact number?
— It's oh-seven-nine-double-four, one-two-three... sorry, one-two-eight, oh-six-five.

Question: Surname: ________  |  Phone: 0794 41 2 ___ 065

Answer: Harrington / 8. The speaker said "three", then corrected it with "sorry, one-two-eight". The last number said is the correct answer; if you write down the first thing you hear and move on, you fall into the trap. "double R" = RR, "oh" = 0.

  • Do: Practise the alphabet and confusable number pairs such as 13/30, 40/14 until you recognise them fluently. For Turkish candidates this is the most common loss.
  • Do: When you hear "sorry", "actually", "I mean", "let me change that", lift your pen — a correction is coming.
  • Don't: Skip the example answer next to the gap (usually one is given); it shows you the required format.

3. Section 2 — Monolog ve Bilgilendirme

İkinci bölüm tek bir konuşmacının günlük/sosyal bir konuyu anlattığı monologdur: bir belediye tesisinin tanıtımı, bir festival programı, yeni bir spor merkezi turu, bir gönüllülük programının açıklaması. Konuşmacı dinleyici kitlesine seslenir, bu yüzden dil hâlâ sadedir ama akış tek yönlüdür — soru-cevap ritmi yoktur, bilgi kesintisiz akar.

Bu bölüm sıklıkla map/plan labelling (harita/kroki etiketleme) veya matching içerir. Konuşmacı bir mekânı sözlü olarak gezdirir; yön ifadelerini (opposite, next to, at the far end, on your left) takip etmeniz gerekir. Ayrıca multiple choice da yaygındır.

Mini transkript + soru

As you come through the main entrance, the café is immediately on your right. Walk past it and you'll reach a junction; the changing rooms are straight ahead, but the pool itself is reached by turning left and going to the very end of the corridor.

Soru: Krokide "Pool" hangi harf? (Girişten sola dön, koridorun sonu.)

Yaklaşım: Parmağını girişe koy ve konuşmacıyla birlikte hareket ettir. "Straight ahead" changing rooms içindir — havuz DEĞİL; bunu havuz sanmak klasik distractor hatasıdır.

  • Yap: Ses başlamadan haritadaki sabit noktaları (giriş, "you are here" işareti, pusula yönü) belirle; hareketi bunlara göre izle.
  • Yap: Yön kelimelerinin sözlüğünü ezberle: adjacent to, beside, in the corner, halfway along, overlooking.
  • Yapma: Bir etiketi kaçırınca donma; konuşmacı ilerler, sen de bir sonrakine geç ve boşluğa sonra dön.

3. Section 2 — Monologue and Briefing

The second section is a monologue in which a single speaker describes an everyday/social topic: introducing a municipal facility, a festival programme, a tour of a new sports centre, an explanation of a volunteering programme. The speaker addresses an audience, so the language is still simple but the flow is one-way — there is no question-answer rhythm, information flows without interruption.

This section often contains map/plan labelling or matching. The speaker guides you around a place verbally; you need to follow the direction expressions (opposite, next to, at the far end, on your left). Multiple choice is also common.

Mini transcript + question

As you come through the main entrance, the café is immediately on your right. Walk past it and you'll reach a junction; the changing rooms are straight ahead, but the pool itself is reached by turning left and going to the very end of the corridor.

Question: Which letter is the "Pool" on the plan? (From the entrance, turn left, end of the corridor.)

Approach: Put your finger on the entrance and move it with the speaker. "Straight ahead" is for the changing rooms — NOT the pool; mistaking this for the pool is the classic distractor error.

  • Do: Before the audio starts, identify the fixed points on the map (entrance, the "you are here" marker, compass direction); track the movement against them.
  • Do: Memorise a glossary of direction words: adjacent to, beside, in the corner, halfway along, overlooking.
  • Don't: Freeze when you miss a label; the speaker moves on, so move to the next one and come back to the gap later.

4. Section 3 — Akademik Tartışma

Üçüncü bölüm eğitim bağlamında, genellikle iki öğrenci ile bir öğretim görevlisi (ya da iki öğrenci) arasında bir tartışmadır: bir ödevin planlanması, bir deney raporunun gözden geçirilmesi, bir sunum için görev paylaşımı. Zorluk sıçraması burada gerçekleşir. Çünkü artık birden fazla ses vardır, konuşmacılar birbirinin sözünü keser, fikir değiştirir ve dolaylı konuşur ("I'm not so sure about that", "You've got a point, but...").

Yaygın soru tipleri: multiple choice, matching (kim hangi görüşü savunuyor / hangi öğe hangi kategoriye ait) ve flowchart/table completion. Anahtar beceri, kelimeleri değil fikir sahibini ve fikrin yönünü takip etmektir: kim katılıyor, kim itiraz ediyor, sonunda hangi karara varılıyor.

Mini transkript + soru

— I thought we'd focus the survey on first-year students.
— Hmm, we could, but honestly the response rate from them is always low. Postgraduates might give us richer data.
— Good point. Let's target them instead.

Soru (MC): Anket hangi gruba yapılacak? A) first-year students   B) postgraduates   C) all students

Cevap: B. "First-year students" ilk önerildi ama "but... low response rate" ile reddedildi; "Let's target them instead" nihai kararı postgraduates'e taşıdı. İlk duyulan seçeneği işaretlemek en sık yapılan Section 3 hatasıdır.

  • Yap: Her konuşmacıya zihinsel bir etiket (isim baş harfi) ver; matching sorularında kimin ne dediğini not al.
  • Yap: Anlaşmazlık ve nihai karar sinyallerini yakala: "Actually...", "On second thought...", "So we've agreed to...", "Let's go with..."
  • Yapma: İlk söylenen fikri cevap sanma; Section 3 fikir değiştirme üzerine kuruludur.

4. Section 3 — Academic Discussion

The third section is a discussion in an educational context, usually between two students and a tutor (or two students): planning an assignment, reviewing an experiment report, dividing tasks for a presentation. The jump in difficulty happens here. Because now there are multiple voices, the speakers interrupt each other, change their minds and speak indirectly ("I'm not so sure about that", "You've got a point, but...").

Common question types: multiple choice, matching (who holds which view / which item belongs to which category) and flowchart/table completion. The key skill is to follow not the words but the owner of the idea and the direction of the idea: who agrees, who objects, what decision is finally reached.

Mini transcript + question

— I thought we'd focus the survey on first-year students.
— Hmm, we could, but honestly the response rate from them is always low. Postgraduates might give us richer data.
— Good point. Let's target them instead.

Question (MC): Which group will the survey be given to? A) first-year students   B) postgraduates   C) all students

Answer: B. "First-year students" was suggested first but rejected with "but... low response rate"; "Let's target them instead" moved the final decision to postgraduates. Marking the first option you hear is the most common Section 3 mistake.

  • Do: Give each speaker a mental label (their initial); in matching questions, note who said what.
  • Do: Catch the signals of disagreement and final decision: "Actually...", "On second thought...", "So we've agreed to...", "Let's go with..."
  • Don't: Assume the first idea mentioned is the answer; Section 3 is built on changing one's mind.

5. Section 4 — Akademik Ders (Monolog)

Dördüncü bölüm tek bir akademisyenin verdiği, kesintisiz bir üniversite dersidir: buzul erozyonu, arıların gezinme davranışı, kentsel tarım, bir tarihsel keşfin ekonomisi. En zor bölümdür çünkü ortada okuma molası yoktur — 10 sorunun tamamını tek bir sürekli akışta cevaplarsınız; kaybederseniz yakalayacak duraklama yoktur.

Neredeyse her zaman note completion (ve bazen sentence completion) içerir; notlar dersin mantıksal yapısını yansıtır: başlık → alt başlık → ayrıntı. İyi haber: notların düzeni dersin sırasını takip eder, yani nerede olduğunuzu takip edebilirsiniz. Zorluk, akademik kelime hazinesi ve konuşmacının hızlı, işaret-ifadeli (signposting) anlatımıdır.

Mini transkript + soru

Early beekeepers assumed the hive was led by a "king". It was only in the seventeenth century that microscopy revealed the central bee was in fact a queen, and that most of the workforce were sterile females. Drones, the males, had a single function: reproduction.

Notlar: Central bee = a ________ (not a king) · Most workers = sterile ________ · Drones' role = ________

Cevaplar: queen · females · reproduction. Dikkat: soru "sterile ______" diyor; boşluğa yalnızca "females" gelir, "sterile females" yazmak tekrar olur ve kelime limitini aşabilir.

  • Yap: Ders başlarken verilen boşlukta tüm 10 notu tara; başlıklardan konunun akışını (kronoloji mi, sebep-sonuç mu, karşılaştırma mı) tahmin et.
  • Yap: İşaret ifadelerini takip et: "Firstly...", "This brings us to...", "A common misconception is...", "The key point here is...".
  • Yapma: Bir boşluğu kaçırınca panikleme ve önceki soruya dönme; ileri git, çünkü ses durmaz.

5. Section 4 — Academic Lecture (Monologue)

The fourth section is an uninterrupted university lecture given by a single academic: glacial erosion, the navigation behaviour of bees, urban farming, the economics of a historical discovery. It is the hardest section because there is no reading break in the middle — you answer all 10 questions in one continuous flow; if you lose your place there is no pause to catch up.

It almost always contains note completion (and sometimes sentence completion); the notes mirror the logical structure of the lecture: heading → sub-heading → detail. The good news: the layout of the notes follows the order of the lecture, so you can track where you are. The difficulty is the academic vocabulary and the speaker's fast, signposted delivery.

Mini transcript + question

Early beekeepers assumed the hive was led by a "king". It was only in the seventeenth century that microscopy revealed the central bee was in fact a queen, and that most of the workforce were sterile females. Drones, the males, had a single function: reproduction.

Notes: Central bee = a ________ (not a king) · Most workers = sterile ________ · Drones' role = ________

Answers: queen · females · reproduction. Careful: the question says "sterile ______"; only "females" goes in the gap, writing "sterile females" is a repetition and may exceed the word limit.

  • Do: During the gap at the start of the lecture, scan all 10 notes; from the headings, predict the flow of the topic (is it chronology, cause-effect, comparison).
  • Do: Follow the signposting expressions: "Firstly...", "This brings us to...", "A common misconception is...", "The key point here is...".
  • Don't: Panic and go back to a previous question when you miss a gap; move forward, because the audio does not stop.

6. Question Type: Completion (Form / Note / Table / Flowchart / Sentence)

Completion questions are the backbone of IELTS Listening. The shared rule: you write the word from the recording exactly (you do not paraphrase), but there is a word limit — such as "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER". If the limit is exceeded, the answer is marked wrong even if you found the right word.

7. Soru Tipi: Multiple Choice ve Matching

Multiple choice (çoktan seçmeli) genelde Section 2 ve 3'te çıkar. Üç seçenekten birini (bazen beşten ikisini) seçersiniz. Zorluğu şudur: doğru cevap kayıtta paraphrase edilir, yanlış seçenekler ise kayıtta geçen kelimelerle birebir görünür. Yani ekrandaki kelimeyi duyduğunuz için bir seçeneği işaretlemek tam da hazırlanan tuzaktır.

Multiple choice mini örnek

Question: Why did the student choose this topic?
A) a lecturer recommended it   B) it links to her future career   C) there was plenty of data

Transcript: "My tutor actually suggested a different subject, but I picked this one because it ties in with the kind of work I want to do after graduating."

Cevap: B. "Ties in with the work I want to do after graduating" = "future career" (paraphrase). A tuzaktır: "tutor suggested" duyulur ama farklı bir konu için ve reddedilir ("but I picked this one").

Matching soruları bir öğe listesini bir seçenek listesiyle eşleştirir (kişi → görüş, yer → özellik). Konuşma sırası genelde soru sırasını takip eder, ama seçenekler öyle değildir. Seçenek kutusunu (options box) önce oku ve her seçeneği bir-iki kelimeyle zihninde özetle ki duyduğunda tanıyabilesin.

  • Yap: Ses başlamadan seçenekleri oku ve paraphrase'lerini hayal et ("increased" → "went up", "rose", "grew").
  • Yap: Elemeyle çalış; yanlışladığın seçeneğin üstünü çiz, seçim daralır.
  • Yapma: Ekranda gördüğün kelimeyi kayıtta duyunca refleksle işaretleme — çoğu zaman kelime eşleşmesi tuzaktır.

7. Question Type: Multiple Choice and Matching

Multiple choice usually appears in Sections 2 and 3. You choose one of three options (sometimes two of five). Its difficulty is this: the correct answer is paraphrased in the recording, while the wrong options appear word for word as they are in the recording. So marking an option because you heard the word on screen is exactly the trap that is set.

Multiple choice mini example

Question: Why did the student choose this topic?
A) a lecturer recommended it   B) it links to her future career   C) there was plenty of data

Transcript: "My tutor actually suggested a different subject, but I picked this one because it ties in with the kind of work I want to do after graduating."

Answer: B. "Ties in with the work I want to do after graduating" = "future career" (paraphrase). A is a trap: "tutor suggested" is heard but for a different topic, and it is rejected ("but I picked this one").

Matching questions match a list of items to a list of options (person → view, place → feature). The order of speaking usually follows the order of the questions, but the options do not. Read the options box first and summarise each option in one or two words in your mind so you can recognise it when you hear it.

  • Do: Read the options before the audio starts and imagine their paraphrases ("increased" → "went up", "rose", "grew").
  • Do: Work by elimination; cross out an option you have ruled out and the choice narrows.
  • Don't: Mark a word reflexively just because you heard it on screen in the recording — a word match is usually a trap.

8. Soru Tipi: Map / Plan Labelling

  • Yap: Kalemini planın üzerinde fiziksel olarak hareket ettir; konuşmacıyla senkron kal.
  • Yapma: Kendi sağ/solunla haritanın sağ/solunu karıştırma — anlatım "you"nun bakış açısındandır, plan yukarıdan görünümdür.

8. Question Type: Map / Plan Labelling

Map/plan labelling usually appears in Section 2. A plan is given (a building, campsite, park, museum floor) and you label the places according to the speaker's description — either with letters (A–H) or by writing names in boxes. It is not vocabulary that is measured but orientation and spatial tracking skill.

Glossary of direction words

Location

opposite, next to, adjacent to, beside, between, in the corner, at the far end, overlooking

Movement

halfway along, just before, right at the end, a short way down, immediately after

  • Do: Physically move your pen over the plan; stay in sync with the speaker.
  • Don't: Confuse your own left/right with the plan's left/right — the description is from "you"'s point of view, the plan is a top-down view.

9. Soru Tipi: Sentence Completion ve Short Answer

Sentence completion'da yarım bir cümleyi kayıttaki kelimelerle tamamlarsınız; cümle hem anlamca hem dilbilgisel olarak doğru olmalıdır. Boşluğa hangi tür kelimenin (isim, fiil, sıfat) gerektiğini önceden çözerseniz, doğru anı çok daha kolay yakalarsınız.

Short answer sorularında kısa bir soruya kayıttan alınan birkaç kelimeyle cevap verirsiniz (yine kelime limitiyle). "List two reasons..." gibi sorularda iki ayrı öğe istenir; ikisini de yakalamanız gerekir.

Sentence completion mini örnek

Transcript: "The main advantage of the new design is that it significantly reduces energy consumption without adding to the cost."

Soru: The new design's main benefit is a reduction in ________. (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS)

Cevap: energy consumption. "In a reduction in ___" gramerine isim öbeği uyar; "reduces energy" yazmak (fiil) cümleyi bozardı.

9. Question Type: Sentence Completion and Short Answer

In sentence completion you complete a half sentence with words from the recording; the sentence must be correct both in meaning and grammar. If you work out in advance what kind of word (noun, verb, adjective) the gap needs, you will catch the right moment far more easily.

In short answer questions you answer a short question with a few words from the recording (again with a word limit). In questions such as "List two reasons..." two separate items are required; you must catch both.

Sentence completion mini example

Transcript: "The main advantage of the new design is that it significantly reduces energy consumption without adding to the cost."

Question: The new design's main benefit is a reduction in ________. (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS)

Answer: energy consumption. A noun phrase fits the grammar of "in a reduction in ___"; writing "reduces energy" (a verb) would break the sentence.

10. Tahmin (Prediction) Stratejisi — Dinlemeden Önce

IELTS Listening'de en güçlü silah, ses başlamadan verilen o kısa boşluktur. Şampiyon adaylar bu saniyeleri boşa harcamaz; soruları okur ve her boşluk için ne bekleyeceğini önceden belirler. Böylece dinlerken "arama" yükü azalır, sadece "doğrulama" kalır.

İpucu

Dinlemeden önce her boşluğun eş anlamlısını da tahmin et. IELTS neredeyse hiç aynı kelimeyi kullanmaz: soruda founded yazıyorsa kayıtta set up ya da established duyarsın. Beklediğin sesi önceden kurarsan yakalaman kolaylaşır.

Boşluk için üç soru sor

  • Ne türü? İsim mi, sayı mı, tarih mi, yer mi, fiil mi? "In ___ 1998" → tarih; "cost £___" → sayı; "made of ___" → malzeme (isim).
  • Hangi gramer? Boşluktan önce "a/an" varsa tekil isim; "the ___ of" varsa isim; "to ___" varsa fiil kökü.
  • Hangi anahtar kelime kulağıma çarpacak? Boşluğun etrafındaki değişmez kelimeleri (isim, özel ad, sayı) işaretle; kayıtta bunları veya paraphrase'lerini duyunca cevap yakında demektir.

Uygulamalı örnek

Soru: "The museum was founded in ________ by a local ________."

Tahmin: İlk boşluk "founded in ___" → bir yıl (sayı). İkinci boşluk "a local ___" → bir meslek/kişi (isim, tekil). Kayıtta "founded" veya eş anlamlısı "established/set up" duyunca kilitlen. Böylece "set up in 1888 by a local merchant" cümlesi geldiğinde "founded" kelimesini beklemesen bile paraphrase'i yakalarsın.

  • Yap: Anahtar kelimelerin eş anlamlılarını (synonym) da tahmin et; IELTS neredeyse hiç aynı kelimeyi kullanmaz.
  • Yap: İki soru arası boşlukta bir sonraki sayfaya "önden koş"; kayıt seni beklemez.
  • Yapma: Talimatları (word limit) okumayı atlama; her soru grubunda değişebilir.

10. Prediction Strategy — Before You Listen

In IELTS Listening the most powerful weapon is that short gap given before the audio. Champion candidates do not waste those seconds; they read the questions and decide in advance what to expect for each gap. That way, while listening, the "searching" load is reduced and only "confirming" remains.

Tip

Before listening, also predict the synonym of each gap. IELTS almost never uses the same word: if the question says founded, in the recording you will hear set up or established. If you set up the sound you are expecting in advance, catching it becomes easier.

Ask three questions for each gap

  • What type? Noun, number, date, place, verb? "In ___ 1998" → date; "cost £___" → number; "made of ___" → material (noun).
  • Which grammar? "a/an" before the gap → singular noun; "the ___ of" → noun; "to ___" → verb stem.
  • Which keyword will hit my ear? Mark the unchanging words around the gap (noun, proper name, number); when you hear these or their paraphrases in the recording, the answer is near.

Worked example

Question: "The museum was founded in ________ by a local ________."

Prediction: The first gap is "founded in ___" → a year (number). The second gap is "a local ___" → a profession/person (noun, singular). Lock in when you hear "founded" or its synonym "established/set up" in the recording. That way, when the sentence "set up in 1888 by a local merchant" comes, you catch the paraphrase even without expecting the word "founded".

  • Do: Also predict the synonyms of the keywords; IELTS almost never uses the same word.
  • Do: In the gap between two questions, "run ahead" to the next page; the recording will not wait for you.
  • Don't: Skip reading the instructions (word limit); it can change with every question group.

11. Dikkat Tuzakları — Fikir Değiştirme, Distractor, Eş Anlam

IELTS Listening puanınızı düşürmek için bilinçli tuzaklar kurar. Bunları tanımak, cevabı bilmek kadar önemlidir. Üç ana tuzak vardır.

Tuzak 1 — Fikir değiştirme (correction / retraction)

Konuşmacı bir şey söyler, sonra geri alır. Sinyal kelimeler: actually, sorry, no wait, I mean, on second thought, let me correct that, or rather. Doğru cevap sonra gelen bilgidir.

"We'll meet on Tuesday — actually, no, the room's booked then, let's make it Thursday." → Cevap: Thursday, Tuesday değil.

Tuzak 2 — Distractor sayı/isim (birden fazla aday)

Kayıt birkaç sayı ya da isim art arda söyler; yalnızca biri soruyu cevaplar. "Reddetme" ipuçlarını dinle: not, isn't, instead of, rather than, we decided against.

"The trip was originally 30 people, but numbers dropped, so 18 finally came — not the 25 we'd hoped for." → Cevap: 18 (30 ve 25 distractor).

Tuzak 3 — Eş anlam / paraphrase (kelime eşleşmesi yanılgısı)

Doğru cevap sorudaki kelimenin eş anlamlısıyla gelir; sorudaki kelimenin birebir aynısı ise çoğu zaman yanlış seçeneğe konur.

Soru: "cheap"  →  Kayıt: "it won't cost you much", "affordable", "reasonably priced", "good value". Kayıtta "cheap" kelimesini beklemek seni yanıltır.

11. Attention Traps — Changing Minds, Distractors, Synonyms

IELTS Listening sets deliberate traps to lower your score. Recognising them is as important as knowing the answer. There are three main traps.

Trap 1 — Changing the mind (correction / retraction)

The speaker says something, then takes it back. Signal words: actually, sorry, no wait, I mean, on second thought, let me correct that, or rather. The correct answer is the information that comes after.

"We'll meet on Tuesday — actually, no, the room's booked then, let's make it Thursday." → Answer: Thursday, not Tuesday.

Trap 2 — Distractor number/name (several candidates)

The recording says several numbers or names in a row; only one answers the question. Listen for "rejection" cues: not, isn't, instead of, rather than, we decided against.

"The trip was originally 30 people, but numbers dropped, so 18 finally came — not the 25 we'd hoped for." → Answer: 18 (30 and 25 are distractors).

Trap 3 — Synonym / paraphrase (the word-match fallacy)

The correct answer comes as a synonym of the question word; the exact same word as in the question is usually placed in a wrong option.

Question: "cheap"  →  Recording: "it won't cost you much", "affordable", "reasonably priced", "good value". Expecting to hear the word "cheap" in the recording will mislead you.

13. Connected Speech and Accent Differences

IELTS recordings include various accents: standard British, Australian, New Zealand, North American and sometimes a Scottish intonation. More importantly, speakers use natural, connected speech: words link together, weak forms are swallowed, "t/d" sounds drop. A candidate who expects the clear pronunciation of a coursebook gets lost in the real recording.

15. Sık Hatalar ve Zaman Yönetimi

En çok yapılan hatalar

  • Bir soruya takılıp arkasından gelen 2–3 soruyu kaçırmak (en pahalı hata).
  • Doğru kelimeyi duyup yanlış yazmak (yazım/çoğul).
  • Kelime limitini aşmak — cevap iptal olur.
  • İlk duyulan sayıyı/ismi yazıp düzeltmeyi kaçırmak.
  • Ekranda gördüğü kelimeyi kayıtta duyunca refleksle işaretlemek (distractor).
  • Talimatları (word limit, kaç cevap) okumadan başlamak.

Zaman ve dikkat yönetimi

  • Okuma boşluklarını "önden koşmak" için kullan; asla dinlenmiş bölüme geri dönme.
  • Kâğıt sınavda: dinlerken soru kitapçığına yaz, en sonda 10 dakikada temize çek.
  • Bilgisayar sınavda: doğrudan kutuya yaz; transfer yoktur, son 2 dakikada yazım kontrol et.
  • Emin olmadığın boşluğu boş bırakma — mantıklı bir tahmin yaz; yanlış cevap ceza getirmez.
  • Dikkatini bir sonraki soruya değil, şu anki boşluğa odakla; ses akıp gider.

Özet: Listening bir "İngilizce anlama" sınavı olduğu kadar bir disiplin ve strateji sınavıdır. Kolay bölümlerde sıfır fire, zor bölümlerde esnek dikkat, her boşlukta önceden tahmin ve yazarken titiz kontrol — bu dört alışkanlık band'inizi tek başına bir tam puan yukarı taşıyabilir. Şimdi bu stratejileri gerçek kayıtlarla, tek dinlemede, pratik ederek pekiştir.

15. Common Mistakes and Time Management

The most common mistakes

  • Getting stuck on one question and missing the next 2–3 (the most expensive mistake).
  • Hearing the right word and writing it wrong (spelling/plural).
  • Exceeding the word limit — the answer is invalidated.
  • Writing the first number/name heard and missing the correction.
  • Marking a word reflexively when heard because it appears on screen (distractor).
  • Starting without reading the instructions (word limit, how many answers).

Time and attention management

  • Use the reading gaps to "run ahead"; never go back to a section already listened to.
  • In the paper exam: write in the question booklet as you listen, then write up neatly in the last 10 minutes.
  • In the computer exam: type straight into the box; there is no transfer, so check spelling in the last 2 minutes.
  • Do not leave a gap you are unsure of blank — write a sensible guess; a wrong answer carries no penalty.
  • Focus your attention not on the next question but on the current gap; the audio flows on.

Summary: Listening is as much a test of discipline and strategy as of "understanding English". Zero slips in the easy sections, flexible attention in the hard sections, prediction ahead of every gap, and careful checking while writing — these four habits alone can lift your band a full point. Now reinforce these strategies by practising with real recordings, on a single listen.

So in one group it is "write what you hear", in the other "don't trust the word you hear". Internalising this distinction alone cuts many slips.

17. A Fully Worked Example — Form Completion

Now let's combine everything we have learned in one small scenario: a short Section 1 dialogue, three gaps and a step-by-step reason for why each answer is what it is. Instruction: NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER.

Mini transcript

— Good afternoon, I'd like to join the library.
— Certainly. Could I take your surname?
— Yes, it's Okonkwo. That's O-K-O-N-K-W-O.
— Thank you. And your address?
— It's 12 Maple Court — sorry, no, Maple Close, not Court.
— Got it. Which membership would you like?
— The premium one, please. I like that it lets me borrow up to eight books at a time.

Library membership formAnswer
Surname: ________ (1)Okonkwo
Street: 12 Maple ________ (2)Close
Books allowed at once: ________ (3)eight (or 8)
  • Gap 1 — Okonkwo: The name is spelled out (O-K-O-N-K-W-O). You must follow the alphabet correctly and write it exactly; a single letter error (e.g. Okonkow) makes the answer wrong — having heard it correctly does not save the point. As a proper noun, start it with a capital letter.
  • Gap 2 — Close: The classic correction trap. The speaker first says "Maple Court", then takes it back with "sorry, no, Maple Close, not Court". The last thing said is correct: Close. If you write the "Court" you heard first, you fall into the trap. Since "Maple" is printed on the form, only Close goes in the gap.
  • Gap 3 — eight: A number question. "up to eight books" — the answer is eight (or 8); both are accepted. Since the word "books" already appears in the question ("Books allowed"), you do not need to write it again; "eight books" is within the limit but unnecessary.

Lesson: All three gaps are won not by "understanding" but by correct behaviour — copy the letters exactly, wait for the correction, do not exceed the word limit. This is exactly where the ease of Section 1 lies: the content is simple, and the losses come from small slips. If you automate these three reflexes, coming out of this section with 10/10 is a realistic goal.
