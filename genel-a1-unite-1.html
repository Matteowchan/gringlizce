Ünite 1 · Nice to Meet You · Bölüm 1/6

Grammar: be (am / is / are) + Subject Pronouns

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden "be" ile Başlıyoruz?

İngilizcede öğreneceğin ilk ve en önemli yapı "be" fiilidir. Kim olduğunu, ne iş yaptığını, nereli olduğunu, nerede olduğunu ve nasıl hissettiğini söylemenin tek yolu budur. Yani kendini tanıtırken kuracağın hemen her cümle "be" ile kurulur:

  • "I am Deniz."Ben Deniz'im. (kimlik)
  • "I am a sales specialist."Satış uzmanıyım. (meslek)
  • "I am from Izmir."İzmirliyim. (memleket)
  • "I am at the office."Ofisteyim. (yer)
  • "I am happy to be here."Burada olduğum için mutluyum. (duygu)

Türkçede bu anlamı eklerle veririz: mühendisim, ofisteyim, yorgunum. İngilizcede ek yoktur; bunun yerine ayrı bir kelime olan "be" fiili kullanılır ve özneye göre am / is / are şeklinde değişir. Bu bölümde bu üç formu, olumsuz ve soru hâllerini, kısa cevapları ve en sık yapılan hataları tek tek işleyeceğiz.

1. Özne Zamirleri (Subject Pronouns)

İngilizcede her cümlenin görünür bir öznesi olmak zorundadır. Türkçede "Mühendisim." deriz, özne gizlidir; İngilizcede özneyi mutlaka söyleriz: "I am an engineer." Özne söylemeden kurulan cümle yanlıştır: "Am an engineer." ✗

PronounTürkçesiNe zaman?
IbenKendinden bahsederken. Her zaman BÜYÜK harfle yazılır: "i" ✗ → "I" ✓
yousen / sizKarşındaki kişi — tekil de çoğul da aynıdır: "you". Nazik hitap için ayrı bir "siz" yoktur.

sheo (kadın)Bir kadından bahsederken: "Selin? She is the new designer."

  • Türkçedeki tek "o", İngilizcede üçe ayrılır: he (erkek), she (kadın), it (cansız). En sık yapılan hatalardan biri kişiler için "it" kullanmaktır: "My manager? It is nice." ✗ → "She is nice." ✓
  • "they" hem insanlar hem nesneler için kullanılır — Türkçedeki "onlar" gibi.

2. Olumlu Cümle (Affirmative)

Kural tektir ve hiç değişmez: I → am, he / she / it → is, you / we / they → are. Cümle kurgusu: Özne + am/is/are + geri kalanı.

Kısa form ne zaman?
  • Konuşmada ve samimi yazışmada neredeyse her zaman kısa form kullanılır: "I'm Deniz."
  • Resmi yazıda (rapor, resmi e-posta) tam form tercih edilir: "I am the new sales specialist."
  • Kesme işaretinin yeri düşen harfin yeridir: am → I'm, is → he's.

3. Olumsuz Cümle (Negative)

Olumsuz yapmak için be'den sonra not ekleriz — Türkçedeki "değil" anlamını verir. Başka hiçbir kelimeye gerek yoktur.

Tam formKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi

İki kısa form da doğrudur
  • "She isn't here." = "She's not here." — ikisi de doğru, ikisi de yaygın.
  • Tek istisna: "I" için yalnızca "I'm not" vardır. "I amn't" diye bir form yoktur. ✗

4. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru yapmak için be fiili öznenin önüne geçer — yer değiştirirler. Türkçedeki "mi" ekinin işini bu yer değişimi yapar: You are busy. → Are you busy?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Am I late?Yes, you are.No, you aren't.
Is he the manager?Yes, he is.No, he isn't.
Is the meeting at 11?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are you from Ankara?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Are they engineers?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Önemli kural
  • Olumlu kısa cevapta kısa form KULLANILMAZ: "Yes, I'm." ✗ → "Yes, I am." ✓ — "Yes, he's." ✗ → "Yes, he is." ✓
  • Olumsuz kısa cevapta ise kısa form kullanılır: "No, he isn't." ✓
  • Soru kelimesiyle de aynı sıra korunur: "Where are you from?", "Who is your manager?", "How are you?"

5. "be" Fiilini Nerelerde Kullanırız?

Aşağıdaki altı durumun hepsinde Türkçe ek kullanır, İngilizce "be" kullanır. Bu tabloyu içselleştirdiğinde kendini tanıtmak için gereken her cümleyi kurabilirsin:

  • Sık yer kalıpları: in a meeting (toplantıda), at work (işte), at the office (ofiste), at my desk (masamda).

Syn: co-worker (iş arkadaşı) · Ant: stranger (yabancı)

Syn: boss (patron) · Ant: employee (çalışan)

Syn: worker (çalışan) · Ant: employer (işveren)

Syn: firm (firma) · Ant: individual (birey)

Syn: workplace (iş yeri) · Ant: home (ev)

Syn: occupied (meşgul/dolu) · Ant: free (müsait)

A New Day at Baytek

Selin is a new employee at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. Today is her first day, and she is a little nervous. She is at the office at nine o'clock — thirty minutes early! Her manager, Mr. Demir, is at the door. He is friendly. 'Good morning, Selin. Welcome to the team,' he says.

Selin lives in a small flat near the office. Every morning she is busy. She gets up early. Her breakfast is simple: bread, cheese, olives and tea. Her cat, Pamuk, is on the sofa. 'Goodbye, Pamuk. I am at work now,' Selin says. Then she takes the bus. The bus is full, but it is not slow. Selin looks at the city. The sky is blue and the day is warm. She is nervous, but she is also happy. 'I am ready. Today is a good day,' she says.

The office is big and modern. There are twenty people in Selin's department. Her colleagues are from different cities: Ankara, Izmir, Bursa and London. Two colleagues, Ali and Merve, are engineers. They are very busy today because there is a meeting at eleven o'clock.

The office is on the fifth floor. There are many desks, computers and green plants. The walls are white, and the windows are big. The light is good. On the desks, there are pens, papers and small cups. Selin's colleagues are kind. They smile and say hello. 'Are you the new designer?' a woman asks. 'Yes, I am,' Selin says. 'Welcome!' the woman says. Her name is Zeynep, and she is an accountant. Zeynep is nice, and Selin is not very nervous now.

There are many people at Baytek. Some are young, and some are older. They are engineers, designers and accountants. They work together every day. In the morning, they say 'Good morning.' In the evening, they say 'Goodbye.' The people are polite and helpful. When Selin has a question, a colleague is always there. 'Where is the meeting room, please?' Selin asks. 'It is next to the kitchen,' Zeynep says. Selin says, 'Thank you very much.'

Before the meeting, Mark, a friendly colleague from London, shows Selin the office. The kitchen is next to the meeting room, and the coffee is very good. 'But it is not free on Fridays!' Mark says. Selin laughs. Her new desk is near the window, so it is a nice place to work.

Mark is a good guide. He shows Selin every room. 'This is the meeting room. This is the kitchen. And here is the printer,' he says. The printer is old, but it works. There is a small quiet room for phone calls too. Near the door, there is a board with photos of the team. 'We are like a family here,' Mark says. Selin looks at the photos and smiles. The office is big, but it is friendly. 'Thank you, Mark,' she says. 'You are welcome,' he says.

At the meeting, Selin says, 'Hello, everyone. I'm Selin. I'm the new designer. I'm happy to be here.' Her colleagues smile. 'Nice to meet you, Selin. Welcome to Baytek!'

The meeting is not long. Mr. Demir talks about a new project. 'We have a new app for a big client,' he says. 'It is important. We are a good team, and we are ready.' Ali and Merve talk about the plan. Selin listens and takes notes. She has many ideas for the design. 'This project is exciting,' she thinks. After the meeting, her colleagues are happy. 'Good job, everyone,' Mr. Demir says. 'Now, it is time for lunch.'

Selin is a designer. Her job is fun. She makes pictures, colours and logos for apps and websites. A good design is clear and beautiful. 'People like simple things,' Selin thinks. On her computer, she has many programs for design. Today, she looks at the new app. The app is blue and white. 'I can make it better,' she says. Her manager likes her ideas. 'Good work, Selin,' Mr. Demir says. Selin smiles. She is a good designer, and Baytek is happy with her.

At one o'clock, Selin and three colleagues are at a small restaurant near the office because it is lunchtime. The food is good and the people are warm. They talk about work, sports and music. Selin is happy because her new job is interesting, and her colleagues are not strangers now — they are friends.

The restaurant is small and warm. There are red chairs and white tables. The waiter is friendly. 'What is your order?' he asks. Selin orders soup and chicken. The soup is hot and good. Her colleagues order rice, salad and water. They laugh and talk. 'Is your first day good?' Merve asks. 'Yes, it is very good,' Selin says. After the meal, they walk back to the office. The street is busy, and the sun is warm. Selin is happy.

Back at the office, Selin has a coffee. The kitchen is busy. Mark is there too. 'The coffee is good, right?' he says. 'Yes, it is!' Selin says. Then she sits at her desk again. Mr. Demir gives her a small task. 'Please look at these designs. They are for the new app,' he says. Selin is happy. This is her job. She looks at the colours and the pictures on the screen. Design is fun for her.

In the afternoon, the office is quiet. Everyone is busy. Some people are at their desks, and some people are in the meeting room. The phones ring, and the printer is busy too. Selin works on the new app. She is happy with her work. At four o'clock, Zeynep comes to her desk. 'Is everything okay, Selin?' she asks. 'Yes, everything is great,' Selin says. 'Thank you.' Her colleagues are kind, and the office is a good place. Selin is not a stranger now. She is part of the team.

After lunch, Selin is at her desk. Her computer is new and fast. There is a lot of work, so the afternoon is very short. At five o'clock, her first day is over. She is tired but happy, because Baytek is a friendly company.

After work, Selin is on the bus again. She is tired, but she smiles. At home, her cat Pamuk is at the door. 'Hello, Pamuk! My first day is over,' Selin says. Then she calls her mother. 'Mum, my new job is great. My colleagues are friendly, and the office is nice,' she says. Her mother is happy too. In the evening, Selin has dinner and reads a book. Then she thinks about tomorrow. 'Tomorrow is a new day at Baytek,' she says. 'I am not nervous now. I am happy.' She is ready for her second day. Selin is a designer at Baytek now, and she loves her new job.

Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (From Sentences to a Text)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. Bir yazıyı cümle yığınından ayıran şey bağlaçlardır (connectors): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A1 seviyesinde beş bağlaç öğreniyoruz — bu beşi, yazacağın her metinde işine yarayacak:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparI am new and I am excited.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI am tired but happy.
orveyaSeçenek sunarIs the meeting at 10 or 11?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirI am new, so I have many questions.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI am happy because my colleagues are friendly.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
I am Deniz. I am new. I am happy. I am a little nervous. My colleagues are friendly. I am Deniz and I am new here. I am happy but a little nervous. I am relaxed now, because my colleagues are very friendly.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirlerini bağla.
  • "so" ve "but" öncesinde genellikle virgül kullanılır: "I am new, so I have questions."

My name is Deniz Arslan and I am the new sales specialist at Baytek. Today is my first day, and I am very happy to be here.

I am from Izmir, but now I am in Istanbul. My department is the sales department. Our office is big and modern, and my colleagues are very friendly.

I am twenty-five years old. My hobbies are music and photography. At the weekend, I am usually at the sea or in the park with my friends. I am a friendly person, and I am always ready to help.

I am new, so I have many questions. Please say hello when you are near my desk. My desk is next to the meeting room. Our first team meeting is on Monday, and I am very excited.

Thank you, and nice to meet you all!

Best regards,
Deniz
