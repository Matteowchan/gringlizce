Ünite 6 · Skills at Work · Bölüm 1/6

Grammar: can / can't + Rica ve İzin

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden "can" Öğreniyoruz?

Bu ünitede iş hayatının en çok kullanılan modal fiilini öğreniyorsun: can. Tek bir küçük kelimeyle iki büyük iş yaparsın: neleri yapabildiğini söylersin (yetenek) ve bir şeyi kibarca istersin (rica / izin). Türkçedeki "-ebilmek" ekinin karşılığıdır: yapabilirim, konuşabilir, gelemem...

  • "I can use Excel."Excel kullanabilirim. (yetenek)
  • "She can speak three languages."O üç dil konuşabilir. (yetenek)
  • "I can't come to the meeting today."Bugün toplantıya gelemem. (olumsuz)
  • "Can you help me?"Bana yardım edebilir misin? (rica)
  • "Could I leave early today?"Bugün erken çıkabilir miyim? (izin)

İş görüşmesinde, e-postada, toplantıda — becerilerden bahsetmenin ve nazikçe istekte bulunmanın yolu "can"dir. Bu bölümde olumlu, olumsuz, soru formlarını, rica ve izin kalıplarını, derece zarflarını ve en sık yapılan hataları tek tek işleyeceğiz.

1. Olumlu Cümle (can + fiil)

Kural son derece basittir ve "be" fiilinden bile kolaydır: can, TÜM öznelerde aynıdır. I, you, he, she, it, we, they — hepsi "can" alır, hiçbir değişiklik olmaz. Can'den sonraki fiil her zaman yalın hâldedir (fiilin sözlükteki hâli): -s, -ing, to eklenmez.

ÖzneYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
Ican + fiilI can use Excel.Excel kullanabilirim.
Youcan + fiilYou can drive very well.Çok iyi araba kullanabiliyorsun.
He / She / Itcan + fiilShe can speak three languages.O üç dil konuşabilir.
Wecan + fiilWe can start on Monday.Pazartesi başlayabiliriz.
Theycan + fiilThey can fix the computers.Bilgisayarları tamir edebilirler.
En önemli kural: -s YOK
  • He/she/it öznelerinde bile fiile -s eklenmez: "She can speaks." ✗ → "She can speak." ✓
  • "to" da eklenmez: "I can to use Excel." ✗ → "I can use Excel." ✓
  • can hiçbir öznede değişmez: "He cans" ✗ diye bir form yoktur.

2. Olumsuz Cümle (can't / cannot)

Olumsuz için can'in sonuna not gelir: cannot. Konuşmada ve günlük yazıda neredeyse her zaman kısa form can't kullanılır. Türkçedeki "-eme/-ama" (yapamam, gelemem) anlamını verir.

Tam formKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi
I cannotI can'tI can't come to the meeting today.Bugün toplantıya gelemem.
He cannotHe can'tHe can't give presentations.O sunum yapamaz.
We cannotWe can'tWe can't finish the report today.Raporu bugün bitiremeyiz.
Dikkat
  • "cannot" tek kelime yazılır: "can not" yerine cannot tercih edilir.
  • Yardımcı fiil eklenmez: "I don't can swim." ✗ → "I can't swim." ✓
  • Olumsuzda da fiil yalın kalır: "She can't drives." ✗ → "She can't drive." ✓

3. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru yapmak için — tıpkı "be" fiilinde olduğu gibi — can öznenin önüne geçer. "Do" veya "does" KULLANILMAZ; can kendi sorusunu kendisi yapar: You can swim. → Can you swim?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Can you use this program?Yes, I can.No, I can't.
Can she speak German?Yes, she can.No, she can't.
Can they come at ten?Yes, they can.No, they can't.
Önemli kural
  • "Do you can...?" ✗ diye bir soru YOKTUR: "Do you can drive?" ✗ → "Can you drive?" ✓
  • Kısa cevapta fiil tekrar edilmez: "Yes, I can swim." yerine kısaca "Yes, I can." yeterlidir.
  • Soru kelimesiyle de aynı sıra korunur: "What can you do?", "How many languages can she speak?"

4. Rica (Requests: Can you...? / Could you...?)

Birinden bir şey yapmasını istemek için Can you...? kullanırız. Daha kibar olmak istediğimizde Could you...? deriz — "could" burada geçmiş zaman değil, naziklik ifadesidir. Cümlenin sonuna please eklemek ricayı daha da yumuşatır.

KalıpNaziklikÖrnekTürkçesi
Can you...?Normal — iş arkadaşları arasındaCan you help me?Bana yardım edebilir misin?
Could you...?Daha kibar — yönetici, müşteriCould you send the report, please?Raporu gönderebilir misiniz lütfen?

Bir ricaya nasıl cevap verilir?

  • "Sure." / "Of course."Tabii. / Elbette. (kabul)
  • "No problem."Sorun değil. (kabul)
  • "Sorry, I can't. I'm very busy now."Üzgünüm, yapamam. Şu an çok meşgulüm. (kibar ret — nedenini söyle)
Dikkat
  • Ricayı reddederken sadece "No." demek kabadır; Sorry + kısa bir neden ekle.
  • "please" başta ya da sonda olabilir: "Could you please help me?" = "Could you help me, please?"

5. İzin (Permission: Can I...? / Could I...?)

Rica, karşındakinin bir şey yapmasını istemektir; izin ise SENİN bir şey yapman için onay istemektir. Özne değişir: Can I...? / Could I...? — Could yine daha kibardır.

  • "Can I open the window?"Pencereyi açabilir miyim?
  • "Could I leave early today?"Bugün erken çıkabilir miyim? (daha kibar)
  • "Can I use your phone?"Telefonunu kullanabilir miyim?

İzin sorusuna cevaplar:

  • "Sure, go ahead."Tabii, buyur.
  • "Of course you can."Elbette yapabilirsin.
  • "Sorry, not now. Maybe later."Üzgünüm, şimdi olmaz. Belki sonra.
Rica mı izin mi?
  • Can you...? → karşındaki yapar (rica): "Can you open the window?" — pencereyi O açar.
  • Can I...? → sen yaparsın (izin): "Can I open the window?" — pencereyi SEN açarsın.

6. Derece Zarfları (well, very well, a little)

Bir şeyi yapabildiğini söylemek çoğu zaman yetmez; ne kadar iyi yapabildiğini de söylemek istersin. Bu zarflar cümlenin sonuna gelir:

ZarfAnlamıÖrnekTürkçesi
very wellçok iyiHe can present very well.O çok iyi sunum yapabilir.
welliyiShe can drive well.O iyi araba kullanabilir.
a littlebirazI can speak English a little.Biraz İngilizce konuşabilirim.
Dikkat
  • Fiili niteleyen zarf well'dir, "good" değil: "He can present good." ✗ → "He can present well." ✓
  • Zarf sona gelir: "I can a little speak English." ✗ → "I can speak English a little." ✓

7. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların "can" ile en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
She can speaks English.She can speak English.can'den sonra fiil yalın kalır; -s eklenmez.
I can to use Excel.I can use Excel.can'den sonra "to" gelmez.
Do you can drive?Can you drive?can kendi sorusunu yapar; do/does kullanılmaz.
I don't can come today.I can't come today.Olumsuz: can + not = can't. "don't can" yoktur.
He cans fix computers.He can fix computers.can hiçbir öznede değişmez; "cans" yoktur.
Can you please to help me?Can you help me, please?please'den sonra "to" gelmez; fiil yalın kalır.

8. Uygulama: Proje Konuşması (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. İki iş arkadaşı yeni bir proje için becerilerini konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Merve, we have a new project. Can you help me?Merve, yeni bir projemiz var. Bana yardım edebilir misin?
  • — Of course. What is it?Elbette. Nedir?
  • — The documents are in German. Can you translate this?Belgeler Almanca. Bunu çevirebilir misin?
  • — Yes, I can, but I can't finish it today. I'm very busy.Evet, çevirebilirim ama bugün bitiremem. Çok meşgulüm.
  • — No problem. Could you send it on Friday, please?Sorun değil. Cuma günü gönderebilir misin lütfen?
  • — Sure. And you? Can you prepare the presentation?Tabii. Peki sen? Sunumu hazırlayabilir misin?
  • — I can prepare the slides, but I can't present very well.Slaytları hazırlayabilirim ama çok iyi sunum yapamam.
  • — Don't worry. Ali can help you. He can present very well.Merak etme. Ali sana yardım edebilir. O çok iyi sunum yapabilir.
  • — Great! Oh, one more thing — could I use your laptop today?Harika! Bir şey daha — bugün senin laptopunu kullanabilir miyim?
Kendini test et
  • "Yes, I can, but I can't finish it today." cümlesindeki bağlaç hangisi ve ne işe yarıyor? (Cevap: but — zıtlık)
  • Diyalogdaki İZİN cümlesi hangisi? (Cevap: "Could I use your laptop today?")
  • "Could you send it on Friday, please?" neden "Can you...?" değil? (Bölüm 4'e bak: Could daha kibardır.)

Baytek'te beceri panosu: Mr. Demir yeni proje ekibini seçiyor.
The New Project Team

Baytek is a company in Istanbul. It is not very big, and it is not very small. About sixty people work here. The office is on the third floor of a tall building. It has many desks, two meeting rooms, and a small kitchen. The people at Baytek make apps and web pages for other companies. They start work at nine o'clock, and they finish at five. They drink a lot of coffee, and they help each other every day. It is a good place to work.

Mr. Demir is the boss. He is fifty years old, and he is a kind man. The workers like him because he is fair and calm. Every morning, he comes to the office early. He says hello to everyone, and then he goes to his office. He reads his emails and drinks his first coffee. But today is a special day, because there is big news for the company.

Baytek has a new project, and Mr. Demir is very excited. The project is for a client from England, so everyone in the project team must speak English. Mr. Demir is in his office. He has a list of names on his desk because he can only choose five people for the team.

The list is long, and there are many good workers at Baytek. But a small team is better for this project. Mr. Demir needs people who can speak English, because the client can't speak Turkish. He also needs different skills. He needs a person for design, a person for code, a person for numbers, and a person for languages. Mr. Demir takes his pen, and he starts to think. This is not an easy job.

First, he thinks about Selin. Selin can design very well. Her posters and web pages are always beautiful. She can also speak a little Spanish. 'Selin is perfect for the team,' Mr. Demir says.

Selin sits near the window. She is thirty years old, and she loves colours. On her desk, there is a big computer and many pens. She can make logos, posters, and web pages. When she works, she often listens to music. Today she is very busy, but she is happy, because she likes her job. She wants to work on the new project.

Ali and Merve are engineers. Ali can write code very fast, but he can't give presentations. He is very shy in front of people. Merve can write code, and she can also give presentations very well. So Merve is the new team leader.

Ali and Merve sit together, and they are good friends. They help each other every day. Ali is a quiet man. He doesn't like big meetings, and he can't speak in front of many people. But he is a very good engineer, and his code is always clean. Merve is different. She is friendly, and she can talk to people very easily. She can explain difficult things in simple words. That is why she is a good leader for the team.

Zeynep is from the finance department. She can use Excel very well, and numbers are never a problem for her. 'Can you check the project budget?' Mr. Demir asks. 'Yes, I can,' she says.

Zeynep works in a small room next to the kitchen. She likes tea, and there is always a cup on her desk. Money and numbers are her job, and she is very good at it. She can read a big report very fast, and she can find every mistake. 'Money is not a problem for me,' she says. 'I can help the team with the budget.'

Mark is from London. He can drive, so he can take the client from the airport. He knows the city very well because he drives to work every day.

Now Mark lives in Istanbul, but his home is in London. He speaks English, and he can speak a little Turkish too. He has a small blue car, and he drives it every day. He knows the streets, the shops, and the best cafes in the city. When there is an important guest, Mark can help. He is a friendly man, and he likes to talk about Istanbul.

Finally, Mr. Demir calls Elif. Elif can speak four languages: Turkish, English, German and French. She can translate all the documents for the client. Now the team is ready, and Mr. Demir is happy. 'We can start on Monday!' he says.

On Monday morning, the team is in the big meeting room. There is coffee and tea on the table, and everyone is ready. Mr. Demir comes in with a big smile. 'Good morning, everybody,' he says. 'Thank you for your help. This is a big project, but we are a good team. Together, we can do it.'

The client is Mr. Smith. He is from England, and he arrives at the airport at ten o'clock in the morning. Mark can go to the airport and take him to the office, because he can drive and he knows the city. At the office, Elif can speak English with Mr. Smith, and she can translate the documents. Selin can show her new design ideas on the big screen.

Everyone in the room has a job. Ali can write the code with Merve, and Merve can give the presentation to the client. Zeynep can check the money and the budget every week. 'We can do this together,' Mr. Demir says. 'When we help each other, the work is easy. One person can't do everything, but a team can.'

The team starts to work. Some people write code on their computers. Some people draw pictures and make web pages. Some people talk on the phone. The office is busy, but it is a happy place. Everyone knows the plan, and everyone knows their job. There are no big problems today.

The first week is good. The team meets every morning at nine o'clock. They talk about the project, and they make a plan for the day. Mr. Smith is happy with the team, and he likes the new design. Selin's web pages are beautiful, and Merve's presentation is clear and simple. 'This is a very good team,' Mr. Smith says. 'You can help my company.'

At the end of the day, Mr. Demir looks at his team, and he smiles. Selin, Ali, Merve, Zeynep, Mark and Elif are all different. They have different skills, and they can do different things. But together, they are strong. Mr. Demir knows one thing very well: a good team can do big things. And this team is ready for the new project.

Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (From Sentences to a Text)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. Bir yazıyı cümle yığınından ayıran şey bağlaçlardır (connectors): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A1 seviyesinde beş bağlaç öğreniyoruz — bu beşi, yazacağın her metinde işine yarayacak:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparI can use Excel and I can fix computers.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI can write code, but I can't present well.
orveyaSeçenek sunarCan you come at 10 or 11?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirI can't drive, so I come by bus.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI am happy because I can learn new skills.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
I can use Excel. I can design. I can't speak German. I want to learn German. I can use Excel and I can design. I can't speak German, but I want to learn it, because it is important for our clients.
Kural

  • "so" ve "but" öncesinde genellikle virgül kullanılır: "I can't drive, so I come by bus."

Task (Görev)

Your manager asks about your skills for a new project. Write a reply email. (Yöneticin yeni proje için becerilerini soruyor; cevap e-postası yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • things you CAN do — at least 3 "can" sentences (yapabildiklerin: en az 3 can'li cümle)
  • things you CAN'T do but want to learn — can't + learn (yapamadıkların ama öğrenmek istediklerin)
  • one polite request: Could you...? (bir rica cümlesi)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Dear ... , / Best regards,)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: skill, speak, use, fix, translate, help, teach, learn, fast, well, presentation...)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"I can ... very well." "I can't ..., but I want to learn." "Could you send me ..., please?"

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~150 words) Dear Mr. Demir,

Thank you for your email about the new project. I am very happy because it is a big chance for me.

I can do many things for the team. I can use Excel very well, and I can design simple pages. I can speak English well, so I can talk to the client. I can also fix small computer problems, and my colleagues say I can teach well.

But I can't do everything. I can't write code, and I can't speak German. I want to learn German because it is important for our clients. I can learn fast!

I have one question. Could you send me the project plan, please? Then I can prepare before the first presentation.

Thank you for this chance. I am ready to help the team.

Best regards,
Deniz
