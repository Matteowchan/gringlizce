Grammar: Countable & Uncountable Nouns

Sayabildiklerin ve sayamadıkların için doğru miktar kelimesini seç. (Choose the right quantifier for countable and uncountable nouns.)

Selam, ben Gri. Bu ünitede isimleri iki gruba ayırıyoruz: sayabildiklerin (books, friends, cafés) ve sayamadıkların (time, money, homework, information). Sayıya göre farklı kelimeler kullanırız: many books ama much time; a few friends ama a little money. Günlük hayatından örneklerle netleşecek. Başlayalım.

Dinleme (Listening)

Kısa bir video dinle ve izle. (Listen and watch a short video.)

Video çok yakında burada olacak!

Ne Demek? (Two Kinds of Nouns)

Bazı isimleri tek tek sayabiliriz (one book, two books) — bunlara countable deriz ve çoğulu vardır. Bazı isimleri sayamayız (time, money, water) — bunlara uncountable deriz; çoğulu yoktur ve hep tekil fiil alır. Fark, hangi miktar kelimesini kullanacağını belirler:

  • I have a few good friends at school.Okulda birkaç iyi arkadaşım var. (sayılabilir)
  • I don't have much time today.Bugün pek vaktim yok. (sayılamaz)
  • How many books? — How much homework?Kaç kitap? — Ne kadar ödev?
Kolay yol
  • Sayabiliyorsan (1, 2, 3) → countable: friends, books, cafés.
  • Sayamıyorsan (time, money, homework) → uncountable: çoğul yok, tekil fiil.

1. Sayılabilir / Sayılamaz (Countable / Uncountable)

İsimlerin çoğu ya sayılabilir ya sayılamazdır. Genç hayatında sık geçenler bunlar; sağ sütundakilerin çoğulu yoktur:

Sayılabilir (countable)Sayılamaz (uncountable)
a book, two bookstime (çoğulu yok)
a friend, five friendsmoney, homework
a café, three cafésinformation, advice
Kolay yol
  • Countable: sayı ile kullan → three cafés. Çoğulu var.
  • Uncountable: sayı yok, çoğul yok → some money (✗ two moneys).

2. much / many / a lot of (Ne kadar / Kaç tane / Çok)

Miktar sormak ve söylemek için: many sayılabilirle, much sayılamazla kullanılır. a lot of ise ikisiyle de kullanılır ve "çok" demektir. much genelde olumsuz ve soru cümlelerinde geçer:

KelimeKullanımÖrnek
manysayılabilirHow many books do you read?
muchsayılamazI don't have much time.
a lot ofikisi de (çok)She has a lot of homework and a lot of friends.
Kolay yol
  • Sayabiliyorsan → many: many books, many students.
  • Sayamıyorsan → much: much time, much money (özellikle olumsuz/soru).
  • "Çok" demek için ikisinde de → a lot of.

3. some / any (Biraz / Hiç)

some = biraz (olumlu cümlelerde). any = hiç (olumsuz cümlelerde ve sorularda). İkisi de hem sayılabilir hem sayılamaz isimlerle kullanılır:

KelimeNe zaman?Örnek
someolumlu (+)I need some information. I have some friends here.
anyolumsuz (−)I don't have any money.
anysoru (?)Do you have any questions?
Kolay yol
  • Olumlu cümle → some: I have some homework tonight.
  • Olumsuz ya da soru → any: There isn't any milk. Any questions?

4. a few / a little (Birkaç / Biraz)

Az bir miktarı anlatmak için: a few sayılabilirle, a little sayılamazla kullanılır. İkisi de "az ama biraz var" demektir:

KelimeKullanımÖrnek
a fewsayılabilir (az)There are a few good cafés near school.
a littlesayılamaz (az)I have a little money left.
Kolay yol
  • Sayabiliyorsan → a few: a few friends, a few books.
  • Sayamıyorsan → a little: a little time, a little information.

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Sayılabilir ve sayılamaz isimleri karıştırmak kolaydır. Bu tabloyu bir kontrol listesi gibi kullan:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I read much books.I read many books.books sayılabilir → many.
He has many money.He has much money.money sayılamaz → much / a lot of.
We have a few water.We have a little water.water sayılamaz → a little.
There are a little cafés.There are a few cafés.cafés sayılabilir → a few.
two informationssome informationinformation sayılamaz → çoğul yok.
Altın kural
  • Önce ismin sayılabilir mi sayılamaz mı olduğunu düşün: sayılabilir → many / a few, sayılamaz → much / a little. Emin değilsen a lot of ikisiyle de çalışır.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Chat)

İki genç ödev, para ve zaman üzerine konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan miktar kelimelerine dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — How much homework do you have this weekend?Bu hafta sonu ne kadar ödevin var?
  • — A lot! I have three essays, but I don't have much free time.Çok! Üç kompozisyonum var ama pek boş vaktim yok.
  • — Do you want to study at a café? There are a few good ones near here.Bir kafede çalışmak ister misin? Buraya yakın birkaç iyi kafe var.
  • — I'd love to, but I only have a little money this week.Çok isterim ama bu hafta sadece biraz param var.
  • — No problem. I have some information about a free study room at the library.Sorun değil. Kütüphanedeki ücretsiz çalışma odası hakkında biraz bilgim var.
  • — Perfect. We don't need much money for that, and there aren't many people there in the morning.Harika. Onun için çok paraya gerek yok ve sabahları orada çok kişi olmuyor.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "much books" ✗ → "many books" ✓ (sayılabilir → many).
  • "many money" ✗ → "much money" ✓ (sayılamaz → much).
  • "a few water" ✗ → "a little water" ✓ (sayılamaz → a little).

Speaking: My Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan. (Learn the words, then use them.)

İsimler ve Miktarlar (Nouns & Quantifiers)

Bazıları isim (homework, money), bazıları miktar kelimesi (much, a few). Her kelimeyi bir örnekle kullan.

homework (n)
(ödev) — sayılamaz
Dikkat: çoğulu yok (✗ homeworks).
"I have a lot of homework."
money (n)
(para) — sayılamaz
"I don't have much money."
time (n)
(zaman) — sayılamaz
"We have a little time."
information (n)
(bilgi) — sayılamaz
Dikkat: çoğulu yok (✗ informations).
"I need some information."
friend / friends (n)
friend → friends (arkadaş) — sayılabilir
"I have a few close friends."
café / cafés (n)
café → cafés (kafe) — sayılabilir
"There are a few good cafés."
much (quantity)
(ne kadar / çok) → sayılamaz
"How much homework do you have?"
many (quantity)
(kaç / çok) → sayılabilir
"How many books do you read?"
a lot of (quantity)
(çok) → ikisiyle de
Not: hem sayılabilir hem sayılamaz.
"She has a lot of homework."
a few (quantity)
(birkaç) → sayılabilir (az)
"I have a few questions."
a little (quantity)
(biraz) → sayılamaz (az)
"I have a little free time."
some / any (quantity)
some (olumlu) / any (olumsuz, soru)
"I have some money, but not any coins."

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Talk about your week: homework, money, time and friends. Write 5 sentences with much / many / a lot of / a few / a little. (Haftanı anlat: ödev, para, zaman ve arkadaşlar. much / many / a lot of / a few / a little ile 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Sayılabilir isimlerle → many / a few (many books, a few friends).
  • Sayılamaz isimlerle → much / a little (much time, a little money). En az bir a lot of kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"I have a lot of ..." "I don't have much ..." "There are a few ..." "How many ...?" "I only have a little ..."
Sample Answer 1. I have a lot of homework this week, so I don't have much free time.
2. There are a few good cafés near my school where I sometimes study.
3. I only have a little money this month because I bought some new books.
4. I have a few close friends, and we help each other with difficult subjects.
5. How many exams do you have before the holidays?

Reading: A Busy Week

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu miktar kelimelerini hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

much · many · a lot of · some · any · a few · a little

İpucu
  • Metin bir gencin yoğun haftasını anlatıyor: ödev, para, zaman, bilgi. Miktar kelimelerine dikkat et.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A Busy Week

Hi! I am Deniz and I am fifteen. This week is really busy, and I don't have much free time. I have a lot of homework — three essays and a science project. I also have a few exams next week, so I am studying every evening.

Let me tell you about my days. I go to school every morning from Monday to Friday. My school is not very big. I have many classes: maths, English, science and art. English is my favourite class. I have a little homework in art, but I have a lot of homework in maths. My bag is heavy because I carry many books every day. I like some subjects, and I don't like some other subjects. But I always try to do my best.

My family is small. I live with my mum, my dad and my little brother. My brother is only seven years old. In the morning, my mum makes breakfast for us. I am not very hungry early in the day, so I don't eat much food. My brother eats a lot of food because he is always hungry! My dad drinks some coffee and reads the news. Then I take my bag and go to the bus stop. The bus is always full of students.

In the morning I don't have much time before school. I drink a little milk and eat some bread, and then I catch the bus. There are many students on the bus, but I don't have many friends in my class this year, so my mornings feel a little quiet.

I don't have much money this month because I bought some new books. I have a little money left, so I can buy lunch at school, but I can't go to the cinema. There are a few good cafés near my school, and my friends and I sometimes study there together.

I want to save some money this year. I have a small money box in my room. Every week I put a little money in it. I don't buy many things. Sometimes I buy a snack or a drink at the café near school. The café is small but nice. There are a few tables and some big windows. My friends and I like it there. We drink some tea and talk about school. It is a good place to relax after class.

After school, I go to the library. It is quiet, and there aren't many people there in the afternoon. I can find a lot of good books and some quiet tables. I don't need much money for the library because it is free. Ela, my best friend, comes with me, and we share a few ideas about our essays.

I love the library. There are many books about many topics: history, science, animals and space. I read a few pages every day. The library has some computers too, and I use them for my projects. My friend Ela is very kind. She helps me with my English, and I help her with maths. We are a good team. We don't talk much in the library because it is a quiet place. We just study and sometimes share a few snacks.

In the evening, I do my homework at my desk. My room is small, but it is quiet. I put my phone away because I don't want many messages during study time. First, I finish the difficult subjects. Then I do the easy ones. I take a short break every hour. Sometimes my brother comes to my room and asks many questions. He is funny, but he is also a little noisy! At ten o'clock, I go to bed. I need a lot of sleep for a busy day.

My teacher gives us a lot of useful information about the exams. She says, "You don't need many hours of study each day; you need a little focus and a good plan." I think she is right. How much free time do you have this week?

I also have some hobbies. I like music, and I play the guitar a little. I am not very good yet, but I practise every week. On the weekend, I have more free time. I watch some films with my family, and I play games with my brother. I don't have much money for the cinema, so we stay at home. Home is comfortable and warm. I also help my mum in the kitchen. We make some food together, and it is always fun.

This weekend I have a little free time on Sunday. I want to read a few chapters of my book and drink some tea. I don't have many plans, and that is okay. Do you have any free time this weekend? Then, on Monday, a new busy week starts again!

Writing: A Week in My Life

Yoğun bir haftanı anlat ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Describe a busy week; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI have a lot of homework and a few exams next week.
butamaI have some money, but I don't have much free time.
becauseçünküI am tired because I have a lot of essays to write.
sobu yüzdenThere aren't many people at the library, so it is quiet.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but, because ya da so.

Task (Görev)

Describe a busy week in your life: your homework, free time, money and plans. (Yoğun bir haftanı anlat: ödevlerin, boş zamanın, paran ve planların.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one uncountable noun with much / a little (much time, a little money)
  • one countable noun with many / a few (many books, a few friends)
  • one sentence with a lot of (a lot of homework)
  • one negative or question (I don't have any ... / How much ...?)
  • one connector: and / but / because / so

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"This week I have a lot of ..." "I don't have much ..., but ..." "There are only a few ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) This week is really busy and I have a lot of homework, so I don't have much free time. I have a few exams next week, and I am studying every evening. I only have a little money this month because I bought some new books. There aren't many quiet places at home, but the library is perfect.

Oyun: Doğru mu? (Countable & Uncountable)

Her cümleye bak. Miktar kelimesi doğru mu, yanlış mı? Cümlenin altındaki Doğru (yeşil) ya da Yanlış (kırmızı) düğmesine dokun. Doğru bilirsen yeşil kilit gelir; yanlış cümlede doğrusunu gösteririm. Hatalı seçersen tekrar dene. (Is the quantifier right or wrong? Tap Doğru or Yanlış.)
