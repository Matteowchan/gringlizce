At School Ne Anlatır? (What is it?)

Okulda dersler (English, Maths, Art) ve eşyalar (book, pen, desk, board) vardır. Sevdiğimiz dersleri like ile söyleriz, sınıftaki eşyaları there is / there are ile söyleriz:

  • I like Maths.Matematiği severim.
  • There is a board.Bir tahta var. (tek)
  • There are ten desks.On sıra var. (çoğul)
Kolay yol
  • Sevdiğin dersi söyle → I like Art. (Resmi severim.)
  • Tek eşya varsa there is, çok eşya varsa there are.

1. like / likes (I like Maths)

Sevdiğin dersi söylemek için like kullanırız. Özne he / she / it ise fiile -s ekleriz: likes.

ÖznefiilÖrnek
I / You / We / TheylikeI like English.
He / She / ItlikesShe likes Art.
He / She / ItlikesHe likes Maths.
Kolay yol
  • I like, you like, we like, they like. Ama he/she/it likes (küçük bir -s)!
  • "She like Art." ✗ → "She likes Art." ✓

2. there is / there are (Bir şey var)

Sınıfta bir şey olduğunu söylemek için there is / there are kullanırız. Tek şey için there is, çok şey için there are:

SayıYapıÖrnek
tek (1)there isThere is a board.
tek (1)there isThere is a pen.
çok (2, 3...)there areThere are ten desks.
Altın kural
  • Tek eşya → There is a book. Çok eşya → There are five books.
  • Çoğulda kelimeye -s gelir: desk → desks, pen → pens.

3. a / an (a book / an eraser)

Tek bir eşya için a ya da an deriz. Kelime a, e, i, o, u sesiyle başlıyorsa an, değilse a:

a / anÖrnekTürkçesi
aa book, a pen, a deskbir kitap, bir kalem, bir sıra
aThere is a board.Bir tahta var.
anan eraser, an applebir silgi, bir elma
İpucu
  • Sesli harfle başlıyorsa an: an eraser, an orange.
  • Diğer her şeyde a: a book, a pen, a teacher.

4. Sorular ve Kısa Cevaplar (Do you...? Is there...?)

Ders sevip sevmediğini sormak için Do you like...?, sınıfta bir şey var mı diye sormak için Is there...? deriz:

SoruEvetHayır
Do you like Maths?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she like Art?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Is there a board?Yes, there is.No, there isn't.
Kolay yol
  • Ders sorusunda Do / Does başa gelir: Do you like English?
  • Eşya sorusunda Is there başa gelir: Is there a pen?

5. is / are Doğru Seç (tek mi çok mu?)

Önce say: tek eşya mı, çok eşya mı? Tek ise there is, çok ise there are. Çoğulda kelimeye -s gelir:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓
There are a board.There is a board.
There is ten desks.There are ten desks.
She like Art.She likes Art.
Altın kural
  • Tek → there is a. Çok → there are + kelime-s.

6. At School (Mini Diyalog)

İki çocuk okulu konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Do you like school?Okulu sever misin?
  • — Yes, I do! I like Maths and Art.Evet, severim! Matematik ve resmi severim.
  • — Is there a board in your class?Sınıfında tahta var mı?
  • — Yes, there is a big board. And there are twenty desks.Evet, büyük bir tahta var. Ve yirmi sıra var.
  • — Does your friend like Science?Arkadaşın feni sever mi?
  • — Yes, he does! He likes Science and English.Evet, sever! Fen ve İngilizceyi sever.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "She like Art." ✗ → "She likes Art." ✓ (he/she/it → -s).
  • "There are a board." ✗ → "There is a board." ✓ (tek eşya).
  • "There is ten desks." ✗ → "There are ten desks." ✓ (çok eşya).

Let's Play! (Alıştırma)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Reading: A Day at School

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

English · Maths · Art · Science · book · pen · pencil · desk · board · teacher

İpucu
  • Metin okulu anlatıyor — o yüzden I like... ve There is / There are... çok geçer.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruların cevaplarını metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A Day at School

Hi! I am Mia. I am seven years old. I go to a nice school in my town. My school is big and I like it a lot.

I walk to school with my mum every morning. My school has a big playground and a blue gate. There are many classrooms and a lot of children. I say hello to my friends at the door. I am seven years old and I am a happy girl. School is a nice place for me.

This is my classroom. There is a big board on the wall. There are twenty desks and twenty chairs. There is a map next to the door. On my desk, there is a book, a pen and a red pencil. My bag is under the chair.

I like my classroom very much. The big board is white and clean, and my teacher writes words on it. My desk is at the front, and my chair is small. I keep my book, my pen and my red pencil on the desk. My school bag is under my chair. Everything has its place, and I am happy here.

My teacher is Miss Lily. She is very kind. Every morning she says, "Good morning, children!" We say, "Good morning, Miss Lily!"

Miss Lily is my favourite teacher. She has a kind smile and she helps us every day. She reads us funny stories and she sings songs with us. When we are good, she says, "Well done, children!" We all love Miss Lily, and she makes our class fun and happy.

I like many subjects. I like Maths because numbers are fun. I like Art too. In Art, we draw and we paint. My best friend Sam likes Science. He likes animals and plants.

In Maths, we count and we play with numbers, and numbers are fun for me! In Art, we draw pictures and we paint with many colours. My best friend Sam sits next to me. Sam loves Science because he likes animals and plants. We help each other and we learn new things together.

On Monday, we have English and Music. On Friday, we have Art. I do not like tests, but I like my friends. At lunch, we eat in the big hall. There are long tables and many children. We talk and we laugh. I am happy at school. There are good books, kind teachers and fun games. Do you like your school too?

We have different lessons on different days. On Monday, we have English and Music. On Friday, we have Art, and that is a good day! I do not like tests, but I like my friends a lot. At lunch time, we walk to the big hall. We sit at the long tables and we eat together. We talk, we laugh, and then we play games. I love my school!

Writing: My School and My Class

Okulunu ve sınıfını anlat, gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about your school and class.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI like Maths and I like Art.
butamaI like English but I don't like tests.
becauseçünküI like Maths because numbers are fun.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your school and your class. (Okulunu ve sınıfını anlat.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one subject you like (sevdiğin bir ders — I like ...)
  • one thing in your class with there is (bir eşya — There is a ...)
  • a sentence with there are (çoğul eşya — There are ...)
  • one sentence about your teacher (öğretmenin — My teacher is ...)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Hi! My school is ..." "I like ..." "There is a ..." "There are ..."

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~45 words) Hi! My school is big and nice. I like Maths and Art because they are fun. In my class, there is a big board. There are twenty desks and many books. My teacher is Miss Lily and she is very kind. I like my school a lot!
