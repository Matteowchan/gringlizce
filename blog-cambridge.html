Cambridge English: B2 First and B1 Preliminary

Cambridge English exams certify your English level step by step, and the certificates never expire. B1 Preliminary (PET) proves an intermediate level, while B2 First (FCE) proves an upper-intermediate level; both are recognised by universities and employers worldwide. The guides below cover the format, scoring and preparation strategy for each exam, with links to free study modules and practice pages at the end.
