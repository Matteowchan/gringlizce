Grammar: Gerund & Infinitive

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

İki Fiil Yan Yana Gelince (Verb + Verb)

İngilizcede çoğu zaman bir cümlede tek bir fiil vardır. But very often we want to put two verbs together: "I like ...", "I want ..." ne? Bir eylem! İşte o zaman ikinci fiil değişir. Türkçede "yüzmeyi severim" ya da "gitmek istiyorum" deriz; İngilizcede de ikinci fiil ya -ing alır (gerund) ya da başına to gelir (infinitive).

  • I enjoy swimming.Yüzmeyi severim / yüzmekten keyif alırım. (enjoy + V-ing)
  • I want to swim.Yüzmek istiyorum. (want + to + V)

Bu ünitede iki soruya cevap vereceğiz: (1) İkinci fiil ne zaman -ing olur? (2) Ne zaman to alır? İyi haber: bu, hangi ilk fiili kullandığına bağlıdır. So you learn a few verbs with -ing and a few verbs with to, and then it becomes easy.

1. Gerund Nedir? (Verb + -ing)

Bir fiile -ing eklediğimizde ona gerund deriz. A gerund is a verb that works like a noun (isim gibi davranan fiil). "swim" bir eylemdir; "swimming" ise "yüzme / yüzmek" gibi bir isimdir. Bazı fiillerden sonra ikinci fiil her zaman gerund olur.

Yazım — küçük -ing kuralları
  • -e ile bitenlerde e düşer: make → making, write → writing.
  • Kısa ünlü + tek sessizde sessiz çiftlenir: swim → swimming, run → running, shop → shopping.
  • Çoğu fiilde sadece -ing eklenir: read → reading, play → playing.

2. -ing Alan Fiiller (like / enjoy / hate / finish / mind)

Aşağıdaki fiillerden sonra ikinci fiil -ing alır. Bunlar genelde bir eylemden hoşlanma / hoşlanmama ya da bir eylemi bitirme anlamı taşır. Listeyi ezberle — cümlelerin çoğu bu fiillerle döner:

FiilAnlamıÖrnek (+ V-ing)Türkçesi
enjoykeyif almakI enjoy cooking.Yemek yapmaktan keyif alırım.
likesevmekShe likes reading.O okumayı sever.
lovebayılmakWe love travelling.Seyahat etmeye bayılırız.
hatenefret etmekHe hates waiting.Beklemekten nefret eder.
finishbitirmekI finished writing my essay.Kompozisyonu yazmayı bitirdim.
mindsakınca görmekI don't mind helping at home.Evde yardım etmekte sakınca görmem.
stopdurdurmak / bırakmakPlease stop talking.Lütfen konuşmayı bırak.
avoidkaçınmakShe avoids eating late.Geç yemek yemekten kaçınır.
practisepratik yapmakWe practise speaking English.İngilizce konuşma pratiği yaparız.
Altın kural 1
  • enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, practise, stop her zaman -ing ister: "I enjoy swimming." ✓ — "I enjoy to swim." ✗

3. to + Fiil Alan Fiiller (want / need / decide / hope / would like)

Aşağıdaki fiillerden sonra ise ikinci fiil başına to alır (infinitive). Bunlar genelde bir istek, plan, karar ya da ihtiyaç anlatır. Yani gelecekle, niyetle ilgilidir:

FiilAnlamıÖrnek (+ to + V)Türkçesi
wantistemekI want to learn Spanish.İspanyolca öğrenmek istiyorum.
needihtiyaç duymakShe needs to rest.Dinlenmeye ihtiyacı var.
decidekarar vermekWe decided to move.Taşınmaya karar verdik.
hopeummakHe hopes to pass the exam.Sınavı geçmeyi umuyor.
would likeistemek (kibar)I would like to help.Yardım etmek isterim.
planplanlamakThey plan to travel.Seyahat etmeyi planlıyorlar.
learnöğrenmekI learned to swim.Yüzmeyi öğrendim.
trydenemek / çalışmakTry to relax.Rahatlamaya çalış.
Altın kural 2
  • want, need, decide, hope, plan, would like her zaman to + yalın fiil ister: "I want to go." ✓ — "I want going." ✗
  • would like = kibar bir "want": "Would you like to sit down?" (Oturmak ister misiniz?)

4. like doing mi, like to do mu? (A Special Case)

Bir sürpriz: like, love, hate fiilleri iki şekilde de kullanılabilir — hem -ing hem to. Anlam neredeyse aynıdır, bu yüzden A2 seviyesinde ikisi de doğrudur:

  • I like cooking. = I like to cook.Yemek yapmayı severim. (iki form da doğru)
  • She hates getting up early. = She hates to get up early.Erken kalkmaktan nefret eder.

Küçük bir nüans: like + -ing genelde "o eylemden zevk almak" (hobi) anlamındadır; like + to ise bazen "iyi/doğru olduğu için tercih etmek" anlamı taşır. A2'de bunu bilmen yeterli:

Altın kural 3
  • like / love / hate ile hem -ing hem to doğrudur: "I love reading" = "I love to read".
  • Ama enjoy yalnızca -ing ister — would like yalnızca to ister. Bunları karıştırma.

5. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların gerund/infinitive konusunda en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I enjoy to read.I enjoy reading.enjoy her zaman -ing ister.
She wants going home.She wants to go home.want her zaman to + fiil ister.
We finished to eat.We finished eating.finish her zaman -ing ister.
I need buying milk.I need to buy milk.need her zaman to + fiil ister.
He decided going.He decided to go.decide her zaman to + fiil ister.
I want to going out.I want to go out.to'dan sonra yalın fiil (going değil).
Would you like coming?Would you like to come?would like her zaman to ister.
Altın kural 4
  • to'dan sonra fiil her zaman yalındır: "to go" ✓ — "to going" ✗, "to went" ✗.

6. Uygulama: Hafta Sonu Planı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about their free time and plans. Koyu yazılan gerund (V-ing) ve infinitive (to + V) yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — What do you like doing at the weekend?Hafta sonu ne yapmayı seversin?
  • — I love hiking! I really enjoy being outside. What about you?Doğa yürüyüşüne bayılırım! Dışarıda olmaktan gerçekten keyif alırım. Ya sen?
  • — I don't mind walking, but I prefer staying at home. I want to finish a good book.Yürümekte sakınca görmem ama evde kalmayı tercih ederim. İyi bir kitabı bitirmek istiyorum.
  • — Nice! I also need to study a little. I hope to pass my English exam.Güzel! Biraz da çalışmam gerek. İngilizce sınavımı geçmeyi umuyorum.
  • — Good luck! Would you like to meet at the park on Sunday?Bol şans! Pazar parkta buluşmak ister misin?
  • — Yes! I'd love to. Let's decide to meet at eleven.Evet! Çok isterim. Saat on birde buluşmaya karar verelim.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane enjoy / love / like + V-ing kalıbı var? (Cevap: 3 — like doing, love hiking, enjoy being)
  • "I want to finish a good book" cümlesinde neden to var? (Cevap: want her zaman to ister.)
  • "I hope to pass" yerine "I hope passing" desek olur muydu? (Cevap: Hayır — hope her zaman to ister.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then talk about what you like and want to do. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra neleri sevdiğini ve yapmak istediğini anlat.)

enjoy (v)
to get pleasure from something (keyif almak)
Syn: love (bayılmak) · Ant: hate (nefret etmek)
"I enjoy listening to music."
hate (v)
to dislike something very much (nefret etmek)
Syn: dislike (hoşlanmamak) · Ant: love (bayılmak)
"She hates getting up early."
finish (v)
to complete something (bitirmek)
Syn: complete (tamamlamak) · Ant: start (başlamak)
"I finished doing my homework."
mind (v)
to feel that something is a problem (sakınca görmek)
Syn: object (itiraz etmek) · Ant: welcome (hoş karşılamak)
"I don't mind waiting for you."
prefer (v)
to like one thing more than another (tercih etmek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: reject (reddetmek)
"I prefer walking to taking the bus."
want (v)
to wish to have or do something (istemek)
Syn: wish (dilemek) · Ant: refuse (reddetmek)
"I want to learn the guitar."
need (v)
to have to have something (ihtiyaç duymak)
Syn: require (gerektirmek) · Ant: waste (israf etmek)
"I need to study for my exam."
decide (v)
to choose after thinking (karar vermek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: hesitate (tereddüt etmek)
"We decided to go by train."
hope (v)
to want something good to happen (ummak)
Syn: wish (dilemek) · Ant: doubt (şüphe etmek)
"I hope to see you soon."
avoid (v)
to try not to do something (kaçınmak)
Syn: escape (kaçmak) · Ant: face (yüzleşmek)
"He avoids eating too much junk food."
practise (v)
to do something often to improve (pratik yapmak)
Syn: train (çalışmak) · Ant: neglect (ihmal etmek)
"I practise speaking every day."
hobby (n)
a free-time activity you enjoy (hobi)
Syn: pastime (uğraş) · Ant: homework (ödev)
"Drawing is my favourite hobby."
free time (n)
time when you are not studying (boş zaman)
Syn: spare time (serbest zaman) · Ant: school time (ders zamanı)
"In my free time, I love painting."
plan (v)
to think about the future and organize it (planlamak)
Syn: arrange (ayarlamak) · Ant: improvise (doğaçlama yapmak)
"We plan to start a band this summer."
Hobiler ve planlar: gerund (V-ing) ve infinitive (to + V) yapılarını kullan.

Production Task (Üretim Görevi)

Write 8 sentences about your free time, hobbies and plans. Use at least 8 of the words above. (Boş zamanın, hobilerin ve planların hakkında 8 cümle yaz. Yukarıdaki kelimelerden en az 8'ini kullan.)

Gramer şartı — bu ünitenin konusunu uygula
  • En az 2 gerund cümlesi yaz (enjoy / like / hate / finish + V-ing).
  • En az 2 infinitive cümlesi yaz (want / need / decide / hope + to + V).
  • En az bir cümlede olumsuz kullan (I don't like / I don't want to...).

Sentence starters (Cümle başlatıcıları — istersen bunlarla başla):

"I enjoy ..." "In my free time I like ..." "I want to ..." "This year I hope to ..." "I don't mind ..."

Reading: Big Plans at School

Read the text, then answer the questions. (Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • What do you enjoy doing in your free time? (Boş zamanında ne yapmaktan keyif alırsın?)
  • What is one thing you want to learn this year? (Bu yıl öğrenmek istediğin bir şey ne?)
  • Look at the title. Is this text about the past or the future? Guess! (Başlığa bak. Bu metin geçmişle mi gelecekle mi ilgili? Tahmin et!)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. -ing (gerund) ve to + fiil (infinitive) yapılarına dikkat et.

Words to spot (Metinde geçen, Speaking bölümünden tanıdığın kelimeler): enjoy · hate · finish · want · need · decide · hope · avoid · practise · prefer

Okulda yeni yıl: herkes bir hedef seçiyor.
Big Plans at School

At Newton College, every new year starts with plans and goals. This January, the class decided to try something different. Everybody wrote down one thing they wanted to learn and one habit they hoped to change.

Newton College is not a big school, but the students are friendly. Most of them are thirteen or fourteen years old, and they come from the same small town. Their classroom is on the second floor, next to the library. On the wall, there is a big colourful poster. The students call it "the goal wall". Every January, they write their plans on small cards and stick them on this wall. When they finish a goal, they draw a happy face next to it. The teacher likes this idea because it helps everyone remember their dreams.

On the first Monday of January, the class was noisy and excited. Some students wanted to talk about the winter holiday, and others wanted to start their new plans at once. Mr. Demir smiled and said, "Let's begin. This year is a new chance for all of us." Then he asked everybody to think quietly for five minutes. He said good goals are not about being perfect; they are about trying a little every day.

Selin enjoys drawing, so she wants to learn a new art app this year. She doesn't like sitting at her desk all day, so she also decided to take a short walk after every lesson. "I need to move more," she said, "and I love spending time outside."

Selin's best friend is Deniz. Deniz loves music and hopes to learn the guitar this year. The two girls decided to help each other. Selin will draw the covers for Deniz's songs, and Deniz will teach Selin some easy chords. On weekends, they often meet at the park near Selin's house. They enjoy walking, talking, and taking photos of the trees and the sky. Selin says fresh air gives her new ideas for her drawings, and she doesn't mind walking in the cold.

Their teacher, Mr. Demir, hates wasting time in long lessons. He hopes to finish every lesson on time, because students stop listening after that. He also wants to start reading one book with the class each month.

When Mr. Demir talked about reading one book each month, some students were worried. "Reading is boring," said one boy at the back. But the teacher had a plan. He chose short, exciting stories about adventure, sport, and friendship. He also promised not to give too much homework. After two weeks, even the boy at the back began to enjoy reading. He finished his first book quickly and asked for another one. "I never thought I could like books," he said with a big smile.

Ali and Merve prefer studying together. They enjoy solving problems as a team, but they need to improve their English. They decided to practise speaking every Friday. At first they didn't like recording their voices, but now they don't mind it.

Ali and Merve are not only good students; they also love sport. Ali plays for the school football team, and Merve is very good at basketball. They believe that sport and study go together. "When we play, we forget our stress," Merve said. "Then we can study better." On Fridays, after they practise their English, they often stay to watch the football training. Many other students come too, and the whole class feels like one big family.

The class also planned some fun activities for the year. In February, they want to make a small English newspaper. In spring, they hope to visit a science museum in the city. Everybody had a different job. Selin will draw the pictures, Deniz will choose the music for the school video, and Ali and Merve will write about the sports news. Mark, of course, wants to design a simple computer game for the school website.

At home, the students talked about their goals too. Selin's mother was happy to hear about the walks. "Moving every day is a great habit," she said. Ali's older brother offered to help him with difficult English words in the evenings. Merve's family started reading together after dinner, because Merve told them about Mr. Demir's book club. Little by little, the small goals from school began to change life at home as well. Even the parents wanted to try something new this year.

Not every day was easy, of course. Sometimes Selin was too tired to walk, and sometimes Ali and Merve forgot to record their English. On those days, they felt a little sad. But their classmates helped them. "Don't give up," they said. "One bad day is not the end." Slowly, the students learned an important lesson: a goal is not a race, and mistakes are just part of learning.

Mark, the new student, wants to become a game designer one day. He isn't afraid of trying new things, and he never avoids asking questions. "Making mistakes is normal," he said. "I just keep learning." By the end of January, nobody had finished their goals, but everybody had started.

Writing: Things You Like and Want to Do

Write and submit your paragraph; your teacher will review it. (Paragrafını yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

Bağlaçlar Yazıyı Cümleden Ayırır (Connectors)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. What turns a list of sentences into a real text is connectors (bağlaçlar): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. Bu ünitede özellikle hobilerini ve planlarını bağlarken şu dört bağlacı kullan:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparI enjoy reading and I love painting.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI like cooking, but I hate washing up.
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirI want to get fit, so I decided to start running.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI hope to learn the guitar because I love music.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
I enjoy running. I want to get fit. I hate swimming. I like cycling. I enjoy running because I want to get fit. I hate swimming, but I like cycling, so I decided to buy a bike.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirlerini bağla.
  • Fiillere dikkat: enjoy / like / hate + V-ing, want / hope / decide + to + V.

Paragraf Yapısı (Paragraph Structure)

Bir "hobiler ve planlar" paragrafı üç bölümden oluşur. This skeleton is the same every time — içini değiştirirsin, yapıyı değiştirmezsin:

BölümNe yazılır?Örnek
1. Giriş
(Opening)		Genel his: boş zamanını nasıl geçirirsinIn my free time, I enjoy doing many things.
2. Gövde
(Body)		Sevdiklerin (V-ing) + sevmediklerin + planların (to + V)I love reading, but I hate cleaning. This year I want to learn...
3. Kapanış
(Closing)		Bir hedef / son bir yorumI hope to have more free time next year.
