Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-9 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-9'da öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: be (am/is/are), özne zamirleri ve a/an; have/has, iyelik sıfatları ve çoğullar; Present Simple (tüm özneler, -s/-es/-ies); sıklık zarfları ve meslekler; there is/are, yer edatları ve some/any; can/can't ile yetenek, rica ve izin; Present Continuous (am/is/are + -ing); sayılabilir/sayılamaz isimler, some/any ve Would you like...?; nesne/iyelik zamirleri, emir kipi ve bağlaçlar. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

A1 Midterm Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · A Busy Week at Baytek

Baytek is a software company in Istanbul, and its employees have very busy weeks. The office opens at eight o'clock, but most people arrive at nine.

Selin is a designer. She usually gets up at seven, has a quick breakfast and takes the metro to work. She never drives to the office because she doesn't have a car. On Mondays she always has a meeting with her team at ten o'clock, so she checks her emails before it, at half past nine.

Mark is an engineer from London. He often starts early, at eight o'clock, because he likes the quiet office in the morning. He drinks two cups of coffee before lunch, but he never drinks tea. At one o'clock he usually has lunch at a small restaurant near the office with Ali and Merve. They are engineers too, and they sometimes talk about work at lunch, but they usually talk about football.

Mrs. Aydın is the manager. She has meetings every day, so she is always busy. She doesn't have time for lunch on Thursdays because she visits the Ankara office that day. She sometimes finishes work at seven o'clock in the evening.

On Fridays, the team has a short meeting at four o'clock. The employees talk about the week and plan the next one. After the meeting, the office is quiet: everybody goes home early because the weekend starts. Selin often meets her friends on Friday evenings, but Mark usually stays at home and watches films.

Text 2 · The New Baytek Office

Baytek has a new office in Istanbul, and the employees love it. The building has five floors, and Baytek is on the third floor.

Next to the entrance, there is a small reception desk. Behind the desk, there is a friendly assistant, Zeynep. She has answers to every question! Opposite the reception, there are two grey sofas for guests, and between the sofas there is a low table with some magazines on it.

The main room is big and full of light because there are ten large windows. There are twenty-five desks in this room. Selin's desk is next to a window, and Mark's desk is behind hers. Under every desk, there is a small box for personal things. There aren't any old computers in the new office — every computer is new and fast.

The meeting room is opposite the kitchen. In the meeting room, there is a long table with twelve chairs, and there is a big screen on the wall. In front of the screen, there is a small camera for online meetings.

The kitchen is everybody's favourite place. There is a coffee machine on the counter, and there is always some fresh coffee in it. There is some tea too, but there isn't any sugar — Mrs. Aydın says sugar isn't healthy! Next to the fridge, there are some plants. Ali waters them every morning.

There is only one problem: there aren't any meeting rooms on the other floors, so the meeting room is often full.

A1 Midterm Sınavı · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: My New Office (60-80 words)

Your company has a new office. Write a short email to a colleague in another city. Describe the new office. (Şirketinin yeni bir ofisi var. Başka şehirdeki bir meslektaşına kısa bir e-posta yaz; yeni ofisi anlat.)

  • Use there is / there are. (there is/are kullan)
  • Use at least 2 place prepositions: in, on, under, next to, between, behind, in front of, opposite. (en az 2 yer edatı)
  • Use some / any. (some/any kullan)
  • 60-80 words. (60-80 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Deniz," / "Dear Deniz," + kısa bir giriş cümlesi.
  • Gövde: ofisi anlat — there is/are + edatlar + some/any.
  • Kapanış: "See you soon," / "Best regards," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My Working Week (120-150 words)

Write a paragraph about your typical working (or school) week: who you are, your job, your daily routines, and your week. (Tipik iş/okul haftanı anlatan bir paragraf yaz: kim olduğun, işin, günlük rutinlerin ve haftan.)

  • Use be (am/is/are) AND have/has. (be ve have birlikte)
  • Use Present Simple for routines — dikkat: he/she için -s/-es. (rutinler için Present Simple)
  • Use at least 3 frequency adverbs: always, usually, often, sometimes, never. (en az 3 sıklık zarfı)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 120-150 words. (120-150 kelime)
