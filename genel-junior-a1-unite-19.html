Grammar: I am wearing...

Ne giydiğini anlat: I am wearing a red hat. She is wearing a blue dress. (Say what you are wearing.)

Merhaba! Ben Gri. Bugün kıyafetleri ve renkleri öğreneceğiz! "Şu anda ne giyiyorsun?" demek için am / is / are + wearing kullanırız. "I am wearing a red hat." Kız kardeşin? "She is wearing a blue dress." Soru da kolay: "What are you wearing?" Hazır mısın?

Ne Giyiyorsun? (am / is / are + wearing)

Şu anda giydiğimiz kıyafeti anlatmak için am / is / are + wearing kullanırız. wear "giymek" demektir. Sonra bir renk ve bir kıyafet ekleriz:

  • I am wearing a red hat.Kırmızı bir şapka giyiyorum. (I → am)
  • She is wearing a blue dress.Mavi bir elbise giyiyor. (She → is)
  • They are wearing green socks.Yeşil çorap giyiyorlar. (They → are)
Kolay yol
  • I → am. He / She / It → is. You / We / They → are.
  • Sonra hep wearing gelir: I am wearing, she is wearing.
  • Renk önce, kıyafet sonra: a red hat, a blue dress.

1. am / is / are (Doğru Yardımcıyı Seç)

Önce özneye bakıp doğru kelimeyi seçeriz: am, is ya da are. Sonra hep wearing gelir:

ÖzneKelimeÖrnek
IamI am wearing a hat.
He / She / ItisShe is wearing a dress.
You / We / TheyareThey are wearing shoes.
Kolay yol
  • I → am. he, she, it → is. you, we, they → are.
  • Kelimeden sonra her zaman wearing yaz.

2. Renkler (Colours)

Kıyafetleri anlatmak için renkleri öğrenelim. Renk, kıyafetin önüne gelir: a red hat, blue socks:

RenkTürkçeÖrnek
redkırmızıa red hat
bluemavia blue T-shirt
greenyeşilgreen socks
yellowsarıa yellow coat
Kolay yol
  • Renk önce, kıyafet sonra: a green hat ✓ (hat green ✗).
  • Daha çok renk: black (siyah), white (beyaz), pink (pembe).

3. Özne + am / is / are (Özet Tablo)

Hangi öznede hangi kelime? İşte kolay tablo. Sadece özneye bak:

amisare
I am ...He / She / It is ...You / We / They are ...
Kolay yol
  • I → am. he, she, it → is. you, we, they → are.
  • "They is wearing hats" ✗ → "They are wearing hats" ✓.

4. Olumsuz (not wearing)

"Giymiyorum / giymiyor" demek için kelimeden sonra not ekleriz. is not = isn't, are not = aren't:

OlumluOlumsuz
I am wearing a coat.I am not wearing a coat.
She is wearing a hat.She isn't wearing a hat.
They are wearing shoes.They aren't wearing shoes.
Kolay yol
  • am + not → am not. is + not → isn't. are + not → aren't.
  • "She aren't wearing a hat" ✗ → "She isn't wearing a hat" ✓.

5. What are you wearing? (Soru + Kısa Cevap)

"Ne giyiyorsun?" diye sormak için What are you wearing? deriz. "Mı/mi" sorusu için Are / Is başa gelir:

SoruCevap
What are you wearing?I am wearing a red T-shirt.
Are you wearing a hat?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is she wearing a dress?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Altın kural
  • I → am; he, she, it → is; you, we, they → are. Sonra hep wearing. Soruda Are / Is en başa gelir. Renk önce, kıyafet sonra.

6. In the Photo (Mini Diyalog)

İki arkadaş fotoğrafa bakıyor ve kim ne giyiyor konuşuyor. Koyu kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What are you wearing today? — I am wearing a red hat and a blue T-shirt.— Bugün ne giyiyorsun? — Kırmızı bir şapka ve mavi bir tişört giyiyorum.
  • — Is your sister wearing a dress? — Yes, she is. She is wearing a green dress.— Kız kardeşin elbise mi giyiyor? — Evet. Yeşil bir elbise giyiyor.
  • — Are they wearing coats? — No, they aren't. It is sunny today.— Ceket mi giyiyorlar? — Hayır. Bugün hava güneşli.
  • — My shoes are white and my socks are yellow!— Ayakkabılarım beyaz, çoraplarım sarı!
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "They is wearing hats" ✗ → "They are wearing hats" ✓ (çoğulda are).
  • "I is wearing a coat" ✗ → "I am wearing a coat" ✓ (I → am).
  • "a hat red" ✗ → "a red hat" ✓ (renk önce gelir).

Our Photo Day

Hello! I am Emma. Look at our photo! Today is a special day. We are at a big party, and everyone is wearing nice clothes.

It is my cousin's birthday party. The party is at a big hall with balloons and flowers everywhere. Everyone is smiling for the photo. The photographer says, "Say cheese!" We all stand close together and wait for the big photo. This photo is very special to me, and I look at it every day. It makes me very happy.

I am wearing a red dress and white shoes. My hat is pink. I love pink! My best friend Mia is next to me. She is wearing a blue T-shirt and blue socks. Her shoes are yellow.

Mia is my best friend, and she is always next to me. We are very happy in the photo. I love my red dress, and Mia loves her blue T-shirt. We hold hands and we laugh at the camera. We smile brightly for everyone. My pink hat is my favourite thing today. Mia says pink is a lovely, bright colour!

My brother Sam is wearing a green coat. He is not wearing a hat today. His shoes are brown. He is happy because his coat is new.

My brother Sam is very proud of his new green coat. He stands tall and he smiles. Sam does not like hats, so he is not wearing one today. My grandma is in the photo too. She is happy and kind. She sits on a chair in the middle of us. We all love her very much.

Look at the little baby! The baby is wearing a small white hat and tiny socks. The socks are orange. So cute! My mum is wearing a long black dress. My dad is wearing a brown coat and a blue shirt. They are not wearing hats.

My parents are not wearing hats today. The baby laughs at the camera. Everyone looks so nice and smart in our photo!

Our teacher, Miss Kate, comes to the party too. She is very kind to all of us, and she smiles in the photo. Our dog Rex sits in front of everybody. He is such a funny dog! Everybody laughs at Rex and his hat. This is a wonderful day that we will always remember.

Our teacher, Miss Kate, is wearing a purple dress. She is smiling. Our dog Rex is here too. He is wearing a funny red hat! What are you wearing today? Are you wearing a hat? It is a happy day, and we are all wearing our best clothes!

Writing: My Clothes Today

Bugün ne giydiğini anlat! (Write about your clothes today.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI am wearing a hat and a red T-shirt.
butamaMy T-shirt is blue, but my shoes are red.
becauseçünküI am not wearing a coat because it is sunny.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your clothes today. (Bugün ne giydiğin hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • am / is / are + wearing (I am wearing a hat. She is wearing a dress.)
  • a clothes word / kıyafet (hat, T-shirt, dress, shoes, socks, coat)
  • a colour / renk (red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, pink)
  • one negative / olumsuz ("I am not wearing a coat." / "He isn't wearing a hat.")

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Today I am wearing ..." "My hat is ..." "My shoes are ..." "I am not wearing ..."

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~45 words) Today I am wearing my favourite clothes. I am wearing a blue T-shirt and black shoes. My hat is red and my socks are green. I am not wearing a coat because it is sunny. My best friend is wearing a yellow dress. We are happy!
