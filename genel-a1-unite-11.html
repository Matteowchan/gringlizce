Ünite 11 · Past Simple · Bölüm 1/7

Grammar: Past Simple (worked / went / didn't / Did...?)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Geçmişte Ne Yaptın? — A1'in Son Gramer Durağı

Ünite 10'da geçmişteki durumları anlatmayı öğrendin: "I was tired.", "The meeting was long." Ama was/were sadece durum anlatır — geçmişte ne yaptığını anlatamaz. "Dün raporu bitirdim", "Geçen hafta müşteriyle görüştük", "Sözleşmeyi imzaladık" demek için Past Simple gerekir:

  • "I worked late yesterday."Dün geç saate kadar çalıştım. (eylem)
  • "We visited the customer last week."Geçen hafta müşteriyi ziyaret ettik. (eylem)
  • "She went to Ankara on Monday."Pazartesi Ankara'ya gitti. (eylem)
  • "They didn't come to the meeting."Toplantıya gelmediler. (olumsuz)
  • "Did you finish the report?"Raporu bitirdin mi? (soru)

Kısacası: was/were = geçmişteki durum, Past Simple = geçmişteki eylem. Bu ikisi birlikte, geçmişi anlatmanın tamamını oluşturur. Past Simple aynı zamanda A2 seviyesine açılan kapıdır — hikaye anlatmanın, deneyim paylaşmanın temeli budur. Bu bölümde düzenli fiilleri (-ed), en önemli düzensiz fiilleri, didn't ile olumsuzu ve Did ile soruyu öğreneceksin.

1. Düzenli Fiiller: -ed (Regular Verbs)

Çoğu fiilin geçmiş hali basittir: fiilin sonuna -ed eklenir. Özneye göre DEĞİŞMEZ — I, she, they fark etmez, form aynıdır:

FiilGeçmiş (V2)ÖrnekTürkçesi
workworkedI worked at the office yesterday.Dün ofiste çalıştım.
callcalledShe called the customer this morning.Bu sabah müşteriyi aradı.
askaskedThey asked many questions.Çok soru sordular.
helphelpedMy team helped me a lot.Ekibim bana çok yardım etti.

Yazımda dört küçük kural vardır — hepsi bu tabloda:

KuralÖrnekler
Fiil -e ile bitiyorsa sadece +darrive → arrived · decide → decided
Sessiz harf + y ile bitiyorsa: y düşer, -iedstudy → studied · try → tried
Sesli harf + y ise değişiklik yok, sadece +edplay → played · stay → stayed
Tek heceli, kısa ünlü + sessiz ile bitenlerde son harf ÇİFT yazılırplan → planned · stop → stopped
Telaffuz mini notu
  • -ed üç şekilde okunur: /t/ (worked, finished), /d/ (called, agreed).
  • Fiil t veya d ile bitiyorsa /ıd/ diye ayrı bir hece okunur: wanted (wan-tıd), needed (nii-dıd), started.
  • En sık hata: her -ed'yi "-ed" diye okumak. "worked" tek hecedir: "workt".

2. Düzensiz Fiiller (Irregular Verbs)

İngilizcenin en sık kullanılan fiilleri kurala uymaz: -ed almazlar, tamamen değişirler. Kötü haber: bunları ezberlemen gerekir. İyi haber: A1'de sadece bu 14 tanesi yeter — ve hepsi iş hayatının en sık fiilleri:

FiilGeçmiş (V2)ÖrnekTürkçesi
gowentI went to a meeting at ten.Onda bir toplantıya gittim.
havehadWe had lunch with the client.Müşteriyle öğle yemeği yedik.
makemadeShe made a new design.Yeni bir tasarım yaptı.
meetmetI met the new manager yesterday.Dün yeni yöneticiyle tanıştım.
comecameHe came to the office early.Ofise erken geldi.
seesawI saw your email this morning.E-postanı bu sabah gördüm.
sendsentThey sent the report on Friday.Raporu cuma günü gönderdiler.
writewroteShe wrote three emails.Üç e-posta yazdı.
taketookI took a taxi to the airport.Havalimanına taksiyle gittim.
getgotHe got his first salary last week.Geçen hafta ilk maaşını aldı.
givegaveThe manager gave me the contract.Yönetici bana sözleşmeyi verdi.
saysaid"Good job," she said."İyi iş," dedi.
dodidWe did a lot of work yesterday.Dün çok iş yaptık.
buyboughtThey bought new computers.Yeni bilgisayarlar aldılar.
Ezber taktiği
  • Düzensiz fiili tek başına değil, bir cümle içinde ezberle: "went" değil, "I went to a meeting."
  • Bu 14 fiili her gün 2 dakika tekrar et — bir haftada oturur.
  • "say" fiilinin geçmişi "said" yazılır ama "sed" diye okunur.

3. Olumsuz Cümle: didn't (Negative)

Olumsuz için tek yardımcı yeter: didn't (did not). Kural çok nettir: didn't + YALIN fiil. Fiil asla -ed almaz, asla V2 olmaz — çünkü geçmiş anlamını "didn't" zaten taşıyor:

OlumluOlumsuzTürkçesi
I finished the report.I didn't finish the report.Raporu bitirmedim.
She called the customer.She didn't call the customer.Müşteriyi aramadı.
They went to the meeting.They didn't go to the meeting.Toplantıya gitmediler.
He sent the email.He didn't send the email.E-postayı göndermedi.
En kritik kural
  • "I didn't finished the report." ✗ → "I didn't finish the report." ✓ — didn't'ten sonra fiil YALIN kalır.
  • Düzensiz fiilde de aynı: "She didn't went" ✗ → "She didn't go" ✓
  • didn't her özne için aynıdır: I didn't, she didn't, they didn't — değişmez.

4. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar: Did ...? (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru için cümlenin başına Did gelir ve fiil yine yalın kalır: You called the customer. → Did you call the customer?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Did you call the customer?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did she finish the design?Yes, she did.No, she didn't.
Did they sign the contract?Yes, they did.No, they didn't.
Did the meeting start late?Yes, it did.No, it didn't.
Dikkat
  • Kısa cevapta ana fiil TEKRAR EDİLMEZ: "Yes, I called." yerine "Yes, I did."
  • Soru kelimesiyle sıra aynı kalır: "What did you do yesterday?", "Where did she go?", "When did they arrive?"
  • Sık zaman ifadeleri: yesterday (dün), last week/month/year (geçen hafta/ay/yıl), two days ago (iki gün önce), on Monday (pazartesi günü).

5. was/were mi, Past Simple mı? (State or Action?)

Bu ünitenin en önemli ayrımı: geçmişten bahsederken hangisini seçeceksin? Cevap basit — cümlede eylem var mı, yok mu?

  • "The meeting was long."Toplantı uzundu. (durum — fiil yok, sıfat var)
  • "The meeting started late."Toplantı geç başladı. (eylem — "start" fiili var)
  • "I was at the office. I worked until seven."Ofisteydim. Yediye kadar çalıştım. (önce durum, sonra eylem)

Soru sorarken de aynı mantık geçerlidir — Did mi, Was/Were mü?

Sorudaki şeyKullanÖrnek
Durum (sıfat, yer, meslek — ana fiil yok)Was / WereWas the meeting long? · Were you at the office?
Eylem (bir fiil var: start, call, go...)Did + yalın fiilDid the meeting start late? · Did you call him?
Pratik test
  • Cümlede be dışında bir fiil görüyorsan (start, work, go, send...) → did/didn't.
  • Sadece sıfat/yer/isim varsa (long, at the office, a manager) → was/were.
  • İkisi asla birlikte kullanılmaz: "Did you were busy?" ✗ → "Were you busy?" ✓

6. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Past Simple'da Türkçe konuşanların en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I didn't went to the meeting.I didn't go to the meeting.didn't'ten sonra fiil yalın kalır.
Did you sent the email?Did you send the email?Did'den sonra da fiil yalın kalır.
He goed home early.He went home early.go düzensizdir: went. "-ed" almaz.
I did finished the report.I finished the report.Olumlu cümlede "did" kullanılmaz; fiil zaten V2 olur.
Yesterday I finish the report.Yesterday I finished the report.Geçmiş zaman ifadesi (yesterday) varsa fiil V2 olmalı.
She maked a design.She made a design.make düzensizdir: made.
Did you were at the office?Were you at the office?Durum sorusunda Was/Were kullanılır; Did ile birleşmez.

7. Uygulama: Dünkü İş Günü (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Aşağıdaki diyalog düzenli fiilleri, düzensiz fiilleri, didn't'i ve Did sorularını birlikte kullanıyor — koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Hi Deniz! I didn't see you yesterday. Where were you?Selam Deniz! Dün seni görmedim. Neredeydin?
  • — I was at the Ankara office. I visited our new customer there.Ankara ofisindeydim. Oradaki yeni müşterimizi ziyaret ettim.
  • — Oh! Did you travel by plane?Ee! Uçakla mı gittin?
  • — No, I didn't. I went by train. It was comfortable.Hayır. Trenle gittim. Rahattı.
  • — Did the meeting go well?Toplantı iyi geçti mi?
  • — Yes, it did. We agreed on the price and they signed the contract!Evet, iyi geçti. Fiyatta anlaştık ve sözleşmeyi imzaladılar!
  • — That's great news! What did you do after the meeting?Harika haber! Toplantıdan sonra ne yaptın?
  • — We celebrated at a restaurant, and I came back late at night.Bir restoranda kutladık ve gece geç saatte döndüm.
  • — You worked a lot yesterday. Did you finish your report too?Dün çok çalışmışsın. Raporunu da bitirdin mi?
  • — No, I didn't finish it — I was very tired. But I started it on the train!Hayır, bitirmedim — çok yorgundum. Ama trende başladım!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane "did" ile kurulmuş soru var? (Cevap: 4 — "What did you do" dahil)
  • "went" ve "came" hangi fiillerin geçmiş halleri? (Cevap: go ve come)
  • Deniz neden "I didn't finish it" diyor da "I didn't finished it" demiyor? (Bölüm 3'teki kurala bak.)

A1'in Son Gramer Konusu (What's Next?)

Tebrikler — bu, A1 seviyesinin son gramer konusuydu. Artık şimdiki zamanı, geniş zamanı, was/were'ü ve Past Simple'ı biliyorsun; yani İngilizcenin üç ana zaman bölgesinde de cümle kurabiliyorsun. Past Simple'ın derinliği — daha fazla düzensiz fiil, hikaye anlatımı, geçmiş alışkanlıklar — A2'de seni bekliyor. Şimdilik bu ünitedeki 14 düzensiz fiili ve didn't/Did kurallarını sağlamlaştır; A2'ye en güçlü halinle gir.

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the verbs with their past forms, then use them in your own sentences. (Fiilleri geçmiş halleriyle öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

finish / finished (v)
to complete something (bitirmek)
Syn: complete (tamamlamak) · Ant: start (başlamak)
"I finished the report yesterday."
start / started (v)
to begin something (başlamak)
Syn: begin (başlamak) · Ant: finish (bitirmek)
"The meeting started late."
visit / visited (v)
to go and see a person or place (ziyaret etmek)
Syn: go to see (görmeye gitmek) · Ant: leave (ayrılmak)
"We visited the Ankara office last week."
sign / signed (v)
to write your name on a document (imzalamak)
Syn: approve (onaylamak) · Ant: reject (reddetmek)
"They signed the contract on Friday."
agree / agreed (v)
to say yes to an idea (anlaşmak, aynı fikirde olmak)
Syn: accept (kabul etmek) · Ant: disagree (aynı fikirde olmamak)
"We agreed on the price."
travel / travelled (v)
to go from one place to another (seyahat etmek)
Syn: go (gitmek) · Ant: stay (kalmak)
"She travelled to Izmir for work."
win / won (v)
to be the best or the first (kazanmak)
Syn: get (elde etmek) · Ant: lose / lost (kaybetmek)
"Our team won the new project."
lose / lost (v)
to not win; to not have something now (kaybetmek)
Syn: miss (kaçırmak) · Ant: win / won (kazanmak)
"We lost a big customer last year."
spend / spent (v)
to use time or money (harcamak — zaman/para)
Syn: use (kullanmak) · Ant: save (biriktirmek)
"I spent two hours on the report."
sell / sold (v)
to give something for money (satmak)
Syn: trade (ticaret yapmak) · Ant: buy / bought (satın almak)
"They sold the old computers."
decide / decided (v)
to choose after thinking (karar vermek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: wait (beklemek)
"We decided on a new plan."
celebrate / celebrated (v)
to do something special for good news (kutlamak)
Syn: have a party (parti yapmak) · Ant: ignore (görmezden gelmek)
"We celebrated after the meeting."
Toplantı sonrası: sözleşme imzalandı, el sıkışılıyor — dün ne olduğunu Past Simple ile anlatıyorlar.

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

  • What do you remember about Selin's first day? (Selin'in ilk günü hakkında ne hatırlıyorsun?)
  • Think about YOUR first month at a job or school. What did you do? (Kendi ilk ayını düşün. Neler yaptın?)
  • Look at the picture below. What does it show? (Aşağıdaki görsele bak. Ne anlatıyor olabilir?)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin — konuyu yakala.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar her zaman metnin içindedir.
  • Bu metin bir geçmiş anlatısıdır: fiillerin V2 hallerine dikkat et (started, made, bought...).

Verbs to spot (Metinde geçen, bu ünitede öğrendiğin geçmiş formlar — gözün bunları arasın): started · showed · made · liked · joined · bought · celebrated · got · went · learned

Selin'in ilk ayı: takvimdeki her gün doldu — yıldızlı günler özel anlar.
Selin's First Month

One month ago, Selin started her job at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. On her first day, she was very nervous, but her team helped her a lot. Now she remembers that month with a big smile.

Selin is twenty-four years old. She is a designer. She lives in Istanbul with her mother, her father, and her little brother. She loves her city. Baytek is not a very big company, but it is a good place to work. About forty people work there. The office is on the fifth floor of a tall building. It has big windows, white desks, and many green plants. On her first day, Selin liked the office very much. Her desk was next to a window, and she could see the street and the sky. 'This is a nice place,' she thought.

On that first morning, Selin arrived early. She was not late. She wore a blue shirt and black shoes. Her manager, Mr. Demir, came to her desk and said, 'Welcome to Baytek, Selin!' Then two colleagues came to say hello. Their names were Mark and Elif. They were very friendly. 'Don't worry,' Elif said with a smile. 'We are all here to help you.' Selin felt calm. The first day was long, but it was a good day. In the evening, she called her mother. 'I like my new job,' she said. 'The people are very kind.'

In the first week, Mark showed her the office and Elif taught her the phone system. Everything was new, so Selin asked many questions, and her colleagues answered them all.

Selin learned many new things in the first week. She learned the names of all her colleagues. She learned the coffee machine and the printer too. Every morning, she made a coffee and started work at nine o'clock. She read her emails first. Then she opened her design programs. At lunch, she ate with her team in the kitchen. They talked about work, about films, and about football. Selin laughed a lot. After only one week, she did not feel like a stranger. She felt like a real part of the team.

Selin also met other people at Baytek. There was Deniz, a funny man from the sales team. There was Aylin, a quiet woman who made the best coffee. Everyone was busy, but everyone was friendly. On Friday of the first week, the team had a small lunch together. They welcomed Selin with a cake. 'Now you are one of us,' Mark said. Selin was very happy. She did not know these people two weeks before, but now they were her friends.

In the second week, Selin made her first design for a customer. She worked on it for three days. Mr. Demir liked it very much. 'This is excellent work,' he said.

The first design was for a small shop in Izmir. The shop wanted a new logo. Selin drew many ideas on paper first. She used a pencil and a ruler. Then she made the logo on her computer. She did not finish it quickly. She worked on it slowly and carefully. She changed the colours many times. Mark helped her with some ideas. When Mr. Demir saw the final design, he smiled and said his kind words. Selin was very proud. It was her first real work for a real customer, and it was good.

In the third week, Selin joined a meeting with a customer from England, Mrs. Collins. Selin spoke very little at the meeting because she was excited. But at the end, there was great news: Mrs. Collins bought the design! Everyone celebrated after the meeting with coffee and cake.

The meeting started at ten o'clock. Mrs. Collins came from London. She was a tall woman with short hair, and she was very kind. Mark and Elif were in the meeting too. They talked about the design and the price. Selin listened and took notes. She did not say many words, but she understood everything. At the end, Mrs. Collins looked at Selin and said, 'I love your design.' Selin was so happy. After the meeting, Mr. Demir said, 'Good job, Selin!' It was a very special day, and Selin did not forget it.

Last Friday, Selin got her first salary. She was very happy, so she went to a shopping centre and bought small presents for her family. At the weekend, they celebrated together at home.

At the shopping centre, Selin bought a book for her father and a warm scarf for her mother. She bought a small toy car for her little brother too. Her family was very happy with the presents. On Saturday, they had a big dinner at home. Selin's mother made her favourite food. They ate, they talked, and they laughed for hours. 'We are very proud of you,' her father said. Selin smiled. One month ago, she was a nervous new worker. Now she had a job, a salary, and many good stories to tell.

Selin also thought about her English. At Baytek, some customers came from other countries. Selin wanted to speak better English. So every evening, she studied for thirty minutes. She learned new words and practised past simple sentences. 'Yesterday I made a design. Last week I joined a meeting,' she said to herself. Little by little, her English got better. Now she was not afraid of English meetings. This was another good change in her first month.

'I learned a lot in one month,' Selin says. 'On my first day, I didn't know anything about the job. I didn't understand the phone system, and I didn't know my colleagues. Now I have new skills and new friends. I am ready for month two!'

Now Selin comes to work with a smile every day. She is not the new person anymore. She helps other people in the office. Last week, a new colleague started at Baytek. His name is Can. He was very nervous on his first day, like Selin one month before. So Selin showed him the office, and she taught him the phone system. 'Don't worry, Can,' she said. 'The first month is not always easy, but it is wonderful. Ask many questions. We are all here to help you.' Selin remembered her own first day, and she was very happy to help a new friend.

Selin knows the road is long. There is a lot more to learn. But she is not afraid now. Every day she learns something new, and every day she feels stronger. 'Month one was wonderful,' she thinks. 'Month two will be even better.' And with a big smile, she opens her computer and starts a new day at Baytek.

Geçmişi Anlatan Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (Writing a Past Narrative)

Geçmiş anlatısı, cümleleri zamana göre dizip bağlaçlarla bağlamaktır. A1'de öğrendiğin beş bağlaç geçmiş anlatıda da aynen çalışır — bu kez Past Simple cümleleri bağlıyorlar:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek (geçmiş anlatı)
andveEkleme yaparI finished the report and sent it to my manager.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI was tired, but I finished the design.
orveyaSeçenek sunarDid you go by bus or by train?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirI was new, so I asked many questions.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirWe celebrated because we won the project.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı anlatı ✓
I started my job. I was nervous. My colleagues helped me. I learned a lot. I am happy. I started my job last month and I was very nervous. But my colleagues helped me, so I learned a lot. Now I am happy because they are my friends.
Geçmiş anlatının iki motoru
  • Zaman ifadeleri anlatıyı sıralar: On my first day... In the first week... Last Friday... After the meeting...
  • was/were + Past Simple karışımı anlatıyı canlandırır: önce durumu ver (I was nervous), sonra eylemi anlat (but I asked questions).

Arkadaşa E-posta Yapısı (Informal Email Structure)

Bu kez ekibe değil, bir arkadaşına yazıyorsun — yapı aynıdır ama ton samimidir:

BölümNe yazılır?Örnek
1. Açılış
(Opening)		Samimi selamlama + hal hatır + konuyu açan ilk cümleHi Ali,
How are you? I finished my first month at my new job!
2. Gövde
(Body)		Geçmiş anlatı: ilk gün nasıldı (was/were), sonra ne yaptın (Past Simple). Zaman ifadeleri + bağlaçlarla bağla.On my first day, I was nervous. But my colleagues helped me, and in the second week I started a big project...
3. Kapanış
(Closing)		Samimi bitiş cümlesi + veda + isimVisit me soon!
Best wishes,
Selin
Sık kullanılan kalıplar
  • Açılış: "Hi ...," · "Hello ...," · "Dear ...,"
  • Kapanış: "Best wishes," · "See you soon," · "Take care,"

Task (Görev)

Write an email to a friend about your first month at your job. (Bir arkadaşına işteki ilk ayını anlatan bir e-posta yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • at least 6 Past Simple verbs, at least 2 irregular (en az 6 geçmiş zaman fiili, en az 2'si düzensiz: went, made, bought, got, won...)
  • at least 1 sentence with didn't (en az 1 didn't'li cümle)
  • 1-2 sentences with was/were for feelings or situations (durum/duygu için 1-2 was/were cümlesi)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: finished, started, visited, signed, celebrated, won, spent...)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Hi ... / Best wishes,)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"Last month I started my new job at ..." "On my first day, I was ..., but ..." "In the second week, I ..."

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~160 words) Hi Ali,

How are you? I finished my first month at Baytek last Friday, and I am very happy!

On my first day, I was nervous because everything was new. But my colleagues helped me a lot. In the first week, Mark showed me the office and I visited every department. In the second week, I started work on a big design project.

Last week was fantastic. We had a meeting with a customer from England, and she bought my design! We celebrated after the meeting, and our manager was very proud. I didn't understand everything at the meeting, but I learned a lot.

On Friday, I got my first salary. I spent some of it on presents for my family, but I didn't spend it all!

I made new friends here, so I am not a stranger now. Visit me soon — lunch is on me!

Best wishes,
Selin
