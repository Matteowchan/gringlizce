Final Sınavı: Ünite 1-21 (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final sınavı, Teen A1 kurundaki 21 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: be (am/is/are), özne zamirleri ve a/an; okul hayatı ve have/has; arkadaşlar ve aile; boş zaman ve Present Simple; spor/hobiler ve sıklık zarfları; there is/are; can/can't; Present Continuous; Present Simple ile Continuous karşılaştırması; sayılabilir/sayılamaz isimler; zamirler ve belirteçler; was/were; yer ve zaman edatları; saat ve günlük rutin; Wh- soruları; like/love + -ing; yemek ve sipariş (would like); Past Simple (düzenli + düzensiz fiiller); karşılaştırma sıfatları (comparatives) ve be going to ile gelecek. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

A1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 3/6

Listening: The New Photography Club (10 soru)

Suggested time: 15 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 15 dakika.) Two students talk in the school garden. (İki öğrenci okul bahçesinde konuşuyor.)

Read the dialogue below carefully, then answer the questions. It is about a new photography club at school. Read the questions first; they follow the order of the dialogue. (Aşağıdaki diyaloğu dikkatle oku, sonra soruları cevapla. Okuldaki yeni bir fotoğrafçılık kulübü hakkında. Önce soruları oku; sorular diyaloğun sırasını izler.)

Ceyda & Bora · In the School Garden

Ceyda: Hi Bora! You look really happy today. What's up?

Bora: Hi Ceyda! I'm happy because I joined the new photography club yesterday. It's amazing!

Ceyda: Wow, that's great! When does the club meet?

Bora: Every Thursday after school, from four to five thirty. We meet in the art room on the second floor.

Ceyda: Nice. What do you do there?

Bora: Last week we learned how to use a real camera, and we took photos of the garden. Next month we are going to visit the old town and take photos of the streets.

Ceyda: That sounds fun. Is it expensive?

Bora: No, it's free for students. You only need a phone or a camera. Why don't you come with me on Thursday?

Ceyda: I'd love to, but I have basketball practice on Thursdays. I usually play until five o'clock.

Bora: No problem. The club is smaller and quieter than the basketball team, so you can come after your practice. We are always here until five thirty.

Ceyda: Okay, I'll try! By the way, are there many students in the club?

Bora: There are about twelve of us now, but it is more popular every week. Our teacher, Miss Deniz, is really kind and patient.

Ceyda: Perfect. I'm going to bring my little brother's camera on Thursday. Thanks, Bora!

Bora: See you there!

A1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Text 1 geçmişi, Text 2 şu anı anlatır. (Text 1 is about the past, Text 2 is about now.)

Text 1 · Mert's First Term at a New School

Mert started at Green Valley School in September. On his first day, he was very nervous because he didn't know anybody, and the building was big and easy to get lost in. His form teacher, Mr. Kaya, met him at the gate at eight o'clock, showed him the classrooms and introduced him to the class. There were twenty-four students in the class, but three of them were on a school trip that day, so some seats were empty.

In his first week, Mert didn't say much. He listened to the lessons, watched the other students and made notes. It wasn't easy, but it was useful, because he learned the school rules. He also had lunch with Kaan, a boy from the football team. Kaan told him about the clubs, and he gave him one important tip: "Join a club — it's the best way to make friends."

In October, Mert joined the science club. He worked on a small robot for two weeks with a partner, and they made three different designs. The first design didn't work, and Mert was sad about it, but the second one was much better. The club showed the robot at the school fair, and Mr. Kaya was very proud of them.

In November, Mert had a difficult month. He was ill for a few days, so he stayed at home and missed an important test. But his friends helped him: Kaan sent him the notes, and a classmate explained the difficult parts. At the end of the term, Mr. Kaya gave him good news: Mert's work was strong, and after the winter break he can be a captain of the science club.

Text 2 · A Busy Morning at Green Valley School

It is nine o'clock on a Monday morning, and Green Valley School is very busy. In the main hall, there are about thirty students, and everybody is doing something different.

Mert is sitting near the window in the science room. He is finishing his robot project, and he is listening to music with his headphones. He can work very fast when the music is on. Behind him, Kaan is talking to a new student from Germany. The new student would like some help with his timetable, so Kaan is showing him the way to the maths class. He can speak a little German, but they are speaking English because it is easier for both of them.

In the sports hall, there is a big volleyball match. Miss Deniz is teaching a class of twelve students. She is explaining the rules, and everybody is listening. There is some water on the bench, but there isn't any juice, so Ela is walking to the canteen to buy some.

In the canteen, two students are waiting for their sandwiches. One of them is telling a funny story, and the other one is laughing. Zeynep, the class monitor, is preparing the classroom for a guest: an important writer would like to visit the school at eleven o'clock, and she wants everything to be perfect.

Only one 'student' is not busy: the school cat, Minnoş. She is sleeping on a warm chair near the door, and she isn't listening to anybody. The students can hear the bell, the voices and the music, but Minnoş can sleep in every situation!

A1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: Yesterday's School Trip (70-90 words)

Your classmate Deniz missed yesterday's school trip. Write a short email and tell Deniz about the trip: where you went, what you did there, and what Deniz needs to do now (for example, copy the notes). (Sınıf arkadaşın Deniz dünkü okul gezisini kaçırdı. Geziyi anlatan kısa bir e-posta yaz: nereye gittiniz, orada ne yaptınız, Deniz şimdi ne yapmalı — örneğin notları alsın.)

  • Use Past Simple: went, saw, was/were, learned... (geziyi geçmiş zamanla anlat)
  • Use at least 2 connectors: and, but, or, so, because. (en az 2 bağlaç)
  • 70-90 words. (70-90 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Deniz," / "Dear Deniz," + kısa bir giriş cümlesi (örn. sorry you missed the trip).
  • Gövde: gezide ne oldu — Past Simple + bağlaçlar; Deniz'in şimdi yapması gereken.
  • Kapanış: "See you tomorrow," / "Best wishes," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My First Months at This School (140-170 words)

Write a paragraph about your first months at your school (real or imaginary): how it was at the beginning, your routine now, and what you are doing these days. (Okulundaki ilk aylarını anlatan bir paragraf yaz — gerçek ya da hayali: başlangıçta nasıldı, şimdi rutinin ne, bu günlerde ne yapıyorsun.)

  • Beginning: use was/were AND Past Simple — started, learned, met... (başlangıç için geçmiş zaman)
  • Routine now: use Present Simple with at least 2 frequency adverbs: always, usually, often, sometimes, never. (şimdiki rutin + en az 2 sıklık zarfı)
  • These days: use Present Continuous — I am working on... (şu an süren işler için Present Continuous)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 140-170 words. (140-170 kelime)
