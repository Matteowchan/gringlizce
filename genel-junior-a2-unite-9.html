Ünite 9 · Present Perfect: Süre · Bölüm 1/6

Grammar: Present Perfect — for / since

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden for / since?

Ünite 8'de Present Perfect ile hayat deneyimlerini konuştun (Have you ever...?, I have never...). Now we use the same tense for a different idea: duration (süre). Bir durum geçmişte başladı ve hâlâ devam ediyor — işte bunu Present Perfect ile, çoğu zaman for ve since kelimeleriyle anlatırız.

  • I have lived in Bursa for ten years.On yıldır Bursa'da yaşıyorum. (hâlâ orada)
  • She has played in the football club since 2019.2019'dan beri futbol kulübünde oynuyor. (hâlâ orada)
  • We have known each other for a long time.Uzun zamandır birbirimizi tanıyoruz.

Dikkat: Türkçede bu cümleleri geniş zamanla ("yaşıyorum", "çalışıyor") çeviririz, ama İngilizcede Present Perfect gerekir. Bu bölümde önce yapıyı hatırlayacağız, sonra for ile since arasındaki farkı ve Present Perfect ile Past Simple arasındaki kritik farkı netleştireceğiz.

1. Köprü: Yapıyı Hatırla (have / has + V3)

Present Perfect'in yapısı Ünite 8'deki gibidir, so this is just a quick bridge. have / has + fiilin 3. hâli (past participle, V3).

ÖzneYardımcıÖrnekTürkçesi
I / you / we / theyhave (+V3)I have played here for a year.Bir yıldır burada oynuyorum.
he / she / ithas (+V3)She has been here since May.Mayıs'tan beri burada.
Altın kural 1
  • Yapı her zaman have/has + V3'tür: lived, worked, known, been, had. Süre anlatırken bile bu değişmez.
  • Olumsuz: haven't / hasn't + V3 — "I haven't seen her since Monday."

2. for vs since (en önemli fark)

Bir süre anlatırken hangisini seçeceğini tek soru belirler: elimdeki bilgi bir uzunluk mu, yoksa bir başlangıç noktası mı? for uzunluk ister, since başlangıç ister.

KelimeNe ile kullanılır?AnlamıÖrnekler
forbir süre / uzunluk (a period)... boyunca / ...dırfor two years · for a week · for ten minutes · for a long time
sincebir başlangıç noktası (a point in time)... -den berisince 2019 · since Monday · since May · since I was a child

Aynı gerçeği iki şekilde söyleyebilirsin:

  • I have had this bike for three years.Bu bisiklete üç yıldır sahibim. (uzunluk)
  • I have had this bike since 2023.Bu bisiklete 2023'ten beri sahibim. (başlangıç noktası)
Altın kural 2 — hızlı test
  • Kelime bir rakam + süre ise (two years, ten minutes) → for.
  • Kelime bir tarih / gün / olay ise (2019, Monday, I was young) → since.
  • Yaygın hata: "since two years" ✗ — doğrusu "for two years" ✓.

3. How long...? (Ne kadar süredir?)

Süreyi sormak için How long ile başlarız, sonra Present Perfect gelir. Cevapta for veya since kullanırsın.

SoruCevap (for)Cevap (since)
How long have you lived here?For five years.Since 2020.
How long has she played there?For six years.Since she started school.
Altın kural 3
  • Kalıp: How long + have/has + özne + V3? — "How long have you known him?"
  • Soruda do/does/did kullanılmaz: "How long do you live here?" ✗ — "How long have you lived here?" ✓.

4. Present Perfect vs Past Simple (bitmemiş vs bitmiş)

Bu, ünitenin en kritik konusu. İki zaman da geçmişten bahseder ama çok farklı şeyler söyler. Fark tek kelimede: durum hâlâ sürüyor mu, yoksa bitti mi?

 Present Perfect (sürüyor)Past Simple (bitti)
DurumGeçmişte başladı, hâlâ devam ediyorGeçmişte oldu ve bitti
Zaman kelimesiBelirli geçmiş zaman YOK (for/since var)Belirli geçmiş zaman VAR (yesterday, in 2019, ago, last...)
ÖrnekI have lived in Izmir for ten years. (hâlâ orada)I lived in Izmir for ten years. (artık orada değil)
ÖrnekShe has played tennis since 2019.She played tennis in 2019. (o zaman)
Altın kural 4 — en çok sınanan kural
  • Cümlede belirli bir geçmiş zaman varsa (yesterday, last week, in 2019, two days ago) → Past Simple. Present Perfect ile bunlar KULLANILMAZ.
  • "I have seen him yesterday." ✗ — "I saw him yesterday." ✓
  • Süre hâlâ sürüyorsa (for/since ile) → Present Perfect: "I have known him since 2019." ✓

5. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların bu konuda en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I live here since 2019.I have lived here since 2019.Süre için Present Perfect gerekir.
She has played here since two years.She has played here for two years.rakam + süre → for (since değil).
We have known him for 2015.We have known him since 2015.tarih → since (for değil).
I have seen her last week.I saw her last week."last week" belirli geçmiş → Past Simple.
How long do you live here?How long have you lived here?How long süre sorar → Present Perfect.
He has here since Monday.He has been here since Monday.V3 unutulmuş: be → been.

6. Uygulama: Yeni Takım Arkadaşı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two children meet at the club on the first day. Koyu yazılan yapıları (Present Perfect, for/since, Past Simple) inceleyerek iki kez oku:

  • — Hi, I'm Deniz. How long have you played for this club?Merhaba, ben Deniz. Ne kadar süredir bu kulüpte oynuyorsun?
  • — I have been here for six years. I joined in 2019.Altı yıldır buradayım. 2019'da katıldım.
  • — Wow! And have you always lived in Istanbul?Vay! Hep İstanbul'da mı yaşadın?
  • — No. I lived in Ankara for ten years, but I have lived here since 2018.Hayır. On yıl Ankara'da yaşadım ama 2018'den beri burada yaşıyorum.
  • — Did you like Ankara?Ankara'yı sevdin mi?
  • — Yes, but I have known Istanbul since I was a child, so it feels like home.Evet, ama İstanbul'u çocukluğumdan beri tanıyorum, o yüzden evim gibi.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane for, kaç tane since var? (Cevap: for → 2, since → 3)
  • "I lived in Ankara for ten years" neden Past Simple? (Çünkü artık orada değil — bitti.)
  • "I have lived here since 2018" neden Present Perfect? (Çünkü hâlâ orada — sürüyor.)

Exercises (Alıştırmalar)

Choose the correct answer. (Doğru cevabı seç.) — 10 soru, her soru anında değerlendirilir.

Ünite 9 · Present Perfect: Süre · Bölüm 2/6

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them to talk about how long you have done things. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra bir şeyi ne kadar süredir yaptığını anlatmak için kullan.)

for (prep)
used with a length of time (bir süre boyunca / ...dır)
Kullanım: for + süre · Ant: since (başlangıç noktası)
"I have studied English for three years."
since (prep)
from a point in the past until now (... -den beri)
Kullanım: since + tarih/olay · Ant: for (süre)
"She has played here since 2019."
how long (phrase)
a question about duration (ne kadar süredir)
Syn: for how much time · Kalıp: How long have you...?
"How long have you lived here?"
experience (n)
knowledge from doing something (deneyim, tecrübe)
Syn: know-how (birikim) · Adj: experienced (deneyimli)
"He has a lot of experience in football."
club (n)
a group that meets to do an activity (kulüp)
Syn: group (grup) · Rel: team (takım)
"She has been in the chess club since 2019."
teammate (n)
a person you play with (takım arkadaşı)
Syn: team-mate (oyun arkadaşı) · Ant: stranger (yabancı)
"I have known my teammates for years."
recently (adv)
a short time ago; lately (son zamanlarda)
Syn: lately (yakınlarda) · Ant: long ago (çok önce)
"She has joined a new club recently."
captain (n)
the leader of a team (kaptan, takım lideri)
Syn: leader (lider) · Rel: team (takım)
"He has been the captain since May."
medal (n)
a prize you win (madalya)
Verb: win a medal (madalya kazanmak) · Rel: prize (ödül)
"She won a gold medal last month."
skill (n)
an ability you learn (beceri, yetenek)
Syn: ability (kabiliyet) · Adj: skilled (becerikli)
"I have learned many new skills this year."
practise (v)
to do something again and again to get better (alıştırma yapmak, çalışmak)
Noun: practice (alıştırma) · Rel: improve (gelişmek)
"He has practised the piano for years."
join (v)
to become part of a group or team (katılmak)
Syn: become a member (üye olmak) · Ant: leave (ayrılmak)
"She joined the team six years ago."
match (n)
a game between two teams (maç)
Syn: game (oyun) · Rel: score (skor)
"They played an exciting match yesterday."
so far (phrase)
up to now (şimdiye kadar)
Syn: until now (şu ana dek) · Rel: yet (henüz)
"So far, I have finished three tasks."
Süre hakkında konuşma: How long...? sorusuna for / since ile cevap ver.

Production Task (Üretim Görevi)

Write 8 sentences about how long you have done things. Use at least 8 of the words above. (Bir şeyleri ne kadar süredir yaptığın hakkında 8 cümle yaz. Yukarıdaki kelimelerden en az 8'ini kullan.)

Gramer şartı — bu ünitenin konusunu uygula
  • En az 2 cümle Present Perfect + for / since içersin ("I have lived here for..." / "since...").
  • En az 1 kontrast yap: bir Present Perfect (sürüyor) + bir Past Simple (bitti) cümlesi ("I have played tennis for two years, but I did karate before.").
  • En az 1 soru sor: "How long have you...?"

Sentence starters (Cümle başlatıcıları — istersen bunlarla başla):

"I have lived in ... for ..." "I have known ... since ..." "How long have you ...?" "Before that, I ... (Past Simple)" "So far, I have ..."

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will review your speaking task.)

Reading: Selin and the Green Stars

Read the text, then answer the questions. (Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • How long have YOU played a sport or done a hobby? (Sen ne kadar süredir bir spor yapıyor ya da bir hobiyle uğraşıyorsun?)
  • The text is about a hobby that is still going on. Which tense will you see a lot? (Metin hâlâ süren bir hobiyi anlatıyor. Hangi zamanı çok göreceksin?)
  • Look for for and since. Which one comes before a date? (for ve since'i ara. Hangisi bir tarihten önce gelir?)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Present Perfect'e (has played, has known, has been) ve for / since kelimelerine dikkat et. Bir de Past Simple (joined, started, won) fiillerini ayırt et — onlar bitmiş olayları anlatır.

Words to spot (Metinde geçen, Speaking bölümünden tanıdığın kelimeler): for · since · club · teammate · captain · practise · join · skill · experience · medal · recently · match

Selin'in kulüpteki yılları: 2019'dan bugüne, hâlâ devam ediyor.
Selin and the Green Stars

The Green Stars is a small football club with a big heart, and its story is full of hard-working young players. Some children stay for only one season, but a few grow up with the team and never leave. Selin is one of those special players, and her journey is a favourite story at the club.

The Green Stars have won many trophies over the years, and photos of old teams cover the club walls. Selin has looked at those photos many times, and she has dreamed of adding her own. The club has always felt like a big, happy family to her, and she has given it her very best.

Selin has played for the Green Stars for six years. She joined the club in 2019, just after she started at a new school. In her first year, she played in the beginners' group, but she didn't stay there for long, because her coaches quickly saw her skills.

Since 2021, Selin has been the team captain. She has played in many big matches, and she has practised hard to learn new skills. Last spring, she won a very difficult final against a strong team from Ankara, and everyone was very happy with her.

As captain, Selin has done much more than play. She has welcomed new players, and she has helped them feel part of the team. Since 2021, she has organised many practices, and she has never missed an important game. Her coaches have often said that she has become a real leader.

'I have known my teammates for a long time,' Selin says. 'We have played together since the beginning, so we are like a family. We lost some hard games in the past, but we always helped each other, and now we trust each other completely.'

Selin has changed a lot since her first day. Six years ago, she was a shy little girl with little experience. Now she is a confident captain, and she has not thought about leaving the club. Her younger teammates have often asked her for advice, because she has learned so much about the game over the years.

The younger players look up to her every day. They have watched how she stays calm, and they have copied her hard work. "Selin has taught us that practice is the secret," one of them said. "She has been here for years, so she knows the game better than anyone."

Recently, the club gave Selin a gold medal. 'I have wanted this for years,' she says with a big smile. 'This club has been like a home since 2019. When I started, I only hoped to play for a year or two, but I have found a real team here.'

Today, the gold medal sits on a shelf in Selin's room, next to her old team photos. It reminds her of every match, every friend, and every year with the Green Stars. She has grown up with this club, and she hopes to keep playing for many more happy seasons.

Writing: How Long Have You...?

Write and submit your paragraph; your teacher will review it. (Paragrafını yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

for / since ile Yazmak (Writing About Duration)

Bu ünitede uzun süredir devam eden bir şeyi (bir hobi ya da bir spor) anlatacaksın. İki dil aracı işini görecek: for / since (süre) ve Present Perfect vs Past Simple farkı. Süren şey için Present Perfect, bitmiş bir olay için Past Simple kullan.

Ne anlatıyorsun?Hangi zaman?Örnek
Hâlâ süren durum (kaç yıldır)Present Perfect + for/sinceI have played the guitar for five years.
Ne zaman / nasıl başladı (bitmiş)Past Simple + belirli zamanI started when I was twelve.
Şimdiye kadar neler yaptınPresent PerfectI have learned many songs.
Bağlaçlar unutma
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan: and, but, so, because, when. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirleri bağla.
  • Örnek: "I started when I was young, but I have only played in a band since last year, so I am still learning."

Paragraf Yapısı (Paragraph Structure)

Bir "ne kadar süredir" paragrafı üç bölümden oluşur. This skeleton is the same every time — içini değiştirirsin, yapıyı değiştirmezsin:

BölümNe yazılır?Örnek
1. Giriş
(Opening)		Ne olduğu + ne kadar süredir (for/since)I have played the guitar for five years.
2. Gövde
(Body)		Nasıl/ne zaman başladın (Past Simple) + şimdiye kadar neler yaptın (Present Perfect)I started when I was twelve, and I have learned a lot since then.
3. Kapanış
(Closing)		Bugün nasıl hissettiğin / genel değerlendirmeI haven't stopped, because it makes me happy.

Task (Görev)

Write about a hobby or a sport you have done for a long time. (Uzun zamandır yaptığın bir hobiyi ya da sporu anlat.) Use the Present Perfect with for / since.

Neler olmalı (What to include)

  • what the hobby or sport is (hobi/spor ne)
  • how long you have done it — use for AND since at least once each (en az birer kez for ve since)
  • when and how you started it (Past Simple — bitmiş olay)
  • two or three things you have done so far (Present Perfect)
  • one thing you did in the past that is now finished (Past Simple kontrast)
  • how you feel about it now
  • en az 3 bağlaç: and, but, so, because, when
  • Speaking bölümünden en az 3 kelime (experience, club, teammate, skill, recently, so far...)

Örnek başlangıçlar (Example openers)

"I have played ... for ..." "I started ... when I was ..." "Since then, I have ..."

Your Paragraph (100-130 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~120 words) I have played the guitar for about eight years, and I have loved it since my first lesson. I started when I was thirteen, because my brother had an old guitar at home. At first, it was very difficult, but I practised every day.

Since then, I have learned many songs, and I have played in two small bands. I have also made good friends through music, so it is more than a hobby for me now. Last summer, I even played at a friend's wedding, and everyone enjoyed it.

I haven't become a professional, but that is fine. I have not stopped playing for a single week, because the guitar makes me happy and relaxed.
