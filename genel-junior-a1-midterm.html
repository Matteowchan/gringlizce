Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-19 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the test. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-19'da öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: be (am/is/are), have/has, Present Simple (I/you/we/they ve he/she/it), there is/are, can, Present Continuous, çoğullar, zamirler, this/that + emir cümleleri, was/were, yer edatları, saat, wh- soruları, hobiler, yiyecek-içecek, hava durumu ve kıyafetler. Sorular kısa, basit ve eğlencelidir. Konuşma hariç dört beceri test edilir.

Listening: Lily's New Puppy (10 soru)

Suggested time: 15 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 15 dakika.) Read the little chat, then answer. (Kısa diyaloğu oku, sonra cevapla.)

In this test there is no sound. Read the short chat between Lily and Tom, then answer the questions below. You can read it as many times as you like. (Bu sınavda ses yok. Lily ile Tom'un kısa sohbetini oku, sonra alttaki soruları cevapla. İstediğin kadar okuyabilirsin.)

A Chat About a Puppy

Lily: Hi Tom! Look, I have a new puppy!

Tom: Wow! What is his name?

Lily: His name is Max. He is small and brown.

Tom: Can he do tricks?

Lily: Yes! Max can sit and jump. He can't swim yet.

Tom: What does he eat?

Lily: He eats meat and rice. He loves apples too!

Tom: Where does Max sleep?

Lily: He sleeps in a little bed in my room. It is next to my toy box.

Tom: You are lucky, Lily! I want a puppy too.

  • Boşluklara diyalogda geçen kelimeyi TEK kelime olarak yaz.

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · My Family

Hi! My name is Emma. I am eight years old, and I live in a small house with my family. We have a cat and a dog.

My dad is a teacher. He is tall and he has short black hair. He likes reading books, and he can play the guitar. Every morning, he drinks coffee and reads the newspaper.

My mum is a doctor. She has long brown hair. She loves flowers, so we have many flowers in our garden. She can cook very well. Her cakes are the best!

My little brother is Leo. He is four. He is small and funny. He likes cars, and he plays with them all day. He can't read yet, but he can count to ten.

On Saturdays, we go to the park. Dad and Leo play football, and Mum and I ride our bikes. Our dog, Rex, runs with us. We are always happy on Saturdays!

A · True / False (Questions 1-5 — Text 1)

Are these sentences true or false? (Bu cümleler doğru mu, yanlış mı?) Dikkat: bazı cümleler mantıklı görünür ama metinde söylenmez.

B · Multiple Choice (Questions 6-10 — Text 1)

Choose the correct answer. (Doğru cevabı seç.)

Text 2 · My Bedroom

This is my bedroom. It is small, but I love it. The walls are blue like the sky.

There is a bed next to the window. On the bed, there are two soft pillows and a big teddy bear. His name is Bobo.

Next to my bed, there is a small table. On the table, there is a lamp and some books. Under the table, there is a box. In the box, there are many toys — cars, balls and blocks.

There is a big shelf on the wall. On the shelf, there are three plants and a clock. The clock is red and round.

Opposite my bed, there is a desk. I do my homework here. There is a chair in front of the desk. There isn't any computer on my desk, but there are lots of colour pens.

There is only one problem: there isn't a window in the corner, so that part of my room is a little dark.

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Note: My Bedroom (20-40 words)

Write a short note to your friend about your bedroom. What is in your room? (Arkadaşına odanı anlatan kısa bir not yaz. Odanda neler var?)

  • Use there is / there are. (there is/are kullan)
  • Use at least 2 place prepositions: in, on, under, next to, in front of. (en az 2 yer edatı)
  • Colours are nice: blue, red, green... (renkleri kullanabilirsin)
  • 20-40 words. (20-40 kelime)
NOT YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Sam," + kısa bir giriş cümlesi.
  • Gövde: odanı anlat — there is / there are + edatlar.
  • Kapanış: "Bye!" / "See you," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My Family (40-60 words)

Write a short paragraph about your family: who they are, and what they can do. (Ailen hakkında kısa bir paragraf yaz: kim oldukları ve neler yapabildikleri.)

  • Use be (am/is/are) AND have/has. (be ve have birlikte)
  • Use Present Simple — dikkat: he/she için -s. (he/she likes / plays)
  • Use can — what can they do? (can kullan)
  • Use and, but, so, because. (bağlaç kullan)
  • 40-60 words. (40-60 kelime)
0 kelime
