Ünite 1 · Hello! I am... · Bölüm 1/7

Grammar: be (am / is / are)

Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmayı çöz. (Read the rules, study the examples, then practise.)

Merhaba! Ben Gri. Bu ünitede İngilizcenin en çok kullanılan fiiliyle tanışıyoruz: "be" (am / is / are). Kendini, arkadaşlarını ve çevreni anlatmanın temeli bu. Hazırsan başlayalım.

Dinleme (Listening)

Kısa bir video dinle ve izle. (Listen and watch a short video.)

Hadi başlayalım!

"be" Nedir? (What is "be"?)

Kendinden, arkadaşlarından ve çevrenden bahsederken İngilizcede "be" fiilini çok sık kullanırsın. Örnekler:

  • "I am fifteen."On beş yaşındayım. (yaş)
  • "She is my best friend."O benim en iyi arkadaşım. (arkadaş)
  • "We are in the same class."Aynı sınıftayız. (okul)
  • "This is my phone."Bu benim telefonum. (eşya)
  • "My friends are online."Arkadaşlarım çevrimiçi. (sosyal medya)
  • "I am good at football."Futbolda iyiyim. (spor)

Gördüğün gibi "be" fiili cümlede am, is veya are olarak karşına çıkar:

  • "I am ready."Hazırım.
  • "She is ready."O hazır.
  • "We are ready."Biz hazırız.

Hangi kelimeyle am, is veya are kullanacağını şimdi birlikte göreceğiz.

1. Kişi Kelimeleri (Subject Pronouns)

Cümlede "kim?" sorusunun cevabı özne kelimesidir. İngilizcedeki yedi kişi kelimesi:

KelimeTürkçesiNe zaman?
IbenKendinden bahsederken. Her zaman BÜYÜK harf: "I am fifteen."
yousen / sizKarşındaki kişi: "You are my friend."
heo (erkek)Bir erkek: "This is Kaan. He is my classmate."
sheo (kız)Bir kız: "This is Ada. She is my best friend."
ito (hayvan, eşya)Telefon, şarkı, top: "This is my phone. It is new."
webizSen + başkaları: "My friends and I? We are online."
theyonlarBirden çok kişi ya da eşya: "They are in my class."
Dikkat
  • Türkçedeki tek "o", İngilizcede üçe ayrılır: he (erkek), she (kız), it (hayvan/eşya).
  • Bir arkadaşın için "it" kullanma: "My friend? It is nice." ✗ → "She is nice." ✓

2. Doğru Formu Seç: am / is / are

Kural sade ve değişmez. Sadece üç grup var:

KişiFormKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi
IamI'mI'm fifteen.On beş yaşındayım.
youareYou'reYou're my friend.Sen benim arkadaşımsın.
heisHe'sHe's my classmate.O benim sınıf arkadaşım.
sheisShe'sShe's my best friend.O benim en iyi arkadaşım.
itisIt'sIt's a good song.O güzel bir şarkı.
weareWe'reWe're in the same class.Aynı sınıftayız.
theyareThey'reThey're online.Onlar çevrimiçi.
Kolay yol
  • I → am. Sadece "I" ile "am" kullanılır.
  • he / she / it (tek kişi/şey) → is.
  • you / we / they (birden çok) → are.
  • Konuşurken kısa form kullanırız: "I am" yerine "I'm", "she is" yerine "she's".

3. "değil" Demek: not (Negative)

Bir şey "değil" demek için be'den sonra not eklersin:

Uzun formKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi
I am notI'm notI'm not tired.Yorgun değilim.
He / She / It is notisn'tIt isn't new.O yeni değil.
You / We / They are notaren'tThey aren't online.Onlar çevrimiçi değil.
Not
  • "I" için sadece "I'm not" vardır. "I amn't" diye bir şey yoktur. ✗

4. Soru Sormak (Questions)

Soru sormak için be fiili öznenin önüne geçer. Türkçedeki "mi" ekinin işini bu yapar:

  • You are online. → Are you online?Çevrimiçisin. → Çevrimiçi misin?
  • He is in your class. → Is he in your class?O senin sınıfında. → O senin sınıfında mı?

Kısa cevaplar da kolaydır:

SoruEvetHayır
Are you fifteen?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is she your friend?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Are they in your team?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Dikkat
  • Evet cevabında kısaltma yapma: "Yes, I'm." ✗ → "Yes, I am." ✓
  • Soru kelimeleriyle de aynı sıra: "What is your name?", "How old are you?"

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I fifteen years old.I am fifteen years old."be" atlanamaz. Her cümlede olmalı.
She are my best friend.She is my best friend.he / she / it → is.
They is in my class.They are in my class.they → are.
i am online.I am online."I" her zaman BÜYÜK harf.
Yes, I'm.Yes, I am.Evet cevabında kısaltma yok.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Classmates Meet)

İki genç okulda tanışıyor. Koyu yazılan "be" kelimelerine dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Hi! I'm Deniz. What is your name?Selam! Ben Deniz. Senin adın ne?
  • — Hey Deniz! I'm Elif. Nice to meet you.Selam Deniz! Ben Elif. Tanıştığımıza sevindim.
  • — Are you in class 9-B?9-B sınıfında mısın?
  • — Yes, I am. We are in the same class then!Evet, öyleyim. O zaman aynı sınıftayız!
  • — Cool. Is that your phone?Güzel. O senin telefonun mu?
  • — Yes, it is. It's new. I am into music, so it is full of songs.Evet, öyle. Yeni. Müziğe meraklıyım, o yüzden şarkılarla dolu.
  • — Are you good at football?Futbolda iyi misin?
  • — I'm okay! My friends are in the school team. We are happy today because it is Friday!Fena değilim! Arkadaşlarım okul takımında. Bugün mutluyuz çünkü cuma!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane soru var? (Cevap: 4)
  • "It is new" nasıl kısalır? (Cevap: It's new)

Alıştırma (Practice)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Speaking: My Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan. (Learn the words, then use them.)

classmate (n)
a person in your class (sınıf arkadaşı)
"He is my classmate."
best friend (n)
the friend you like the most (en iyi arkadaş)
"She is my best friend."
phone (n)
you call and text with it (telefon)
"This is my phone. It is new."
online (adj)
connected to the internet (çevrimiçi)
Zıttı: offline (çevrimdışı)
"My friends are online now."
team (n)
a group that plays together (takım)
"We are in the same team."
hobby (n)
a thing you like to do in free time (hobi)
"Music is my hobby."
music (n)
songs you listen to (müzik)
"This music is great."
football (n)
a sport with a ball and two teams (futbol)
"Football is my favourite sport."
favourite (adj)
the one you like the most (en sevdiğin)
"My favourite colour is blue."
good at (phrase)
you can do it well (-de iyi)
Zıttı: bad at (-de kötü)
"I am good at maths."

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Introduce yourself. Write 5 sentences. (Kendini tanıt. 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Her cümlede am / is / are kullan.
  • İsmini, yaşını, bir arkadaşını ve iyi olduğun bir şeyi (spor, müzik, hobi) söyle.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"My name is ..." "I am ... years old." "My best friend is ..." "I am good at ..." "My favourite ... is ..."

2. I am fifteen years old.
3. My best friend is Zeynep. She is in my class.
4. I am good at basketball.
5. My favourite singer is Adele, and I am happy today!

Reading: All About Elif

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

classmate · best friend · phone · online · team · hobby · music · football · favourite

İpucu
  • Önce hızlıca oku — her kelimeyi anlaman gerekmez.
  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
All About Elif

Hi! My name is Elif and I am fifteen years old. I am from İzmir, a big city by the sea. I am a student at Atatürk High School, and I am in class 9-B. English is my favourite lesson, and I am good at it. Maths is not easy for me, but I am okay.

My school day is busy but fun. I get up at seven o'clock every day. I go to school by bus. The bus is always full. My first lesson is at eight o'clock. I have six lessons every day. My favourite lessons are English and art. Art is easy and fun for me. I am good at drawing. My English teacher is Mr. Kaya. He is young and kind. Our classroom is big and bright. My desk is next to the window. I am happy in my class.

This is my family. My mother is a doctor and she is very busy. My father is an engineer. This is my little brother. His name is Can and he is seven years old. He is funny, but he is not always kind! We are a happy family.

My grandmother lives with us. Her name is Nur. She is sixty-eight years old. She is very kind and calm. She is a great cook, and her food is delicious. We have a pet too. It is a cat. Its name is Pamuk. Pamuk is white and soft. He is very lazy, but he is cute. He sleeps all day. I play with him after school. My grandmother and my cat are always at home. My home is warm and happy. I love my family very much.

This is my best friend. Her name is Zeynep. We are in the same class, and we are always together. She is funny and kind. My classmates are nice too, so I am never alone at school. We are a good group.

There are twenty students in my class. We are a big group, and we are all friends. My classmate Deniz is very funny. He is good at maths, and he helps me. Our maths teacher is Mrs. Demir. She is strict but fair. At break time, we are always together in the school garden. We talk, we laugh, and we eat our sandwiches. Sometimes we play simple games. School is not boring for me because my friends are nice. I am never alone, and I am happy here.

Sports and music are my hobbies. I am good at basketball, and I am in the school team. My teammates are great, and football is fun too. My favourite singer is Sezen Aksu, and her music is wonderful. Music is important for me, and my playlist is always full.

On the weekend, I have a lot of free time. I like books and films. My favourite film is a cartoon about a little cat. I watch it on Sunday. I read comics too. Comics are short and easy. Sometimes I draw pictures of Pamuk in my notebook. Drawing is my hobby, and it is relaxing. I also sing songs at home. My voice is not great, but it is fun! My brother laughs at me. My weekend is calm and happy. I am free, and I am never bored.

This is my phone. It is new and it is blue. It is my favourite thing. My friends are online in the evening, so we are never bored. We are happy on the internet together. Saturday is my favourite day because there is no school and I am free.

I use my phone every day. I have many apps on it. I chat with my friends, and I watch short videos. I take photos of my cat too. In the evening, my friends and I play a game online. It is a fun game, and I am good at it. We are a small team in the game. My favourite food is pizza. I eat pizza on Friday with my family. Water and orange juice are my favourite drinks. I am always happy when we eat together at home.

Every day is a little different for me. On school days, I am busy with lessons and homework. On free days, I am with my friends and my family. I like my simple life. My city is beautiful, and the sea is close to my home. In summer, we swim in the sea. In winter, we drink hot tea at home. I am a lucky girl. My family is kind, my friends are fun, and my school is good.

I am not a morning person, so mornings are hard for me! I am always tired at seven o'clock. But today I am really happy because it is Friday. My weekend is free, and it is time for fun. Are you a student too? Tell me about yourself!

Writing: All About Me

Kendini tanıt ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Introduce yourself; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI am fifteen and I am a student.
butamaI am good at football but not at tennis.
becauseçünküI am happy because it is Friday.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about yourself. (Kendin hakkında yaz.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • your name (isim)
  • your age (yaş)
  • your best friend or your class (en iyi arkadaşın ya da sınıfın)
  • one thing you are good at (iyi olduğun bir şey)
  • how you feel today (bugün nasıl hissettiğin)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Hello! My name is ..." "I am ... years old." "I am good at ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) Hello! My name is Mert and I am fifteen years old. I am from Ankara. This is my best friend, Can. We are in the same class, and we are good friends. I am good at football, but I am not good at maths. My favourite singer is Adele. Today I am happy because it is the weekend!
