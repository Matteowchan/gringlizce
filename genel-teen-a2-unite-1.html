Grammar: The Past Simple

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Past Simple?

Şimdiye kadar hep bugünden ve genel gerçeklerden konuştun. Now it is time to talk about the past. When you tell a friend what you did yesterday, where you went last weekend, or what happened on your holiday, you need a new tense: the Past Simple. Past Simple, geçmişte başlayıp biten olayları anlatır — bir kez oldu, tamamlandı ve kapandı.

  • Yesterday I played football with my friends.Dün arkadaşlarımla futbol oynadım.
  • Last week we visited my grandmother.Geçen hafta babaannemi ziyaret ettik.
  • In 2019 she moved to Istanbul.2019'da İstanbul'a taşındı.

Bu bölümde önce be fiilinin geçmişini (was / were) kısaca hatırlayacağız. Then we will study regular verbs (-ed) and irregular verbs, questions and negatives with did / didn't, short answers, and past time expressions. Adım adım gidelim.

1. Köprü: be Fiilinin Geçmişi (was / were)

A2'ye başlarken bunu zaten biliyorsun, so this is just a quick bridge. "Olmak" fiilinin geçmiş hâli iki şekildedir: was ve were. These describe states in the past — where somebody was, how they felt, what something was like.

ÖzneOlumluOlumsuzÖrnek
I / he / she / itwaswasn'tShe was tired yesterday.
you / we / theywereweren'tWe were at school.
Dikkat
  • be fiilinde did kullanılmaz: "Were you happy?" ✓ — "Did you be happy?" ✗
  • Ama diğer bütün fiillerde (go, work, eat...) geçmiş soru ve olumsuz için did / didn't gerekir. Bu farkı bu bölümde netleştireceğiz.

2. Düzenli Fiiller (Regular Verbs: -ed)

Çoğu fiil geçmişte -ed alır. This is the easy part: no new word to memorize, you just add an ending. Ama yazımda (spelling) dört küçük kural var. Bunları öğren, çünkü sınavda ve yazıda en çok burada hata yapılır.

KuralNasıl?Örnek
Çoğu fiil+ edwork → worked · watch → watched · play → played
-e ile biten+ dlive → lived · arrive → arrived · like → liked
Sessiz + y ile biteny → iedstudy → studied · try → tried · carry → carried
Kısa ünlü + tek sessizsessizi çiftle + edstop → stopped · plan → planned · shop → shopped
Küçük not
  • play, enjoy gibi fiillerde y'den önce sesli harf var, so we do NOT use -ied: played ✓, plaied ✗.
  • Telaffuz üç türlüdür (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) ama yazım her zaman -ed'dir; bu ünitede yazıma odaklan.

3. Düzensiz Fiiller (Irregular Verbs)

Some verbs do not follow the -ed rule. Bunların geçmiş hâli tamamen farklı bir kelimedir ve ezberlenmesi gerekir. İyi haber: en çok kullanılanlar bir avuç kadardır. Aşağıdaki listeyi iyi öğren — günlük konuşmanın çoğu bu fiillerle döner.

FiilGeçmiş

dodid

FiilGeçmiş

Nasıl çalışılır?
  • Bu fiilleri tek tek değil, cümle içinde öğren: "I went home", "She bought a car", "We had lunch."
  • Olumlu cümlede düzensiz formu kullan; ama did / didn't ile birlikte yalın hâle döner (aşağıda göreceğiz).

4. Soru ve Olumsuz (did / didn't)

Olumlu cümlede fiili değiştiririz. But for questions and negatives, we use a helper: did. Bütün öznelerde aynıdır — he/she/it için bile "did" (does değil). En önemli kural şu: did / didn't'ten sonra fiil yalın hâle döner.

TürYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
Olumluözne + geçmiş fiilShe went to Ankara.Ankara'ya gitti.
Olumsuzözne + didn't + yalın fiilShe didn't go to Ankara.Ankara'ya gitmedi.
SoruDid + özne + yalın fiilDid she go to Ankara?Ankara'ya gitti mi?
Altın kural
  • Geçmiş bilgisi zaten did'in içindedir, so the main verb goes back to base form: "Did you go?" ✓ — "Did you went?" ✗
  • Aynı şekilde: "He didn't know." ✓ — "He didn't knew." ✗

5. Kısa Cevaplar (Short Answers)

Bir Past Simple sorusuna kısa cevap vermek çok kolaydır, because you only need did or didn't — fiili tekrar etmezsin. Özne ne olursa olsun kalıp aynıdır.

SoruOlumluOlumsuz
Did you call her?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did he finish his homework?Yes, he did.No, he didn't.
Did they enjoy the trip?Yes, they did.No, they didn't.
Not
  • Cevapta ana fiili tekrar etme: "Yes, I called." yerine "Yes, I did." yeterlidir ve daha doğaldır.

6. Geçmiş Zaman İfadeleri (Past Time Expressions)

Past Simple genellikle bir zaman ifadesiyle birlikte gelir. These words tell the listener that the action is finished and in the past. En sık kullanılanlar şunlardır:

İfadeAnlamıÖrnek
yesterdaydünI saw them yesterday.
last night / week / yeardün gece / geçen hafta / geçen yılWe met last week.
... ago... önceHe left two hours ago.
in 2019 / in May2019'da / Mayıs'taShe started in 2019.
when I was younggençken / çocukkenWhen I was a child, I lived in Bursa.
Sıra kuralı
  • ago her zaman zaman diliminden sonra gelir: "a week ago", "three days ago" — "ago a week" ✗.
  • Zaman ifadesi cümlenin başında ya da sonunda olabilir: "Yesterday I worked late." = "I worked late yesterday."

7. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların Past Simple'da en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I goed home.I went home.go düzensizdir; "goed" yoktur.
Did you went home?Did you go home?did'ten sonra yalın fiil.
She didn't went.She didn't go.didn't'ten sonra yalın fiil.
He did not knew.He did not know.did not = didn't; sonrası yalın fiil.
We was late.We were late.you / we / they → were.
Yesterday I go to school.Yesterday I went to school."yesterday" varsa fiil geçmiş olmalı.
Two days ago I have called.Two days ago I called.Belirli geçmiş zamanla Past Simple kullanılır.

8. Uygulama: Pazartesi Sabahı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about their weekend on Monday morning. Koyu yazılan geçmiş zaman yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — Good morning, Selin! How was your weekend?Günaydın Selin! Hafta sonun nasıldı?
  • — It was great, thanks! On Saturday I visited my parents and we had a big lunch.Harikaydı, sağ ol! Cumartesi ailemi ziyaret ettim ve büyük bir öğle yemeği yedik.
  • — Nice! Did you go out on Sunday?Güzel! Pazar dışarı çıktın mı?
  • — No, I didn't. I stayed at home and watched two films. What about you?Hayır, çıkmadım. Evde kaldım ve iki film izledim. Ya sen?
  • — I went to a concert with my sister. We bought the tickets a month ago.Kız kardeşimle bir konsere gittim. Biletleri bir ay önce almıştık.
  • — Did you enjoy it?Beğendin mi?
  • — Yes, I did! The music was amazing, but we didn't get home until midnight.Evet, çok! Müzik muhteşemdi ama gece yarısına kadar eve varamadık.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane did sorusu var? (Cevap: 3)
  • "We had a big lunch" cümlesindeki fiilin yalın hâli nedir? (Cevap: have)
  • "We bought the tickets a month ago." — neden "ago" kullanıldı, cümledeki yeri nerede? (Bölüm 6'ya bak.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • Think about YOUR last busy week. What did you do? (Son yoğun haftanı düşün. Neler yaptın?)
  • The story is about a past week. Which tense will you see a lot? (Metin geçmiş bir haftayı anlatıyor. Hangi zamanı çok göreceksin?)
  • Look at the title. Is this a happy story or a sad story? Guess! (Başlığa bak. Mutlu mu üzücü mü bir hikâye? Tahmin et!)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. Fiillerin geçmiş hâllerine (went, made, came...) dikkat et.

Okulda geçen hafta: yoğun ama güzel bir hafta.
A Busy Week at School

Selin is fifteen years old, and she goes to a big school in Izmir. She is in class 9-B, and she has many good friends there. Selin loves science, but she also likes basketball and drawing. Every day after school, she plays basketball with her friends for one hour. On weekends, she draws pictures of animals and plants in her notebook. This year, her class had a special science project, and Selin was very happy about it.

The project was about plants and clean water. Mr. Demir, the science teacher, put the students into small teams. Selin was the team leader, and her team had four people: Selin, Ali, Merve, and Mark. They were all good friends, and they sat together in class. The team had only one week to finish the project. It was not a lot of time, so everybody was a little nervous. But they were also excited, because the project was interesting.

Last week was very busy at school. On Monday morning, Selin arrived early because she had an important project. Her teacher, Mr. Demir, wanted a great science project, because a guest judge from Ankara was coming on Friday. Selin worked hard all day, so she didn't take a long break at lunch.

In the morning, Selin opened her books and started to plan. She wrote a list of jobs for her team on a big sheet of paper. Then she drew a small picture of a plant in a glass of water. At lunch, she only ate a quick sandwich, and then she went back to work. Her friends helped her, and they carried some boxes and plants to the classroom. Selin was tired, but she felt happy because the plan looked good.

On Tuesday, two classmates, Ali and Merve, travelled to Ankara for a short science workshop. They met the other students at ten o'clock and showed them the first plan. The students liked it, but they asked for a few changes. Ali called Selin and told her about the new ideas.

At the workshop in Ankara, Ali and Merve learned many new things. A young scientist showed them how plants drink water through their roots. She also gave them some useful books and photos. Ali took a lot of notes, and Merve took photos with her phone. After lunch, they practised a short talk in front of the other students. In the evening, they came back home by bus, and they were very tired but happy.

On Wednesday and Thursday, the whole team worked together. Selin made the new poster, Mark wrote the report, and Merve drew the diagrams. They didn't finish early — on Thursday they stayed until six o'clock. Everybody was tired, but nobody complained because the project was exciting.

They worked as a good team, and everybody had a job. Selin cut the paper and put the pictures on the poster. Mark checked all the words in the report, because he was good at English. Merve used bright colours for the diagrams, so they looked clear and nice. Sometimes they took a short break and ate biscuits together. They told funny jokes and laughed, and this made the hard work easier. Selin's mother also helped them, and she made some sandwiches for the team.

On Friday, the guest judge came to the school. Before he arrived, the team cleaned the classroom and bought some snacks and drinks. When the judge saw the final project, he smiled. 'This is excellent,' he said. 'Well done, everyone.'

During the visit, the team showed their poster and their report to the judge. Selin talked about the plants, and Merve pointed to the colourful diagrams. Ali answered some questions, and Mark read a short part of the report. The other students in the class watched quietly and clapped at the end. The judge asked two easy questions, and the team answered them well. Mr. Demir looked at his students and smiled, because he was very proud of them.

After the visit, the whole team went to a small café near the school. They ate pizza, talked about the week, and laughed a lot. It was a hard week, but it was also a good one. Selin learned a lot, and she felt proud of her class.

On Saturday, Selin slept late because she was very tired after the busy week. In the afternoon, she called Merve, and they talked about the project on the phone. 'We were a great team,' Merve said, and Selin agreed with her. On Sunday, Selin's family went to the park together. She played football with her little brother and ate ice cream in the sun. She thought about the week at school again, and she smiled. Now Selin knows that hard work and good friends can make a difficult week feel special.

Bağlaçlar Yazıyı Cümleden Ayırır (Connectors)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. What turns a list of sentences into a real text is connectors (bağlaçlar): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A2 seviyesinde A1'in bağlaçlarına üç yeni zaman bağlacı ekliyoruz: when, after, before. These are perfect for telling a story about the past.

In the afternoon, I visited my grandparents. They cooked a big lunch, and we talked about the past. In the evening, I watched a film at home, but I didn't finish it because I fell asleep on the sofa.

On Sunday, I cleaned my room and studied English. I didn't travel anywhere, but I enjoyed my quiet weekend a lot.
