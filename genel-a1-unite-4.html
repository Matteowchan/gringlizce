Grammar: Present Simple (he / she / it) + Sıklık Zarfları

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Bu Konu?

Ünite 3'te kendi rutinini anlattın: "I check my emails." Peki ya başkalarının işini anlatmak istersen? "Yöneticim her sabah toplantı yapar", "Muhasebecimiz faturaları hazırlar" gibi cümleler iş hayatının her günü kullanılan cümleleridir ve hepsinde özne he / she / it'tir:

  • "My manager starts the day with a meeting."Yöneticim güne bir toplantıyla başlar.
  • "She works in the finance department."O, finans departmanında çalışır.
  • "Our accountant prepares the invoices."Muhasebecimiz faturaları hazırlar.
  • "The company opens at nine."Şirket dokuzda açılır.

Dikkat ettin mi? Fiillerin hepsinin sonunda -s var: starts, works, prepares, opens. İşte kural bu kadar basit ama bir o kadar da kritik: özne he / she / it ise fiil -s takısı alır. Bu küçük -s, Türk öğrencilerin İngilizcede en sık unuttuğu şeydir — "She work" dediğin anda cümle bozulur. Bu bölümde -s kuralını, olumsuz ve soru hâllerini ve "her zaman / genellikle / asla" gibi sıklık zarflarını adım adım işleyeceğiz.

1. -s / -es / -ies Yazım Kuralları (Spelling)

He / she / it öznesiyle fiile -s eklerken yazım dört gruba ayrılır. Kuralı bilirsen hiç ezber yapmana gerek kalmaz:

KuralFiillerÖrnek
Çoğu fiil: sadece +swork → works · check → checks · prepare → preparesShe works in sales.
Sonu -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o ile bitenler: +eswatch → watches · finish → finishes · go → goes · do → doesHe goes to work at eight.
Sessiz harf + y: y düşer, +iesstudy → studies · carry → carriesShe studies English at night.
Sesli harf + y: sadece +splay → plays · stay → staysHe plays football on Sundays.
İstisna:have → hasShe has a meeting at nine.
Dikkat
  • have fiilinin -s hâli "haves" değildir; tamamen değişir: has. "He has a big desk."
  • "goes" ve "does" yazarken +es aldığını unutma: "gos" ✗, "dos" ✗.
  • -s kuralı yalnızca he / she / it için geçerlidir. I / you / we / they yalın kalır: "They work." — "She works."

2. Olumsuz Cümle (Negative: doesn't)

He / she / it için olumsuzluk doesn't (does not) ile yapılır. Kritik kural: doesn't'tan sonra fiil YALIN hâline döner — -s'i doesn't zaten taşır, fiile ikinci kez eklenmez:

OlumluOlumsuzTürkçesi
She works on Fridays.She doesn't work on Fridays.Cuma günleri çalışmaz.
He travels a lot.He doesn't travel a lot.Çok seyahat etmez.
She has a meeting today.She doesn't have a meeting today.Bugün toplantısı yok.
The office opens at eight.The office doesn't open at eight.Ofis sekizde açılmaz.
En kritik kural
  • "She doesn't works." ✗ → "She doesn't work." ✓ — doesn't varsa fiil daima yalın!
  • "has" da yalına döner: "He doesn't has..." ✗ → "He doesn't have..." ✓
  • Diğer öznelerde (I / you / we / they) olumsuzluk don't iledir: "They don't work on Sundays."

3. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Does he...? — Yes, he does.)

He / she / it için soru Does ile başlar ve fiil yine YALIN kalır. Kısa cevaplar da does / doesn't ile verilir:

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Does he work in sales?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Does she prepare the reports?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Does the office open at nine?Yes, it does.No, it doesn't.
Önemli
  • Soruda fiile -s eklenmez: "Does she works?" ✗ → "Does she work?" ✓ — -s'i "Does" zaten taşıyor.
  • Kısa cevapta fiil tekrar edilmez: "Yes, he works." yerine kibar ve doğal olan "Yes, he does."
  • Soru kelimeleriyle aynı sıra korunur: "What does she do?" (Ne iş yapar?) · "Where does he work?" (Nerede çalışır?)

4. Sıklık Zarfları (Adverbs of Frequency)

Bir işin ne sıklıkla yapıldığını söylemek için sıklık zarfları kullanılır. Beş temel zarfı yüzdeleriyle birlikte öğren:

ZarfSıklıkTürkçesiÖrnek
always%100her zamanShe always answers the phone.
usually~%80genellikleHe usually works from the office.
often~%60sık sıkMark often travels for work.
sometimes~%30bazenShe sometimes works from home.
never%0asla, hiçHe is never late.

Zarfın cümledeki YERİ tek kuralla belirlenir — bu kuralı ezberle:

KuralÖrnekTürkçesi
be fiilinden SONRAHe is always early.O her zaman erkencidir.
Normal fiilden ÖNCEShe usually arrives at nine.Genellikle dokuzda gelir.
How often...?
  • Sıklığı sormak için How often...? (Ne sıklıkla?) kullanılır: "How often does he travel?" — "Every month." / "He often travels."
  • "He always is late." ✗ → "He is always late." ✓ — zarf be'nin önüne geçmez.
  • "never" zaten olumsuzdur; doesn't ile birlikte kullanılmaz: "She never doesn't help." ✗ → "She never helps." ✓ (Bkz: Yaygın Hatalar)

5. İş ve Departman Anlatma Kalıpları (Talking About Jobs)

Bir kişinin işini anlatırken en çok bu üç kalıbı kullanacaksın. Üçünü de -s kuralıyla birlikte otomatikleştir:

KalıpÖrnekTürkçesi
works in the ... departmentHe works in the sales department.O, satış departmanında çalışır.
is responsible for ...She is responsible for reports.O, raporlardan sorumludur.
Özne + fiil-s + nesneOur accountant prepares the invoices.Muhasebecimiz faturaları hazırlar.
Kalıp notları
  • Departman söylerken in kullanılır: "in the marketing department", kısaca "in marketing" da denir.
  • "What does she do?" sorusunun iki tip cevabı vardır: meslek ("She is a designer." — a/an unutma!) veya görev ("She designs the screens.").
  • "is responsible for" kalıbında be kullanıldığı için zarf be'den sonra gelir: "He is always responsible for the plan."

7. Uygulama: Yeni Çalışana Departman Turu (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Deniz, yeni çalışana ekibi tanıtıyor. Diyalog bu bölümde öğrendiğin yapıların tamamını kullanıyor — koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Deniz, what does Merve do?Deniz, Merve ne iş yapıyor?
  • — She's an engineer. She develops the app, and she usually works from the office.O bir mühendis. Uygulamayı geliştiriyor ve genellikle ofisten çalışıyor.
  • — And Selin? Does she develop the app too?Peki Selin? O da mı uygulamayı geliştiriyor?
  • — No, she doesn't. She's the designer, so she designs the screens.Hayır. O tasarımcı, bu yüzden ekranları tasarlıyor.
  • — I see. Does Mark work in the engineering department?Anladım. Mark mühendislik departmanında mı çalışıyor?
  • — No, he doesn't. He works in the sales department. He often travels for work.Hayır. Satış departmanında çalışıyor. İş için sık sık seyahat eder.
  • — How often does he travel?Ne sıklıkla seyahat ediyor?
  • — Every month. And Zeynep prepares the invoices in finance. She is never late!Her ay. Zeynep de finansta faturaları hazırlıyor. Asla geç kalmaz!
  • — Great! Our team always helps new people, so please ask your questions.Harika! Ekibimiz yeni gelenlere her zaman yardım eder, o yüzden sorularını çekinmeden sor.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane "Does...?" sorusu var, bulabilir misin? (Cevap: 2)
  • "She usually works from the office." — zarf fiilin önünde mi, arkasında mı? (Önünde: usually + works. Normal fiil kuralı.)
  • "She is never late." — burada zarf neden fiilden önce değil? (Çünkü fiil "be"; zarf be'den SONRA gelir.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them in your own sentences. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

department (n)
a part of a company (departman)
Syn: division (bölüm) · Ant: whole company (tüm şirket)
"He works in the sales department."
sales (n)
selling products to customers (satış)
Syn: selling (satma) · Ant: purchasing (satın alma)
"The sales team talks to customers."
marketing (n)
telling people about products (pazarlama)
Syn: promotion (tanıtım) · Ant: production (üretim)
"She makes the ads in marketing."
finance (n)
the money work of a company (finans)
Syn: money matters (para işleri) · Ant: sales work (satış işleri)
"Zeynep works in finance."
human resources (n)
the department for employees (insan kaynakları)
Syn: HR / personnel (personel) · Ant: customers (müşteriler)
"Human resources hires new people."
engineer (n)
a person who builds technical things (mühendis)
Syn: developer (geliştirici) · Ant: user (kullanıcı)
"Merve is an engineer. She develops the app."
accountant (n)
a person who works with money and numbers (muhasebeci)
Syn: bookkeeper (muhasebe görevlisi) · Ant: spender (para harcayan)
"Our accountant prepares the invoices."
designer (n)
a person who plans how things look (tasarımcı)
Syn: visual artist (görsel sanatçı) · Ant: copier (kopyalayan)
"Selin is the designer. She designs the screens."
receptionist (n)
the person at the front desk (resepsiyonist)
Syn: front-desk clerk (ön masa görevlisi) · Ant: guest (misafir)
"The receptionist answers the phones."
responsible (adj)
it is your job to do it (sorumlu)
Syn: in charge (görevli) · Ant: uninvolved (ilgisiz)
"She is responsible for the reports."

Syn: make ready (hazır etmek) · Ant: cancel (iptal etmek)
"He prepares the sales plan every week."

Syn: document (belge) · Ant: chat (sohbet)
"She sends the report on Mondays."

Baytek binası: her katta bir departman.
Who Does What at Baytek?

Baytek is a software company in Istanbul, and it has five departments: engineering, design, finance, sales and human resources. Who does what? Let's meet the team.

The Baytek office is not very big, but it is nice. The building has five floors. Every floor is for one department. The engineering team is on the first floor, and the design desk is there too. The finance department is on the second floor. The sales team is on the third floor. The manager's office is on the top floor. From his window, Mr. Demir can see the city.

What does Baytek make? It makes a mobile app. The app helps people find a good doctor. Many people in Turkey use it every day. The engineers write the code for the app. The designers make it beautiful. The sales team sells it to hospitals and clinics. So every department is important, and every person helps the app grow.

Mr. Demir is the manager, and he is responsible for the whole company. Every morning, he has a short meeting with the team, and he always starts at nine o'clock. He knows every employee's name because he talks to everyone every day.

Mr. Demir is a kind manager. He is not angry, and he is not loud. He listens to his team. When there is a problem, he helps. He wants every person to feel happy at work. On Fridays, he brings cake for the team, so everyone likes this day.

A normal day at Baytek is busy but happy. The office opens at half past eight. Most people arrive before nine o'clock. They say "Good morning" to each other. They make coffee or tea in the kitchen. Then they go to their desks and start work. The office is quiet in the morning because everyone is busy.

Ali and Merve work in the engineering department. They are engineers. Merve develops the app, and she usually works from the office. Ali often helps her, but he sometimes works from home. Selin is the designer, so she designs the screens of the app. Her colleagues love her work.

Merve comes to the office early because she likes the quiet morning. She writes code on her computer for many hours. Ali is a little different. He works from home two days a week. On these days, he calls the team on the computer. Selin sits near the window. She draws the app on a big screen, and she uses many colours. Her screens are simple and clean, so the users love the app.

Zeynep works in the finance department. She is an accountant, and she prepares the invoices and the money reports. She is never late because she loves her job — and her numbers!

Zeynep is calm and careful. Numbers are her friends. She checks every invoice two times because she does not want mistakes. Her desk is very tidy. She has a small plant and a photo of her cat. She drinks green tea, not coffee. At the end of the month, she is very busy, but she is always ready.

Mark works in the sales department. He talks to customers on the phone, and he travels to Ankara every month because Baytek has big customers there. At the front desk, Elif is the receptionist. She answers the phones and welcomes the guests. She is always friendly, so the guests feel happy at Baytek.

Mark is friendly and loud. He talks a lot because his job is sales. He has many customers, and he knows all their names. Sometimes he is not in the office because he is on a trip. Elif sits at the front desk near the door. She is the first person you see at Baytek. When the phone rings, she answers fast. When a guest arrives, she brings water or coffee.

The departments are not alone. They work together every day. When Mark gets a new customer, he tells the engineers. When Merve finds a problem in the code, she tells Selin. When Zeynep needs a number, she asks Mark. Good teamwork is the secret of Baytek, and nobody works alone here.

At one o'clock, it is lunch time. Some people eat at their desks, but many people go out together. There is a small café next to the building. The food is cheap and good. After lunch, the team drinks coffee and talks. They talk about work, films and football. This break is short, but it is important because it makes the team happy.

Every person has a laptop and a big screen. The internet is fast. In the kitchen, there is a coffee machine, a water machine and a small fridge. On the wall, there is a big board with many colourful notes. These notes show the plan for the week, so the team looks at the board every morning.

The week at Baytek is not the same every day. On Monday, there is a big meeting for all departments. On Wednesday, the design team shows new ideas. On Friday, the office is calm, and some people leave early. The office closes at six o'clock, and the last person turns off the lights.

After work, some people go home fast because they have families. Others stay for a short time and talk. On the first Friday of the month, the team goes to a restaurant together. They eat, laugh and tell stories. They are colleagues, but they are also friends.

Baytek is a happy place, but it is also a busy place. There is always a lot of work. The phone rings, the emails arrive, and the meetings start. But the team is strong. When one person is tired, another person helps. When there is good news, everyone is happy. This is why people like their job at Baytek.

Baytek is a young company, but it grows fast. Today there are more than twenty people. Every month, the team is bigger. Soon the company needs a bigger office with more floors. Mr. Demir is very happy because his small idea is now a real company. He always says one thing to the team: "A good team makes a good company."

Now, back to the human resources department. Aylin works there. She is friendly and organised, and she reads many emails every day. People from all over the country want a job at Baytek. Aylin talks to them on the phone or on the computer. She looks for good and kind people, not only clever people, because teamwork is important here.

And the human resources department? It hires new employees for every team. Maybe you are next!

Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (From Sentences to a Text)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. Bir yazıyı cümle yığınından ayıran şey bağlaçlardır (connectors): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A1 seviyesindeki beş bağlacı bu ünitede kişileri ve işlerini anlatırken kullanacaksın:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparHe works in sales and he travels a lot.
butamaZıtlık gösterirShe is busy, but she always helps.
orveyaSeçenek sunarDoes he work in finance or in sales?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirShe is never late, so the manager is happy.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirHe travels every month because our customers are in Ankara.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
Mark works in sales. He talks to customers. He travels a lot. He is friendly. Mark works in sales and talks to customers. He often travels because our customers are in Ankara, so he is not always at the office.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirlerini bağla.
  • "so" ve "but" öncesinde genellikle virgül kullanılır: "She is busy, but she always helps."

Sample Answer (~160 words) Hi Alex,

Welcome to Baytek! Here is some information about three people in our team, so your first week is easy.

First, Mr. Demir is our manager. He is responsible for the whole company. He always starts the day with a short meeting, and he usually asks about our work. He is friendly, but he doesn't like late reports!

Second, Zeynep works in the finance department. She is our accountant. She prepares the invoices and the money reports, and she never makes mistakes. When you have a question about money, she always helps.

Finally, Mark works in the sales department. He talks to customers every day, and he often travels to Ankara because our big customers are there. He knows everyone, so ask him your questions.

I hope this helps! My desk is near the window, and I am usually at the office before nine.

Best regards,
Deniz
