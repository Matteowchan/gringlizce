C1 Final — Tüm Üniteler (1-19) (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final, C1 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: ileri zaman ve görünüş; ileri modal anlam ve modal perfect'ler (needn't have, must have); devrik yapılar ve devrik koşullar (Not only..., Had I known...); ileri ve karışık koşullar (but for, were I to); vurgu yapıları (cleft & emphatic); ileri ortaç cümlecikleri (having been done); isimleştirme (nominalisation); ileri edilgen ve rapor yapıları (is believed to have...); eksiltme ve yerine koyma (ellipsis & substitution); ileri ilgi cümlecikleri (few of whom); ileri ödün ve zıtlık (much as, whereas); karmaşık ad öbekleri; akademik ihtiyat (hedging); bağdaşıklık ve söylem belirleyicileri; emir kipi ve kalıp ifadeler (the subjunctive); eşdizim ve deyimsel doğruluk; kayıt ve üslup denetimi. Beş bölümde dil bilgisi, İngilizce kullanımı, okuma, dinleme ve yazma test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

C1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/7

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) İki akademik metin: Text 1 çoklu görev (multitasking) üzerine, Text 2 kentsel yeşil altyapı üzerinedir. (Two academic texts: multitasking and urban green infrastructure.)

Text 1 · The Limits of Attention

It has become fashionable to praise multitasking as a hallmark of the modern professional, yet a growing body of research suggests that the human brain is remarkably ill-suited to it. What we casually call multitasking is, in most cases, rapid task-switching: the mind disengages from one activity and reattaches itself to another, incurring a small but measurable cost each time. These switching costs, though negligible in isolation, accumulate over a working day into a significant drain on both speed and accuracy.

Researchers have found that individuals who consider themselves proficient multitaskers tend, paradoxically, to perform worst on tests of divided attention. Confidence, it appears, is a poor guide to competence. Far from training the brain to handle several streams of information at once, habitual multitasking may erode the very capacity for sustained concentration on which demanding work depends.

The implications reach well beyond the office. If attention is indeed a finite resource, then the constant interruptions of digital life may be reshaping not merely how we work, but how deeply we are able to think at all.

Text 2 · Rethinking the Concrete City

For much of the twentieth century, urban planning treated nature as an ornament — something to be added, in the form of tidy parks, once the essential business of building had been completed. That assumption is now being overturned. A new discipline, often labelled green infrastructure, argues that vegetation is not decorative but functional, performing tasks that would otherwise fall to expensive engineering.

Trees, for instance, are increasingly valued for their ability to absorb rainwater that would otherwise overwhelm urban drains. Networks of planting can lower city temperatures during heatwaves, reducing the demand for energy-hungry air conditioning. Wetlands, once drained without a second thought, are being restored precisely because they filter pollutants and cushion the impact of floods.

Critics caution that such schemes are sometimes promoted more for their public image than for measurable benefit, and that maintenance is frequently underfunded once the initial enthusiasm has faded. Yet the underlying shift is difficult to dispute. Where nature was formerly regarded as the opposite of the city, it is now being recognised as one of its most valuable assets.

C1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/7

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 13 min, Task 2: 22 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 13 dk, Görev 2: 22 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Formal Email: A Course That Was Not as Described (130-170 words)

You recently paid for a professional online course, but it did not match the description you were given when you enrolled. Write a formal email to the course provider. Explain what was promised and what actually happened, describe the impact this has had on you, and state clearly what action you expect them to take. (Kısa süre önce mesleki bir çevrimiçi kursa ücret ödedin, ama kurs kayıt sırasında sana verilen açıklamayla örtüşmedi. Kurs sağlayıcısına resmi bir e-posta yaz: ne vaat edildiğini ve gerçekte ne olduğunu açıkla, bunun seni nasıl etkilediğini anlat ve ne yapmalarını beklediğini net şekilde belirt.)

  • Use a formal opening and closing: "Dear Sir / Madam," ... "Yours faithfully," / "Yours sincerely,". (resmi açılış ve kapanış)
  • Use at least one passive / reporting structure: "I was assured that...", "It was stated that the course would...". (edilgen / rapor yapısı)
  • Include reported speech: "The website claimed that...". (dolaylı anlatım)
  • Make a firm but polite request with would / could: "I would therefore be grateful if you could...". (kesin ama kibar rica)
  • 130-170 words, formal register throughout. (130-170 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Dear Sir / Madam," + yazma sebebin (I am writing to complain about / regarding...).
  • Gövde: vaat edilen (passive + reported speech) → gerçekte olan → etkisi → beklediğin işlem (would/could + refund/repeat).
  • Kapanış: "I look forward to your prompt response." + "Yours faithfully," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Argument Essay: Space Exploration vs Problems on Earth (200-260 words)

Some people argue that governments should invest heavily in space exploration, while others believe the money would be better spent solving problems here on Earth. Discuss both views and give your own opinion, supporting it with clear reasons and examples. (Bazıları hükümetlerin uzay araştırmalarına büyük yatırım yapması gerektiğini savunurken, bazıları bu paranın Dünya'daki sorunları çözmeye harcanmasının daha iyi olacağına inanıyor. Her iki görüşü tartış ve kendi görüşünü net gerekçeler ve örneklerle destekle.)

  • Discuss both views before giving your own opinion. (kendi görüşünden önce iki görüşü de tartış)
  • Use at least three concession/contrast linkers: whereas, despite, nevertheless, although, however. (en az üç zıtlık bağlacı)
  • Include one conditional (Third or Mixed): "If governments had invested more in..., we would...". (bir koşul cümlesi)
  • Use passive and reporting structures (it is argued that..., is believed to be...). (edilgen ve rapor yapıları)
  • 200-260 words, organised into clear paragraphs. (200-260 kelime)
0 kelime
