Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-8 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-8'de öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: Present Perfect ve Past Simple farkı (for/since, been/gone); Present Perfect Continuous; Past Perfect; gelecek yapıları (will / going to / Present Continuous); Second Conditional; Passive Voice; Reported Speech ve aktarılan sorular/emirler. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Konular okul, arkadaşlar, spor, hobiler ve aile etrafında döner. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · The Students Who Built a Radio Station

Two years ago, nobody at Maple Hill School had ever made a radio programme. Today, the school has its own radio station, and it is run entirely by students. The idea began with a fifteen-year-old girl called Lara, who loved music and wanted to share it with her friends.

At first, the teachers weren't sure. They thought the project would take too much time, and they worried that students would forget their normal lessons. However, Lara had already prepared a clear plan. She showed the head teacher how the station could be used for school news, interviews and even language practice. After she had explained everything, the teachers finally agreed.

The first months were difficult. The old computer in the music room broke twice, and the microphone was so quiet that nobody could hear the presenters. Lara and her team have solved most of these problems since then. New equipment was bought with money from a school fair, and a local engineer taught the students how to record clean sound.

Today the station broadcasts every Friday afternoon. Studies by the teachers have shown that students who join the club speak more confidently in class, not less. Some shy students, who never spoke in lessons before, now present their own shows. The programmes are listened to by hundreds of families at home.

Lara believes the future will be even brighter. 'If every school had a station like ours,' she says, 'students would enjoy learning much more. A microphone is not just a toy — it is a way to find your voice.'

Text 2 · The Trip That Almost Didn't Happen

Last spring, our class won a competition, and the prize was a three-day trip to the mountains. We had wanted to go there for years, so everyone was excited. But two weeks before the trip, we almost lost our chance.

The problem was money. The school had promised to pay for the bus, but the company suddenly raised its prices. Our teacher, Mr. Aydın, told us that the trip might be cancelled. We were very disappointed, because we had already packed our bags in our heads.

Then my friend Zeynep had a clever idea. 'If we organised a small market,' she said, 'we could sell cakes and old books to raise the money ourselves.' At first some students laughed, but soon the whole class was baking. The market was held in the school garden on a sunny Saturday, and hundreds of parents came.

To everyone's surprise, we raised enough money in a single afternoon. The extra cash was given to the school library as a gift. A week later, the bus arrived, and we finally travelled to the mountains. The air was cold, but the view was amazing.

On the last night, we sat around a fire and talked about the market. Mr. Aydın smiled and said, 'You have learnt something more important than any lesson. When a group works together, almost nothing is impossible.'

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 12 min, Task 2: 23 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 12 dk, Görev 2: 23 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: Some Good News (100-130 words)

Recently something good happened to you: you passed an important exam, made the school sports team, won a competition, or learned a new skill. Write an email to a friend and share your news. (Yakın zamanda güzel bir şey oldu: önemli bir sınavı geçtin, okul takımına girdin, bir yarışma kazandın ya da yeni bir beceri öğrendin. Bir arkadaşına e-posta yazıp haberini paylaş.)

  • Use Present Perfect for your news ("I have just passed..."). (haber için Present Perfect)
  • Use Past Simple for the details — when, where, how. (ayrıntılar için Past Simple)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: because, so, but, and, after. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 100-130 words. (100-130 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Ela," + haber cümlesi ("I have some great news! I have just...").
  • Gövde: ne oldu, nasıl oldu? — Present Perfect (haber) + Past Simple (ayrıntı) + because/so.
  • Kapanış: "Let's meet soon and celebrate!" / "Take care," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: Homework Every Day? (140-170 words)

Some students think schools should give homework every day. Others disagree. What is your opinion? Give reasons and examples. (Bazı öğrenciler okulların her gün ödev vermesi gerektiğini düşünür; bazıları katılmaz. Senin görüşün ne? Sebep ve örnek ver.)

  • State your opinion clearly at the start. (görüşünü baştan net belirt)
  • Use a Second Conditional sentence ("If we had less homework, ..."). (bir Second Conditional cümlesi)
  • Use at least 4 connectors: however, because, so, although, for example. (en az 4 bağlaç)
  • 140-170 words. (140-170 kelime)
