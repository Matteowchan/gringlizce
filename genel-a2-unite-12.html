Grammar: Relative Clauses

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Relative Clauses?

Bir kişiyi ya da nesneyi tanıtmak istediğinde çoğu zaman iki cümle kurarsın: "I have a friend. She lives in Paris." Sounds a little broken, right? İyi bir İngilizcede bu iki cümleyi tek cümlede birleştiririz: "I have a friend who lives in Paris." İşte bu koyu kısma relative clause (ilgi cümleciği) denir — bir ismi tanımlayan, açıklayan küçük bir cümledir.

  • The man who lives next door is a doctor.Yan komşuda oturan adam bir doktor.
  • This is the phone which I bought last week.Bu, geçen hafta satın aldığım telefon.
  • Baytek is a company that designs furniture.Baytek, mobilya tasarlayan bir şirket.
  • That is the café where we first met.Şurası ilk tanıştığımız kafe.

Bu bölümde dört kelimeyi öğreneceğiz: who (kişiler için), which (nesneler için), that (her ikisi için) ve where (yerler için). Then we will learn how to join two sentences into one and how to describe people, things and places clearly. Adım adım gidelim.

1. Dört Anahtar Kelime (who / which / that / where)

Relative clause bir ilgi zamiriyle başlar. Which word you choose depends on the noun you describe — bir kişi mi, bir nesne mi, yoksa bir yer mi tanımlıyorsun? Aşağıdaki tablo bütün mantığı özetliyor:

KelimeNe için?Türkçe mantığıÖrnek
whokişiler (people)-en / -an kişithe woman who works here
whichnesneler / hayvanlar (things)-en / -an şeythe book which I read
thatkişiler ve nesneler (both)her ikisi de olurthe man that called · the car that broke
whereyerler (places)-diği yerthe city where I was born
Kolay hatırlatma
  • who içinde "w-h-o" var — hep bir insanı düşün: "Who is that?" = "O kim?"
  • which nesneler içindir; where ise "here / there" gibi bir yer bildirir.
  • Emin değilsen kişide de nesnede de that kullanabilirsin — güvenli seçimdir.

2. Tanımlayan İlgi Cümleleri (Defining Relative Clauses)

A defining relative clause tells us which person or thing we mean. Yani cümleden çıkarırsan, kimden ya da neden bahsettiğimizi anlayamayız — bu yüzden "tanımlayan" denir; bilgi zorunludur. Yapı çok basittir:

Yapı
  • isim + (who / which / that / where) + fiil...
  • the girl + who + sings = the girl who sings (şarkı söyleyen kız)
  • A doctor is a person who helps sick people.Doktor, hasta insanlara yardım eden kişidir.
  • A dictionary is a book which explains words.Sözlük, kelimeleri açıklayan bir kitaptır.
  • A kitchen is a room where we cook food.Mutfak, yemek pişirdiğimiz odadır.
Dikkat
  • Tanımlayan ilgi cümlesinde virgül kullanmayız: "The man who called was angry." ✓ (virgülsüz)
  • İlgi zamiri (who/which/that) tanımladığı ismin hemen ardından gelir.

3. İki Cümleyi Birleştirmek (Joining Two Sentences)

Relative clause'un en güçlü işi budur: iki kısa cümleyi tek, akıcı bir cümle yapmak. The trick: iki cümlede ortak olan ismi bul; ikinci cümledeki tekrar eden kelimeyi (he, she, it...) at ve yerine who / which / where koy.

İki cümleBirleşmiş cümle
I have a friend. She lives in Paris. I have a friend who lives in Paris.
This is the pen. I use it every day. This is the pen which I use every day.
That is the school. I studied there. That is the school where I studied.
Nasıl yapılır — 3 adım
  • 1. Ortak ismi bul (friend, pen, school).
  • 2. İkinci cümledeki tekrarı sil (she, it, there).
  • 3. Yerine doğru kelimeyi koy: kişi → who, nesne → which/that, yer → where.

4. that: Güvenli Seçim (that for both)

Konuşma dilinde en sık duyacağın kelime that'tir. In everyday English, that can replace who (for people) and which (for things). Yani emin değilsen "that" de, çoğu zaman doğru olur.

who / which ilethat ile (aynı anlam)
the man who called methe man that called me
the car which broke downthe car that broke down
the people who live herethe people that live here
Küçük not
  • where yerine "that" kullanılmaz: "the place where I work" ✓ — "the place that I work" genelde ✗ (yer için where kullan).
  • that hem kişi hem nesne için çalışır; ama who sadece kişi, which sadece nesne içindir.

5. where: Yerleri Tanımlamak (Describing Places)

Bir yeri tanımlarken (bir şehir, oda, bina, kafe...) where kullanırız. It means "in that place" — o yerde bir şey olur. Türkçedeki "-diği yer" mantığına çok benzer.

  • A library is a place where you read books.Kütüphane, kitap okuduğun yerdir.
  • This is the office where I work.Burası çalıştığım ofis.
  • Bursa is the city where I was born.Bursa, doğduğum şehir.
Karıştırma
  • Kişi → who · Nesne → which · Yer → where. "the town who..." ✗ — "the town where..." ✓

6. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların ilgi cümleciklerinde en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
The man which lives here...The man who lives here...Kişi için who (which değil).
A doctor is a person which helps you.A doctor is a person who helps you.person = kişi → who.
The book who I read was good.The book which/that I read was good.Nesne için which/that (who değil).
This is the city who I live.This is the city where I live.Yer için where.
The girl who she sings is my sister.The girl who sings is my sister.who zaten özne; "she"yi tekrar etme.
The phone what I bought is fast.The phone which/that I bought is fast."what" ilgi cümleciğinde kullanılmaz.

7. Uygulama: Yeni Ofis (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two colleagues talk about the new office and the people in it. Koyu yazılan ilgi cümleciklerine dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — Is this the office where you work now?Burası şimdi çalıştığın ofis mi?
  • — Yes! And that is Selin, the designer who made the chair that won a prize.Evet! Şu da Selin, ödül kazanan sandalyeyi tasarlayan tasarımcı.
  • — Nice! Who is the man who sits near the window?Güzel! Pencerenin yanında oturan adam kim?
  • — That's Ali, an engineer who checks every product which leaves the factory.O Ali, fabrikadan çıkan her ürünü kontrol eden bir mühendis.
  • — And the room where you have meetings?Peki toplantı yaptığınız oda?
  • — It's the bright room that has the big table. It's the place where we share all our ideas.Büyük masası olan aydınlık oda. Bütün fikirlerimizi paylaştığımız yer orası.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane where var? (Cevap: 3)
  • "the designer who made the chair" — burada who neden kullanıldı, kişi mi nesne mi? (Cevap: kişi)
  • "every product which leaves the factory" cümlesinde which yerine hangi kelime de olurdu? (Cevap: that)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then describe people and places in your own sentences. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kişileri ve yerleri kendi cümlelerinde tanımla.)

person (n)
a human being (kişi, insan)
Syn: human (insan) · Ant: machine (makine)
"She is a person who helps everyone."
place (n)
a particular position or area (yer, mekân)
Syn: location (konum) · Ant: nowhere (hiçbir yer)
"This is the place where we met."
thing (n)
an object; something that is not a person (şey, nesne)
Syn: object (nesne) · Ant: person (kişi)
"A pen is a thing which we write with."
neighbour (n)
a person who lives near you (komşu)
Syn: next-door (yan komşu) · Ant: stranger (yabancı)
"The neighbour who lives above me is kind."
designer (n)
a person who makes designs (tasarımcı)
Syn: creator (yaratıcı) · Ant: user (kullanıcı)
"Selin is the designer who made the chair."
engineer (n)
a person who builds or checks machines (mühendis)
Syn: technician (teknisyen) · Ant: —
"He is an engineer who checks the products."
building (n)
a place with walls and a roof (bina)
Syn: structure (yapı) · Ant: field (açık alan)
"That is the building where I work."
machine (n)
a thing that does work with power (makine)
Syn: device (cihaz) · Ant: hand tool (el aleti)
"A printer is a machine which prints paper."
tool (n)
a thing you use to do a job (alet, araç)
Syn: instrument (araç) · Ant: —
"A hammer is a tool that we use to hit nails."
describe (v)
to say what someone or something is like (tanımlamak, betimlemek)
Syn: explain (açıklamak) · Ant: hide (gizlemek)
"Can you describe the man who called?"
friendly (adj)
kind and easy to talk to (arkadaş canlısı)
Syn: kind (nazik) · Ant: unfriendly (soğuk)
"She is a friendly woman who knows everyone."
famous (adj)
known by many people (ünlü)
Syn: well-known (tanınmış) · Ant: unknown (bilinmeyen)
"He is a writer who is famous in Turkey."
useful (adj)
helpful; good for a purpose (faydalı, yararlı)
Syn: helpful (yardımcı) · Ant: useless (işe yaramaz)
"A phone is a thing which is very useful."
library (n)
a place where you can read and borrow books (kütüphane)
Syn: reading room (okuma odası) · Ant: —
"A library is a place where people study."
Kişileri ve nesneleri tanımlama: who, which, that ve where kullan.

  • The text describes people and places. Which words will you see a lot? (Metin kişileri ve yerleri tanımlıyor. Hangi kelimeleri çok göreceksin?)
  • Look at the title. Is this text about one person or about a whole company? (Başlığa bak. Metin bir kişiden mi yoksa bütün bir şirketten mi bahsediyor?)
Nasıl okumalı?

  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. who, which, that, where ile başlayan tanımlara dikkat et.

Baytek: insanların mutlu olduğu bir yer.
People and Places at Baytek

Baytek is a company that designs modern furniture. The person who started it is Mr. Demir, a man who loves simple, clean design. He built the office in a quiet street where the traffic is never a problem.

The building where Baytek works is not very big. It is a white building that has two floors and many windows. The windows let in a lot of light, so the rooms are always bright. In the morning, the first person who arrives is Mr. Demir. He is a man who likes to be early. He opens the door, he turns on the lights, and he makes the first pot of tea. Baytek is a place where the day starts in a calm and quiet way. The street where the office stands is short and clean. There are trees on both sides, and there is a small park where people walk their dogs. It is a nice place to work.

Baytek is a company that started many years ago. Mr. Demir was a young man who had one small room and a few tools. He made his first chair alone, and it was a chair that his friends loved. Slowly, more people who liked his work came to help him. First there was one worker, then five, and then twenty. Today Baytek is a place where many people work together, but it is still not a big company. Mr. Demir often says, "I like a company that stays small, because a small company is a place where everybody knows everybody." The workers who hear this always agree with him.

The woman who sits near the window is Selin. She is the designer who made the chair that won a prize last year. Selin works with Ali, an engineer who checks every product that leaves the factory. Ali is a careful person who never forgets a detail.

Selin is a person who loves her job. She has a desk that is full of pens, paper, and small models. On her desk there is a lamp which gives a warm light. Selin draws chairs, tables, and shelves that people use at home. She likes wood, and she likes colours that are soft and natural. Near Selin there is a young woman who is called Derya. Derya is a new designer who joined the team this year. She is a person who asks many questions, and Selin is happy to help her. "A good designer is someone who never stops learning," Selin says. The two women work at a long table where they share ideas every day.

The room where the team has meetings is bright and warm. It has a big table that Mr. Demir bought in Ankara, and a plant which Selin waters every morning. On the wall there is a photo that shows the first product the company made.

Behind the office there is a workshop where the workers build the furniture. It is a large room that smells of wood and paint. The machines which cut the wood are loud, so the workers wear special glasses that protect their eyes. Ali is the engineer who checks the machines every week. He is a careful man who wants everything to be safe. The people who work in the workshop are kind and friendly. One of them is Kemal, a worker who has been here for ten years. Kemal is a man who knows every tool in the room. He often helps the new workers who do not know the machines yet. The furniture that they make here goes to shops all over Turkey.

When you enter Baytek, the first thing that you see is a small desk near the door. This is the desk where Aylin works. Aylin is the person who welcomes every visitor. She is a kind woman who always has a big smile. On her desk there is a book which shows the names of all the guests. Aylin gives coffee to the people who wait, and she tells them about the company. Behind her there is a wall that has many photos. The photos show the chairs, the tables, and the shelves that Baytek has made over the years. Every guest who comes here looks at this wall and says, "What a beautiful place."

Next to the office there is a small café where the workers have lunch. The person who runs the café is Zeynep, a friendly woman who knows everybody's name. She makes the coffee that everyone loves.

At lunch time, the café where the workers eat is full of noise and laughter. Zeynep, who is always busy, moves fast between the tables. She remembers the food that each person likes. She knows the man who wants tea with no sugar, and she knows the woman who loves her cake. Next to the café there is a small garden where the workers sit when the weather is warm. In the garden there is an old tree that gives good shade. Under the tree there is a wooden bench which Kemal made himself. It is a quiet spot where people rest, read, or talk about their work. Many workers say the garden is the place that they love the most.

On Friday afternoons, Baytek is a company that does something special. All the workers stop for one hour and meet in the big room where they usually have meetings. They drink coffee, they eat cake, and they talk about the week that has passed. Mr. Demir thanks the people who worked hard, and everybody claps. It is a simple tradition that makes the team feel like a family. Sometimes a customer who bought a chair or a table sends a nice message, and Selin reads it out loud. These are the small moments that make the workers smile. Baytek is not only a place where people work; it is a place where people care about each other.

Baytek also thinks about the world that we live in. The company uses wood that comes from good forests, and it does not waste materials. The old pieces of wood which the workers do not use become small toys for children. There is a box near the door where people can leave things that they want to give away. Mr. Demir believes in a company that helps other people. Every year, Baytek gives some chairs and tables to a school where the children need them. The teachers who receive these gifts write kind letters, and Aylin puts the letters on the wall. This is the reason why the workers are proud of the place where they spend their days.

Baytek is not a big company, but it is a place where people feel happy. The workers who come here every day say the same thing: it is a company that treats people well, and that is the reason they stay.

Warm-up: The Describing Game (Tanımlama Oyunu)

Bir kelime söylemeden, o şeyi tanımlayarak anlat — tıpkı Türkçedeki "Tabu" gibi. Describe a person, a place or a thing using who, which or where, and let your partner guess the word. Önce örnek diyaloğa bak:

A:It's a person who helps you when you are sick.Hasta olduğunda sana yardım eden bir kişi.
B:A doctor!Doktor!
A:Yes! Now you. Describe a place.Evet! Şimdi sen. Bir yer tanımla.
B:It's a place where you can read and borrow books.Kitap okuyup ödünç alabileceğin bir yer.
A:A library! And a thing?Kütüphane! Peki bir nesne?

A:A pen! Great — your turn again.Kalem! Harika — yine sıra sende.

Your Turn: Describe (Tanımlama Sırası Sende)

Şimdi sıra sende. Describe each of these out loud with a relative clause — sesli olarak, who/which/where kullanarak. Mümkünse bir arkadaşınla tahmin oyunu oyna. Amaç: bir ismi rahatça tanımlayabilmek.

  • a person in your family (aile üyesi) → "My ... is a person who ..."
  • the place where you live (yaşadığın yer) → "I live in a place where ..."
  • a thing you use every day (her gün kullandığın bir nesne) → "It's a thing which ..."
  • your favourite shop or café (en sevdiğin dükkân/kafe) → "It's a place where ..."
İpucu — akıcı konuşmak için
  • Kişi için who, nesne için which/that, yer için where — Grammar bölümündeki mantığı uygula.
  • Cümleleri bağlaçlarla uzat: "She is a person who is very kind and who always helps me."

Writing: The People and Places in My Life

Write and submit your paragraph; your teacher will review it. (Paragrafını yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

Relative Clauses Yazını Zenginleştirir (Richer Writing)

Kısa, kopuk cümleler bir yazıyı zayıf gösterir. What makes writing sound natural and mature is relative clauses: kişileri, yerleri ve nesneleri tek cümlede tanımlar, iki fikri akıcı biçimde birleştirirler. Bir de bağlaçları (and, but, so, because, when...) eklersen, yazın gerçek bir metne dönüşür.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Relative clause + bağlaç ile ✓
I have a friend. Her name is Deniz. She lives in Izmir. Izmir is a city. I love it. I have a friend who is called Deniz, and she lives in Izmir, a city which I love because the sea is very close.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 relative clause kullan (who / which / that / where).
  • Ayrıca en az 3 bağlaç kullan: and, but, so, because, when, after, before.

Bağlaç Hatırlatması (Connectors Recap)

A thing which is very useful to me every day is my old bicycle. It is not new, but it is the thing that takes me everywhere, so I never sell it.

Unit Check (Ünite Kontrolü)

Mixed questions from the whole unit, then check what you can do. (Ünitenin tamamından karışık sorular; sonra neler yapabildiğini işaretle.)

14 questions: grammar + vocabulary + reading. (14 soru: gramer + kelime + okuma.) Her soru anında değerlendirilir.

Can-Do Check (Yapabiliyorum Listesi)

Tick what you can do now. Honest answers help your teacher. (Şu an yapabildiklerini işaretle. Dürüst cevaplar öğretmenine yol gösterir.)
