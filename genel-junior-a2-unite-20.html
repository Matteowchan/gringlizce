Grammar: The Past Simple

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Past Simple?

Şimdiye kadar hep bugünden ve genel gerçeklerden konuştun. Now it is time to talk about the past. When you tell a friend what you did yesterday, where you went last weekend, or what happened on your holiday, you need a new tense: the Past Simple. Past Simple, geçmişte başlayıp biten olayları anlatır — bir kez oldu, tamamlandı ve kapandı.

  • Yesterday I watched a cartoon.Dün bir çizgi film izledim.
  • Last week we visited my grandma.Geçen hafta babaannemi ziyaret ettik.
  • In 2019 she moved to Istanbul.2019'da İstanbul'a taşındı.

Bu bölümde önce be fiilinin geçmişini (was / were) kısaca hatırlayacağız. Then we will study regular verbs (-ed) and irregular verbs, questions and negatives with did / didn't, short answers, and past time expressions. Adım adım gidelim.

1. Köprü: be Fiilinin Geçmişi (was / were)

A2'ye başlarken bunu zaten biliyorsun, so this is just a quick bridge. "Olmak" fiilinin geçmiş hâli iki şekildedir: was ve were. These describe states in the past — where somebody was, how they felt, what something was like.

ÖzneOlumluOlumsuzÖrnek
I / he / she / itwaswasn'tShe was tired yesterday.
you / we / theywereweren'tWe were at the park.
Dikkat
  • be fiilinde did kullanılmaz: "Were you happy?" ✓ — "Did you be happy?" ✗
  • Ama diğer bütün fiillerde (go, work, eat...) geçmiş soru ve olumsuz için did / didn't gerekir. Bu farkı bu bölümde netleştireceğiz.

2. Düzenli Fiiller (Regular Verbs: -ed)

Çoğu fiil geçmişte -ed alır. This is the easy part: no new word to memorize, you just add an ending. Ama yazımda (spelling) dört küçük kural var. Bunları öğren, çünkü sınavda ve yazıda en çok burada hata yapılır.

KuralNasıl?Örnek
Çoğu fiil+ edwork → worked · watch → watched · play → played
-e ile biten+ dlive → lived · arrive → arrived · like → liked
Sessiz + y ile biteny → iedstudy → studied · try → tried · carry → carried
Kısa ünlü + tek sessizsessizi çiftle + edstop → stopped · plan → planned · shop → shopped
Küçük not
  • play, enjoy gibi fiillerde y'den önce sesli harf var, so we do NOT use -ied: played ✓, plaied ✗.
  • Telaffuz üç türlüdür (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) ama yazım her zaman -ed'dir; bu ünitede yazıma odaklan.

3. Düzensiz Fiiller (Irregular Verbs)

Some verbs do not follow the -ed rule. Bunların geçmiş hâli tamamen farklı bir kelimedir ve ezberlenmesi gerekir. İyi haber: en çok kullanılanlar bir avuç kadardır. Aşağıdaki listeyi iyi öğren — günlük konuşmanın çoğu bu fiillerle döner.

FiilGeçmiş

dodid
makemade

FiilGeçmiş

FiilGeçmiş

Nasıl çalışılır?
  • Bu fiilleri tek tek değil, cümle içinde öğren: "I went home", "She bought a car", "We had lunch."
  • Olumlu cümlede düzensiz formu kullan; ama did / didn't ile birlikte yalın hâle döner (aşağıda göreceğiz).

4. Soru ve Olumsuz (did / didn't)

Olumlu cümlede fiili değiştiririz. But for questions and negatives, we use a helper: did. Bütün öznelerde aynıdır — he/she/it için bile "did" (does değil). En önemli kural şu: did / didn't'ten sonra fiil yalın hâle döner.

TürYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
Olumluözne + geçmiş fiilShe went to Ankara.Ankara'ya gitti.
Olumsuzözne + didn't + yalın fiilShe didn't go to Ankara.Ankara'ya gitmedi.
SoruDid + özne + yalın fiilDid she go to Ankara?Ankara'ya gitti mi?
Altın kural
  • Geçmiş bilgisi zaten did'in içindedir, so the main verb goes back to base form: "Did you go?" ✓ — "Did you went?" ✗
  • Aynı şekilde: "He didn't know." ✓ — "He didn't knew." ✗

5. Kısa Cevaplar (Short Answers)

Bir Past Simple sorusuna kısa cevap vermek çok kolaydır, because you only need did or didn't — fiili tekrar etmezsin. Özne ne olursa olsun kalıp aynıdır.

SoruOlumluOlumsuz
Did you call her?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did he finish his homework?Yes, he did.No, he didn't.
Did they enjoy the trip?Yes, they did.No, they didn't.
Not
  • Cevapta ana fiili tekrar etme: "Yes, I called." yerine "Yes, I did." yeterlidir ve daha doğaldır.

6. Geçmiş Zaman İfadeleri (Past Time Expressions)

Past Simple genellikle bir zaman ifadesiyle birlikte gelir. These words tell the listener that the action is finished and in the past. En sık kullanılanlar şunlardır:

İfadeAnlamıÖrnek
yesterdaydünI saw them yesterday.
last night / week / yeardün gece / geçen hafta / geçen yılWe met last week.
... ago... önceHe left two hours ago.
in 2019 / in May2019'da / Mayıs'taWe moved here in 2019.
when I was younggençken / çocukkenWhen I was a child, I lived in Bursa.
Sıra kuralı
  • ago her zaman zaman diliminden sonra gelir: "a week ago", "three days ago" — "ago a week" ✗.
  • Zaman ifadesi cümlenin başında ya da sonunda olabilir: "Yesterday I worked late." = "I worked late yesterday."

7. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların Past Simple'da en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I goed home.I went home.go düzensizdir; "goed" yoktur.
Did you went home?Did you go home?did'ten sonra yalın fiil.
She didn't went.She didn't go.didn't'ten sonra yalın fiil.
He did not knew.He did not know.did not = didn't; sonrası yalın fiil.
We was late.We were late.you / we / they → were.
Yesterday I go to school.Yesterday I went to school."yesterday" varsa fiil geçmiş olmalı.
Two days ago I have called.Two days ago I called.Belirli geçmiş zamanla Past Simple kullanılır.

8. Uygulama: Pazartesi Sabahı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about their weekend on Monday morning. Koyu yazılan geçmiş zaman yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — Good morning, Mia! How was your weekend?Günaydın Mia! Hafta sonun nasıldı?
  • — It was great, thanks! On Saturday I visited my grandparents and we had a big lunch.Harikaydı, sağ ol! Cumartesi büyükannemleri ziyaret ettim ve büyük bir öğle yemeği yedik.
  • — Nice! Did you go out on Sunday?Güzel! Pazar dışarı çıktın mı?
  • — No, I didn't. I stayed at home and watched two cartoons. What about you?Hayır, çıkmadım. Evde kaldım ve iki çizgi film izledim. Ya sen?
  • — I went to the zoo with my sister. We bought the tickets a month ago.Kız kardeşimle hayvanat bahçesine gittim. Biletleri bir ay önce almıştık.
  • — Did you enjoy it?Beğendin mi?
  • — Yes, I did! The animals were amazing, but we didn't get home until dark.Evet, çok! Hayvanlar muhteşemdi ama hava kararana kadar eve varamadık.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane did sorusu var? (Cevap: 3)
  • "We had a big lunch" cümlesindeki fiilin yalın hâli nedir? (Cevap: have)
  • "We bought the tickets a month ago." — neden "ago" kullanıldı, cümledeki yeri nerede? (Bölüm 6'ya bak.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • The story is about a past holiday. Which tense will you see a lot? (Metin geçmiş bir tatili anlatıyor. Hangi zamanı çok göreceksin?)
  • Look at the title. Is this a happy story or a sad story? Guess! (Başlığa bak. Mutlu mu üzücü mü bir hikâye? Tahmin et!)
Nasıl okumalı?

  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. Fiillerin geçmiş hâllerine (went, made, came...) dikkat et.

Geçen yaz deniz kenarında geçen güzel bir tatil.
My Summer Holiday by the Sea

My name is Elif, and I am ten years old. Last summer was the best holiday of my life. School finished in June, so my family and I packed our big blue suitcase and travelled to a small town by the sea. My grandparents live there in a white house with a garden full of lemon trees.

We left home very early in the morning. The trip was long, and I was tired, but I was also very excited. When we arrived, my grandma hugged us and made a big lunch. We ate fresh fish and salad in the garden. After lunch, my little brother Kaan and I ran straight to the beach.

The beach was wonderful. The sea was warm and blue, and the sky was sunny every day. On the first day, I swam for a long time, and Kaan built a big sandcastle near the water. We collected pretty shells and small stones, and we put them in a little bag. I didn't want to go home for dinner!

Every morning after breakfast, we went to the seaside again. Grandpa came with us because he loved to swim too. He taught me how to float on my back. At first I was afraid, but then I felt safe, and I laughed a lot. Grandma didn't swim, but she sat under a big umbrella and read her book.

One afternoon, we didn't go to the beach. Instead, we visited an old castle on a hill. We climbed many steps, and from the top we saw the whole town and the blue sea. It was amazing. Before we went home, we bought cold ice cream from a small shop. Mine was chocolate, and Kaan's was strawberry.

The little town was quiet and pretty. Every evening, we walked along the harbour and watched the fishing boats come back. The sky turned pink and orange, and the air smelled of salt and flowers. Sometimes we ate dinner at a small restaurant near the water, and I always chose grilled fish because it was Grandpa's favourite too.

Not every day was perfect. On Thursday, dark clouds came, and it rained hard. We couldn't go outside, so we stayed at home and played board games with Grandpa. He told us funny stories about his own summers when he was a boy. We laughed until our stomachs hurt. The rain wasn't a problem — it was a happy day too.

At the weekend, my cousins arrived from the city. Now we were a big group of children, and the house was noisy and fun. We camped one night in the garden in a green tent. We didn't sleep much because we talked and looked at the stars. In the morning, we were tired, but nobody was sad.

The last day was hard. I didn't want to leave. I hugged my grandparents for a long time, and Grandma gave me a jar of homemade jam for the trip. On the way home, I looked at my little bag of shells and smiled.

That night, I wrote in my diary: "This was my best summer. The sea, the sandcastles, the ice cream, and my family made it perfect. Next year, I want to come back again." Then I closed my eyes and dreamed about the blue sea.

Bağlaçlar Yazıyı Cümleden Ayırır (Connectors)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. What turns a list of sentences into a real text is connectors (bağlaçlar): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. A2 seviyesinde A1'in bağlaçlarına üç yeni zaman bağlacı ekliyoruz: when, after, before. These are perfect for telling a story about the past.

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek

sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirIt rained, so we stayed inside.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
I woke up late. I met my friend. We walked in the park. I was tired. I was happy. I woke up late because I was tired. After breakfast, I met my friend and we walked in the park. I was tired, but I was happy.
