Grammar: have / has

"have / has" Ne İşe Yarar? (What is "have / has"?)

Bir şeye sahip olduğumuzu ("... var", "benim ...-im var") anlatmak için have ya da has kullanırız. Önce örneklere bakalım:

  • "I have a sister."Bir kız kardeşim var.
  • "She has a cat."Onun bir kedisi var.
  • "My brother has a red bike."Kardeşimin kırmızı bir bisikleti var.
  • "We have a big dog."Büyük bir köpeğimiz var.
  • "They have two pets."Onların iki evcil hayvanı var.

Kural çok kısa: bazı kişilerle have, bazılarıyla has deriz. Şimdi hangisinin ne zaman geldiğini görelim!

1. have mi, has mi? (have or has?)

Örnek cümlelere bak. Sağdaki fiil hangi kişiyle kullanılıyor?

KişiFiilÖrnekTürkçesi
IhaveI have a ball.Bir topum var.
youhaveYou have a bag.Bir çantan var.
hehasHe has a bike.Onun bir bisikleti var.
shehasShe has a doll.Onun bir oyuncak bebeği var.
ithasIt has a ball.Onun bir topu var.
wehaveWe have a dog.Bir köpeğimiz var.
theyhaveThey have toys.Onların oyuncakları var.
Kolay yol
  • I / you / we / they → have.
  • he / she / it (tek kişi ya da şey) → has.

2. "yok" Demek (Negative)

Bir şeyimiz "yok" demek için don't have ya da doesn't have deriz:

KişiOlumsuzÖrnekTürkçesi
I / you / we / theydon't haveI don't have a cat.Bir kedim yok.
he / she / itdoesn't haveShe doesn't have a dog.Onun bir köpeği yok.
Dikkat!
  • doesn't ve don't ile her zaman have gelir, "has" değil: "She doesn't have a dog." ✓

3. Soru Sormak (Questions)

Bir şeyi olup olmadığını sormak için başa Do ya da Does koyarız:

SoruEvetHayır
Do you have a pet?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she have a cat?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Dikkat!
  • Do / Does ile de her zaman have gelir: "Does he have a bike?" ✓ (has değil)

4. Kimin? (my, your, his, her...)

Bir şeyin kime ait olduğunu söylemek için iyelik sıfatları kullanırız. "benim topum" gibi:

KelimeAnlamıÖrnek
mybenimThis is my ball.
yourseninYour bag is nice.
hisonun (erkek)His bike is red.
heronun (kız)Her cat is small.
itsonun (hayvan/eşya)The cat is here. Its name is Boncuk.
ourbizimOur dog is big.
theironlarınTheir toys are new.
Dikkat!
  • Erkek için his, kız için her: "She has a doll. Her name is Lila." ✓
  • its (kesme işareti yok) = iyelik: "onun adı". "Its name is Boncuk."

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I has a sister.I have a sister.I / you / we / they → have.
She have a cat.She has a cat.he / she / it → has.
He don't have a bike.He doesn't have a bike.he → doesn't have.
This is me ball.This is my ball."benim" = my (me değil).
She has a doll. His name is Lila.Her name is Lila.Kız için her, erkek için his.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Talking About Family)

İki çocuk aileleri hakkında konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — This is my family. I have one sister.Bu benim ailem. Bir kız kardeşim var.
  • — What is her name?Onun adı ne?
  • — Her name is Elif. She has a little cat.Onun adı Elif. Onun küçük bir kedisi var.
  • — Do you have a pet?Senin evcil hayvanın var mı?
  • — Yes, I do. I have a dog. His name is Karabaş.Evet, var. Bir köpeğim var. Onun adı Karabaş.
  • — Does your brother have a bike?Kardeşinin bisikleti var mı?
  • — Yes, he does. He has a red bike!Evet, var. Onun kırmızı bir bisikleti var!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç kişi konuşuyor? (Cevap: 2)
  • "She ___ a little cat" boşluğa ne gelir? (Cevap: has)

family · sister · brother · cat · dog · pet · bike · toy · ball · bag

İpucu

My Family

Hello again! Do you remember me? I am Kaan. I live in a small house with a red door. My family is big and fun. We have a dog, a cat, and a lot of toys. Every day in my house is busy and happy. Let me tell you about my family and my things.

Hello! My name is Kaan. I am eight years old, and I have a big family. I have one sister and one brother. My sister's name is Elif. She is ten, and she has a little cat. Its name is Boncuk. Boncuk is white and very soft.

My brother is four. His name is Emre. He has a red bike. He likes his bike a lot. We have a dog too. Its name is Karabaş. He is big and friendly. He has a green ball. Karabaş is a good dog.

I have a lot of toys. My favourite toy is a blue ball. My toys are in a big box. I don't have a bike, but I have a kite. My kite is yellow and red.

My mother has a nice bag, and my father has a small car. My grandma has a garden. We do not have a bird, but we have a happy dog. We are a happy family!

My sister Elif is very kind. She loves her cat Boncuk, and Boncuk sleeps all day. Elif reads books to me in the evening, and I listen. She is a good big sister. My brother Emre is small, but he is fast on his red bike. He rides in the garden every day. Our dog Karabaş runs after the green ball. He is big and friendly, and he is never sad. Boncuk and Karabaş are good friends too.

My grandma has a beautiful garden. There are flowers, a big tree, and green grass. My grandma is kind, and she gives us apples from her tree. The apples are red and sweet. On Sundays we are all in the garden. My mother brings her nice bag, and my father comes in his small car. We play, we laugh, and Karabaş runs with us. My kite is yellow and red, and it flies high in the blue sky. I don't have a bike, but my kite is the best!

On school days I get up early. I put my books in my bag, and I go to school with Emre. Our school is not far. After school we are hungry, and we run home fast. Boncuk is at the door, and Karabaş is happy to see us. In the evening, my toys come out of the big box, and we all play together. Home is my favourite place.

Do you have a family like this? Do you have a pet? Tell me about your family and your toys. Write to me soon!

Writing: My Family and My Things

Aileni ve eşyalarını anlat, gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about your family; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI have a cat and a dog.
butamaMy dog is big but friendly.
becauseçünküI am happy because I have a pet.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your family and your things. (Ailen ve eşyaların hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • a person in your family: sister, brother, mother... (ailenden biri)
  • one thing you have: a toy, a ball, a bike, or a pet (sahip olduğun bir şey)
  • a name of a pet or a family member (bir evcil hayvanın ya da aile üyesinin adı)
  • how you feel today (bugün nasıl hissettiğin)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"I have ..." "My ... has ..." "Her/His name is ..."

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~40 words) Hello! My name is Deniz and I have a big family. I have one sister. Her name is Ada, and she has a little cat. I have a red bike and a lot of toys. Today I am very happy because it is my birthday!
