Günler ve Aylar Nedir? (Days and Months)

Bir haftada 7 gün vardır. Bir yılda 12 ay vardır. İngilizcede günler ve aylar hep büyük harfle başlar:

  • "Today is Monday."Bugün Pazartesi.
  • "My birthday is in May."Doğum günüm Mayıs'ta.
  • "There are seven days in a week."Bir haftada yedi gün var.
  • "There are twelve months in a year."Bir yılda on iki ay var.
Dikkat!
  • Gün ve ay isimleri her zaman büyük harfle başlar: Monday ✓ (monday ✗), June ✓ (june ✗).

1. Haftanın Günleri (Days of the Week)

Haftada yedi gün vardır. İşte hepsi, sırayla:

İngilizceTürkçeKısa
MondayPazartesiMon
TuesdaySalıTue
WednesdayÇarşambaWed
ThursdayPerşembeThu
FridayCumaFri
SaturdayCumartesiSat
SundayPazarSun
Kolay yol
  • Saturday ve Sunday = weekend (hafta sonu). Okul yok, oyun var!
  • Bir günden bahsederken on kullanırız: "on Monday" (Pazartesi günü).

2. Yılın Ayları (Months of the Year)

Bir yılda on iki ay vardır. Her ay bir mevsime aittir:

İngilizceTürkçeMevsim
JanuaryOcakwinter (kış)
FebruaryŞubatwinter (kış)
MarchMartspring (bahar)
AprilNisanspring (bahar)
MayMayısspring (bahar)
JuneHaziransummer (yaz)
JulyTemmuzsummer (yaz)
AugustAğustossummer (yaz)

OctoberEkimautumn (sonbahar)
NovemberKasımautumn (sonbahar)
DecemberAralıkwinter (kış)
Sıralı sayılar (first, second)
  • January ilk aydır → the first month (birinci).
  • February ikinci aydır → the second month (ikinci). December ise the last month (sonuncu).

3. When is your birthday? (Doğum Günün Ne Zaman?)

Doğum gününü sormak için When is your birthday? deriz. Cevap verirken is ve ayla birlikte in kullanırız:

  • — When is your birthday?Doğum günün ne zaman?
  • — It is in May. / My birthday is in May.Mayıs'ta. / Doğum günüm Mayıs'ta.
SoruCevapTürkçesi
When is your birthday?It is in June.Haziran'da.
When is his birthday?It is in October.Ekim'de.
Is your birthday in May?Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.Evet. / Hayır.
Dikkat!
  • Aylarla her zaman in: "in May" ✓ (at May ✗, on May ✗).
  • Cümlede is kullan: "My birthday is in July." ✓ (My birthday are ✗).

4. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
My birthday is at May.My birthday is in May.Aylarla "in" kullan.
When are your birthday?When is your birthday?Tek doğum günü → "is".
today is monday.Today is Monday.Gün isimleri büyük harfle.
There are seven day.There are seven days.Yediden fazla → çoğul "days".
I have ten years old.I am ten years old.Yaşta "am/is" kullan.

5. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Talk)

İki çocuk günler ve doğum günleri hakkında konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What day is it today?Bugün günlerden ne?
  • — Today is Friday! Tomorrow is Saturday — the weekend!Bugün Cuma! Yarın Cumartesi — hafta sonu!
  • — I like the weekend. When is your birthday?Hafta sonunu severim. Doğum günün ne zaman?
  • — My birthday is in May. May is a spring month. When is your birthday?Doğum günüm Mayıs'ta. Mayıs bir bahar ayıdır. Seninki ne zaman?
  • — It is in December. December is the last month of the year.Aralık'ta. Aralık yılın son ayıdır.
  • — Great! We have two happy months: May and December!Harika! İki mutlu ayımız var: Mayıs ve Aralık!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç doğum günü ayı geçiyor? (Cevap: 2 — May ve December)
  • "Tomorrow is Saturday." ne demek? (Cevap: Yarın Cumartesi.)

Let's Play! (Alıştırma)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Speaking: My Words

Günler, aylar ve doğum günü kelimelerini öğren! (Learn the day, month and birthday words.)

day (n)
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday... (gün)
Zıttı: night (gece)
"Today is a happy day."
week (n)
seven days together (hafta)
"There are seven days in a week."
weekend (n)
Saturday and Sunday (hafta sonu)
Zıttı: weekday (hafta içi)
"I play on the weekend."
month (n)
January, February, March... (ay)
"May is my favourite month."
year (n)
twelve months together (yıl)
"There are twelve months in a year."
birthday (n)
the day you are born, once a year (doğum günü)
"My birthday is in May."
today (adv)
this day now (bugün)
Zıttı: tomorrow (yarın)
"Today is Monday."
first (adj)
number one in order (birinci, ilk)
Zıttı: last (sonuncu)
"January is the first month."
second (adj)
number two in order (ikinci)
"February is the second month."
When...? (q)
a question about time (ne zaman?)
"When is your birthday?"
Bahçedeki arkadaşlar: uğur böceği, kelebek ve çiçek.

Your Turn! (Senin Sıran)

Talk about the days and your birthday. Write 5 sentences. (Günler ve doğum günün hakkında konuş. 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Gün ve ay isimlerini büyük harfle yaz: Monday, May.
  • Bugünün gününü, sevdiğin günü ve doğum gününün ayını söyle. Aylarla in kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"Today is ..." "My favourite day is ..." "My birthday is in ..." "There are seven ..." "January is the first ..."
Sample Answer 1. Today is Monday. It is a school day.
2. My favourite day is Saturday, because it is the weekend.
3. There are seven days in a week and twelve months in a year.
4. My birthday is in May. May is a spring month.
5. January is the first month and December is the last month.

Reading: My Week and My Birthday

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

day · week · weekend · month · year · birthday · first · today

İpucu
  • Önce hızlıca oku — her kelimeyi anlaman gerekmez.
  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
My Week and My Birthday

Hello! My name is Elif. There are seven days in a week. My favourite day is Saturday. On Saturday, there is no school! I play in the park with my friends. Sunday is also fun, because my family and I are together at home. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday are school days. I like school days too, but Saturday is the best!

Every day of the week is special for me. On Monday, I have my English lesson at school. On Tuesday, I play with my cat at home. On Wednesday, I paint colourful pictures. On Thursday and Friday, I do my homework after school. But I wait all week for Saturday! On Saturday, I am free and I feel very happy. Sunday is a calm family day at home.

There are twelve months in a year. January is the first month, and December is the last month. My favourite month is May. Why? My birthday is in May! I am ten years old. My birthday is on a Saturday this year. That is my lucky day!

There are twelve months in a year, and each one is different. The first month is January, and it is very cold. The spring months are March, April and May. The summer months are June, July and August. My birthday is in May, in the warm spring. I love May because the flowers are beautiful. On my birthday, I am ten years old!

My best friend is Can. When is his birthday? His birthday is in October. October is an autumn month. My mum's birthday is in the summer, in July. My dad's birthday is in winter, in January.

In my family, everyone has a birthday in a different season. My birthday is in spring, in May. My mum's birthday is in summer, in July. My dad's birthday is in winter, in January. My best friend Can has his birthday in autumn, in October. We all love birthday cakes, candles and presents very much!

There are four seasons in a year: spring, summer, autumn and winter. Each season has three months. I like all of the seasons, but spring is warm and sunny. My birthday month, May, is in the spring. That is one more happy reason to love the spring!

My birthday is on a Saturday this year, and that is the best day of all! I will have a big party in the park. My friends will come, and we will play fun games together. My mum will make a big chocolate cake for me. I am so excited! May is truly my favourite month of the year. I can't wait for my special day!

I have a small calendar in my room. Every day, I look at it. Today is Monday. It is a school day. But my birthday is soon! Do you know the days of the week? When is your birthday?

Writing: My Week and My Birthday

Günlerini ve doğum gününü anlat, gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about your week and your birthday; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveSaturday and Sunday are the weekend.
butamaI like Monday but I love Saturday.
becauseçünküI am happy because my birthday is soon.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about your week and your birthday. (Haftan ve doğum günün hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • what day it is today (bugün günlerden ne)
  • your favourite day (en sevdiğin gün)
  • the month of your birthday (doğum gününün ayı)
  • one first or last month (bir ilk / son ay)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Hello! Today is ..." "My favourite day is ..." "My birthday is in ..." "January is the first ..."

My Writing (30-50 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~40 words) Hello! Today is Monday. There are seven days in a week. My favourite day is Saturday, because it is the weekend. My birthday is in May and May is a spring month. January is the first month, but December is the last month!
