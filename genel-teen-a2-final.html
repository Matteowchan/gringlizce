Sınav Hakkında

Bu final sınavı, Teen A2 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: Past Simple (düzenli + düzensiz fiiller); Past Continuous ve while/when; karşılaştırma-üstünlük yapıları, as...as ve too/enough; be going to; will (tahmin ve ani karar — going to karşıtlığı); must/have to/should ve mustn't ile don't have to farkı; much/many ve a few/a little; Present Perfect (ever/never/just/already/yet); Present Perfect for/since ve Past Simple karşıtlığı; gerund ve infinitive; First Conditional ve unless; relative clauses (who/which/where); Zero Conditional; used to (geçmiş alışkanlıklar); zarflar (manner) ve zarf karşılaştırması; too/enough ve so/such; geçmiş yeterlilik (could/was able to); artikeller (a/an/the); question tags. Konular okul, arkadaşlar, spor, hobiler, aile ve geziler etrafında döner. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Teen A2 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Text 1 geçmiş deneyimleri, Text 2 gelecek planlarını anlatır. (Text 1 is about past experiences, Text 2 is about future plans.)

Text 1 · Two Years in the School Band: Ela Looks Back

Ela has played in the Green Valley School band since 2024, and in that time the band has changed a lot. When she joined, there were only three members, and they practised in a tiny room next to the kitchen. Now there are more than twenty students in the band, and Ela has become the leader — the youngest leader in the band's history.

Her first year was not easy. While she was learning her first difficult song, the band lost an important competition, and everybody felt sad. Some members left, but Ela stayed because she believed in the band. That winter, the group worked hard on a new show, and it won a regional prize in May. Since then, more students have wanted to join, and the little room has never been quiet again.

Ela has never forgotten her worst day in the band. Two summers ago, she deleted all their recorded songs by mistake while she was cleaning the old files on the band's computer. She has made many mistakes since her first day, she says, but that one taught her the most important lesson: always make a copy before you touch anything.

Last month, the band moved to a bigger room near the sports hall. The move took two days, and everybody carried instruments while the rain was falling outside. Ela has already decorated the new room with posters, but she hasn't learned every instrument yet. "I have been in this band for two years," she says, "and it still feels like my first week — there is always a new song. I haven't finished learning, and I hope I never will."

Text 2 · The Band's Plans for Next Year

The Green Valley School band is going to have a very busy year. In February, the band is going to start a new group for younger students, and about fifteen children will join it. Mr. Kaya, who has led the music lessons for ten years, will help both groups, but he isn't going to travel to other schools. "I'll invite other bands here instead," he says. "If we play well at the spring festival, the school will invite us every year."

The biggest project is a new song which helps younger students remember their notes. The students who wrote last year's show are going to write it, and Ela will lead the singing. The song must be ready before the spring festival in June, because the whole school wants to hear it in July. If the festival goes well, the school will invite the band again next year. "It's the most important project we have ever had," says Ela. "Unless we practise every week, we will not be ready in time."

There are also some smaller plans. The band is going to practise twice a week, so Ela is planning the new timetable these days. Students who join before March will get free lessons. The old music room, which is too small for twenty people, is going to become a quiet study room, and the school will build new shelves near the door, where there is more space.

Not everyone is happy about every change. A few students think the new hall is too far from the classrooms, and they should not have to carry heavy instruments so far. But most people agree: next year is going to be the most exciting year in the band's history.

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: News About Your Club (70-90 words)

Your English friend Alex wants news from you. You have just joined a new club or team at school (real or imaginary). Write a short email: what has changed, what you did in your old free time, and how you feel now. (İngiliz arkadaşın Alex senden haber bekliyor. Okulda yeni bir kulübe ya da takıma katıldın — gerçek ya da hayali. Kısa bir e-posta yaz: ne değişti, eski boş zamanında neler yaptın, şimdi nasıl hissediyorsun.)

  • Use Present Perfect for the news: I have just joined..., I have started... (haber için Present Perfect)
  • Use Past Simple for the past: I played..., I watched... (geçmiş için Past Simple)
  • Use at least 2 connectors: and, but, so, because, when. (en az 2 bağlaç)
  • 70-90 words. (70-90 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Alex," + kısa bir giriş cümlesi (örn. I have some big news).
  • Gövde: haber — Present Perfect; geçmiş — Past Simple; bağlaçlar.
  • Kapanış: "Write soon," / "Best wishes," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My Plans, Experiences and Goals (140-170 words)

Write a paragraph about your life (real or imaginary): something important you did in the past, things you have done or learned recently, and your plans and goals for the future. (Hayatın hakkında bir paragraf yaz — gerçek ya da hayali: geçmişte yaptığın önemli bir şey, son zamanlarda yaptıkların veya öğrendiklerin, gelecek plan ve hedeflerin.)

  • Past: use Past Simple — started, joined, won... (geçmiş için Past Simple)
  • Recently: use Present Perfect — I have learned..., I have never... (son dönem için Present Perfect)
  • Future: use be going to and will — I am going to..., I will... (gelecek için going to / will)
  • Include 1 conditional sentence with if or unless. (if/unless ile 1 koşul cümlesi)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: and, but, so, because, when. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 140-170 words. (140-170 kelime)
