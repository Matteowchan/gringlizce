Grammar: be going to (gelecek planları)

Gelecekteki planlarını anlat: bu hafta sonu, gelecek ay, tatilde. (Talk about future plans with "be going to".)

Selam, ben Gri. Bu ünitede gelecekteki planlarını anlatmayı öğreniyoruz: be going to. Yani am / is / are + going to + fiil. Yapmayı planladığın şeyleri söylerken kullanırız: "I am going to study tonight." ve "Are you going to travel this summer?" Özneye bakıp doğru be biçimini (am/is/are) seçeceğiz. Başlayalım.

Ne Demek? (The "going to" Future)

Gelecekteki bir plan ya da niyet hakkında konuşurken be going to kullanırız. Yapısı basittir: am / is / are + going to + yalın fiil. Hangi be biçimini kullanacağın özneye bağlıdır:

  • I am going to study tonight.Bu gece ders çalışacağım. (I → am going to)
  • She is going to travel next month.Gelecek ay seyahat edecek. (she → is going to)
  • They are going to visit us.Bizi ziyaret edecekler. (they → are going to)
Kolay yol
  • Önce be: I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are.
  • Sonra hep aynı: going to + fiil (yalın hâl: go, study, travel).

1. am / is / are going to (Hangi Özne Hangi Biçim?)

Doğru biçimi seçmek için önce özneye bak. Bu tablo tüm özneleri gösterir; gelecek zaman ifadeleriyle (tonight, next weekend) birlikte kullan:

ÖzneBiçimÖrnek
Iam going toI am going to cook dinner tonight.
he / she / itis going toShe is going to start a new course.
you / we / theyare going toWe are going to travel next week.
Kolay yol
  • be biçimini özneye göre seç: am / is / are.
  • "She are going to" ✗ → "She is going to" ✓ (she tekil → is).

2. Olumsuz (not going to)

Olumsuz yapmak için be'den sonra not ekleriz: am not / is not / are not going to. Konuşurken kısa hâli kullanırız:

OlumluOlumsuzTürkçe
I am going to be late.I 'm not going to be late.Geç kalmayacağım.
He is going to come.He isn't going to come.Gelmeyecek.
They are going to help.They aren't going to help.Yardım etmeyecekler.
Kolay yol
  • not, hep be ile going to arasına girer: is not going to.
  • "I don't going to" ✗ → "I 'm not going to" ✓ (don't kullanma).

3. Soru (Are you going to ...?)

Soru sormak için be (am/is/are) özneden önce gelir. Kısa cevaplarda aynı be biçimini kullanırız:

SoruKısa cevapTürkçe
Are you going to travel?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.Seyahat edecek misin?
Is she going to help?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.Yardım edecek mi?
Kolay yol
  • Soruda be başa gelir: Are you...? Is he...?
  • What / Where ile: What are you going to do tomorrow?

4. Ne Zaman Kullanırız? (Plans & Predictions)

be going to iki durumda kullanılır: (1) önceden yaptığın bir plan / niyet, (2) kanıta bakıp yaptığın bir tahmin:

KullanımAnlamÖrnek
plan / intentionplan, niyetWe are going to visit Antalya next month.
predictionkanıtlı tahminLook at the clouds! It is going to rain.
Kolay yol
  • Zaman ifadeleri ipucudur: tomorrow, tonight, next weekend, next month.
  • "Look!", "Be careful!" → genelde bir tahmin gelir: it's going to fall.

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

be going to yaparken be biçimini ya da fiili karıştırmak kolaydır. Bu tabloyu bir kontrol listesi gibi kullan:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I going to study.I am going to study.be (am) unutma.
She are going to travel.She is going to travel.she (tekil) → is.
They is going to help.They are going to help.they (çoğul) → are.
He is going to plays.He is going to play.going to + yalın fiil.
I don't going to go.I 'm not going to go.olumsuzda don't değil, be + not.
Altın kural
  • Önce özneye göre be seç (am/is/are), sonra hep going to + yalın fiil. Olumsuzda be'nin yanına not, soruda be başa gelir. going to ile asla do/don't kullanma.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Chat)

İki genç hafta sonu ve tatil planlarını konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan going to biçimlerine dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What are you going to do this weekend?Bu hafta sonu ne yapacaksın?
  • — I am going to help my parents, and then Aylin is going to visit me.Ailene yardım edeceğim, sonra Aylin beni ziyaret edecek.
  • — Nice! Are you going to study together?Güzel! Birlikte ders mi çalışacaksınız?
  • — Yes, we are. We aren't going to waste the day; we are going to watch a film too.Evet. Günü boşa harcamayacağız; bir de film izleyeceğiz.
  • — Is your family going to travel this summer?Ailen bu yaz seyahat edecek mi?
  • — We are going to fly to Antalya, but my brother isn't going to come. He is going to stay with our grandparents.Antalya'ya uçacağız ama kardeşim gelmeyecek. Büyükannemlerde kalacak.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "She are going to" ✗ → "She is going to" ✓ (tekil → is).
  • "They is going to" ✗ → "They are going to" ✓ (çoğul → are).
  • "I don't going to" ✗ → "I 'm not going to" ✓ (be + not).

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Talk about your plans for next weekend or the holiday. Write 5 sentences with "going to". (Gelecek hafta sonu ya da tatil planlarını anlat. going to ile 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • I ile → am going to. Tekil özneyle → is going to. Çoğul özneyle → are going to.
  • En az bir olumsuz (not going to) ve bir gelecek zaman ifadesi (tomorrow, next weekend) kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"Next weekend I am going to ..." "My family is going to ..." "We are going to ..." "I'm not going to ..." "Are you going to ...?"
Sample Answer 1. Next weekend I am going to visit my grandparents with my family.
2. On Saturday my friends and I are going to play football in the park.
3. My sister is going to study for her exam, so she isn't going to come.
4. I'm not going to stay at home all day; I am going to help my mum too.
5. Are you going to do something fun next weekend?

Reading: My Plans for the Holiday

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu going to biçimlerini hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

am going to · is going to · are going to · isn't going to · not going to

İpucu
  • Metin bir gencin gelecek planlarını anlatıyor: hafta sonu işleri, arkadaş ziyareti, tatil. going to biçimlerine dikkat et.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
My Plans for the Holiday

Hi! I am Deniz and I am sixteen. Next week our school is going to close for the summer holiday, so my friends and I are going to have a lot of free time. I am really excited because we are going to do many fun things together.

First, this weekend I am going to help my parents at home. On Saturday morning I am going to clean my room, and in the afternoon my dad and I are going to paint the garage. It isn't going to be easy, but it is going to be useful.

On Sunday my best friend Aylin is going to visit me. We are going to study English for two hours, and then we are going to watch a film. We are also going to make some snacks and revise in the garden. Aylin isn't going to stay for dinner because her family is going to travel to their village.

Next month my family is going to go on holiday. We are going to fly to Antalya and stay near the beach for a week. My little brother is going to swim every day, and my mum is going to read her new books. My cousin is going to join us for a few days too. I am going to take a lot of photos and learn to surf. My dad says the hotel is going to be quiet and comfortable.

I am not going to study during the holiday, but I am going to practise my English with a mobile app. Aylin and I are going to write short messages in English every day. Are you going to travel this summer? What are you going to do in your free time? I hope your plans are going to be as exciting as mine!

Writing: My Weekend Plans

Gelecek hafta sonu ya da tatil planlarını anlat ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Describe your weekend or holiday plans; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI am going to swim and my sister is going to surf.
butamaWe are going to travel, but my brother isn't going to come.
becauseçünküI am going to rest because the holiday is going to be long.
sobu yüzdenWe are going to leave early, so we aren't going to be late.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but, because ya da so.

Task (Görev)

Describe your plans for next weekend or the holiday: where you are going to go, who is going to be with you and what you are going to do. (Gelecek hafta sonu ya da tatil planlarını anlat: nereye gideceksin, yanında kim olacak ve ne yapacaksın.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one sentence with am going to (I am going to ...)
  • one sentence with is/are going to (She is going to ... / We are going to ...)
  • one future time expression (tomorrow, next weekend, next month)
  • one negative (I'm not going to / isn't going to / aren't going to)
  • one connector: and / but / because / so

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Next weekend I am going to ..." "We are going to ..., but ..." "My family is going to ..."
