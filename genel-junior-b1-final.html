Final Sınavı: Tüm Üniteler (1-19) (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final, B1 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: Present Perfect karşıtlıkları (been/gone, for/since, Past Simple ile fark); Present Perfect Continuous; Past Perfect; gelecek zaman biçimleri (will / going to / present continuous / present simple); Second Conditional; edilgen çatı (Passive); dolaylı anlatım (Reported Speech); dolaylı sorular ve emirler; olasılık modalları (may/might/could); zorunluluk modalları (must/have to/mustn't/don't have to); gerund ve infinitive; ilgi cümlecikleri (who/which/whose/where, defining/non-defining); used to; soru biçimleri (dolaylı sorular, özne-nesne soruları, question tags); bağlaçlar (although/despite/because of/so...that); belirteçler ve niceleyiciler (determiners & quantifiers); artikellerin ileri kullanımı (articles); phrasal verbs; sıfat ve zarflar (adjectives & adverbs). Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

You will watch a short TED-Ed video, "Why can't some birds fly?", about how and why some birds lost the ability to fly. Watch it twice, then answer the twenty questions below. B1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Text 1 bir çocuğun geçmiş hikâyesini, Text 2 bir okul bahçesi projesinin geleceğini anlatır. (Text 1 tells a child's past story; Text 2 is about a school garden's future plan.)

Text 1 · Lina and the Little Robot

Lina had never been very interested in science until the winter she turned eleven. Her older cousin, who had studied engineering at university, came to stay for the holidays and brought a small box of motors and wires. By the end of the first week, Lina had already built a tiny robot that could move across the kitchen table. "You have a real talent for this," her cousin told her, and Lina has remembered those words ever since.

Building the robot was not easy. Lina tried three times before it finally worked, and while she was waiting for new parts, she had been reading every science book in the school library. Her teacher, Mr Kaya, noticed how hard she was working and lent her an old laptop. Lina says that this help was more important than any lesson, because it taught her never to give up when something went wrong.

Since that winter, Lina has entered two school competitions. Last year her robot won first prize, and now younger children often ask her to teach them. She told the school newspaper that she still could not quite believe it. "If I had given up after my first failure," she said, "none of this would have happened."

Text 2 · A New Garden for Our School

The head teacher has just announced an exciting plan for the empty yard behind our school, which has been closed to pupils for almost ten years. If the plan is accepted next month, work will begin in the spring, and the whole area is going to be turned into a garden and a small outdoor classroom.

Many pupils say that the garden could become the best part of the school. "If we don't start now, the old shed will fall down completely," said one teacher. Under the plan, the old greenhouse, which was built in 1995, is going to be repaired rather than knocked down, because a lot of people believe it should be saved. A new path will connect the garden to the sports field, so pupils will be able to walk there in two minutes.

Not everyone is happy. Some parents are worried that the building work will be noisy for a whole year. Others argue that the money would be better spent on new computers. A report which was written last week suggested that the project might cost twice as much as the school expects.

The head teacher has promised that pupils and parents will be asked for their ideas before any final decision is made. "If parents were against the plan," she said, "we would not go ahead." The first meeting is going to be held next Friday.

B1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 12 min, Task 2: 23 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 12 dk, Görev 2: 23 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Semi-formal Email: A Problem with a Club (100-130 words)

You signed up for the Saturday art club at your school, but you have just received a message saying the club has been moved to a different day. Write a semi-formal email to your teacher. Explain the problem, say how the change affects you, and politely ask what can be done. (Okulundaki cumartesi resim kulübüne kaydoldun, ama kulübün başka bir güne alındığına dair bir mesaj aldın. Öğretmenine yarı-resmi bir e-posta yaz: sorunu açıkla, bu değişikliğin seni nasıl etkilediğini söyle ve kibarca ne yapılabileceğini sor.)

  • Use a semi-formal opening and closing: "Dear Mr / Ms ...," ... "Yours sincerely," / "Best regards,". (yarı-resmi açılış ve kapanış)
  • Use at least one passive: "I was told that...", "the club has been moved...". (en az bir edilgen yapı)
  • Use reported speech: "The message said that...". (dolaylı anlatım)
  • Make a polite request with would / could: "I would be grateful if you could...". (kibar rica)
  • 100-130 words. (100-130 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Dear Ms ...," + yazma sebebin (I am writing about / regarding...).
  • Gövde: sorun (passive + reported speech) → nasıl etkiledi → kibar rica (would/could).
  • Kapanış: "I look forward to hearing from you." + "Yours sincerely," / "Best regards," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Opinion Paragraph: Pets at Home (170-220 words)

Some children think that every family should have a pet, while others think that pets are too much work. What is your opinion? Write a paragraph giving your view with clear reasons and examples. (Bazı çocuklar her ailenin bir evcil hayvanı olması gerektiğini, bazıları ise evcil hayvanların çok iş çıkardığını düşünür. Senin görüşün ne? Görüşünü net gerekçeler ve örneklerle bir paragrafta yaz.)

  • State your opinion clearly and support it. (görüşünü net belirt ve gerekçelendir)
  • Use at least two linkers: although, despite, because of, so...that. (en az iki bağlaç)
  • Include one Second Conditional sentence: "If I had a pet, I would...". (bir Second Conditional cümlesi)
  • Use Present Perfect and modals (should, could, might). (Present Perfect ve modallar)
  • 170-220 words. (170-220 kelime)
