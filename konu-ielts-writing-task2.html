1. Task 2 Nedir? Temel Kurallar

IELTS Writing Task 2, sana verilen bir prompt (soru + yönerge) üzerine en az 250 kelimelik akademik bir deneme yazmanı ister. Task 1 ile birlikte toplam 60 dakikan vardır; Task 2 daha yüksek puanlı olduğu için 40 dakika ayırman önerilir. Task 2 puanı, toplam Writing bandının %66'sını (Task 1 %33) oluşturur — yani buradaki başarı, yazma notunu doğrudan belirler.

Süre

40 dakika (planlama + yazma + kontrol). Task 1'i 20 dakikada bitir, Task 2'ye 40 dakika bırak.

Uzunluk

En az 250 kelime. Altında kalırsan ceza alırsın. İdeal aralık: 260-290 kelime (yeterli gelişim + zaman).

Format

Resmî akademik deneme: giriş + 2 gövde paragrafı + sonuç. Madde işareti (bullet) veya not yok; tam cümlelerle yaz.

Ton

Yarı-resmî akademik. Kısaltma yok (don't → do not), günlük deyim yok, kişisel abartı yok.

Task 2 puanı dört kriterin ortalamasıdır; her biri eşit ağırlıktadır (%25). Yani mükemmel gramer tek başına 9 getirmez — soruyu tam yanıtlaman, fikirleri mantıklı bağlaman ve zengin kelime kullanman da gerekir. Bir kriterdeki zayıflık toplam bandı aşağı çeker.

1. What is Task 2? The Basic Rules

IELTS Writing Task 2 asks you to write an academic essay of at least 250 words on a given prompt (question + instruction). Together with Task 1 you have 60 minutes in total; because Task 2 is worth more, it is recommended that you allow 40 minutes for it. The Task 2 score makes up 66% of the total Writing band (Task 1 is 33%) — so success here directly determines your writing grade.

Time

40 minutes (planning + writing + checking). Finish Task 1 in 20 minutes and leave 40 for Task 2.

Length

At least 250 words. Fall below and you are penalised. Ideal range: 260-290 words (enough development + time).

Format

A formal academic essay: introduction + 2 body paragraphs + conclusion. No bullet points or notes; write in full sentences.

Tone

Semi-formal academic. No contractions (don't → do not), no everyday idioms, no personal exaggeration.

The Task 2 score is the average of four criteria, each weighted equally (25%). So perfect grammar alone does not earn a 9 — you also need to answer the question fully, connect ideas logically and use rich vocabulary. A weakness in one criterion pulls the total band down.

2. The Four Assessment Criteria (In Depth)

The IELTS examiner scores your essay on four separate criteria. Below you see what each criterion measures and what concretely changes between band 5 and 9.

2.1 Task Response — 25%

Did you answer exactly what the question asks? Is there a clear position (thesis), did you address all parts, did you develop your ideas with examples and explanation? The most frequent band loss comes from here: misreading the question or skipping part of it.

BandWhat Task Response looks like
5Position unclear/inconsistent; main ideas present but undeveloped or drifting off topic; the question is only partly answered.
6Position clear but development uneven; some ideas insufficiently supported; a weak conclusion.
7A clear position is maintained throughout; main ideas are extended and supported; occasional over-generalisation.
9The question is fully answered; well-developed, relevant, well-supported ideas; the position is entirely clear.

2.2 Coherence & Cohesion — 25%

Do the ideas flow in a logical order (coherence) and are sentences/paragraphs linked with the right connectors (cohesion)? Paragraphing, reference (this, such, the latter), use of connectors and avoiding repetition are assessed here.

BandWhat Coherence & Cohesion looks like
5Connectors present but mechanical/incorrect (Moreover, But so...); weak or no paragraphing; unclear reference.
6Information logically organised; connectors effective but sometimes over/under-used; each paragraph has a central idea.
7Ideas progress logically; connectors are varied and correct; reference and avoidance of repetition are fluent; clear paragraphing.
9Cohesion is unobtrusive — reading is entirely fluent; paragraphing is flawless; connectors work without ever drawing attention.

2.3 Lexical Resource — 25%

The range, accuracy and natural use of your vocabulary. Collocation (correct word partnerships), less common words, paraphrasing skill and spelling/word-choice errors are measured here. It is not the memorised "fancy word" but the apt word that earns marks.

BandWhat Lexical Resource looks like
5Limited vocabulary; adequate for the topic but repetitive; noticeable spelling/choice errors can hinder communication.
6Adequate vocabulary; some less common words attempted but with errors; collocation occasionally wrong.
7Flexible use of vocabulary; awareness of less common words and collocation; occasional word-choice/spelling error.
9Natural and sophisticated vocabulary; rare words perfectly placed; flawless collocation; virtually no errors.

2.4 Grammatical Range & Accuracy — 25%

The variety (a mix of simple + complex sentences) and accuracy of the structures you use. Error-free simple sentences alone do not earn a high band — you must use structures like conditionals, relative clauses and the passive correctly.

BandWhat Grammar looks like
5Limited structures; attempts at complex sentences are very error-prone; errors are frequent and strain the reader.
6A mix of simple and complex structures; errors present but they rarely obscure meaning.
7A variety of complex structures; most sentences error-free; a few errors remain but are minor.
9Full flexibility and accuracy; error-free apart from rare "slips"; variety of structure is natural.

The practical upshot: if you are aiming for band 7 you must hit at least 7 in every criterion. A single 6 (e.g. grammar), even with the others at 7, pulls the average down to 6.5. So focus on your weakest criterion.

"Your band is determined not by your strongest criterion but your weakest — if one of the four legs is short, the table still wobbles.

3. Essay Tipleri: Her Biri Ayrı İskelet + Tez Kalıbı

Task 2'de beş temel soru tipi karşına çıkar. Her tipin kendine özgü bir yapısı ve tez (thesis) kalıbı vardır. Yapılan en büyük hata, her soruyu aynı şablonla yazmaktır. Önce soruyu doğru sınıflandır, sonra o tipe uygun iskeleti kur.

Tip 1 — Opinion (Agree/Disagree)

Tanıma: "To what extent do you agree or disagree?" veya "Do you think... ?". Sana kendi görüşün sorulur ve baştan sona net bir pozisyon beklenir.

Some people believe that children should be taught to compete, while others think cooperation is more valuable. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

İskelet:

  • Giriş: soruyu parafraz et + net pozisyon (ör. tamamen katılıyorum / kısmen katılıyorum).
  • Gövde 1: pozisyonunu destekleyen 1. neden + açıklama + örnek.
  • Gövde 2: pozisyonunu destekleyen 2. neden + açıklama + örnek (ya da karşı görüşü kabul edip çürüt).
  • Sonuç: pozisyonu yeniden ifade et + özet.

Tez kalıbı:

While competitiveness has its merits, I firmly believe that teaching children to cooperate better prepares them for adult life.
(Kısmi/net pozisyon: "While X, I firmly believe Y" — hem karşı tarafı tanır hem net durur.)

Tip 2 — Discussion (Discuss Both Views + Your Opinion)

Tanıma: "Discuss both these views and give your own opinion." İki görüşü de adil biçimde tartışman ve kendi görüşünü belirtmen istenir. En sık atlanan parça: "give your own opinion".

Some argue that university education should be free for everyone, while others believe students should pay their own tuition. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

İskelet:

  • Giriş: parafraz + iki görüşün olduğunu belirt + kendi görüşünü kısaca ilan et.
  • Gövde 1: birinci görüş + neden bazıları böyle düşünür + örnek.
  • Gövde 2: ikinci görüş + neden diğerleri böyle düşünür + örnek + hangisine ve neden katıldığın.
  • Sonuç: her iki görüşü özetle + net kendi pozisyonun.

Tez kalıbı:

Although free higher education promotes equality, I would argue that a contributory system is more sustainable in the long run.
(Her iki tarafı da tanı, ama "I would argue that..." ile kendi tarafını netleştir.)

Tip 3 — Advantages / Disadvantages

Tanıma: "What are the advantages and disadvantages?" veya "Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?" İkinci varyantta mutlaka bir tartı (hangisi ağır basıyor) yapman gerekir.

More and more people are working from home rather than in an office. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

İskelet ("outweigh" varyantı):

  • Giriş: parafraz + hangi tarafın ağır bastığına dair net yargı.
  • Gövde 1: avantajlar + açıklama + örnek.
  • Gövde 2: dezavantajlar + açıklama + neden yine de avantajların ağır bastığı (ya da tersi).
  • Sonuç: tartıyı tekrarla.

Tez kalıbı:

Despite certain drawbacks such as social isolation, I believe the benefits of remote work — chiefly flexibility and productivity — clearly outweigh them.
("outweigh" sorusunda net bir tartı şart: "the benefits... clearly outweigh them".)

Tip 4 — Problem / Solution (Cause / Solution)

Tanıma: "What problems does this cause and what solutions can you suggest?" veya "What are the causes and what measures can be taken?" Her paragrafta bir problem/nedeni bir de ona bağlı çözümü ele almak en güçlü yapıdır.

Traffic congestion is becoming a serious issue in many large cities. What are the causes of this problem and what measures could be taken to solve it?

İskelet:

  • Giriş: parafraz + iki nedeni ve çözümleri ele alacağını belirt.
  • Gövde 1: ana neden(ler) + açıklama + örnek.
  • Gövde 2: uygulanabilir çözüm(ler) + nasıl işe yarar + örnek.
  • Sonuç: neden + çözümü özetle.

Tez kalıbı:

This essay will first examine the main drivers of urban congestion, namely rising car ownership and inadequate public transport, before proposing practical remedies.
(Yol haritası niyetli tez: neyi hangi sırayla ele alacağını söyler.)

Tip 5 — Two-Part Question (Direct Questions)

Tanıma: İki ayrı doğrudan soru içerir; genelde "agree/disagree" veya "discuss" ifadesi yoktur. İkisini de yanıtlamazsan Task Response düşer.

Many people today spend a lot of money on their appearance. Why do they do this? Is this a positive or negative development?

İskelet:

  • Giriş: parafraz + iki soruya da kısa yanıt önizlemesi.
  • Gövde 1: 1. soruyu yanıtla (neden) + açıklama + örnek.
  • Gövde 2: 2. soruyu yanıtla (olumlu mu olumsuz mu) + gerekçe + örnek.
  • Sonuç: her iki yanıtı birleştir.

Tez kalıbı:

People invest heavily in their looks largely because of social media pressure, and while this brings some confidence, I regard it as a mostly negative trend.
(Tek cümlede iki soruya da yanıt: "because of X" + "I regard it as a mostly negative trend".)

3. Essay Types: Each Its Own Skeleton + Thesis Template

In Task 2 you face five basic question types. Each type has its own structure and thesis template. The biggest mistake made is writing every question with the same template. First classify the question correctly, then build the skeleton that fits that type.

Type 1 — Opinion (Agree/Disagree)

How to recognise it: "To what extent do you agree or disagree?" or "Do you think... ?". You are asked for your own opinion and a clear position is expected throughout.

Some people believe that children should be taught to compete, while others think cooperation is more valuable. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Skeleton:

  • Introduction: paraphrase the question + a clear position (e.g. fully agree / partly agree).
  • Body 1: reason 1 supporting your position + explanation + example.
  • Body 2: reason 2 supporting your position + explanation + example (or acknowledge the opposing view and rebut it).
  • Conclusion: restate the position + summary.

Thesis template:

While competitiveness has its merits, I firmly believe that teaching children to cooperate better prepares them for adult life.
(Partial/clear position: "While X, I firmly believe Y" — it acknowledges the other side yet stands firm.)

Type 2 — Discussion (Discuss Both Views + Your Opinion)

How to recognise it: "Discuss both these views and give your own opinion." You are asked to discuss both views fairly and state your own opinion. The most commonly skipped part: "give your own opinion".

Some argue that university education should be free for everyone, while others believe students should pay their own tuition. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Skeleton:

  • Introduction: paraphrase + note that there are two views + briefly declare your own opinion.
  • Body 1: the first view + why some think this + example.
  • Body 2: the second view + why others think this + example + which one you agree with and why.
  • Conclusion: summarise both views + your clear position.

Thesis template:

Although free higher education promotes equality, I would argue that a contributory system is more sustainable in the long run.
(Acknowledge both sides, but make your own side clear with "I would argue that...".)

Type 3 — Advantages / Disadvantages

How to recognise it: "What are the advantages and disadvantages?" or "Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?" In the second variant you must make a judgement (which one outweighs).

More and more people are working from home rather than in an office. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

Skeleton ("outweigh" variant):

  • Introduction: paraphrase + a clear verdict on which side outweighs.
  • Body 1: advantages + explanation + example.
  • Body 2: disadvantages + explanation + why the advantages still outweigh (or vice versa).
  • Conclusion: restate the verdict.

Thesis template:

Despite certain drawbacks such as social isolation, I believe the benefits of remote work — chiefly flexibility and productivity — clearly outweigh them.
(In an "outweigh" question a clear verdict is essential: "the benefits... clearly outweigh them".)

Type 4 — Problem / Solution (Cause / Solution)

How to recognise it: "What problems does this cause and what solutions can you suggest?" or "What are the causes and what measures can be taken?" Addressing one problem/cause and a linked solution in each paragraph is the strongest structure.

Traffic congestion is becoming a serious issue in many large cities. What are the causes of this problem and what measures could be taken to solve it?

Skeleton:

  • Introduction: paraphrase + state that you will address two causes and their solutions.
  • Body 1: the main cause(s) + explanation + example.
  • Body 2: workable solution(s) + how it works + example.
  • Conclusion: summarise cause + solution.

Thesis template:

This essay will first examine the main drivers of urban congestion, namely rising car ownership and inadequate public transport, before proposing practical remedies.
(A roadmap-style thesis: it says what you will address and in what order.)

Type 5 — Two-Part Question (Direct Questions)

How to recognise it: It contains two separate direct questions; usually there is no "agree/disagree" or "discuss". If you don't answer both, Task Response drops.

Many people today spend a lot of money on their appearance. Why do they do this? Is this a positive or negative development?

Skeleton:

  • Introduction: paraphrase + a short preview answer to both questions.
  • Body 1: answer question 1 (why) + explanation + example.
  • Body 2: answer question 2 (positive or negative) + justification + example.
  • Conclusion: combine both answers.

Thesis template:

People invest heavily in their looks largely because of social media pressure, and while this brings some confidence, I regard it as a mostly negative trend.
(Both questions answered in one sentence: "because of X" + "I regard it as a mostly negative trend".)

4. Paragraf Yapısı: Her Bloğun İşlevi

Yüksek bandlı bir denemenin iskeleti nettir: giriş → 2 gövde paragrafı → sonuç. Her paragrafın belirli bir işi vardır; bu işi yerine getirmediğinde okur (değerlendirici) kaybolur.

Giriş = Parafraz + Tez

İki cümle yeter. (1) Sorunun konusunu kendi kelimelerinle yeniden ifade et — sorudaki kelimeleri kopyalama. (2) Net tezini/pozisyonunu ver. Giriş kısa olmalı; asıl puan gövdededir.

Zayıf (kopya parafraz): Nowadays children should be taught to compete or cooperate.
Güçlü: There is ongoing debate over whether young people benefit more from a competitive or a collaborative upbringing. While rivalry can be motivating, I am convinced that cooperative skills are ultimately more beneficial.

Gövde = Topic Sentence + Açıklama + Örnek (+ bağ)

Her gövde paragrafı tek bir ana fikir içerir. Yapı: PEEL — Point (topic sentence), Explain (neden/nasıl), Example (somut örnek), Link (teze geri bağla). Örnekler uydurma-somut olabilir; gerçek istatistik gerekmez, mantıklı ve konuya uygun olması yeter.

Cooperation equips children with skills that the modern workplace demands. (Point) Most professional achievements today depend on teamwork rather than individual brilliance. (Explain) Engineers, for instance, rarely build a bridge alone; they coordinate with architects, planners and technicians. (Example) A child who learns to share ideas early is therefore better positioned for such collaborative careers. (Link)

Sonuç = Pozisyonu Yeniden İfade + Özet

Yeni fikir ekleme. Tezini farklı kelimelerle tekrarla ve gövdedeki ana noktaları birkaç kelimeyle topla. Bir "so what" (sonuç/öneri) cümlesi kapanışı güçlendirir.

In conclusion, although a degree of competition can spur children on, the ability to collaborate is a far more valuable asset. Schools would therefore do well to prioritise cooperative learning.

4. Paragraph Structure: The Function of Each Block

The skeleton of a high-band essay is clear: introduction → 2 body paragraphs → conclusion. Each paragraph has a specific job; when it fails to do that job, the reader (examiner) gets lost.

Introduction = Paraphrase + Thesis

Two sentences are enough. (1) Restate the topic of the question in your own words — do not copy the words in the question. (2) Give your clear thesis/position. The introduction should be short; the real marks are in the body.

Weak (copied paraphrase): Nowadays children should be taught to compete or cooperate.
Strong: There is ongoing debate over whether young people benefit more from a competitive or a collaborative upbringing. While rivalry can be motivating, I am convinced that cooperative skills are ultimately more beneficial.

Body = Topic Sentence + Explanation + Example (+ link)

Each body paragraph contains a single main idea. Structure: PEEL — Point (topic sentence), Explain (why/how), Example (a concrete example), Link (back to the thesis). Examples can be invented-but-concrete; you don't need real statistics, only something logical and relevant.

Cooperation equips children with skills that the modern workplace demands. (Point) Most professional achievements today depend on teamwork rather than individual brilliance. (Explain) Engineers, for instance, rarely build a bridge alone; they coordinate with architects, planners and technicians. (Example) A child who learns to share ideas early is therefore better positioned for such collaborative careers. (Link)

Conclusion = Restate the Position + Summary

Do not add a new idea. Repeat your thesis in different words and gather the main points of the body in a few words. A "so what" (conclusion/recommendation) sentence strengthens the closing.

In conclusion, although a degree of competition can spur children on, the ability to collaborate is a far more valuable asset. Schools would therefore do well to prioritise cooperative learning.

5. Bağdaşıklık: Bağlaçlar, Referans, Tekrar-Önleme

Cohesion, cümleleri "yapıştıran" mekanizmadır. Ama dikkat çeken cohesion (her cümle başına Firstly, Moreover, In addition) band düşürür. Amaç: bağlantı fark edilmeden akmalı.

Yapma: her paragrafa Firstly / Secondly / Lastly dizmek; Moreover, But... gibi hatalı eşleştirme; virgül hatalı bağlaç (However it is... — nokta ya da noktalı virgül gerek).

Referans ve tekrar-önleme

Aynı ismi tekrar tekrar yazmak yerine this, these, such, the former/the latter, it, they ve eş anlamlılar kullan. Bu hem cohesion hem lexical resource puanını yükseltir.

Tekrarlı: Pollution is rising. Pollution harms health. To reduce pollution, governments must act.
Bağdaşık: Air pollution is rising steadily. This growing threat harms public health, and to curb it, governments must act decisively.

5. Cohesion: Connectors, Reference, Avoiding Repetition

Cohesion is the mechanism that "glues" sentences together. But conspicuous cohesion (a Firstly, Moreover, In addition at the start of every sentence) lowers your band. The aim: linking should flow unnoticed.

Use connectors correctly (apt, varied)

FunctionNatural options
AdditionFurthermore, In addition, What is more, Beyond this
ContrastHowever, Nevertheless, On the other hand, That said
Cause/effectConsequently, As a result, This is largely because, Hence
ExampleFor instance, To illustrate, A case in point is
Conclusion/summaryIn conclusion, To sum up, On balance

Don't: line up Firstly / Secondly / Lastly in every paragraph; mismatch connectors like Moreover, But...; comma-splice a connector (However it is... — a full stop or semicolon is needed).

Reference and avoiding repetition

Instead of writing the same noun over and over, use this, these, such, the former/the latter, it, they and synonyms. This raises both your cohesion and lexical resource score.

Repetitive: Pollution is rising. Pollution harms health. To reduce pollution, governments must act.
Cohesive: Air pollution is rising steadily. This growing threat harms public health, and to curb it, governments must act decisively.

6. Güçlü Tez ve Argüman Geliştirme

"Zayıf tez konuyu duyurur; güçlü tez pozisyon alır. Okur daha girişte hangi tarafı savunduğunu bilmeli.

Tez (thesis), denemenin omurgasıdır: okur girişte ne savunacağını bilmeli. Zayıf tez belirsizdir ("This essay will discuss both sides."); güçlü tez pozisyon alır.

Zayıf tezGüçlü tez

I will discuss both views.While both approaches have merit, I am firmly convinced that prevention is more cost-effective than treatment.

Argümanı geliştirmek: fikir yeterli değil, gelişim şart

Band 5-6 denemelerde fikirler listelenir ama açılmaz. Band 7+ için her fikri şu üç adımla derinleştir:

  1. Neden? — fikri mantıkla gerekçelendir (This is because...).
  2. Nasıl/örnek? — somut bir senaryoya indir (For example, a company that...).
  3. Sonuç? — bu fikrin doğurduğu etkiyi göster (As a result...).
Remote work boosts productivity. (sadece iddia — yetersiz) → Remote work tends to boost productivity because employees avoid long commutes and can structure their day around peak focus hours. A software developer, for example, may complete in six focused hours what an office would stretch across eight. Consequently, output per worker often rises.

6. A Strong Thesis and Developing the Argument

"A weak thesis announces the topic; a strong thesis takes a position. The reader should know which side you defend from the introduction itself.

The thesis is the backbone of the essay: the reader must know from the introduction what you will defend. A weak thesis is vague ("This essay will discuss both sides."); a strong thesis takes a position.

Weak thesisStrong thesis
There are advantages and disadvantages to this.Although online learning widens access, its lack of interaction makes it inferior for younger students.
I will discuss both views.While both approaches have merit, I am firmly convinced that prevention is more cost-effective than treatment.

Developing the argument: an idea is not enough, development is essential

In band 5-6 essays ideas are listed but not opened up. For band 7+ deepen every idea with these three steps:

  1. Why? — justify the idea with logic (This is because...).
  2. How/example? — bring it down to a concrete scenario (For example, a company that...).
  3. Result? — show the effect this idea produces (As a result...).
Remote work boosts productivity. (claim only — insufficient) → Remote work tends to boost productivity because employees avoid long commutes and can structure their day around peak focus hours. A software developer, for example, may complete in six focused hours what an office would stretch across eight. Consequently, output per worker often rises.

7. Academic Language: Hedging, Nominalisation, Collocation

A high band requires not "big words" but academic maturity. Three tools make the difference:

7.1 Hedging (cautious language)

Avoid absolute/exaggerated claims; keep claims measured with expressions like tend to, may, is likely to, in many cases, arguably, to some extent. This adds academic credibility.

Exaggerated: Social media destroys all real friendships.
Hedged: Social media can, in some cases, weaken face-to-face relationships.

For deeper examples and templates: Academic Hedging.

7.2 Nominalisation (turning verbs into nouns)

Academic writing packs information into noun phrases rather than verbs. This makes a sentence more formal and information-dense.

Everyday: Because the population grew quickly, cities became crowded.
Academic: Rapid population growth led to urban overcrowding.

7.3 Collocation (correct word partnerships)

Words come with the right "partner". make research is wrong, conduct research is correct. Correct collocation is the heart of lexical resource.

9. Tam Model Essay (Band 8+) — Paragraf-Paragraf Açıklamalı

Soru (Opinion tipi): Some people believe that governments should invest more in public transport, while others think building more roads is a better solution to traffic problems. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

(Not: Bu aslında bir Discussion sorusu; net kendi görüş şart. Aşağıdaki model bu gereksinimi karşılar.)

Giriş — parafraz + iki görüş + net tez

As urban traffic worsens, opinion is divided over the best remedy: some advocate greater spending on public transport, whereas others favour the construction of additional roads. Although expanding the road network offers short-term relief, I firmly believe that investment in public transport provides a more sustainable long-term solution.

Neden çalışıyor: Soru kopyalanmadan parafraz edildi ("opinion is divided"), iki görüş de tanındı, altı çizili cümlede net kişisel pozisyon var — Task Response'un çekirdeği.

Gövde 1 — road-building görüşü (adilce sun, sınırını göster)

Those who support building more roads argue that increased capacity eases congestion directly. When a city adds lanes or bypasses, vehicles can disperse more freely, reducing bottlenecks at peak times. Los Angeles, for example, has repeatedly widened its highways in an attempt to accommodate growing traffic. However, this approach often triggers "induced demand": as roads expand, more drivers are encouraged onto them, and congestion soon returns to its former level.

Neden çalışıyor: Karşı görüş güçlü sunuldu (topic sentence + açıklama + örnek), ardından altı çizili cümlede zarifçe eleştirildi — bu, gövdeyi kendi teze bağlar.

Gövde 2 — public transport görüşü + kendi pozisyon

In contrast, investing in public transport addresses the root of the problem by reducing the number of private vehicles altogether. Efficient metro and bus networks allow large numbers of commuters to travel without adding cars to the road, which in turn lowers both congestion and emissions. Cities such as Vienna, where affordable and reliable transit is prioritised, enjoy noticeably smoother traffic flow. For this reason, I am convinced that public transport represents the wiser investment, since it delivers environmental as well as practical benefits.

Neden çalışıyor: İkinci görüş gelişti ve altı çizili cümle kişisel görüşü gerekçesiyle netleştirdi ("since it delivers... benefits"). Discussion sorusunun üçüncü şartı (own opinion) burada karşılandı.

Sonuç — özet + pozisyonu yeniden ifade

In conclusion, while constructing more roads may relieve congestion temporarily, this benefit tends to be short-lived. A sustained commitment to public transport, by contrast, tackles the underlying causes of traffic, and it is therefore the option I would strongly endorse.

Neden çalışıyor: Yeni fikir yok; iki görüş özetlendi ve tez farklı kelimelerle tekrarlandı. "short-lived", "underlying causes" gibi yerinde kelime lexical resource'u destekler.

Toplam ~275 kelime — ideal aralıkta. Dört kriter de karşılanıyor: net pozisyon (TR), akıcı bağdaşıklık (CC), zengin ve doğru kelime (LR), çeşitli karmaşık yapı (GRA).

9. A Full Model Essay (Band 8+) — Explained Paragraph by Paragraph

Question (Opinion type): Some people believe that governments should invest more in public transport, while others think building more roads is a better solution to traffic problems. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

(Note: This is actually a Discussion question; a clear own opinion is essential. The model below meets that requirement.)

Introduction — paraphrase + both views + clear thesis

As urban traffic worsens, opinion is divided over the best remedy: some advocate greater spending on public transport, whereas others favour the construction of additional roads. Although expanding the road network offers short-term relief, I firmly believe that investment in public transport provides a more sustainable long-term solution.

Why it works: The question is paraphrased without copying ("opinion is divided"), both views are acknowledged, and the underlined sentence gives a clear personal position — the core of Task Response.

Body 1 — the road-building view (present it fairly, show its limit)

Those who support building more roads argue that increased capacity eases congestion directly. When a city adds lanes or bypasses, vehicles can disperse more freely, reducing bottlenecks at peak times. Los Angeles, for example, has repeatedly widened its highways in an attempt to accommodate growing traffic. However, this approach often triggers "induced demand": as roads expand, more drivers are encouraged onto them, and congestion soon returns to its former level.

Why it works: The opposing view is presented strongly (topic sentence + explanation + example), then gracefully critiqued in the underlined sentence — this ties the body back to your thesis.

Body 2 — the public transport view + your own position

In contrast, investing in public transport addresses the root of the problem by reducing the number of private vehicles altogether. Efficient metro and bus networks allow large numbers of commuters to travel without adding cars to the road, which in turn lowers both congestion and emissions. Cities such as Vienna, where affordable and reliable transit is prioritised, enjoy noticeably smoother traffic flow. For this reason, I am convinced that public transport represents the wiser investment, since it delivers environmental as well as practical benefits.

Why it works: The second view is developed and the underlined sentence makes the personal opinion clear with its justification ("since it delivers... benefits"). The third requirement of a Discussion question (own opinion) is met here.

Conclusion — summary + restate the position

In conclusion, while constructing more roads may relieve congestion temporarily, this benefit tends to be short-lived. A sustained commitment to public transport, by contrast, tackles the underlying causes of traffic, and it is therefore the option I would strongly endorse.

Why it works: No new idea; both views are summarised and the thesis is repeated in different words. Apt words like "short-lived", "underlying causes" support lexical resource.

Total ~275 words — in the ideal range. All four criteria are met: clear position (TR), fluent cohesion (CC), rich and accurate vocabulary (LR), varied complex structure (GRA).

10. Her essay tipi için örnek soru + fikir bankası + model essay

§9'da bir Discussion sorusunu paragraf paragraf çözdük. Aşağıda kalan dört tip için ayrı ayrı: gerçek sınav sorusu, boş sayfaya bakıp donmanı önleyen bir fikir bankası ve tam bir band 8+ model essay var. Fikir bankasını kopyalama — bir fikir seç, kendi cümlenle yaz, kendi örneğini ekle.

10.1 · Opinion (Agree / Disagree)

Some people believe that schools should teach practical skills such as cooking and money management instead of academic subjects like history. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Fikir bankası

Katılma tarafı

  • Bütçe yönetimi bilmeyen genç borca giriyor — üniversite ilk yılında kredi kartı borcu.
  • Pratik beceriler evrensel: herkes yemek yer, para harcar; herkes tarih bilgisine muhtaç değil.
  • Okul hayata hazırlamalı: 12 yıl okuyup vergi beyannamesi dolduramamak.

Karşı taraf

  • Akademik dersler düşünme biçimi kazandırır: tarih, kanıt değerlendirmeyi öğretir.
  • Pratik beceri sonradan öğrenilir, eleştirel düşünme yıllar alır.
  • İşverenler zaten analiz ve problem çözme arıyor.

En güçlü pozisyon: pratik beceriler eklenmeli ama akademik derslerin yerine değil, yanına (alongside, not instead of). Böylece iki tarafı da kullanırsın.

Model essay (~270 kelime)

Giriş: It is sometimes argued that schools ought to replace traditional academic subjects with practical life skills such as cooking and budgeting. While I agree that these skills are undervalued, I do not believe they should come at the expense of academic learning; the two are better taught together.
Gövde 1: There is a strong case for teaching practical skills, because many young people leave school unable to manage the basic demands of adult life. Financial literacy is the clearest example: a large proportion of university students fall into credit-card debt in their first year, simply because no one has explained how interest works. A single term of money management could prevent years of hardship, which suggests that such skills deserve a genuine place in the curriculum.
Gövde 2: However, abandoning academic subjects would be a serious mistake, since disciplines like history do more than transmit facts. History teaches students to weigh evidence, compare conflicting accounts and question the reliability of a source — habits of mind that cannot be picked up over a weekend. Employers themselves consistently rank analytical thinking above narrow technical ability, precisely because it is so difficult to acquire later in life.
Sonuç: In conclusion, I partly agree that practical skills matter, but the solution is to add them alongside academic study rather than to substitute one for the other. A curriculum that produces citizens who can both balance a budget and think critically serves students far better than one that sacrifices either.

Neden yüksek band: giriş net pozisyon alıyor (yanına, yerine değil) ve sonuna kadar korunuyor; her gövde iddia + gerekçe + somut örnek (borç / tarih); at the expense of, habits of mind, substitute one for the other gibi doğal kelime.

10.2 · Advantages and Disadvantages

More and more people are working from home rather than in an office. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

Fikir bankası

Avantajlar

  • Yol süresi kazanılır — İstanbul'da günde 2 saat spora/aileye/uykuya döner.
  • Esneklik: ebeveynler ve sağlık sorunu olanlar iş hayatında kalabilir.
  • Şirket maliyeti düşer, daha geniş havuzdan işe alım.

Dezavantajlar

  • İzolasyon: aidiyet ve motivasyon kaybı.
  • İş–ev sınırı kaybolur, mesai uzar.
  • Genç çalışan deneyimliyi izleyerek öğrenemez.

En güçlü pozisyon: avantajlar ağır basar, çünkü dezavantajlar yönetilebilir (hibrit model, net mesai saati), avantajlar ise yapısaldır.

Model essay (~275 kelime)

Giriş: Remote working has expanded rapidly, with a growing number of employees now based at home rather than in a traditional office. Although this shift brings genuine drawbacks, I would argue that its advantages clearly outweigh them, particularly because the disadvantages can be managed.
Gövde 1 (avantaj): The principal benefit is the elimination of the daily commute, which returns valuable time to workers. In a city like Istanbul, an employee may spend two hours a day travelling; recovered, that time can be devoted to exercise, family or rest. Home working also offers flexibility to those who would otherwise leave the workforce altogether, such as parents of young children, while allowing companies to recruit talent regardless of location.
Gövde 2 (dezavantaj + neden hafif): The disadvantages are real but largely solvable. Isolation can erode motivation, and the boundary between work and home life often blurs, so that the working day never quite ends. Younger employees, moreover, may struggle to learn from experienced colleagues. Yet each of these problems has a practical remedy: hybrid schedules restore social contact, clear working hours protect personal time, and structured mentoring supports newcomers.
Sonuç: On balance, therefore, the benefits of working from home are more significant than its costs. Because the drawbacks can be addressed through sensible policies, whereas the gains in time and flexibility are structural, I firmly believe the advantages prevail.

Neden yüksek band: "outweigh" sorusunda net tartı kuruluyor; dezavantajları inkâr etmeden çürütüyor (real but largely solvable); on balance, prevail, structural ile değerlendirme dili.

10.3 · Problem / Solution (Cause + Measure)

Traffic congestion is becoming a serious problem in many large cities. What are the main causes of this problem and what measures could be taken to solve it?

Fikir bankası

Sebepler

  • Özel araç sahipliği artıyor; yollar daha küçük bir nüfusa göre tasarlanmıştı.
  • Toplu taşıma dış mahallelere ulaşmıyor — alternatif yok.
  • Dağınık şehir planı: konut, işyeri, okul birbirinden uzak.

Çözümler (her biri bir sebebe cevap)

  • Raylı sisteme yatırım → araç eklemeden çok insan taşır.
  • Tıkanma ücreti → ama önce alternatif (metro) olmalı.
  • Karma kullanımlı planlama → yolculuk ihtiyacını azaltır.

En güçlü hamle: pozisyon değil eşleşme. Her çözüm bir sebebe doğrudan cevap versin — en çok puan getiren yapı budur.

Model essay (~270 kelime)

Giriş: Severe traffic congestion has become a defining feature of life in many large cities. This essay will examine the two principal causes of the problem — rising car ownership and inadequate public transport — before proposing measures that address each directly.
Gövde 1 (sebepler): The first cause is the sheer growth in private vehicle ownership. As incomes rise, cars become both affordable and desirable, yet most road networks were designed for a far smaller population. A second, closely related cause is the weakness of public transport: in many cities, metro and bus routes fail to reach outlying districts, leaving residents there with no realistic alternative to driving.
Gövde 2 (eşleşen çözümler): Each cause suggests its own remedy. To reduce dependence on private cars, governments should invest heavily in rail systems, which can move large numbers of commuters without adding a single vehicle to the road. Where a reliable alternative already exists, a congestion charge can then discourage unnecessary driving, as London has demonstrated. Finally, encouraging mixed-use urban planning — placing homes, workplaces and schools closer together — tackles the problem at its root by removing the need for many journeys altogether.
Sonuç: In conclusion, urban congestion stems chiefly from growing car use and insufficient transport alternatives. By combining investment in public transport, carefully timed congestion charges and smarter planning, cities can address not merely the symptoms of the problem but its underlying causes.

Neden yüksek band: çözümler sebeplerle birebir eşleşiyor (Each cause suggests its own remedy); somut örnek (Londra); at its root, symptoms vs underlying causes ile derinlik.

10.4 · Two-part (Direct Questions)

In many countries, people are working longer hours than in the past. Why is this happening, and what effect does it have on family life?

Fikir bankası

Neden (1. soru)

  • Yükselen yaşam maliyeti — tek maaş yetmiyor.
  • Rekabetçi iş kültürü: uzun mesai "bağlılık" sanılıyor.
  • Teknoloji mesaiyi eve taşıyor — iş asla bitmiyor.

Etki (2. soru)

  • Ebeveyn-çocuk zamanı azalır.
  • Stres ve tükenmişlik ilişkileri zorlar.
  • Ortak yemek/etkinlik gibi aile ritüelleri kaybolur.

Kritik kural: two-part'ta her iki soru da yanıtlanmalı. Bir gövde "neden", bir gövde "etki" — en temiz yapı budur.

Model essay (~265 kelime)

Giriş: In many parts of the world, employees are spending more time at work than previous generations did. This essay will first consider why working hours have lengthened, and then examine the impact of this trend on family life.
Gövde 1 (neden): The main driver is economic pressure. As the cost of living, especially housing, has risen far faster than wages, a single income is often no longer enough to support a household, obliging both partners to work long hours. This is reinforced by a competitive workplace culture in which long hours are mistaken for commitment, so that employees fear leaving before their managers do. Technology compounds the problem, since emails and messages follow workers home and blur the point at which the working day ends.
Gövde 2 (etki): The consequences for family life are considerable. Parents who work late have less time to spend with their children, and shared rituals such as the evening meal gradually disappear. Beyond lost time, chronic overwork breeds stress and exhaustion, which strain relationships between partners. In my own country, it is increasingly common for both parents to arrive home only after their children are asleep, a pattern that weakens the emotional bonds a family depends upon.
Sonuç: In conclusion, longer working hours stem largely from financial necessity and workplace expectations, and they take a real toll on family relationships. Unless a healthier balance is restored, the cost will be measured not in productivity but in the quality of family life.

Neden yüksek band: iki soru ayrı gövdelerde tam yanıtlanıyor; giriş "first... then..." ile yol haritası veriyor; obliging, compounds, take a real toll gibi ileri kelime + kişisel örnek.

10.2 · Advantages and Disadvantages

Idea bank

Advantages

  • Commuting time is saved — in Istanbul, 2 hours a day go back to exercise/family/sleep.

Body 1 (advantage): The principal benefit is the elimination of the daily commute, which returns valuable time to workers. In a city like Istanbul, an employee may spend two hours a day travelling; recovered, that time can be devoted to exercise, family or rest. Home working also offers flexibility to those who would otherwise leave the workforce altogether, such as parents of young children, while allowing companies to recruit talent regardless of location.
Body 2 (disadvantage + why it is minor): The disadvantages are real but largely solvable. Isolation can erode motivation, and the boundary between work and home life often blurs, so that the working day never quite ends. Younger employees, moreover, may struggle to learn from experienced colleagues. Yet each of these problems has a practical remedy: hybrid schedules restore social contact, clear working hours protect personal time, and structured mentoring supports newcomers.
Conclusion: On balance, therefore, the benefits of working from home are more significant than its costs. Because the drawbacks can be addressed through sensible policies, whereas the gains in time and flexibility are structural, I firmly believe the advantages prevail.

Why a high band: in an "outweigh" question a clear verdict is established; it rebuts the disadvantages without denying them (real but largely solvable); evaluative language with on balance, prevail, structural.

10.3 · Problem / Solution (Cause + Measure)

Traffic congestion is becoming a serious problem in many large cities. What are the main causes of this problem and what measures could be taken to solve it?

Idea bank

Causes

  • Private car ownership is rising; roads were designed for a smaller population.
  • Public transport does not reach outer districts — no alternative.
  • Sprawling urban planning: homes, workplaces and schools far apart.

Solutions (each answering a cause)

  • Investment in rail → moves many people without adding cars.
  • Congestion charge → but an alternative (metro) must exist first.
  • Mixed-use planning → reduces the need to travel.

The strongest move: not a position but a match. Let each solution answer a cause directly — this is the structure that earns the most marks.

Model essay (~270 words)

Introduction: Severe traffic congestion has become a defining feature of life in many large cities. This essay will examine the two principal causes of the problem — rising car ownership and inadequate public transport — before proposing measures that address each directly.
Body 1 (causes): The first cause is the sheer growth in private vehicle ownership. As incomes rise, cars become both affordable and desirable, yet most road networks were designed for a far smaller population. A second, closely related cause is the weakness of public transport: in many cities, metro and bus routes fail to reach outlying districts, leaving residents there with no realistic alternative to driving.
Body 2 (matching solutions): Each cause suggests its own remedy. To reduce dependence on private cars, governments should invest heavily in rail systems, which can move large numbers of commuters without adding a single vehicle to the road. Where a reliable alternative already exists, a congestion charge can then discourage unnecessary driving, as London has demonstrated. Finally, encouraging mixed-use urban planning — placing homes, workplaces and schools closer together — tackles the problem at its root by removing the need for many journeys altogether.
Conclusion: In conclusion, urban congestion stems chiefly from growing car use and insufficient transport alternatives. By combining investment in public transport, carefully timed congestion charges and smarter planning, cities can address not merely the symptoms of the problem but its underlying causes.

Why a high band: the solutions match the causes one-to-one (Each cause suggests its own remedy); a concrete example (London); depth with at its root, symptoms vs underlying causes.

10.4 · Two-part (Direct Questions)

In many countries, people are working longer hours than in the past. Why is this happening, and what effect does it have on family life?

Idea bank

Why (question 1)

  • Rising cost of living — a single salary is not enough.
  • Competitive work culture: long hours are mistaken for "commitment".
  • Technology brings work home — the job never ends.

Effect (question 2)

  • Parent–child time decreases.
  • Stress and burnout strain relationships.
  • Family rituals such as shared meals/activities disappear.

Critical rule: in a two-part question both questions must be answered. One body for "why", one body for "effect" — this is the cleanest structure.

Model essay (~265 words)

Introduction: In many parts of the world, employees are spending more time at work than previous generations did. This essay will first consider why working hours have lengthened, and then examine the impact of this trend on family life.
Body 1 (why): The main driver is economic pressure. As the cost of living, especially housing, has risen far faster than wages, a single income is often no longer enough to support a household, obliging both partners to work long hours. This is reinforced by a competitive workplace culture in which long hours are mistaken for commitment, so that employees fear leaving before their managers do. Technology compounds the problem, since emails and messages follow workers home and blur the point at which the working day ends.
Body 2 (effect): The consequences for family life are considerable. Parents who work late have less time to spend with their children, and shared rituals such as the evening meal gradually disappear. Beyond lost time, chronic overwork breeds stress and exhaustion, which strain relationships between partners. In my own country, it is increasingly common for both parents to arrive home only after their children are asleep, a pattern that weakens the emotional bonds a family depends upon.
Conclusion: In conclusion, longer working hours stem largely from financial necessity and workplace expectations, and they take a real toll on family relationships. Unless a healthier balance is restored, the cost will be measured not in productivity but in the quality of family life.

Why a high band: the two questions are fully answered in separate bodies; the introduction gives a roadmap with "first... then..."; advanced vocabulary like obliging, compounds, take a real toll + a personal example.

11. Sık Hatalar (Türk Öğrenci Odaklı)

1. Soruyu tam yanıtlamama

Discussion sorusunda "own opinion"ı atlamak, two-part'ta ikinci soruyu unutmak, "outweigh"de net tartı yapmamak. Yazmaya başlamadan soruyu tipine göre etiketle.

2. Ezberlenmiş kalıp (memorized template)

"In this modern era, it is a hotly debated topic that..." gibi kalıplar Task Response'u düşürür. Değerlendirici bunları tanır ve içerik saymaz. Soruya özgü, doğal giriş yaz.

3. Konu dışı (off-topic) örnek

Genel bir örneği zorla iliştirmek. Örnek, o paragrafın ana fikrini doğrudan desteklemeli. Alakasız örnek gelişim saymaz.

4. Çok kısa / çok uzun

250 altı ceza; 320+ ise zaman kaybı ve hata riski. Hedef 260-290. Uzunluğu kelime doldurarak değil, fikir geliştirerek sağla.

5. Bağlaç istifleme

Her cümleye Moreover / Furthermore / In addition koymak yapaylaşır. Bağlacı yalnızca gerçek bir mantık geçişi olduğunda kullan.

6. Türkçe düşünüp çevirme

"make a decision" yerine "do a decision", "heavy rain" yerine "strong rain" gibi collocation hataları. İngilizce eş-dizimi ezberle, kelimeyi tek tek çevirme.

7. Kısaltma ve gayriresmî dil

don't, can't, kids, a lot of, stuff akademik denemede uygun değil. do not, children, many, aspects kullan.

8. Paragrafsız blok yazı

Tek uzun blok Coherence puanını düşürür. Net dört paragraf: giriş, gövde 1, gövde 2, sonuç. Paragraflar arası boşluk bırak.

11. Common Mistakes (Turkish-Student Focused)

1. Not answering the question fully

Skipping "own opinion" in a Discussion question, forgetting the second question in a two-part, not making a clear verdict in an "outweigh". Before you start writing, label the question by its type.

2. A memorised template

Templates like "In this modern era, it is a hotly debated topic that..." lower Task Response. The examiner recognises them and does not count them as content. Write a natural introduction specific to the question.

3. Off-topic examples

Forcing a generic example in. The example must directly support the main idea of that paragraph. An irrelevant example does not count as development.

4. Too short / too long

Under 250 is a penalty; 320+ is wasted time and risk of error. Target 260-290. Achieve length by developing ideas, not padding with words.

5. Stacking connectors

Putting Moreover / Furthermore / In addition at the start of every sentence becomes artificial. Use a connector only when there is a genuine logical transition.

6. Thinking in Turkish and translating

Collocation errors like "do a decision" instead of "make a decision", "strong rain" instead of "heavy rain". Memorise the English partnership, don't translate word by word.

7. Contractions and informal language

don't, can't, kids, a lot of, stuff are not appropriate in an academic essay. Use do not, children, many, aspects.

8. Paragraph-less block writing

A single long block lowers the Coherence score. Clear four paragraphs: introduction, body 1, body 2, conclusion. Leave a gap between paragraphs.

12. Zaman Yönetimi ve Planlama (40 Dakika)

AşamaSüreNe yaparsın
Analiz + planlama~5 dkSoru tipini belirle, pozisyon al, 2 gövde fikri + örnek not et, tezi zihinde kur.
Giriş~3 dkParafraz + net tez (2-3 cümle).
Gövde 1~10 dkTopic sentence + açıklama + örnek + bağ.
Gövde 2~10 dkTopic sentence + açıklama + örnek + (kendi görüş).
Sonuç~5 dkPozisyonu yeniden ifade + özet.
Kontrol~5 dkKelime sayısı, dilbilgisi (özne-yüklem, tekil/çoğul, artikel), yazım, bağlaç.
Planlama şart — atlanamaz

5 dakikalık planlama "kayıp zaman" değil, en yüksek getirili yatırımdır. Plansız başlayan öğrenci ikinci gövdede tıkanır, tekrar eder ve konu dışına çıkar. Kısa bir iskelet (tez + 2 topic sentence + 2 örnek anahtarı) yeterli.

  • Yap: soruyu 2 kez oku; her anahtar yönergenin altını çiz; tipini etiketle.
  • Yap: kontrol aşamasında en sık yaptığın 2-3 hatayı özellikle ara (artikel, özne-yüklem uyumu, tekil/çoğul).
  • Yapma: plansız yazmaya başlama; son dakikada tez değiştirme; kelime saymayı atlama.

12. Time Management and Planning (40 Minutes)

StageTimeWhat you do
Analysis + planning~5 minIdentify the question type, take a position, note 2 body ideas + examples, form the thesis in your mind.
Introduction~3 minParaphrase + clear thesis (2-3 sentences).
Body 1~10 minTopic sentence + explanation + example + link.
Body 2~10 minTopic sentence + explanation + example + (own opinion).
Conclusion~5 minRestate the position + summary.
Checking~5 minWord count, grammar (subject-verb, singular/plural, articles), spelling, connectors.
Planning is essential — it cannot be skipped

5 minutes of planning is not "lost time" but the highest-return investment. A student who starts without a plan gets stuck in the second body, repeats themselves and drifts off topic. A short skeleton (thesis + 2 topic sentences + 2 example keys) is enough.

  • Do: read the question twice; underline every key instruction; label its type.
  • Do: in the checking stage, specifically hunt for your 2-3 most frequent errors (articles, subject-verb agreement, singular/plural).
  • Don't: start writing without a plan; change your thesis at the last minute; skip the word count.
