C1 Final — Tüm Üniteler (1-19) (Genel Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu final, C1 kurundaki 19 ünitenin tamamını ölçer: ileri zaman ve görünüş; ileri modal anlam ve modal perfect'ler (needn't have, must have); devrik yapılar ve devrik koşullar (Not only..., Had I known...); ileri ve karışık koşullar (but for, were I to); vurgu yapıları (cleft & emphatic); ileri ortaç cümlecikleri (having been done); isimleştirme (nominalisation); ileri edilgen ve rapor yapıları (is believed to have...); eksiltme ve yerine koyma (ellipsis & substitution); ileri ilgi cümlecikleri (few of whom); ileri ödün ve zıtlık (much as, whereas); karmaşık ad öbekleri; akademik ihtiyat (hedging); bağdaşıklık ve söylem belirleyicileri; emir kipi ve kalıp ifadeler (the subjunctive); eşdizim ve deyimsel doğruluk; kayıt ve üslup denetimi. Beş bölümde dil bilgisi, İngilizce kullanımı, okuma, dinleme ve yazma test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) İki akademik metin: Text 1 gençlerin uyku düzeni üzerine, Text 2 okul bahçelerinin dönüşü üzerinedir. (Two academic texts: teenage sleep and the return of the school garden.)

Text 1 · Why Teenagers Stay Up Late

For decades, teenagers who could not fall asleep before midnight were dismissed as lazy or undisciplined. A substantial body of research now suggests that the problem lies not in their willpower but in their biology. During adolescence, the body's internal clock shifts later, delaying the release of melatonin, the hormone that signals it is time to sleep. As a result, many teenagers are simply not tired at the hours their parents consider reasonable.

This biological delay would matter less if school timetables were flexible, but most begin early in the morning, forcing students to wake long before their bodies are ready. The consequence is a chronic sleep debt that accumulates across the week and cannot be repaid by a single long lie-in at the weekend. Tired students are more likely to lose concentration, forget what they have learned and struggle to regulate their moods.

Some schools that have experimented with later start times report noticeable improvements in attendance and academic performance. Critics point out that such changes are difficult to organise and can disrupt family routines and transport. Yet the underlying evidence is hard to ignore: for teenagers, the timing of sleep is not a matter of choice but of development.

Text 2 · The Return of the School Garden

For much of the twentieth century, the school garden was regarded as a pleasant but inessential extra, the first thing to be sacrificed whenever budgets were tight. That attitude is now being reconsidered. A growing number of educators argue that a garden is not merely decorative but genuinely useful, teaching lessons that a classroom alone cannot.

Working with plants, supporters claim, gives students a direct experience of biology that no textbook can match. Tending vegetables demands patience and responsibility, since a plant that is neglected for a week will not recover overnight. Gardens can also improve the atmosphere of a school, providing a calm space where anxious students can escape the noise of the corridors.

Sceptics warn that such projects are sometimes launched more for publicity than for lasting benefit, and that enthusiasm often fades once the novelty has worn off and the watering becomes a chore. Even so, the wider shift is difficult to dispute. Where the garden was once seen as an ornament, it is increasingly treated as a valuable part of school life.

Teen C1 Final Sınavı · Bölüm 5/7

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 13 min, Task 2: 22 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 13 dk, Görev 2: 22 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Formal Email: A Club That Was Not as Described (130-170 words)

You paid to join a weekend science club at a local youth centre, but it did not match the description you were given when you signed up. Write a formal email to the centre manager. Explain what was promised and what actually happened, describe the impact this has had on you, and state clearly what action you expect them to take. (Yerel bir gençlik merkezindeki hafta sonu bilim kulübüne ücret ödeyerek katıldın, ama kulüp kayıt sırasında sana verilen açıklamayla örtüşmedi. Merkez yöneticisine resmi bir e-posta yaz: ne vaat edildiğini ve gerçekte ne olduğunu açıkla, bunun seni nasıl etkilediğini anlat ve ne yapmalarını beklediğini net şekilde belirt.)

  • Use a formal opening and closing: "Dear Sir / Madam," ... "Yours faithfully," / "Yours sincerely,". (resmi açılış ve kapanış)
  • Use at least one passive / reporting structure: "I was assured that...", "It was stated that the club would...". (edilgen / rapor yapısı)
  • Include reported speech: "The website claimed that...". (dolaylı anlatım)
  • Make a firm but polite request with would / could: "I would therefore be grateful if you could...". (kesin ama kibar rica)
  • 130-170 words, formal register throughout. (130-170 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Dear Sir / Madam," + yazma sebebin (I am writing to complain about / regarding...).
  • Gövde: vaat edilen (passive + reported speech) → gerçekte olan → etkisi → beklediğin işlem (would/could + refund/repeat).
  • Kapanış: "I look forward to your prompt response." + "Yours faithfully," + adın.

Task 2 · Argument Essay: Tablets vs Printed Books at School (200-260 words)

Some people argue that schools should replace printed textbooks entirely with tablets and laptops, while others believe printed books are still better for learning. Discuss both views and give your own opinion, supporting it with clear reasons and examples. (Bazıları okulların basılı ders kitaplarını tamamen tablet ve dizüstü bilgisayarlarla değiştirmesi gerektiğini savunurken, bazıları basılı kitapların öğrenme için hâlâ daha iyi olduğuna inanıyor. Her iki görüşü tartış ve kendi görüşünü net gerekçeler ve örneklerle destekle.)

  • Discuss both views before giving your own opinion. (kendi görüşünden önce iki görüşü de tartış)
  • Use at least three concession/contrast linkers: whereas, despite, nevertheless, although, however. (en az üç zıtlık bağlacı)
  • Include one conditional (Third or Mixed): "If schools had introduced tablets earlier, students would...". (bir koşul cümlesi)
  • Use passive and reporting structures (it is argued that..., is believed to be...). (edilgen ve rapor yapıları)
  • 200-260 words, organised into clear paragraphs. (200-260 kelime)
