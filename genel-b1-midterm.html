Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-8 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-8'de öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: Present Perfect ve Past Simple farkı (for/since, been/gone); Present Perfect Continuous; Past Perfect; gelecek yapıları (will / going to / Present Continuous); Second Conditional; Passive Voice; Reported Speech ve aktarılan sorular/emirler. Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

B1 Midterm Sınavı · Bölüm 4/6

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · The Rise of Remote Work

Ten years ago, most people had never worked from home. They travelled to an office every morning, spent hours in traffic, and returned late in the evening. Since then, everything has changed. Today, millions of employees around the world work remotely, at least for part of the week.

The change did not happen overnight. For years, a few companies had already been experimenting with flexible hours, but most managers didn't trust the idea. They believed that workers would be lazy at home. However, when offices were suddenly closed during the health crisis of 2020, companies had no choice: almost everyone was sent home to work online.

The results surprised many bosses. Studies have shown that remote workers are often more productive than office workers, not less. People save time because they don't travel, and many say they feel happier and less stressed. Some companies have even closed their expensive city offices and saved millions.

Of course, remote work is not perfect. New employees sometimes feel lonely, and it is harder to learn from experienced colleagues when you never meet them. Important information is not always shared quickly, and some workers find it difficult to 'switch off' at the end of the day.

Experts believe the future will be 'hybrid'. Most people will not return to the office five days a week, but they will not stay at home every day either. If companies offered a mix of both, they would keep their staff happy and their costs low. As one manager said last month, 'We have learnt that the office is a place to meet, not a place to sit alone at a desk.'

Text 2 · A Job That Changed My Life

When Deniz finished university, she had no idea what she wanted to do. She had studied engineering, but she wasn't sure she wanted to work in a factory or an office. For six months she applied for dozens of jobs, and slowly she was becoming worried about her future.

Then a friend told her about a small company that built wind turbines. The company was looking for young engineers who were willing to travel. Deniz had never thought about renewable energy before, but she decided to apply. To her surprise, she was offered the job a week later.

The first months were difficult. She had to learn everything quickly, and she was often sent to windy fields far from the city. She had never worked outdoors before, and at first she hated the cold. But she also felt proud: the electricity produced by 'her' turbines was used by thousands of homes.

Three years have passed since that day, and Deniz has never regretted her decision. She has been promoted twice, and she now leads a team of ten engineers. 'If I hadn't taken that risk,' she says, 'I would still be sending my CV to companies that never reply. My advice is simple: say yes to the job that scares you a little.'

B1 Midterm Sınavı · Bölüm 5/6

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 12 min, Task 2: 23 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 12 dk, Görev 2: 23 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: Some Good News (100-130 words)

Recently something good happened to you: you passed an important exam, started a new job, finished a big project, or learned a new skill. Write an email to a friend and share your news. (Yakın zamanda güzel bir şey oldu: önemli bir sınavı geçtin, yeni bir işe başladın, büyük bir projeyi bitirdin ya da yeni bir beceri öğrendin. Bir arkadaşına e-posta yazıp haberini paylaş.)

  • Use Present Perfect for your news ("I have just passed..."). (haber için Present Perfect)
  • Use Past Simple for the details — when, where, how. (ayrıntılar için Past Simple)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: because, so, but, and, after. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 100-130 words. (100-130 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Merve," + haber cümlesi ("I have some great news! I have just...").
  • Gövde: ne oldu, nasıl oldu? — Present Perfect (haber) + Past Simple (ayrıntı) + because/so.
  • Kapanış: "Let's meet soon and celebrate!" / "Take care," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: Working from Home (140-170 words)

Some people think that working or studying from home is better than going to an office or a school. Others disagree. What is your opinion? Give reasons and examples. (Bazı insanlar evden çalışmanın/okumanın ofise ya da okula gitmekten daha iyi olduğunu düşünür; bazıları katılmaz. Senin görüşün ne? Sebep ve örnek ver.)

  • State your opinion clearly at the start. (görüşünü baştan net belirt)
  • Use a Second Conditional sentence ("If everyone worked from home, ..."). (bir Second Conditional cümlesi)
  • Use at least 4 connectors: however, because, so, although, for example. (en az 4 bağlaç)
  • 140-170 words. (140-170 kelime)
