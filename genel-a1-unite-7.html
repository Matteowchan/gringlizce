Grammar: Present Continuous (am / is / are + -ing)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden Present Continuous?

Şimdiye kadar rutinleri anlattın: "I work at Baytek." (Baytek'te çalışırım.) Peki tam şu anda olan bir şeyi nasıl söylersin? Bunun için Present Continuous kullanırız — Türkçedeki "-yor" ekinin tam karşılığıdır ve telefonda en çok ihtiyaç duyacağın zamandır. Çünkü telefonda hep "şu an" konuşulur: kim ne yapıyor, kim müsait, kim meşgul?

  • "He is talking to a customer right now."O şu an bir müşteriyle konuşuyor.
  • "I am calling about the invoice."Fatura hakkında arıyorum.
  • "She is having lunch at the moment."O şu anda öğle yemeği yiyor.
  • "The phones are ringing all the time!"Telefonlar sürekli çalıyor!

Yapı iki parçadan oluşur: Ünite 1'den tanıdığın be fiili (am/is/are) + fiilin -ing'li hâli. İkisi birlikte "şu an oluyor" anlamını verir. Bu bölümde yapıyı, -ing yazım kurallarını, olumsuz ve soru hâllerini, Present Simple ile farkını ve telefon kalıplarını tek tek işleyeceğiz.

1. Yapı (am / is / are + fiil-ing)

Kural tektir: özneye göre doğru be formunu seç (Ünite 1'deki kuralın aynısı), sonra fiile -ing ekle. Cümle kurgusu: Özne + am/is/are + fiil-ing + geri kalanı.

Öznebe + -ingÖrnekTürkçesi
Iam working (I'm working)I am writing the report.Raporu yazıyorum.
Youare working (You're working)You are speaking very fast!Çok hızlı konuşuyorsun!
Heis working (He's working)He is talking on the phone.O telefonda konuşuyor.
Sheis working (She's working)She is talking to a customer.O bir müşteriyle konuşuyor.
Itis working (It's working)The phone is ringing.Telefon çalıyor.
Weare working (We're working)We are working on the new app.Yeni uygulama üzerinde çalışıyoruz.
Theyare working (They're working)They are having lunch.Onlar öğle yemeği yiyorlar.
Dikkat
  • İki parça da zorunludur: be'siz cümle yanlıştır ("I working" ✗), -ing'siz cümle yanlıştır ("I am work" ✗). Doğrusu: "I am working." ✓
  • be seçimi Ünite 1'deki kuralın aynısıdır: I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are.

2. -ing Yazım Kuralları (Spelling Rules)

Çoğu fiile -ing doğrudan eklenir, ama üç yazım kuralı vardır. Bu kurallar sınavlarda ve yazıda çok sorulur:

KuralÖrnekler
1. Normal durum: fiil + ingwork → working · call → calling · answer → answering · wait → waiting
2. Sonu -e ile bitiyorsa: -e düşer, sonra + ingwrite → writing · have → having · take → taking · make → making
3. Tek heceli, kısa ünlü + tek sessiz: son harf çift yazılırsit → sitting · get → getting · put → putting · run → running
4. Sonu -y ile bitiyorsa: -y aynen kalırstudy → studying · stay → staying · buy → buying
Sık karıştırılanlar
  • "writeing" ✗ → "writing" ✓ (-e düşer) · "siting" ✗ → "sitting" ✓ (çift t)
  • -y hiçbir zaman değişmez: "studing" ✗ → "studying" ✓

3. Olumsuz Cümle (Negative)

Olumsuz için Ünite 1'den bildiğin yolu izle: be'den sonra not ekle, -ing aynen kalır. Yapı: Özne + am not / isn't / aren't + fiil-ing.

Tam formKısa formÖrnekTürkçesi
I am not workingI'm not workingI'm not waiting for a call.Bir çağrı beklemiyorum.
He / She / It is not workingisn't workingHe isn't answering his phone.O telefonuna cevap vermiyor.
You / We / They are not workingaren't workingThey aren't working today.Onlar bugün çalışmıyorlar.
Hatırla
  • İki kısa form da doğrudur: "She isn't talking." = "She's not talking."
  • "I" için yalnızca "I'm not" vardır: "I amn't" ✗

4. Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (Questions & Short Answers)

Soru için be öznenin önüne geçer — Ünite 1'deki kuralın aynısı. -ing'li fiil yerinde kalır: You are calling. → Are you calling?

SoruOlumlu kısa cevapOlumsuz kısa cevap
Are you calling about the invoice?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is she talking to a customer?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Is the phone ringing?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are they having lunch?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Önemli kural
  • Olumlu kısa cevapta kısaltma yapılmaz: "Yes, I'm." ✗ → "Yes, I am." ✓
  • Soru kelimeleriyle sıra aynıdır: "What are you doing?" (Ne yapıyorsun?), "Who is she talking to?" (Kiminle konuşuyor?), "Why is the phone ringing?" (Telefon neden çalıyor?)

5. Present Simple ile Farkı (Simple vs Continuous)

Bu iki zamanı ayırmak A1'in en önemli eşiğidir. Kural basittir: rutin ve genel gerçekler → Present Simple, şu an olan → Present Continuous. Türkçesi bile farklıdır: "kontrol ederim" / "kontrol ediyorum".

Present Simple (rutin)Present Continuous (şu an)
I check emails every morning.
Her sabah e-postaları kontrol ederim.		I am checking emails now.
Şu an e-postaları kontrol ediyorum.
She talks to customers every day.
Her gün müşterilerle konuşur.		She is talking to a customer right now.
Tam şu an bir müşteriyle konuşuyor.
He usually answers the phone.
Genellikle telefona o cevap verir.		He isn't answering the phone at the moment.
Şu anda telefona cevap vermiyor.
Sinyal kelimeler
  • Present Continuous sinyalleri: now, right now, at the moment, today, Listen!, Look!
  • Present Simple sinyalleri: every day, every morning, usually, always, often, never
  • Cümlede bu sinyalleri ara — hangi zamanın doğru olduğunu sana söylerler.

8. Uygulama: Telefonda (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Aşağıdaki telefon görüşmesi bu bölümde öğrendiğin yapıların ve kalıpların tamamını kullanıyor — santral açıyor, aktarma deneniyor, mesaj alınıyor. Koyu yazılan yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Good morning, Baytek. How can I help you?Günaydın, Baytek. Size nasıl yardımcı olabilirim?
  • — Hello, this is Kerem Yılmaz from Arda Market. Can I speak to Mr. Demir, please?Merhaba, ben Arda Market'ten Kerem Yılmaz. Bay Demir ile görüşebilir miyim?
  • — One moment, please... I'm sorry, he is in a meeting at the moment.Bir saniye lütfen... Üzgünüm, kendisi şu anda toplantıda.
  • — I see. Is he coming back soon?Anladım. Yakında dönüyor mu?
  • — I don't know, but the meeting is long. Can I take a message?Bilmiyorum ama toplantı uzun. Mesajınızı alabilir miyim?
  • — Yes, please. I am calling about the invoice for June. It is urgent.Evet, lütfen. Haziran faturası hakkında arıyorum. Acil.
  • — Of course. Are you calling from your office number?Tabii. Ofis numaranızdan mı arıyorsunuz?
  • — No, I'm using my mobile: 0532 111 22 33.Hayır, cep telefonumu kullanıyorum: 0532 111 22 33.
  • — Got it. I am writing your message now. He can call you back after the meeting.Aldım. Mesajınızı şimdi yazıyorum. Toplantıdan sonra sizi geri arayabilir.
  • — Thank you very much. Goodbye!Çok teşekkürler. Hoşça kalın!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane Present Continuous cümlesi var, bulabilir misin? (Cevap: 5 — "Is he coming back?", "I am calling about...", "Are you calling from...?", "I'm using my mobile", "I am writing your message")
  • Arayan kişi kendini tanıtırken hangi kalıbı kullanıyor? (Cevap: "This is Kerem Yılmaz" — telefonda "I am" değil "This is"!)
  • Resepsiyonist mesaj almak için hangi soruyu soruyor? (Cevap: "Can I take a message?")

A Busy Morning at Reception

Elif is the receptionist at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. She usually answers about twenty calls a day, but today is very different. This morning the phones are ringing all the time because the new app is out, and many customers are calling with questions.

Elif arrives at the office at half past eight every morning. She is a kind and friendly woman, and she likes her job very much. Her desk is next to the front door, so she sees everyone who comes in. On her desk there are two phones, a computer, a pen, and many small notes. There is also a green plant and a photo of her family. Today Elif is wearing a blue shirt. She has a cup of tea next to her computer, but the tea is cold now. She is very busy this morning, and she has no time for tea.

Right now, two phones are ringing at the same time. Elif answers the first phone. "Good morning, Baytek. How can I help you?" she says. The caller is a customer, and he wants to speak to a manager. Elif is always polite on the phone. She says, "One moment, please," and she looks for the right person. Then the second phone rings again. It is a busy morning, and Elif is working very hard. She is not tired yet, but she is very busy.

It is ten o'clock, and everyone in the office is busy. Mr. Demir, the manager, is in a meeting with an important customer, so Elif is taking messages for him. His phone is on silent, and he isn't answering any calls right now.

In the meeting room, Mr. Demir is talking about the new app. The important customer is asking many questions. Mr. Demir is showing some numbers on a big screen, and he is answering slowly and clearly. He is a calm man, and he is smiling. The meeting is going well. Elif knows this meeting is very important, so she is not sending any calls to his room. She is writing down every message for him on a small note.

Ali, an engineer, is fixing a bug in the new app. He is working very fast because customers are waiting. Next to him, Merve is talking to a customer from England. Her English is very good, and she is smiling.

Not every call is easy. One caller is angry, because his app is not working. "Please help me," he says. "My app is not opening!" Elif stays calm and kind. "I am very sorry," she says. "Ali is fixing the app right now. Can I take your number? He can call you back soon." The caller gives his mobile number, and he feels better. Elif writes his number on a note for Ali.

A new call comes in for Merve. "Hello, this is David from London," the caller says. "Can I speak to Merve, please?" Elif looks at Merve, but Merve is still on the phone with another customer. "I am sorry, she is busy at the moment," Elif says. "Can I take a message?" "Yes, please," says David. "Tell her I am calling about the new app. It is not urgent." Elif writes the message and puts it on Merve's desk.

Selin, the designer, is designing a new advertisement for the app. She is drinking coffee at her desk, and she is listening to music with her headphones.

The office is big and bright, with large windows. There are about ten desks and many computers. Some people are typing, and some people are talking on the phone. A printer near the window is working all the time. The coffee machine in the kitchen is very busy today, because everyone wants coffee. It is a hard morning, but the team is happy. The new app is popular, and many people are buying it and using it right now.

At ten past ten, a delivery man comes to the door. He has a big box for the office. "Good morning. This is a package for Baytek," he says. Elif signs the paper and takes the box. "Thank you very much," she says. The box is heavy, so she puts it under her desk. She is helping people all the time this morning — on the phone and at the door. Her job is never boring, and she likes that.

At the reception, there is a big sign with the company name: Baytek. Under the sign, there are some chairs for visitors. This morning, one visitor is waiting on a chair. He is reading a magazine, and he is drinking a glass of water. He has an appointment at eleven o'clock. Elif smiles at him and says, "Please wait a moment. Someone is coming soon." The visitor is happy to wait.

At half past ten, a caller leaves an urgent message. "Hello, this is Mr. Yılmaz. I am calling about an invoice. Please call me back — it is urgent." Elif writes the message on a note and puts it on Mr. Demir's desk.

Elif takes many messages this morning. For each call, she writes the name, the time, and a short message on a note. Some messages are urgent, and some messages are not. She puts the urgent notes on the top of the pile. Mr. Demir has seven notes on his desk now. Elif is very good at her job, because she is careful and fast. She never loses a message, and she always writes clearly.

Sometimes Elif cannot answer a call, because both phones are busy. Then the call goes to voicemail. Later, Elif listens to the voicemail and calls the caller back. "Hello, this is Elif from Baytek. You have a message for us," she says. She is friendly with every caller. For Elif, every caller is important, and every call is a chance to help.

At eleven o'clock, the phones become quiet, so Elif gets a coffee from the kitchen. She is tired but happy, because she is doing a good job on a very busy morning.

After her coffee, Elif feels better. She checks her messages one more time. She has five notes for Mr. Demir and two notes for Merve, and she puts them on the right desks. At twelve o'clock, Mr. Demir comes out of the meeting. "Thank you, Elif," he says. "You are doing a great job today." Elif is very happy. It is only lunchtime, but she is ready for the afternoon. She knows the phones are going to ring again very soon, and she is not afraid of a busy day.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
Mr. Yılmaz is on the phone. He is calling about the invoice. It is urgent. You are in a meeting. I am taking a message. Mr. Yılmaz is on the phone, and he is calling about the invoice. It is urgent, but you are in a meeting, so I am taking a message.
Kural
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan. Cümle cümle yazma; fikirlerini bağla.
  • "so" ve "but" öncesinde genellikle virgül kullanılır: "He is waiting, so please call him back."

E-posta Yapısı (Email Structure)

İş e-postası üç bölümden oluşur. Bu iskelet her e-postada aynıdır — içini değiştirirsin, yapıyı değiştirmezsin. Çağrı özeti e-postasında içerik şöyle dağılır:

BölümNe yazılır?Örnek
1. Açılış
(Opening)		Selamlama + neden yazdığını söyleyen ilk cümleDear Mr. Demir,
I am writing about a phone call for you.
2. Gövde
(Body)		Asıl bilgiler: kim aradı, ne hakkında, şu an ne oluyor (Present Continuous!). Bağlaçlarla bağla.Mr. Yılmaz is calling about the invoice. It is urgent because his team is checking the numbers today...
3. Kapanış
(Closing)		Ne yapılmalı + nazik bitiş + veda + isimCan you call him back after your meeting?

Elif
Sık kullanılan kalıplar
  • Açılış: "Dear Mr. Demir," · "Hello," · "I am writing about..."
  • Kapanış: "Best regards," · "Kind regards," · "Thank you very much."

Task (Görev)

You answered a phone call for your manager. Write a short email to your manager about the call. (Yöneticin adına bir çağrı yanıtladın. Yöneticine çağrıyı özetleyen kısa bir e-posta yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • who is calling — name and company (kim aradı: isim + şirket)
  • what the call is about (ne hakkında)
  • what is happening right now — at least 3 Present Continuous sentences (şu an ne oluyor — en az 3 Present Continuous cümlesi)
  • what your manager should do (ne yapılmalı)
  • at least 1 telephone phrase: "He is in a meeting at the moment." / "I'm calling about..." / "Can I take a message?" (en az 1 telefon kalıbı)
  • at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: call, answer, message, urgent, available, line, mobile...)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"I am writing about a phone call for you." "Mr. ... is calling about ..." "He is waiting on the line, so ..."

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~155 words) Dear Mr. Demir,

I am writing about a phone call for you. You are in a meeting at the moment, so I am answering your calls.

Mr. Yılmaz from Arda Market is on the line right now, and he is waiting for you. He is calling about the invoice for June. He says it is urgent because his team is checking the numbers today, but the invoice isn't in their system.

Right now, I am looking at the invoice list on my computer, and Merve is helping me. We are checking the old emails too, but we can't see his company name on the list.

Can you call Mr. Yılmaz back after your meeting? His mobile number is on your desk, on a message note. He is available today before five o'clock or tomorrow morning.

Thank you very much.

Best regards,
Elif
