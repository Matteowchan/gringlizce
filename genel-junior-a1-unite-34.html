Bugün Ne Öğreniyoruz? (What Do We Learn Today?)

Bugün hayvanat bahçesine gidiyoruz! Hayvanları anlatmak için üç küçük araç kullanacağız. Hepsini daha önce gördün — şimdi bir arada kullanalım:

  • There is a lion. · There are two monkeys.Bir aslan var. · İki maymun var. (ne var?)
  • Elephants can swim. · A penguin can't fly.Filler yüzebilir. · Penguen uçamaz. (yapabilir / yapamaz)
  • The monkey is climbing. · The birds are singing.Maymun tırmanıyor. · Kuşlar ötüyor. (şu an oluyor)
Dikkat!
  • Bunların hepsi tekrar — hepsini biliyorsun! Bugün hayvanat bahçesinde birlikte kullanacağız.

1. There is / There are (... var)

Hayvanat bahçesinde ne olduğunu söylemek için kullanırız. Bir tane varsa There is, iki ya da daha çok varsa There are:

Kaç tane?YapıÖrnekTürkçesi
1 taneThere isThere is a lion.Bir aslan var.
2+ taneThere areThere are three zebras.Üç zebra var.
yokThere isn'tThere isn't a bear.Ayı yok.
Kolay yol
  • Bir tane → is (a lion). İki ya da daha çok → are (two lions).

2. can / can't (yapabilir / yapamaz)

Bir hayvanın bir şeyi yapabildiğini söylemek için can, yapamadığını söylemek için can't kullanırız. can'den sonra fiil hep sade kalır:

YapıÖrnekTürkçesi
canBirds can fly.Kuşlar uçabilir.
canMonkeys can climb.Maymunlar tırmanabilir.
can'tA penguin can't fly.Penguen uçamaz.

Soru ve kısa cevaplar kolaydır:

SoruEvetHayır
Can a fish swim?Yes, it can.No, it can't.
Can monkeys climb?Yes, they can.No, they can't.
Dikkat!
  • can / can't sonra fiil sade kalır: "can swim" ✓ (can swims ✗).

3. Şu An Ne Oluyor? (-ing / Present Continuous)

Tam şu anda olan bir şeyi anlatmak için is / are + fiil-ing kullanırız:

Kim?is / are + -ingÖrnekTürkçesi
The lion (1)is sleepingThe lion is sleeping.Aslan uyuyor.
The monkey (1)is climbingThe monkey is climbing.Maymun tırmanıyor.
The birds (2+)are singingThe birds are singing.Kuşlar ötüyor.
Kolay yol
  • Bir tane → is + -ing. İki ya da daha çok → are + -ing.
  • Fiile -ing ekleriz: sleep → sleeping, climb → climbing, sing → singing.

4. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
There is two lions.There are two lions.İki tane → are.
There are a bear.There is a bear.Bir tane → is.
Elephants can swims.Elephants can swim.can + sade fiil.
The monkey climbing.The monkey is climbing.-ing'den önce is gerekir.
A penguin can fly.A penguin can't fly.Penguen uçamaz (anlam).

5. Birlikte Görelim (At the Zoo)

İki çocuk hayvanat bahçesinde hayvanları anlatıyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Look! There is a big lion. It is sleeping.Bak! Büyük bir aslan var. Uyuyor.
  • — There are two monkeys too. They are climbing a tree.İki maymun da var. Bir ağaca tırmanıyorlar.
  • — Can monkeys climb high?Maymunlar yükseğe tırmanabilir mi?
  • — Yes, they can! Elephants can swim, but they can't fly.Evet! Filler yüzebilir ama uçamaz.
  • — Look at the birds! They are singing.Kuşlara bak! Ötüyorlar.
  • — I love the zoo. There are so many animals here!Hayvanat bahçesini seviyorum. Burada çok hayvan var!
Kendini test et
  • Kaç hayvan adı geçti? (Cevap: 4 — lion, monkeys, elephants, birds)
  • "They are climbing a tree." ne demek? (Cevap: Bir ağaca tırmanıyorlar.)

Speaking: Zoo Animals

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan! (Learn the words, then use them.)

lion (n)
a big cat with a lot of hair (aslan)
"There is a big lion."
monkey (n)
an animal that can climb trees (maymun)
"The monkey is climbing."
elephant (n)
a very big grey animal (fil)
"Elephants can swim."
zebra (n)
a black and white animal like a horse (zebra)
"There are three zebras."
penguin (n)
a black and white bird that swims (penguen)
"A penguin can't fly."
climb (v)
to go up a tree or a wall (tırmanmak)
"Monkeys can climb."
swim (v)
to move in the water (yüzmek)
"Fish can swim."
fly (v)
to move in the sky (uçmak)
Zıttı: can't fly (uçamaz)
"Birds can fly."
run (v)
to move very fast with your legs (koşmak)
"The zebra is running."
sleep (v)
to close your eyes and rest (uyumak)
"The lion is sleeping."
Hayvanat bahçesinin küçük dostları: uğur böceği, kelebek ve çiçek.

Your Turn! (Senin Sıran)

Talk about the zoo animals: what you can see, what they can do, and what they are doing. Write 5 sentences. (Hayvanat bahçesindeki hayvanları anlat: ne görüyorsun, ne yapabilirler, şu an ne yapıyorlar? 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Bir hayvan olduğunu söyle: There is / There are.
  • Bir hayvanın ne yapabildiğini söyle: can / can't. Şu an ne yaptığını söyle: is / are + -ing.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"There is ..." "There are ..." "... can ..." "The ... is ...ing" "I can see ..."
Sample Answer 1. There is a big lion at the zoo.
2. There are two monkeys. They are climbing a tree.
3. Elephants can swim, but they can't fly.
4. The birds are singing and the zebra is running.
5. I can see many animals. I love the zoo!

Reading: A Day at the Zoo

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

lion · monkey · elephant · zebra · penguin · climb · swim · fly

İpucu
  • Önce hızlıca oku — her kelimeyi anlaman gerekmez.
  • Sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A Day at the Zoo

Today is Saturday. My name is Elif and I am eight years old. My family and I go to the zoo. I am very happy!

I go to the zoo with my mum, my dad and my little brother. We go by bus. My brother is small and he holds my hand. The zoo is very big and there is a map at the gate. We can see many animals here. The sun is out and the sky is blue. I have my hat and my water bottle. I am ready for a fun day!

At the zoo, there are many animals. There is a big lion. It is sleeping in the sun. Next to the lion, there are two monkeys. They are climbing a tall tree. Monkeys can climb very well!

The monkeys are very funny. They jump from branch to branch and they eat bananas. One monkey looks at me and I laugh. The monkeys are brown and small. They are quick and playful. I like them a lot. They can climb, and they climb all day long!

Look! There is a grey elephant. It is drinking water. Elephants are big and strong. They can swim, but they can't fly. There are three zebras too. They are running and eating grass.

The big elephant has large ears and a long nose. It eats grass and it drinks a lot of water. Elephants are heavy and strong. The zebras have black and white lines on their bodies. They run fast and they eat green grass all day. I count the animals and I smile. I like the zebras!

I like the birds. There are many birds and they are singing. Birds can fly high in the sky. Near the water, there are two penguins. Penguins are black and white. They can swim fast, but they can't fly.

The penguins are so cute! They are small and they walk in a funny way. They jump into the water and they swim fast. Penguins love the cool water. The birds are many colours and they sing a nice song. When I look up, I see them fly. I watch them for a long time. The sky is blue and beautiful.

My little brother likes the tigers. There is one tiger. It is not sleeping — it is walking. My mum takes many photos. We eat lunch at the zoo. It is a fun day. I love animals! Do you like the zoo? What animals can you see?

After the animals, we sit down and eat our lunch. We have sandwiches and juice. My brother talks about the tiger and says, "The tiger can walk and it can run!" I take a picture with my mum. We drink water and we rest on a bench. The zoo is a happy place, and I want to come again next Saturday!

Writing: A Day at the Zoo

Hayvanat bahçesindeki hayvanları anlat, gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak! (Write about the zoo animals; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveThere is a lion and there are two monkeys.
butamaElephants can swim but they can't fly.
becauseçünküI am happy because I love animals.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but ya da because.

Task (Görev)

Write about a day at the zoo. (Hayvanat bahçesinde bir gün hakkında yaz.) 3-5 cümle yeterli!

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • an animal you can see (There is / There are ...)
  • what an animal can or can't do (can / can't ...)
  • what an animal is doing now (The ... is ...ing)
  • one animal you like (sevdiğin bir hayvan)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"There is ..." "... can ..." "The ... is ...ing" "I like ..."

Sample Answer (~45 words) Hello! Today I am at the zoo. There is a big lion and there are two monkeys. The monkeys are climbing a tree. Elephants can swim, but they can't fly. The birds are singing. I like the penguins because they are funny!
