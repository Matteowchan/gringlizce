Grammar: Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Rutin mi, tam şu an mı? İki zamanı ayırt et. (Routine or right now? Tell the two tenses apart.)

Selam, ben yine Gri. Bu ünitede iki zamanı karşılaştırıyoruz. Present Simple her gün tekrar eden rutinleri ve alışkanlıkları anlatır: I usually study at night. She plays tennis on Sundays. Present Continuous ise tam şu an olan eylemi anlatır: Right now I am watching a series. Sır işaret kelimelerinde: usually / every day → Simple; now / right now / at the moment → Continuous. Başlayalım.

İki Zaman, İki İş (Two Tenses)

Present Simple, her zaman tekrar eden rutinleri, alışkanlıkları ve genel gerçekleri anlatır. Present Continuous ise tam şu an olan eylemi anlatır. Aynı gün, iki farklı zaman:

  • I usually study at night, but right now I am watching a series.Genelde geceleri ders çalışırım, ama tam şu an bir dizi izliyorum.
  • She plays tennis on Sundays.O pazarları tenis oynar. (rutin)
  • Look! He is running in the park right now.Bak! O tam şu an parkta koşuyor.
Kolay yol
  • Her gün / genelde tekrar eden şey → Present Simple (base fiil, he/she/it için -s).
  • Tam şu an olan şey → Present Continuous (am/is/are + fiil-ing).

1. Present Simple: Rutin ve Alışkanlık (routine / habit)

Rutin ve alışkanlıklar için Present Simple kullanırız. Fiil çıplak kalır; ama he / she / it için sona -s ekleriz:

ÖzneFiilÖrnekTürkçesi
I / You / We / TheybaseI study every day.Her gün ders çalışırım.
He / She / Itbase + sShe plays tennis on Sundays.O pazarları tenis oynar.
He / She / Itgo → goesHe goes to school by bus.O okula otobüsle gider.
Kolay yol
  • he / she / it gördüğünde fiile -s ekle: play → plays, study → studies, go → goes.
  • Sık kullanılan işaretler: usually, always, every day, on Sundays, after school.

2. Present Continuous: Şu An (am / is / are + verb-ing)

Tam şu an olan eylemler için Present Continuous kullanırız. Yapı: am / is / are + fiil-ing:

ÖznebeÖrnekTürkçesi
IamI am studying right now.Tam şu an ders çalışıyorum.
He / She / ItisShe is watching a series.O bir dizi izliyor.
You / We / TheyareThey are practising now.Onlar şu an antrenman yapıyor.
Kolay yol
  • Cümle iki parçadır: önce be (am/is/are), sonra fiil-ing. İkisi de olmalı.
  • Sık kullanılan işaretler: now, right now, at the moment, today.

3. İşaret Kelimeleri (Signal Words)

Hangi zaman? İşaret kelimesine bak. Bu kelimeler sana Simple mi Continuous mu olduğunu söyler:

Present Simple (rutin)Present Continuous (şu an)
usually, always, oftennow, right now
every day, every morningat the moment
on Sundays, after schooltoday, Look! Listen!
İpucu
  • usually / every day / on Sundays gördüğünde → Present Simple.
  • now / right now / at the moment gördüğünde → Present Continuous.

4. Olumsuz ve Soru (don't / doesn't · Do / Are you...?)

İki zamanın olumsuz ve soru yapısı farklıdır. Simple için don't / doesn't ve Do / Does; Continuous için am not / isn't / aren't ve Am / Is / Are:

 Present SimplePresent Continuous
OlumsuzShe doesn't play tennis.She isn't playing now.
SoruDo you study every day?Are you studying now?
Kısa cevapYes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Dikkat
  • Simple soruda Do / Does kullan: "Does she play?" ("Is she play?" değil.)
  • Continuous soruda be başa gelir: "Are you studying?" ("Do you studying?" değil.)

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
She go to school every day.She goes to school every day.he/she/it → fiil + s.
They study now.They are studying now.now → Present Continuous.
I usually am watching TV.I usually watch TV.usually → Present Simple.
I am wanting a coffee.I want a coffee.want fiili -ing almaz (durum fiili).
Do you studying now?Are you studying now?Continuous soruda be başa gelir.
Altın kural
  • Bazı fiiller (want, like, know, need, love) durum fiilidir ve -ing almaz: "I want a coffee." (❌ I am wanting).

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Chat)

İki genç rutinlerini ve şu an ne yaptıklarını konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What do you usually do after school?Okuldan sonra genelde ne yaparsın?
  • — I usually study for two hours, and then I watch a series.Genelde iki saat ders çalışırım, sonra bir dizi izlerim.
  • — And what are you doing right now?Peki tam şu an ne yapıyorsun?
  • — Right now I am sitting in the garden and I am writing in my notebook.Şu an bahçede oturuyorum ve defterime yazıyorum.
  • — Does your brother study every evening?Kardeşin her akşam ders çalışır mı?
  • — He usually does, but he isn't studying today because he is playing football.Genelde çalışır, ama bugün futbol oynadığı için çalışmıyor.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "She play tennis on Sundays." ✗ → "She plays..." ✓ (he/she/it + s).
  • "They study now." ✗ → "They are studying now." ✓ (now → Continuous).
  • "I am wanting a drink." ✗ → "I want a drink." ✓ (durum fiili).

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Write 5 sentences: some about your routine (Present Simple) and some about right now (Present Continuous). (5 cümle yaz: bazıları rutinin hakkında, bazıları tam şu an olan hakkında.)

Kurallar
  • Rutin için → Present Simple: I study, she plays (he/she/it + s).
  • Şu an için → Present Continuous: I am studying, she is playing. En az bir right now / at the moment kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"I usually ..." "Every day, I ..." "Right now, I am ...ing" "At the moment, she is ...ing" "On Sundays, we ..."
Sample Answer 1. I usually get up at seven o'clock on school days.
2. Right now, I am sitting in my room and writing this.
3. My best friend studies every evening, but she isn't studying now.
4. We play basketball on Sundays, and we practise every Friday.
5. At the moment, my brother is watching a series in the living room.

Reading: A School Day and Right Now

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okurken iki zamana dikkat et: rutin cümleler (Present Simple) ve tam şu an olan cümleler (Present Continuous). İşaret kelimelerini ara:

usually · always · after school · on Saturdays (rutin) · right now · at the moment · today (şu an)

İpucu
  • Metin bir gencin okul rutinini ve tam şu an ne yaptığını anlatıyor — iki zaman iç içe geçer.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A School Day and Right Now

Hi! I am Deniz and I am fifteen years old. I live in a small flat with my family, and I go to a big school near the city centre. On school days I have a busy routine. I usually get up at seven o'clock and I always have breakfast with my family. I don't like cold mornings, but I never skip my breakfast.

My family is small and friendly. I have one brother, and his name is Kaan. My mother and my father work hard, and they are very kind. In the morning I brush my teeth and I wash my face. Then I put on my school uniform and I pack my school bag. My mother makes eggs and bread for breakfast, and I drink a big glass of milk. My brother is often slow in the morning, so I help him. We laugh a lot at the table. I like my mornings when the house is warm and busy.

I go to school by bus, and the journey takes about twenty minutes. After school I study for about two hours, because I want good marks this year. In the evening I often watch a series or listen to music. I don't usually go to bed late on school days, because I need my sleep.

My school is big and I have many friends there. My best friend is Ela, and we sit together in class. I like English and science, but I don't like maths very much. Every day I have five or six lessons. At lunch time I eat a sandwich with my friends in the school garden. We talk about films, music and games. After the last lesson I take the bus home. On the bus I listen to music or I read a short story. My teachers are nice, and they help me when I have a problem.

But right now everything is different because it is Saturday. At the moment I am sitting in the garden and I am writing in my notebook. The sun is warm and the birds are singing. I am not wearing my school uniform today; I am wearing my favourite old jumper.

Today the weather is lovely and the sky is blue. I am not busy at all right now. I am drinking a glass of orange juice, and my cat is sleeping next to me. My phone is on the table, but I am not using it now. A soft wind is moving the leaves in the garden. I feel calm and happy. On school days I never sit like this, because I am always busy. But today I have free time, and I am enjoying every minute.

My brother isn't studying today; he is playing basketball with his friends in the park. My mother usually works on Saturdays, but today she is relaxing at home with a cup of tea. My father is cooking lunch in the kitchen, and it smells really good.

My family is busy with different things this morning. My mother is reading a magazine and she is smiling. My father is listening to the radio while he cooks, and he is singing a little song. My brother is not at home right now; he is at the park with his team. Our neighbour is walking her dog in the street. Everyone is doing something different, but the house feels calm and happy. I love these slow Saturday mornings with my family.

I love my weekdays because they are organised, but I also enjoy weekends because I can rest. Normally I go to bed early; however, tonight I am staying up late to finish a good book about space.

Tonight I have a fun plan too. I usually go to bed early, but today is special. My book is about space, stars and planets, and it is very exciting. Right now I am not reading it, because I am still in the garden. But I want to read it tonight in my bed. I don't want to stop until the last page. I like quiet nights when I can read and think about the stars.

On most weekends I feel free and relaxed. I usually meet my friends on Sunday, and we play games in the park. Sometimes we watch a film at Ela's house. Right now I am alone in the garden, but I am not sad. I am happy with my book, my juice and the warm sun.

So this is my normal week and my special Saturday. I usually study hard, but right now I am relaxing. What do you usually do after school, and what are you doing right now?

Writing: My Routine and Right Now

Rutinini ve şu an olanı anlat, gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Describe your routine and what is happening now.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Rutin ile şu anı karşılaştırmak için özellikle but çok işine yarar:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI usually study and I always do my homework.
butamaI usually study at night, but right now I am relaxing.
becauseçünküHe isn't studying today because it is Saturday.
sobu yüzdenThe exam is tomorrow, so I am studying hard now.
İpucu
  • Rutin ile şu anı karşılaştır: "I usually ..., but right now I am ...ing."

Task (Görev)

Describe your usual routine and what is happening right now. (Genel rutinini ve tam şu an olanı anlat.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one routine action (rutin bir eylem — I usually ...)
  • one action happening now (şu an olan bir eylem — I am ...ing)
  • a contrast with but (usually ... but right now ...)
  • a time word: usually / every day / on Sundays / right now / at the moment
  • one negative (olumsuz bir cümle — ... don't / doesn't / isn't ...) + one connector

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"On school days, I usually ..." "But right now, I am ..." "My brother usually ..., but today ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) On school days I have a fixed routine. I usually get up at seven and I always study after school. But right now it is Saturday, so everything is different. At the moment I am sitting in a café and I am writing this text. My brother isn't studying today; he is playing football with his friends.
