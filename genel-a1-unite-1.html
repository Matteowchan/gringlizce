Grammar: be (am / is / are) + Subject Pronouns

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Özne Zamirleri (Subject Pronouns)

İngilizcede her cümlenin bir öznesi olmak zorundadır. Türkçede "Mühendisim." diyebiliriz; İngilizcede ise mutlaka özneyi söyleriz: "I am an engineer." Önce özne zamirlerini öğrenelim:

"be" Fiili (am / is / are)

"be" fiili Türkçedeki "-im, -sin, -dir" eklerinin karşılığıdır ve özneye göre üç şekil alır: am, is, are. Günlük konuşmada neredeyse her zaman kısa formlar (I'm, you're, he's...) kullanılır.

İş Hayatından Örnekler (Work-Context Examples)

  • "I am an engineer."Mühendisim.
  • "She is our manager."O bizim yöneticimiz.
  • "We are in a meeting."Toplantıdayız.
  • "Are you from the Istanbul office?"İstanbul ofisinden misin?
  • "He isn't here today."O bugün burada değil.
Kullanım Notları
  • Meslek söylerken a / an kullanırız: "I am a designer.", "He is an engineer." (Türkçede karşılığı yoktur, İngilizcede zorunludur.)
  • Sık kalıplar: "in a meeting" (toplantıda), "at work" (işte), "at the office" (ofiste).

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them in your own sentences. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

colleague (n)
a person you work with (iş arkadaşı)
Syn: co-worker (iş arkadaşı) · Ant: stranger (yabancı)
"This is my colleague, Ayşe."
manager (n)
the person who leads a team (yönetici)
Syn: boss (patron) · Ant: employee (çalışan)
"Our manager is in a meeting."
employee (n)
a person who works for a company (çalışan)
Syn: worker (çalışan) · Ant: employer (işveren)
"Baytek has 200 employees."
company (n)
a business organization (şirket)
Syn: firm (firma) · Ant: individual (birey)
"Our company is in Istanbul."
office (n)
the place where people work at desks (ofis)
Syn: workplace (iş yeri) · Ant: home (ev)
"The office is open at 9."
meeting (n)
a time when people talk about work (toplantı)
Syn: discussion (görüşme) · Ant: break (mola)
"The meeting is at 11 o'clock."
busy (adj)
with a lot of work (meşgul)
Syn: occupied (meşgul/dolu) · Ant: free (müsait)
"I am busy today."
new (adj)
starting now; not old (yeni)
Syn: recent (yeni) · Ant: old (eski)
"She is the new designer."
early (adj/adv)
before the usual time (erken)
Syn: ahead of time (vaktinden önce) · Ant: late (geç)
"He is early for the meeting."
friendly (adj)

Syn: kind (nazik) · Ant: rude (kaba)
"My colleagues are friendly."
guest (n)

Syn: visitor (ziyaretçi) · Ant: host (ev sahibi)
"We have a guest from Ankara."
job (n)

Syn: work (iş) · Ant: hobby (hobi)
"Her job is interesting."

Reading: A New Day at Baytek

Read the text, then answer the questions. (Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Baytek ofisinde yeni bir gün.
A New Day at Baytek

Selin is a new employee at Baytek, a software company in Istanbul. Today is her first day. She is at the office at nine o'clock. Her manager, Mr. Demir, is friendly. 'Welcome to the team,' he says.

The office is big and modern. There are twenty people in Selin's department. Her colleagues are from different cities. Two colleagues, Ali and Merve, are engineers. They are very busy today because there is a meeting at eleven o'clock.

At the meeting, Selin says, 'Hello, everyone. I'm Selin. I'm the new designer. I'm happy to be here.' Her colleagues smile. 'Nice to meet you, Selin. Welcome to Baytek!'

After the meeting, Selin is at her desk. Her computer is new and fast. She is tired but happy. Her first day is a good day.

Writing: An Email to Your New Team

Write and submit your email; your teacher will review it. (E-postanı yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

Task (Görev)

You are a new employee. Write an email to your team. Introduce yourself. (Yeni bir çalışansın. Ekibine bir e-posta yaz, kendini tanıt.)

What to include (Neler olmalı)

  • your name and job
  • where you are from
  • your department and company
  • two or three sentences about you (city, interests)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Dear team, / Best regards,)

Example openers (Örnek başlangıçlar)

"My name is ... and I am the new ..." "I am from ..." "I am happy to be here."

Your Email (150-180 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will evaluate your writing.)

Sample Answer (~150 words) Dear team,

My name is Deniz Arslan and I am the new sales specialist at Baytek. Today is my first day, and I am very happy to be here.

I am from Izmir, but now I am in Istanbul. My department is the sales department. Our office is big and modern, and my colleagues are very friendly.

I am twenty-five years old. My hobbies are music and photography. At the weekend, I am usually at the sea or in the park with my friends. I am a friendly person, and I am always ready to help.

I am new, so I have many questions. Please say hello when you are near my desk. My desk is next to the meeting room. Our first team meeting is on Monday, and I am very excited.

Thank you, and nice to meet you all!

Best regards,
Deniz
