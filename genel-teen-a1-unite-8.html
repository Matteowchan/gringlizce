Grammar: Present Continuous

Tam şu an olan eylemleri anlat: am/is/are + fiil-ing. (Say what is happening right now.)

Selam, ben yine Gri. Bu ünitede tam şu an olan eylemleri anlatıyoruz: mesajlaşmak, müzik dinlemek, ders çalışmak, spor yapmak. Yapı hep aynı: am / is / are + fiil-ing: I am texting my friend. She is listening to music. They are studying for the exam. Türkçesi "...-ıyor / -iyor" demektir. Başlayalım.

Present Continuous Ne Demek? (What is it?)

Present Continuous, tam şu an olan eylemleri anlatır. Türkçesi "...-ıyor / -iyor" demektir. Yapı her zaman aynıdır: am / is / are + fiil-ing. Genelde now, right now, at the moment gibi işaret kelimeleriyle gelir:

  • I am texting my friend.Arkadaşıma mesaj atıyorum. (şu an)
  • She is listening to music.O müzik dinliyor. (şu an)
  • They are studying for the exam right now.Onlar tam şu an sınava çalışıyor.
Kolay yol
  • Cümle iki parçadır: önce be (am/is/are), sonra fiil-ing. İkisi de olmalı.
  • now, right now, at the moment gördüğünde bu zamanı düşün: bir şey tam şu an oluyor.

1. am / is / are Seç (be + verb-ing)

Önce özneye göre doğru be kelimesini seçeriz: I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are. Sonra fiile -ing ekleriz:

ÖznebeÖrnekTürkçesi
IamI am texting a friend.Bir arkadaşıma mesaj atıyorum.
He / She / ItisShe is listening to music.O müzik dinliyor.
You / We / TheyareThey are studying together.Onlar birlikte çalışıyor.
Kolay yol
  • I am, he/she/it is, you/we/they are. Bunu bir ritim gibi ezberle.
  • be'yi unutma: "She listening." ✗ → "She is listening." ✓

2. Fiile -ing Ekle (spelling)

Fiilin sonuna -ing ekleriz. Çoğu fiil çok kolaydır; birkaç küçük yazım kuralı vardır:

KuralFiil-ing hâli
çoğu fiil: +ingstudystudying
çoğu fiil: +ingtexttexting
sonda -e varsa: -e at, +ingtaketaking
kısa fiil: son harfi ikizlerunrunning
Altın kural
  • Çoğu fiil için sadece +ing yeter: listen → listening, watch → watching.
  • take → taking (e düşer), run → running (n ikizlenir).

3. Şu An Oluyor (now / right now / at the moment)

Bu zaman, tam şu an olan şeyler içindir. Türkçe -ıyor / -iyor ile biter. Sık kullanılan işaret kelimeleri:

İşaretÖrnekTürkçesi
nowI am studying now.Şimdi ders çalışıyorum.
right nowHe is chatting online right now.O tam şu an internetten sohbet ediyor.
at the momentWe are watching a video at the moment.Şu an bir video izliyoruz.
İpucu
  • now, right now, at the moment gördüğünde → fiil-ing kullan.
  • Türkçesi hep -ıyor ile biter: texting = mesajlaşıyor.

4. Olumsuz, Soru ve Kısa Cevaplar (isn't / aren't · Are you...?)

Olumsuz için be'den sonra not gelir: am not / isn't / aren't. Soru sormak için be'yi başa alırız: Am / Is / Are + özne + fiil-ing? Kısa cevaplar çok kolaydır:

OlumluOlumsuzSoru
I am texting.I am not texting.Am I texting?
She is listening.She isn't listening.Is she listening?
They are studying.They aren't studying.Are they studying?

Kısa cevaplarda fiili tekrar etmeyiz:

SoruEvetHayır
Are you studying?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is he listening to music?Yes, he is.No, he isn't.
Dikkat
  • Soruda be en başa gelir: Are you texting? ("You are texting?" değil.)
  • Kısa cevapta fiili tekrarlamayız: "Yes, I am." ("Yes, I am studying." demeyiz.)

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Bu tabloyu kontrol listen yap. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu hataları yapma:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I texting my friend.I am texting my friend.be eksik → am + fiil-ing.
She is listen to music.She is listening to music.fiil-ing olmalı.
They is studying now.They are studying now.they → are.
You are running?Are you running?soruda be başa gelir.
He is takeing a photo.He is taking a photo.take → taking (e düşer).

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Chat)

İki genç şu an ne yaptıklarını mesajlaşarak konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan kelimelere dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — Hey, what are you doing?Selam, ne yapıyorsun?
  • — I am studying for the maths exam, and I am listening to music too.Matematik sınavına çalışıyorum, bir de müzik dinliyorum.
  • — Is your brother studying as well?Kardeşin de çalışıyor mu?
  • — No, he isn't. He is playing football with his friends right now.Hayır. O tam şu an arkadaşlarıyla futbol oynuyor.
  • — Are you texting Deniz at the moment?Şu an Deniz'le mesajlaşıyor musun?
  • — Yes, I am. We are planning our project because it is due tomorrow.Evet. Projemizi planlıyoruz çünkü yarın teslim.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "She listening." ✗ → "She is listening." ✓ (be'yi unutma).
  • "I am study." ✗ → "I am studying." ✓ (fiil-ing olmalı).
  • "You are texting?" ✗ → "Are you texting?" ✓ (soruda be başa gelir).

Alıştırma (Practice)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Speaking: My Words

Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan. (Learn the words, then use them.)

Bir gencin gün içinde yaptığı eylemler. Örnek cümleler am / is / are + fiil-ing ile yazıldı.

text → texting (mesajlaşıyor)

"She is listening to music."

watching (v-ing)
watch → watching (izliyor)
"He is watching a video."
chatting (v-ing)
chat → chatting (sohbet ediyor)

"We are chatting online."

running (v-ing)

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

What are you and your friends doing right now? Write 5 sentences with am / is / are + verb-ing. (Sen ve arkadaşların şu an ne yapıyorsunuz? am / is / are + fiil-ing ile 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Cümle iki parçadır: be + fiil-ing (I am studying).
  • Doğru be'yi seç: I am, he/she/it is, you/we/they are. En az bir now / right now / at the moment kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"I am ...ing" "She is ...ing" "They are ...ing now" "Are you ...ing?" "He isn't ...ing"
Sample Answer 1. I am studying for the science exam right now.
2. My best friend is texting me about the homework.
3. My brother isn't studying; he is playing football with his team.
4. We are listening to music and taking photos at the moment.
5. Are you watching the match now? I am scrolling on my phone!
Ünite 8 · Present Continuous · Bölüm 3/7

Reading: A Busy Afternoon

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu kelimeleri hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

texting · listening · studying · watching · chatting · playing · running · taking · practising · scrolling

İpucu
  • Metin tam şu an olan şeyleri anlatıyor — o yüzden am / is / are + fiil-ing çok geçer.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
A Busy Afternoon

Hi! I am Elif and I am sixteen. It is Wednesday afternoon and everyone at home is busy right now. I am sitting at my desk and I am studying for the biology exam. My phone is next to me, so I am also texting my classmate about the homework.

My room is not big, but I love it. A big window is next to my desk, and the afternoon sun is coming in. My biology book is open, and my green pen is in my hand. I am making a short list of important words for the test. Sometimes I stop and I look out of the window for a minute. Then I read my notes again. Studying is hard work, but I feel calm and happy right now.

The big exam is on Friday, so I am reading my notes again and again. I am not watching any videos now because I really want a good grade. My cat Pamuk is sitting on my books, and she is sleeping in the warm sun. Are you studying hard this week too?

Pamuk is a lovely white cat, and she is very lazy today. She is not moving at all. Her eyes are closed, and she is breathing slowly. I want a photo of her, so I am picking up my phone now. "Stay there, Pamuk!" I am saying softly. She is still sleeping, so she is not listening to me. My best friend Ada is texting me too. She is asking about question five, and I am helping her with the answer.

Our class has a group chat on the phone. Many students are online right now. Some friends are asking questions, and other friends are sharing answers. One boy is sending funny pictures, so everybody is laughing. I am reading the messages quickly, but I am not chatting too much, because I really need to study. After the exam on Friday, we all want to relax together.

My older brother Kaan is in his room. He isn't studying at the moment — he is playing an online game and chatting with his teammates. My little sister Zeynep is listening to music with her headphones, and she is scrolling on her tablet.

Kaan loves computer games, and he plays every afternoon. His room is very noisy right now. His friends are talking on the microphone, and they are laughing together. Kaan is holding his controller, and his eyes are big and happy. He is not doing his homework yet, but he says he will start later. I am shaking my head and smiling, because my brother is always the same.

Kaan is happy because his team is winning, so he is shouting at the screen. "Come and watch!" he is saying, but I am not moving from my desk. Zeynep isn't doing her homework yet; she is dancing to her favourite song in front of the mirror.

Zeynep is only ten, and she loves music videos. She is copying the dance from her favourite singer. Her feet are moving fast, and she is having a lot of fun. "Look at me!" she is saying, but nobody is watching her. Kaan is still playing his game, and I am still reading my notes. Our house is loud and busy, but this is normal for a Wednesday.

In the garden, my father is practising basketball because there is a company match on Saturday. My mother is running on the treadmill, and she is watching a series at the same time. Our neighbour is helping my father, and the two men are laughing a lot.

The garden is full of noise too. My father is wearing his old sports shoes, and he is jumping to reach the basket. He is not very fast, but he is trying hard. My mother is on the treadmill near the door, and she is running quite fast today. She is laughing at the funny parts of her series. Everybody in my family is doing sport or something fun this afternoon.

Soon I am getting a little hungry, so I am thinking about a snack. A nice smell is coming from the kitchen downstairs. I am closing my book for two minutes, and I am walking to the door. My family is still busy, and everyone is smiling. I am taking one more photo, because I want to remember this happy day.

My grandmother is in the kitchen, and she is making tea for everybody right now. She is also baking a cake because it is my birthday tomorrow! Everybody is doing something different, but we are all enjoying this busy, happy afternoon together. What are you doing right now? Are you having fun too?

Ünite 8 · Present Continuous · Bölüm 5/7

Writing: What Is Happening Now?

Şu an olanları anlat ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Describe what is happening now; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI am studying and my sister is listening to music.
butamaHe is playing a game, but he isn't studying.
becauseçünküShe is practising hard because there is a match on Saturday.
sobu yüzdenThe exam is tomorrow, so we are studying together.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but, because ya da so.

Task (Görev)

Describe what you and the people around you are doing right now. (Sen ve etrafındakiler şu an ne yapıyorsunuz, anlat.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • what you are doing (ne yapıyorsun — I am ...ing)
  • what someone else is doing (biri ne yapıyor — He / She is ...ing)
  • what two or more people are doing (They / We are ...ing)
  • one negative (olumsuz bir cümle — ... isn't / aren't ...ing)
  • a time word: now / right now / at the moment + one connector (and / but / because / so)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"Right now, I am ..." "My friend is ..., but ..." "At the moment, we are ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) It is Sunday evening and everyone at home is busy right now. I am studying for the English exam, and I am texting my friend about the project. My brother isn't studying; he is watching a match. My parents are listening to music in the kitchen because they are cooking dinner. We are all having a relaxed evening.
Dinleme

Listening

Videoyu izle, ardından dinleme görevlerini tamamla (eşleştirme, çoktan seçmeli, boşluk, tablo, sıralama).
