Grammar: Gerund & Infinitive

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

İki Fiil Yan Yana Gelince (Verb + Verb)

İngilizcede çoğu zaman bir cümlede tek bir fiil vardır. But very often we want to put two verbs together: "I like ...", "I want ..." ne? Bir eylem! İşte o zaman ikinci fiil değişir. Türkçede "yüzmeyi severim" ya da "gitmek istiyorum" deriz; İngilizcede de ikinci fiil ya -ing alır (gerund) ya da başına to gelir (infinitive).

  • I enjoy swimming.Yüzmeyi severim / yüzmekten keyif alırım. (enjoy + V-ing)
  • I want to swim.Yüzmek istiyorum. (want + to + V)

Bu ünitede iki soruya cevap vereceğiz: (1) İkinci fiil ne zaman -ing olur? (2) Ne zaman to alır? İyi haber: bu, hangi ilk fiili kullandığına bağlıdır. So you learn a few verbs with -ing and a few verbs with to, and then it becomes easy.

1. Gerund Nedir? (Verb + -ing)

Bir fiile -ing eklediğimizde ona gerund deriz. A gerund is a verb that works like a noun (isim gibi davranan fiil). "swim" bir eylemdir; "swimming" ise "yüzme / yüzmek" gibi bir isimdir. Bazı fiillerden sonra ikinci fiil her zaman gerund olur.

Yazım — küçük -ing kuralları
  • -e ile bitenlerde e düşer: make → making, write → writing.
  • Kısa ünlü + tek sessizde sessiz çiftlenir: swim → swimming, run → running, shop → shopping.
  • Çoğu fiilde sadece -ing eklenir: read → reading, play → playing.

2. -ing Alan Fiiller (like / enjoy / hate / finish / mind)

Aşağıdaki fiillerden sonra ikinci fiil -ing alır. Bunlar genelde bir eylemden hoşlanma / hoşlanmama ya da bir eylemi bitirme anlamı taşır. Listeyi ezberle — cümlelerin çoğu bu fiillerle döner:

3. to + Fiil Alan Fiiller (want / need / decide / hope / would like)

Aşağıdaki fiillerden sonra ise ikinci fiil başına to alır (infinitive). Bunlar genelde bir istek, plan, karar ya da ihtiyaç anlatır. Yani gelecekle, niyetle ilgilidir:

Altın kural 2
  • want, need, decide, hope, plan, would like her zaman to + yalın fiil ister: "I want to go." ✓ — "I want going." ✗

4. like doing mi, like to do mu? (A Special Case)

Bir sürpriz: like, love, hate fiilleri iki şekilde de kullanılabilir — hem -ing hem to. Anlam neredeyse aynıdır, bu yüzden A2 seviyesinde ikisi de doğrudur:

  • I like cooking. = I like to cook.Yemek yapmayı severim. (iki form da doğru)
  • She hates getting up early. = She hates to get up early.Erken kalkmaktan nefret eder.

Küçük bir nüans: like + -ing genelde "o eylemden zevk almak" (hobi) anlamındadır; like + to ise bazen "iyi/doğru olduğu için tercih etmek" anlamı taşır. A2'de bunu bilmen yeterli:

Altın kural 3
  • like / love / hate ile hem -ing hem to doğrudur: "I love reading" = "I love to read".
  • Ama enjoy yalnızca -ing ister — would like yalnızca to ister. Bunları karıştırma.

6. Uygulama: Hafta Sonu Planı (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about their free time and plans. Koyu yazılan gerund (V-ing) ve infinitive (to + V) yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — What do you like doing at the weekend?Hafta sonu ne yapmayı seversin?
  • — I love hiking! I really enjoy being outside. What about you?Doğa yürüyüşüne bayılırım! Dışarıda olmaktan gerçekten keyif alırım. Ya sen?
  • — I don't mind walking, but I prefer staying at home. I want to finish a good book.Yürümekte sakınca görmem ama evde kalmayı tercih ederim. İyi bir kitabı bitirmek istiyorum.
  • — Nice! I also need to study a little. I hope to pass my English exam.Güzel! Biraz da çalışmam gerek. İngilizce sınavımı geçmeyi umuyorum.
  • — Good luck! Would you like to meet for a coffee on Sunday?Bol şans! Pazar bir kahve içmek için buluşmak ister misin?
  • — Yes! I'd love to. Let's decide to meet at eleven.Evet! Çok isterim. Saat on birde buluşmaya karar verelim.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane enjoy / love / like + V-ing kalıbı var? (Cevap: 3 — like doing, love hiking, enjoy being)
  • "I want to finish a good book" cümlesinde neden to var? (Cevap: want her zaman to ister.)
  • "I hope to pass" yerine "I hope passing" desek olur muydu? (Cevap: Hayır — hope her zaman to ister.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then talk about what you like and want to do. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra neleri sevdiğini ve yapmak istediğini anlat.)

enjoy (v)
to get pleasure from something (keyif almak)
Syn: love (bayılmak) · Ant: hate (nefret etmek)
"I enjoy listening to music."
hate (v)
to dislike something very much (nefret etmek)
Syn: dislike (hoşlanmamak) · Ant: love (bayılmak)
"She hates getting up early."
finish (v)
to complete something (bitirmek)
Syn: complete (tamamlamak) · Ant: start (başlamak)
"I finished doing my homework."
mind (v)
to feel that something is a problem (sakınca görmek)
Syn: object (itiraz etmek) · Ant: welcome (hoş karşılamak)
"I don't mind waiting for you."
prefer (v)
to like one thing more than another (tercih etmek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: reject (reddetmek)
"I prefer walking to driving."
want (v)
to wish to have or do something (istemek)
Syn: wish (dilemek) · Ant: refuse (reddetmek)
"I want to learn the guitar."
need (v)
to have to have something (ihtiyaç duymak)
Syn: require (gerektirmek) · Ant: waste (israf etmek)
"I need to buy some milk."
decide (v)
to choose after thinking (karar vermek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: hesitate (tereddüt etmek)
"We decided to go by train."
hope (v)
to want something good to happen (ummak)
Syn: wish (dilemek) · Ant: doubt (şüphe etmek)
"I hope to see you soon."
avoid (v)
to try not to do something (kaçınmak)
Syn: escape (kaçmak) · Ant: face (yüzleşmek)
"He avoids drinking coffee at night."
practise (v)
to do something often to improve (pratik yapmak)
Syn: train (çalışmak) · Ant: neglect (ihmal etmek)
"I practise speaking every day."
hobby (n)
a free-time activity you enjoy (hobi)
Syn: pastime (uğraş) · Ant: work (iş)
"Cooking is my favourite hobby."
free time (n)
time when you are not working (boş zaman)
Syn: spare time (serbest zaman) · Ant: work time (mesai)
"In my free time, I love painting."
plan (v)
to think about the future and organize it (planlamak)
Syn: arrange (ayarlamak) · Ant: improvise (doğaçlama yapmak)
"They plan to open a new office."
Hobiler ve planlar: gerund (V-ing) ve infinitive (to + V) yapılarını kullan.

Big Plans at Baytek

At Baytek, the new year always starts with plans and goals. This January, the team decided to try something different. Everybody wrote down one thing they wanted to learn and one habit they hoped to change.

This idea is a tradition at Baytek. Every January, the workers meet in the big room. They drink tea and coffee, and they talk about the new year. On the wall, there is a large board. At the top, the board says "Our Goals." Everybody writes a goal on a small card, and then they put the card on the board. It is a simple habit, but people really like doing it. Writing a goal on paper feels good. It helps you to remember your plan. The manager, Mr. Demir, started this tradition five years ago. He believes that making a plan is the first step. "You cannot finish a goal," he often says, "if you never start it." The team enjoys reading the cards together. Some goals are about work, and some goals are about free time and hobbies.

Selin enjoys designing, so she wants to learn a new software this year. She doesn't like sitting at her desk all day, so she also decided to take a short walk every afternoon. "I need to move more," she said, "and I love spending time outside."

Selin works in the design team. She enjoys drawing on her computer, and she loves making new logos for clients. But she doesn't like sitting still for many hours. Her back hurts, and she feels tired. So this year she decided to change her habit. Every afternoon, at three o'clock, she stops working for ten minutes. She goes outside and takes a short walk in the small park near the office. She enjoys looking at the trees and listening to the birds. Sometimes a friend from work joins her, and they prefer walking together. "Walking helps me to think," Selin says. After the walk, she comes back and starts designing again. She feels fresh, and she avoids getting too tired. Learning a new software is not always easy, but Selin likes trying new things.

Mr. Demir hates wasting time in long meetings. He hopes to finish every meeting in thirty minutes, because people stop listening after that. He also wants to start reading one business book each month.

Mr. Demir is a busy manager, but he is kind. He hates wasting time, so he always tries to keep meetings short. In the past, some meetings were two hours long. People felt bored, and they stopped listening. Now Mr. Demir uses a small timer. When thirty minutes finish, the meeting is over. Everybody is happy about this new rule. Mr. Demir also wants to read more this year. Every morning, before work, he enjoys reading for twenty minutes with a cup of coffee. Reading helps him to learn new ideas. "I don't want to stop learning," he says. "A good manager needs to grow, too." He keeps a list of books on his desk, and he loves finishing a book and starting a new one.

Ali and Merve prefer working together. They enjoy solving problems as a team, but they need to improve their English. They decided to practise speaking every Friday. At first they didn't like recording their voices, but now they don't mind it.

Ali and Merve work in the sales team. They enjoy talking to customers, but sometimes the customers speak only English. This is a problem, because Ali and Merve are still learning. They don't want to make mistakes in front of clients. So they decided to practise more. Every Friday afternoon, they sit in a quiet room for one hour. They read short texts, they ask each other questions, and they record their voices on a phone. "My voice sounds strange!" Merve said at first. But listening to their mistakes helps them to improve. After two weeks, Ali could already speak faster. They both hope to speak English well by the end of the year. Practising every week is hard work, but they enjoy learning together.

Other people at Baytek made goals, too. Deniz, from the office kitchen, wants to cook healthier food for the team. He decided to stop using too much sugar, and he plans to add more fruit and vegetables. Ayşe, from the accounting team, hates sitting all day, so she decided to start cycling to work. She enjoys riding her bike in the morning, and she saves money on the bus. Ozan is very shy, and he wants to speak more in meetings. He is afraid of talking in front of people, but he is trying to be brave. "I need to practise," he says. "I don't want to stay quiet all the time." Every person has a different goal, but they all want to grow and to feel better.

At Baytek, people also like sharing their hobbies. On Wednesdays, some workers stay after lunch for a short "hobby hour." It is not a work meeting; it is just for fun. Last week, Selin showed her drawings, and everybody enjoyed looking at them. Merve loves baking, so she brought a cake, and the team enjoyed eating it together. Ali plays the guitar, and he wants to learn one new song each month. He isn't a great player yet, but he keeps practising. "I don't want to be perfect," he says. "I just enjoy playing." These small moments help the team to relax. When people enjoy spending time together, they also work better. Some workers now want to start new hobbies at home, too. Deniz hopes to learn to paint, and Ayşe wants to try photography. They all agree that having a hobby is good for the mind.

Mark, the newest member, wants to become a manager one day. He isn't afraid of trying new things, and he never avoids asking questions. "Making mistakes is normal," he said. "I just keep learning." By the end of January, nobody had finished their goals, but everybody had started.

February came, and the team kept working on their goals. Some days were easy, and some days were hard. Selin sometimes forgot to take her walk, and Mr. Demir sometimes had a long meeting. But nobody wanted to give up. On the goal board, people started to add small stars next to their cards. A star means "I did it today." Slowly, the board filled with stars. The team learned an important lesson this year: finishing a big goal takes time, but starting is the most important step. At Baytek, everybody keeps trying, and everybody keeps learning. And that is the real reason the new year always begins with plans and goals.
