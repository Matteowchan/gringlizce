Sentence & Summary CompletionCompletion (Sentence / Summary / Note / Table)

Completion sorularında başarı tek cümleyle özetlenir: boşluğu pasajdaki kelimeyle, aynen ve kelime sınırını aşmadan doldur. Bu sayfa bunu adım adım Türkçe anlatıyor.

Success in completion questions can be summed up in one sentence: fill the gap with the word from the passage, exactly and without going over the word limit. This page explains that step by step.

Completion (tamamlama) soru ailesi, IELTS Academic Reading bölümünün en çok puan getiren ama en çok da dikkatsizlikten kaybettiren tipidir. Sentence (cümle), summary (özet), note (not), table (tablo) ve flow-chart (akış şeması) tamamlama olmak üzere beş alt türü vardır ve hepsi aynı çekirdek beceriye dayanır: pasajdaki bilgiyi bulup boşluğa uyan kelimeyi aynen kopyalamak.

Bu tipte cevapların neredeyse tamamı pasajda birebir geçer; senin eş anlamlı kelime üretmen istenmez. Kritik iki kural: kelime sınırı (örn. “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS”) ve dilbilgisi uyumu. Bu sayfada beş alt türü, adım adım stratejiyi, sınır + dilbilgisi tablosunu, tam çözümlü bir örneği ve sık tuzakları göreceksin.

The Completion question family is the type in the IELTS Academic Reading section that earns the most marks but also loses the most through carelessness. It has five sub-types — sentence, summary, note, table and flow-chart completion — and they all rest on the same core skill: finding the information in the passage and copying the word that fits the gap exactly.

Almost all answers of this type appear in the passage word for word; you are not asked to produce a synonym of your own. Two critical rules: the word limit (e.g. “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS”) and grammatical agreement. On this page you will see the five sub-types, the step-by-step strategy, the limit + grammar table, a fully worked example and common traps.

Konu Özeti
  • Kelime sınırını oku: “ONE WORD”, “TWO WORDS”, “THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER” — talimat değişir, her seti başta kontrol et.
  • Sayı sayar gibi say: tire (well-being) tek kelime; “a / the” gibi artişeller de kelime sayılır.
  • Aynen kopyala: pasajdaki kelimeyi değiştirme, eş anlamlı yazma, çoğul/tekil bozma.
  • Dilbilgisi testi: boşluğa yazdığını cümleye koy — isim mi fiil mi, tekil mi çoğul mu uyuyor mu?
  • Sıra takip eder: summary/note/table soruları genelde pasaj sırasını izler; kaybolursan bir önceki cevabı çapa yap.
  • Boş bırakma: emin değilsen bile en olası kelimeyi yaz; yanlış cevap ekstra ceza getirmez.
Quick Reference
  • Read the word limit: “ONE WORD”, “TWO WORDS”, “THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER” — the instruction changes, so check each set at the start.
  • Count as if counting numbers: a hyphenated word (well-being) is one word; articles like “a / the” also count as words.
  • Copy exactly: do not change the word in the passage, do not write a synonym, do not break singular/plural.
  • Grammar test: put what you wrote into the sentence — does a noun or a verb, singular or plural, fit?
  • Order is followed: summary/note/table questions usually follow the order of the passage; if you get lost, use the previous answer as an anchor.
  • Do not leave blanks: even if you are unsure, write the most likely word; a wrong answer carries no extra penalty.

Completion (tamamlama), boş bırakılmış bir metni pasajdaki bilgiyle doldurduğun soru ailesidir. Beş alt türü vardır ve hepsi aynı temele dayanır: pasajı tarayıp doğru kelimeyi bulmak ve aynen kopyalamak.

  • Sentence Completion (cümle tamamlama): Yarım bırakılmış cümleleri pasajdaki kelimelerle tamamlarsın. Örn. “The factory reduced its energy use by installing ________.”
  • Summary Completion (özet tamamlama): Pasajın bir bölümünün kısa özeti verilir, boşlukları doldurursun. İki biçimi vardır: (a) pasajdan kelime al, (b) verilen bir kutudan (word bank) kelime seç — bu ikincisinde kelimeler çoğunlukla paraphrase (yeniden ifade) edilmiştir.
  • Note Completion (not tamamlama): Başlık ve alt maddeler halinde düzenlenmiş kısa notları doldurursun. Kısa, madde işareti tarzı metindir.
  • Table Completion (tablo tamamlama): Bilgi satır/sütun halinde düzenlenir; boşlukta ne tür bilgi (tarih, isim, sayı) beklendiği çoğunlukla sütun başlığından bellidir.
  • Flow-chart Completion (akış şeması tamamlama): Bir sürecin (deney, üretim, doğal olay) adımları oklarla verilir; boşlukları sırayla doldurursun. Pasajda genelde bir “process” (süreç) paragrafına dayanır.

Ortak kural. Beş türün de başında bir kelime sınırı talimatı vardır: “NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER”, “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS” ya da “ONE WORD ONLY”. Bu sınır her soru setinde değişebilir — kesinlikle her setin başındaki talimatı yeniden oku.

Önemli fark: kutulu (word-bank) summary dışındaki tüm completion türlerinde cevap pasajda birebir geçer. Kutulu summary’de ise pasajı anlayıp anlamı karşılayan kelimeyi kutudan seçersin; orada eş anlamlı düşünmek gerekir.

Completion is the question family in which you fill a gapped text with information from the passage. It has five sub-types, and they all rest on the same base: scanning the passage, finding the right word and copying it exactly.

  • Sentence Completion: You complete half-finished sentences with words from the passage. E.g. “The factory reduced its energy use by installing ________.”
  • Summary Completion: A short summary of part of the passage is given, and you fill the gaps. It comes in two forms: (a) take a word from the passage, (b) choose a word from a given box (word bank) — in this second form the words are mostly paraphrased (reworded).
  • Note Completion: You fill in short notes arranged as a heading and sub-items. It is short, bullet-point-style text.
  • Table Completion: Information is arranged in rows/columns; what kind of information (date, name, number) is expected in the gap is usually clear from the column heading.
  • Flow-chart Completion: The steps of a process (experiment, production, natural event) are given with arrows; you fill the gaps in order. It usually rests on a “process” paragraph in the passage.

Common rule. All five types begin with a word limit instruction: “NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER”, “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS” or “ONE WORD ONLY”. This limit can change in each question set — always re-read the instruction at the start of every set.

An important difference: in every completion type except the word-bank summary, the answer appears in the passage word for word. In the word-bank summary, however, you understand the passage and pick the word that matches the meaning from the box; there you do need to think in synonyms.

Completion soruları aceleye gelir. Aşağıdaki sıra, hem doğru kelimeyi bulmanı hem de kelime sınırı/dilbilgisi hatalarından kaçınmanı sağlar.

  1. Talimatı ve kelime sınırını oku. “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER” mı? Bu sınırı kafanın bir köşesine yaz; her cevabı buna göre kırp.
  2. Boşluğun dilbilgisi türünü tahmin et. Boşluktan önce/sonra gelen kelimelere bak. Bir artişel (“a/an/the”) veya sıfat varsa boşluk muhtemelen isim; bir özne varsa fiil; “by / of / in” gibi edat sonrası çoğunlukla isim/isim-fiil gelir. Böylece pasajda ne tür bir kelime arayacağını bilirsin.
  3. Anahtar kelimeleri belirle ve pasajda bul. Boşluğun etrafındaki değişmesi zor kelimeleri (özel isim, sayı, teknik terim) seç; bunları pasajda tarayarak (scanning) doğru cümleyi bul. Unutma: soru cümlesi çoğunlukla paraphrase edilmiştir, cevap kelimesi ise genelde birebir durur.
  4. Kelimeyi pasajdan AYNEN al. Bulduğun cümledeki doğru kelimeyi harfi harfine kopyala. Kendi eş anlamlını yazma; zaman/çoğul ekini bozma.
  5. Kelime sınırını say. Cevabın kaç kelime? Sınırı aşıyorsan gereksiz kelimeleri (artişel, sıfat) at ve çekirdeği bırak — ama anlam bozulmasın.
  6. Dilbilgisi uyumunu kontrol et. Cevabı boşluğa koyup cümleyi zihninde oku. Akıcı ve doğru mu? “The number of ________ increase” gibi bir yerde fiilin/ismin uyumu tutmuyorsa yanlış kelimeyi almışsındır.
Özet refleks: Tahmin et (tür) → tara (anahtar) → kopyala (aynen) → say (sınır) → dene (dilbilgisi). Bu beş hareket ezberlendiğinde completion soruları neredeyse mekanik hale gelir.

Completion questions get rushed. The order below helps you both find the right word and avoid word-limit and grammar mistakes.

  1. Read the instruction and the word limit. Is it “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER”? Keep this limit in a corner of your mind; trim every answer to fit it.
  2. Predict the grammatical type of the gap. Look at the words before/after the gap. If there is an article (“a/an/the”) or an adjective, the gap is probably a noun; if there is a subject, a verb; after a preposition like “by / of / in” a noun/gerund usually follows. This tells you what kind of word to look for in the passage.
  3. Identify keywords and find them in the passage. Choose the hard-to-change words around the gap (proper noun, number, technical term); scan for them in the passage to find the right sentence. Remember: the question sentence is usually paraphrased, while the answer word usually stays word for word.
  4. Take the word from the passage EXACTLY. Copy the correct word from the sentence you found letter for letter. Do not write your own synonym; do not break the tense/plural ending.
  5. Count the word limit. How many words is your answer? If it goes over the limit, drop the unnecessary words (article, adjective) and keep the core — but do not break the meaning.
  6. Check grammatical agreement. Put the answer in the gap and read the sentence in your head. Is it fluent and correct? Somewhere like “The number of ________ increase”, if the verb/noun agreement does not hold, you have taken the wrong word.
Summary reflex: Predict (type) → scan (keyword) → copy (exactly) → count (limit) → test (grammar). Once these five moves are memorised, completion questions become almost mechanical.

Completion’da doğru kelimeyi bulmak yeter miydi? Hayır. Öğrenciler puanlarını en çok kelime sınırını aşarak ve dilbilgisi uyumunu kaçırarak kaybeder. Kelime sayma kuralları:

  • Tireli kelimeler tek kelime sayılır: “well-being”, “long-term” = 1 kelime.
  • Sayılar rakamla yazılırsa (“1996”, “40%”) genelde tek “number” sayılır; talimat “AND/OR A NUMBER” diyorsa sayı kelime kotanı ekstra doldurmaz.
  • Artişeller (a/an/the) ve edatlar da kelimedir; “the factory” iki kelimedir.
  • Kısaltmalar (“UN”) genelde tek kelime kabul edilir.

Dilbilgisi uyumu. Boşluk bir fiil bekliyorsa pasajdaki isim halini fiile çevirmen gerekmez — doğru cümleyi bulduğunda uygun biçim zaten oradadır. Örn. boşluk “The cells ________ light into electricity” ise cevap convert (çoğul öznee uyumlu fiil) olur; pasajda “converts” geçse bile özne “cells” çoğul olduğu için dilbilgisi seni “convert” formuna götürmelidir. Bu yüzden cevabı her zaman cümleye koyup oku.

Kutulu (word-bank) summary farklıdır. Burada pasajdan kopyalamazsın; verilen kutudan anlamca doğru kelimeyi seçersin ve kelimeler çoğunlukla paraphrase edilmiştir. Örn. pasajda “reduced sharply” geçerken kutuda “plummeted” bulunabilir. Burada strateji: pasajı anla, sonra kutudaki eş anlamlıyı eşle; dilbilgisi türü (isim/fiil/sıfat) yine boşluğa uymalı.

In completion, is finding the right word enough? No. Students lose most of their marks by going over the word limit and missing grammatical agreement. Word-counting rules:

  • Hyphenated words count as one word: “well-being”, “long-term” = 1 word.
  • Numbers written as figures (“1996”, “40%”) usually count as a single “number”; if the instruction says “AND/OR A NUMBER”, the number does not use up your word quota.
  • Articles (a/an/the) and prepositions are also words; “the factory” is two words.
  • Abbreviations (“UN”) are usually accepted as one word.

The table below shows which answer is valid/invalid under different limits (assume the passage contains “...powered entirely by solar energy...”):

Instruction (limit)Valid answerInvalid answer (reason)
ONE WORD ONLYsolarsolar energy (2 words, limit exceeded)
NO MORE THAN TWO WORDSsolar energythe solar energy (3 words)
THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBERsolar energy / 40% solar energysynonym: sunlight power (not in the passage, changed)

Grammatical agreement. If the gap expects a verb, you do not need to turn the noun form in the passage into a verb — when you find the right sentence the correct form is already there. E.g. if the gap is “The cells ________ light into electricity”, the answer is convert (a verb agreeing with the plural subject); even if the passage says “converts”, because the subject “cells” is plural the grammar should lead you to the “convert” form. That is why you should always put the answer into the sentence and read it.

The word-bank summary is different. Here you do not copy from the passage; you pick the meaning-correct word from the given box, and the words are mostly paraphrased. E.g. where the passage says “reduced sharply”, the box may contain “plummeted”. The strategy here: understand the passage, then match the synonym in the box; the grammatical type (noun/verb/adjective) must still fit the gap.

Aşağıda kısa bir akademik pasaj ve ona bağlı bir summary completion sorusu var. Talimat: “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS”. Önce pasajı oku, sonra boşluklu özeti gör, ardından her boşluğun adım adım Türkçe çözümünü incele.

Pasaj.

The Arctic tern undertakes the longest migration of any animal on Earth. Each year, it travels from its breeding grounds in the Arctic to the Antarctic and back, covering roughly 70,000 kilometres. Researchers tracked the birds using tiny geolocators weighing less than two grams, which record light levels to estimate location. The data revealed that terns do not fly in a straight line; instead, they follow a winding route shaped by prevailing winds, which reduces the energy they must expend. Remarkably, the birds pause in the North Atlantic for about a month to feed on plankton before continuing south. Because they experience two summers each year, Arctic terns see more daylight than any other creature.

Özet (boşlukları doldur — NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS).

The Arctic tern has the longest (1) ________ of any animal, flying about 70,000 km yearly. Scientists followed the birds with (2) ________ that weigh under two grams and measure light to work out position. Rather than travelling directly, terns take a (3) ________ route influenced by winds, which lowers the (4) ________ they use. They also stop in the North Atlantic to eat (5) ________ before heading south.

1) migration Boşluk “the longest ________ of any animal” — bir isim gerekiyor. Pasajın ilk cümlesi “undertakes the longest migration of any animal” diyor. Kelimeyi aynen al: migration (1 kelime, sınır içinde). “Journey” yazmak eş anlamlı olsa da pasajda geçmediği için risklidir; aynen kopyala.
2) geolocators Boşluk “with ________ that weigh under two grams” — bir isim/araç beklenir. Pasaj: “tracked the birds using tiny geolocators weighing less than two grams”. “less than two grams” = “under two grams” paraphrase’i seni doğru cümleye götürür. Cevap geolocators (1 kelime). Yazımı birebir kopyala — yanlış harf sıfır puandır.
3) winding Boşluk “take a ________ route” — “route” ismini niteleyen bir sıfat gerekiyor. Pasaj: “they follow a winding route shaped by prevailing winds”. “Rather than travelling directly” = “do not fly in a straight line” paraphrase’i doğru yeri işaret eder. Cevap winding (1 kelime).
4) energy Boşluk “lowers the ________ they use” — bir isim. Pasaj: “reduces the energy they must expend”; “lowers” = “reduces”, “they use” = “they must expend”. Cevap energy (1 kelime). Dikkat: “energy they must expend” yazmak sınırı aşardı (2’den fazla), çekirdek kelime yeter.
5) plankton Boşluk “stop in the North Atlantic to eat ________” — bir isim/besin. Pasaj: “pause in the North Atlantic for about a month to feed on plankton”; “stop” = “pause”, “eat” = “feed on”. Cevap plankton (1 kelime). Sıra da uyuyor: 5. boşluk pasajın sonlarında geçiyor.

Beş cevap da pasajda birebir geçti, hepsi tek kelime ve dilbilgisi olarak cümleye oturdu. İşte hedeflenen refleks budur.

Below is a short academic passage and a summary completion question based on it. The instruction: “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS”. First read the passage, then look at the gapped summary, and then study the step-by-step solution to each gap.

Passage.

The Arctic tern undertakes the longest migration of any animal on Earth. Each year, it travels from its breeding grounds in the Arctic to the Antarctic and back, covering roughly 70,000 kilometres. Researchers tracked the birds using tiny geolocators weighing less than two grams, which record light levels to estimate location. The data revealed that terns do not fly in a straight line; instead, they follow a winding route shaped by prevailing winds, which reduces the energy they must expend. Remarkably, the birds pause in the North Atlantic for about a month to feed on plankton before continuing south. Because they experience two summers each year, Arctic terns see more daylight than any other creature.

Summary (fill the gaps — NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS).

The Arctic tern has the longest (1) ________ of any animal, flying about 70,000 km yearly. Scientists followed the birds with (2) ________ that weigh under two grams and measure light to work out position. Rather than travelling directly, terns take a (3) ________ route influenced by winds, which lowers the (4) ________ they use. They also stop in the North Atlantic to eat (5) ________ before heading south.

1) migration The gap “the longest ________ of any animal” needs a noun. The first sentence of the passage says “undertakes the longest migration of any animal”. Take the word exactly: migration (1 word, within the limit). Writing “journey” may be a synonym, but since it is not in the passage it is risky; copy it exactly.
2) geolocators The gap “with ________ that weigh under two grams” expects a noun/device. Passage: “tracked the birds using tiny geolocators weighing less than two grams”. “less than two grams” = the paraphrase of “under two grams” leads you to the right sentence. The answer is geolocators (1 word). Copy the spelling exactly — a wrong letter is zero marks.
3) winding The gap “take a ________ route” needs an adjective modifying the noun “route”. Passage: “they follow a winding route shaped by prevailing winds”. “Rather than travelling directly” = the paraphrase of “do not fly in a straight line” points to the right place. The answer is winding (1 word).
4) energy The gap “lowers the ________ they use” is a noun. Passage: “reduces the energy they must expend”; “lowers” = “reduces”, “they use” = “they must expend”. The answer is energy (1 word). Note: writing “energy they must expend” would exceed the limit (more than 2), so the core word is enough.
5) plankton The gap “stop in the North Atlantic to eat ________” is a noun/food. Passage: “pause in the North Atlantic for about a month to feed on plankton”; “stop” = “pause”, “eat” = “feed on”. The answer is plankton (1 word). The order fits too: the 5th gap appears near the end of the passage.

All five answers appeared in the passage word for word, all were single words and fitted the sentence grammatically. This is exactly the reflex you are aiming for.

1) Kelime sınırını aşmak. En yaygın hata. “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS” dese de öğrenci “the winding route” gibi 3 kelime yazar ve doğru bilgiyi bulmuş olsa bile puanı kaybeder. Cevabı yazdıktan sonra kelimeleri parmakla say.

2) Kelimeyi değiştirmek / eş anlamlı yazmak. Pasajda “migration” geçerken “journey”, “geolocators” geçerken “trackers” yazmak yanlıştır. Kutulu summary dışında cevap her zaman pasajda geçen kelimedir; kendi kelimeni üretme.

3) Dilbilgisi uyumsuzluğu. Boşluk çoğul isim beklerken tekil (“wind” yerine “winds”), fiil beklerken isim koymak. Cevabı cümleye yerleştirip yüksek sesle okur gibi kontrol et; “akıcı değil” hissi genelde yanlış türü ele verir.

4) Yazım / imla hatası. IELTS’te yanlış yazılan kelime yanlış sayılır — “accommodation”, “environment” gibi kelimelerde harf atlamak sık görülür. Cevabı cevap kağıdına geçirirken pasajdaki yazımla harf harf karşılaştır.

5) Yanlış cümleyi eşleştirmek. Aynı anahtar kelime pasajda birden fazla geçebilir. Sadece bir kelimeye değil, boşluğun etrafındaki tüm anlam öbeğine bak; “reduces the energy” gibi kalıbın tamamını doğrula.

1) Going over the word limit. The most common mistake. Even when it says “NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS”, a student writes 3 words like “the winding route” and loses the mark even though they found the right information. After writing the answer, count the words on your fingers.

2) Changing the word / writing a synonym. Where the passage says “migration”, writing “journey”, or “trackers” for “geolocators”, is wrong. Except in the word-bank summary, the answer is always the word that appears in the passage; do not invent your own word.

3) Grammatical mismatch. Putting a singular where the gap expects a plural noun (“wind” instead of “winds”), or a noun where a verb is expected. Place the answer in the sentence and check it as if reading aloud; a feeling of “not fluent” usually gives away the wrong type.

4) Spelling mistakes. In IELTS a misspelled word is marked wrong — dropping a letter in words like “accommodation”, “environment” is common. When you transfer the answer to the answer sheet, compare it letter by letter with the spelling in the passage.

5) Matching the wrong sentence. The same keyword can appear more than once in the passage. Look not just at one word but at the whole meaning chunk around the gap; verify the entire pattern, such as “reduces the energy”.

Son söz: Completion, IELTS Reading’de “en hızlı puan” alanıdır — ama ancak sınıra ve yazıma sadık kalırsan. Doğru bilgiyi bulmak yarısı; onu doğru biçimde yazmak diğer yarısıdır.

In the exam, run through this list in your mind before and after every completion set:

  • ✓ Read the instruction? Is the word limit (ONE / TWO / THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER) clear?
  • ✓ Predicted the type of the gap? (noun / verb / adjective / number)
  • ✓ Chose keywords and found the right sentence in the passage?
  • ✓ Copied the answer exactly from the passage? (no synonym)
  • ✓ Does the word count stay within the limit? (did you count articles/prepositions?)
  • ✓ When placed in the sentence, does the grammar fit?
  • ✓ Is the spelling identical to the passage?
  • ✓ If it is a word-bank summary: did you pick the meaning-correct (paraphrase) word from the box?
  • ✓ Was no gap left blank?
Final word: Completion is the “fastest marks” area of IELTS Reading — but only if you stay faithful to the limit and the spelling. Finding the right information is half; writing it in the right form is the other half.
