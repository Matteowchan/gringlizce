Grammar: Preferences (like / love / enjoy / prefer + -ing)

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neyi Seversin? Neyi Tercih Edersin? (Talking About Preferences)

Bir işyerinde sadece görevleri konuşmayız — neyi sevdiğimizi, neyden hoşlandığımızı, neyi tercih ettiğimizi de paylaşırız. "Burada çalışmayı seviyorum", "Müşterilerle tanışmaya bayılırım", "Kahveyi çaya tercih ederim" gibi cümleler, hem kendini anlatmanın hem de small talk (kısa sohbet) yapmanın temelidir:

  • "I like working here. The team is friendly."Burada çalışmayı seviyorum. Ekip cana yakın.
  • "She loves meeting new clients."Yeni müşterilerle tanışmaya bayılır.
  • "We enjoy chatting during the coffee break."Kahve molasında sohbet etmekten keyif alırız.
  • "I prefer coffee to tea."Kahveyi çaya tercih ederim.
  • "They don't like working late."Geç saatlere kadar çalışmayı sevmezler.

Bu ünitede tercih bildiren fiilleri (like, love, enjoy, prefer, hate, don't mind) doğru kullanmayı, bunlardan sonra fiile neden -ing eklendiğini, olumsuz ve soru kurmayı ve iş yerinde small talk açıp sürdürmeyi öğreneceksin.

1. like / love / enjoy + -ing

Bir eylemi sevdiğini söylerken (yürümek, çalışmak, okumak...), tercih fiilinden sonra gelen ikinci fiil -ing alır. Özneye göre değişen, ilk fiildir; ikinci fiil hep -ing kalır:

Fiil+ -ingÖrnekTürkçesi
likelike workingI like working in a team.Takımda çalışmayı severim.
lovelove meetingShe loves meeting new people.Yeni insanlarla tanışmaya bayılır.
enjoyenjoy readingWe enjoy reading in our free time.Boş vaktimizde okumaktan keyif alırız.
hatehate waitingHe hates waiting in traffic.Trafikte beklemekten nefret eder.

-ing yazımında iki küçük kural vardır:

KuralÖrnekler
Fiil -e ile bitiyorsa e düşer, -ingmake → making · write → writing
Tek heceli, kısa ünlü + sessiz ile bitende son harf çift yazılırchat → chatting · swim → swimming
Not
  • Tercih fiili özneye uyar: I like, she likes, they like.
  • İkinci fiil ise HER ZAMAN -ing kalır: She likes working (asla "She likes to works" değil).
  • İsimle de kullanılır: "I like coffee.", "She loves her job."

2. prefer: Tercih Etmek (prefer + -ing / prefer X to Y)

prefer = birini diğerine tercih etmek. İki şekilde kullanılır — eylemler için -ing, iki şeyi karşılaştırmak için to:

YapıÖrnekTürkçesi
prefer + -ing (eylem)I prefer working in the morning.Sabahları çalışmayı tercih ederim.
prefer + isimShe prefers tea.Çayı tercih eder.
prefer X to Y (karşılaştırma)I prefer coffee to tea.Kahveyi çaya tercih ederim.
prefer + -ing to -ingHe prefers walking to driving.Araba sürmektense yürümeyi tercih eder.
En kritik kural
  • Karşılaştırmada than değil, to kullanılır: "I prefer coffee than tea" ✗ → "I prefer coffee to tea" ✓
  • she/he ile fiile -s eklenir: She prefers tea.

3. Olumsuz: don't like / don't mind / hate (Negatives)

Bir şeyi sevmediğini söylemek için don't/doesn't like kullanılır; ikinci fiil yine -ing kalır. Ayrıca iki faydalı ifade daha vardır:

İfadeAnlamÖrnek
don't like + -ingsevmemekI don't like working late.
hate + -ingnefret etmek (güçlü)She hates sitting all day.
don't mind + -ingsorun etmemek, aldırmamakI don't mind working from home.
Dikkat
  • she/he için doesn't: "She doesn't like waiting."
  • olumsuzda da ikinci fiil -ing kalır: "I don't like working late."
  • don't mind = ne severim ne sevmem, ama sorun değil (nötr).

4. Soru Sormak (Do you like...? / Do you prefer...?)

Tercih sormak, small talk'un kalbidir. Sorular Do / Does ile açılır ve tercih fiili yalın kalır:

SoruKısa cevapTürkçesi
Do you like working here?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.Burada çalışmayı sever misin?
Does she enjoy meetings?Yes, she does.Toplantılardan hoşlanır mı?
Do you prefer tea or coffee?I prefer coffee.Çay mı kahve mi tercih edersin?
What do you like doing at the weekend?I like reading.Hafta sonu ne yapmayı seversin?
Kalıp
  • "What do you like doing?" — burada "doing" -ing kalır (like + -ing).
  • "or" ile seçenek sunulur: "Do you prefer tea or coffee?"
  • Cevapta tercih fiilini kısaltabilirsin: "— Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do."

5. Small Talk: Kısa Sohbet (Everyday Phrases)

Small talk, iş yerinde ilişkileri sıcak tutan havadan sudan sohbettir: hava, hafta sonu, kahve, filmler... İşte en sık kullanılan açılış ve karşılık kalıpları:

Açılış (soru)Karşılık (cevap)
How are you today?Fine, thanks. And you?
How was your weekend?It was nice, thanks. I love cycling.
What do you like doing in your free time?I enjoy reading and cooking.
Do you prefer tea or coffee?I prefer tea, thanks.
Nice weather today, isn't it?Yes, lovely! I don't like rainy days.
İpucu
  • Small talk'ta kısa ve nazik ol; ardından karşındakine de soru sor: "...And you?"
  • Güvenli konular: hava, hafta sonu, kahve/çay, filmler, hobiler.
  • Bir tercih paylaş, sonra dinle: "I prefer coffee. What about you?"

6. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Tercih ve small talk konusunda Türkçe konuşanların en sık yaptığı hatalar şunlardır. Kendi cümlelerini kontrol ederken bu listeyi kullan:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I like to working here.I like working here.like'tan sonra tek biçim: -ing.
She like meeting clients.She likes meeting clients.she/he öznesinde fiile -s eklenir.
I prefer coffee than tea.I prefer coffee to tea.prefer'de karşılaştırma 'to' iledir.
He doesn't likes waiting.He doesn't like waiting.doesn't'tan sonra fiil yalın kalır.
Do you like work here?Do you like working here?like + -ing: working.
I enjoy to read.I enjoy reading.enjoy her zaman -ing alır, 'to' almaz.

7. Uygulama: Kahve Molası (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Aşağıdaki small talk, tercih fiillerini, -ing yapısını, olumsuzu ve soruları birlikte kullanıyor — koyu yerlere dikkat ederek iki kez oku; ikinci okumada Türkçesini kapatmayı dene:

  • — Hi Deniz! How was your weekend?Selam Deniz! Hafta sonun nasıldı?
  • — It was great, thanks. I love cycling, so I usually go to the park. And you?Harikaydı, sağ ol. Bisiklete binmeye bayılırım, o yüzden genelde parka giderim. Ya sen?
  • — I prefer staying at home. I really enjoy reading and cooking.Ben evde kalmayı tercih ederim. Okumaktan ve yemek yapmaktan gerçekten keyif alırım.
  • — Nice! Do you like coffee?Güzel! Kahve sever misin?
  • — Not really. I prefer tea to coffee. And I don't like drinking coffee in the afternoon.Pek değil. Çayı kahveye tercih ederim. Ayrıca öğleden sonra kahve içmeyi sevmem.
  • — Same here. Do you prefer working from home or at the office?Ben de öyle. Evden mi ofisten mi çalışmayı tercih edersin?
  • — I don't mind both, but I love working with the team here.İkisine de aldırmam ama buradaki ekiple çalışmaya bayılırım.
  • — Me too. What do you like doing after work?Ben de. İşten sonra ne yapmayı seversin?
  • — I enjoy watching films. I prefer comedies. How about you?Film izlemekten keyif alırım. Komedileri tercih ederim. Ya sen?
  • — I like action films! Well, back to work — nice chat!Ben aksiyon filmlerini severim! Neyse, işe dönelim — güzel sohbetti!
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane "Do" ile kurulmuş soru var? (Cevap: 4 — "What do you like doing" dahil)
  • "prefer tea to coffee" neden 'than' değil 'to' ile kuruldu? (Bölüm 2'ye bak.)
  • "don't mind" burada ne anlama geliyor? (Bölüm 3'e bak.)

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the preference words, then use them in your own sentences. (Tercih kelimelerini öğren, sonra kendi cümlelerinde kullan.)

like / likes (v)
to find something nice or good (sevmek, hoşlanmak)
Syn: enjoy (keyif almak) · Ant: dislike (sevmemek)
"I like working with my team."
love / loves (v)
to like something very much (bayılmak)
Syn: adore (çok sevmek) · Ant: hate (nefret etmek)
"She loves meeting new clients."
enjoy / enjoys (v)
to get pleasure from something (keyif almak)
Syn: like (sevmek) · Ant: dislike (sevmemek)
"We enjoy chatting at lunch."
prefer / prefers (v)
to like one thing more than another (tercih etmek)
Syn: choose (seçmek) · Ant: reject (reddetmek)
"I prefer tea to coffee."
hate / hates (v)
to strongly dislike something (nefret etmek)
Syn: dislike (sevmemek) · Ant: love (bayılmak)
"He hates waiting in traffic."
don't mind (phr)
to not have a problem with something (aldırmamak, sorun etmemek)
Syn: accept (kabul etmek) · Ant: hate (nefret etmek)
"I don't mind working late."
relax / relaxes (v)
to rest and feel calm (rahatlamak, dinlenmek)
Syn: rest (dinlenmek) · Ant: worry (endişelenmek)
"I relax at home at the weekend."
chat / chats (v)
to talk in a friendly, easy way (sohbet etmek)
Syn: talk (konuşmak) · Ant: argue (tartışmak)
"They chat during the coffee break."
small talk (n)
light, friendly conversation (havadan sudan kısa sohbet)
Syn: chit-chat (laf sohbeti) · Ant: silence (sessizlik)
"Small talk helps colleagues relax."
weekend (n)
Saturday and Sunday (hafta sonu)
Syn: days off (izin günleri) · Ant: weekday (hafta içi)
"How was your weekend?"
free time (n)
time when you are not working (boş zaman)
Syn: spare time (boş vakit) · Ant: work time (mesai)
"In my free time, I read books."
hobby / hobbies (n)
an activity you do for fun (hobi)
Syn: pastime (uğraş) · Ant: chore (angarya)
"Cooking is my favourite hobby."
Kahve molasında small talk: tercihlerini paylaşıyorlar — like / love / enjoy / prefer.

Production Task (Üretim Görevi)

Write 8 sentences about your preferences. Use at least 8 of the words above. (Yukarıdaki kelimelerden en az 8'ini kullanarak tercihlerini anlatan 8 cümle yaz.)

Gramer şartı — bu ünitenin konusunu uygula
  • En az 4 cümlede tercih fiili + -ing kullan (I like working, She loves meeting...).
  • En az 1 cümlede prefer X to Y kullan (I prefer coffee to tea).
  • En az 1 olumsuz cümle yaz (I don't like / I don't mind...).
  • En az 2 soru cümlesi yaz (Do you like...? / Do you prefer...?).

Sentence starters (Cümle başlatıcıları — istersen bunlarla başla):

"I like ...ing ..." "I prefer ... to ..." "In my free time, I ..." "Do you like ...?"

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Selin, Deniz ve Elif'i hatırlıyor musun? Bu kez onları bir kahve molasında görüyoruz. İş dışında da sohbet ediyorlar — tercihlerini, hafta sonlarını, hobilerini paylaşıyorlar. Okumaya başlamadan önce şu üç soruyu düşün (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • Do you prefer tea or coffee? (Çayı mı kahveyi mi tercih edersin?)
  • What do you like doing in your free time? (Boş zamanında ne yapmayı seversin?)
  • Look at the picture below. What does it show? (Aşağıdaki görsele bak. Ne anlatıyor olabilir?)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin — konuyu yakala.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar her zaman metnin içindedir.
  • Bu metin tercihler hakkında: like/love/enjoy/prefer + -ing yapılarına dikkat et.

Verbs to spot (Metinde geçen, bu ünitede öğrendiğin tercih yapıları — gözün bunları arasın): enjoy talking · love cycling · prefer staying · enjoy reading · prefer tea to coffee · loves working · prefers working · like working · hates · don't mind

Tercihler farklıdır: kimi kahveyi, kimi çayı; kimi bisikleti, kimi kitabı sever.
Coffee Break at Baytek

Baytek is a small office in the city. Nine people work here. They are a friendly team. Every day starts at nine o'clock. Everybody says 'Good morning!' Selin turns on her computer. Deniz checks his emails. Elif opens the window for fresh air. They work hard for two hours. Then it is time for a break. 'Break time!' Deniz says. He is always happy at eleven o'clock. The three friends walk to the kitchen together. They talk and they laugh on the way.

It is eleven o'clock, and it is coffee break time at Baytek. Selin, Deniz, and Elif are in the kitchen. They enjoy talking together every morning.

The kitchen is small, but it is nice. There is a big table in the middle. There are four chairs and a coffee machine. There is a small window too. Selin likes the window, because she can see the trees. The break is only fifteen minutes, but it is a happy time. People make coffee or tea. They stand near the table, and they talk. 'I love coffee breaks,' Selin says. 'I can relax and talk to my friends.'

'How was your weekend, Deniz?' Selin asks. 'It was great, thanks. I love cycling, so I usually go to the park on Saturdays. What about you?' 'I prefer staying at home at the weekend,' Selin says. 'I really enjoy reading and cooking.'

Deniz likes sport very much. He rides his bike every Saturday. 'Cycling is my favourite hobby,' he says. 'I ride in the park, and I feel free.' On Sundays, he watches football with his brother. Selin is different. She does not like sport. She prefers quiet days at home. 'On Saturday morning, I read a good book,' she says. 'In the afternoon, I cook. I love making soup and cakes.' Elif smiles. 'I like your cakes, Selin! You are a good cook.'

Elif makes some tea. 'Do you like coffee, Elif?' Deniz asks. 'Not really. I prefer tea to coffee,' she says. 'And I don't like drinking coffee in the afternoon. It's too strong for me.'

Elif drinks tea all day. She has a small cup on her desk. 'Tea is warm and light,' she says. 'I like it in the morning and in the afternoon.' Deniz loves coffee. He drinks two cups every day. 'Coffee gives me energy,' he says. 'I need it for my work.' Selin likes both drinks. 'I drink coffee in the morning,' she says, 'but I prefer tea after lunch.' They all like biscuits with their drinks. Elif brings biscuits from home, and everybody says thank you.

At Baytek, people have different preferences. Deniz loves working with the design team, because he enjoys meeting new clients. Selin prefers working in a quiet room. 'I like working from home on Fridays,' she says. 'I can focus better there.'

People work in different ways at Baytek. Deniz likes people. He enjoys meetings, and he loves new projects. 'I feel happy with the team,' he says. Selin is quiet. She likes calm places. 'Too much noise is hard for me,' she says. 'I work better alone.' Elif is friendly and calm. She helps everybody. 'I don't mind hard work,' she says, 'but I need my tea!' They are a good team, because they are all different. Different people bring different ideas.

Elif has many plants on her desk. She loves green plants. 'Plants make me calm,' she says. 'I water them every morning.' Elif can speak three languages, so she often talks to clients from other countries. 'I enjoy meeting new people,' she says. 'It is fun to learn about them.' Deniz has photos of his bike on his desk. Selin has a small clock and a cup for her pens. Their desks are different, like their preferences.

They also talk about small things — the weather, films, and food. Elif hates rainy days, but she loves watching films at home. Deniz prefers action films, and Selin likes comedies.

Today it is sunny and warm. Elif is happy. 'I love sunny days,' she says. 'I don't like rain. On rainy days, I stay at home and watch films.' Deniz likes action films with fast cars and heroes. 'They are exciting!' he says. Selin prefers funny films. 'I like comedies,' she says. 'I laugh, and I feel good.' They also talk about food. Deniz loves pizza. Selin prefers pasta. Elif likes fish and rice. Everybody is hungry now, and they laugh about lunch.

At one o'clock, the team has lunch together. They go to a small cafe near the office. Deniz orders pizza. Selin orders pasta. Elif orders fish. They talk about many things at lunch. They talk about music, holidays, and their families. Selin loves the summer. 'I want to go to the sea,' she says. 'I love swimming.' Deniz prefers the mountains. 'I like walking and fresh air,' he says. Elif enjoys the city. 'I love cafes and shops,' she says. Everybody is different, and that is good.

'What are your plans for next weekend?' Elif asks. Deniz smiles. 'I want to go cycling with my friends,' he says. 'We ride for two hours, and then we have a big breakfast.' Selin has a different plan. 'I want to stay at home,' she says. 'I have a new book, and I want to read it.' Elif laughs. 'I want to see my family,' she says. 'My mother makes wonderful food. I love her cooking.'

Small talk is easy and kind. You ask a question, and then you listen. 'How are you?' 'How was your day?' 'Do you like this weather?' These questions are short, but they are important. They make people feel welcome. At Baytek, the team is like a small family. They help each other, and they smile a lot. Good talk makes good work. When people feel happy, they work well together.

Their manager is Mr Kaya. He is a kind man. He often joins the coffee break. 'Good morning, team!' he says. 'How are you today?' He likes small talk too. He asks about the weekend, and he listens well. A good manager cares about people, not only about work. The team feels lucky. They like their office, and they like each other very much.

The coffee break is almost over now. Selin finishes her tea. Deniz eats the last biscuit. Elif washes the cups. 'This is a nice break,' Selin says. 'I feel ready for work again.' 'Me too,' says Deniz. The break is short, but it is good for everybody.

Small talk is important at work. It helps colleagues relax and feel comfortable. 'I don't mind talking about work,' Deniz says with a smile, 'but I prefer talking about football!' Everybody laughs, and then they go back to their desks.

Tercihlerini Anlatan Bir Yazı Nasıl Kurulur? (Writing About Preferences)

Tercihlerini anlatmak, cümleleri bağlaçlarla bağlayarak akıcı hale getirmektir. A1'de öğrendiğin bağlaçlar burada da aynen çalışır — bu kez tercih cümlelerini bağlıyorlar:

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek (tercih)
andveEkleme yaparI like reading and I love cooking.
butamaZıtlık gösterirI like coffee, but I prefer tea.
orveyaSeçenek sunarDo you prefer tea or coffee?
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirI love cycling, so I go to the park.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI enjoy this job because the team is friendly.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı anlatı ✓
I like my job. I like working here. I don't like meetings. I prefer coffee. I am happy. I like working here because the team is friendly. I don't like long meetings, but I don't mind them. I prefer coffee to tea, so I love the coffee break!
Tercih anlatının iki motoru
  • Tercih fiilleri + -ing anlatıyı taşır: I like working... I love meeting... I prefer staying...
  • Small talk konuları anlatıyı zenginleştirir: hafta sonu, hava, filmler, hobiler, kahve/çay.

Task (Görev)

Write an email to a friend about your preferences at work and in your free time. (Bir arkadaşına işteki ve boş zamandaki tercihlerini anlatan bir e-posta yaz.)

WHAT TO INCLUDE (NELER OLMALI)

  • at least 5 sentences with like/love/enjoy/prefer + -ing (en az 5 cümlede tercih fiili + -ing: I like working, I love meeting...)
  • at least 1 "prefer X to Y" sentence (en az 1 'prefer X to Y' cümlesi: I prefer coffee to tea)
  • at least 1 negative: don't like / don't mind + -ing (en az 1 olumsuz cümle)
  • at least 3 small-talk topics: weekend, weather, films, food, hobbies (en az 3 small-talk konusu)
  • at least 5 words from the Speaking section (Speaking bölümünden en az 5 kelime: like, love, enjoy, prefer, hate, relax, chat, hobby...)
  • a friendly opening and closing (Hi ... / Best wishes,)

EXAMPLE OPENERS (ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR)

"Hi ..., How are you? I love my job because ..." "At work, I like ...ing ..." "In my free time, I prefer ..."

Sample Answer (~140 words) Hi Ali,

How are you? I love my new job at Baytek, so I want to tell you about it!

I really like working with my team, and I enjoy meeting new clients every week. I prefer working in the morning, because I can focus better then. I don't like working late, but I don't mind busy days.

At the coffee break, we always chat about small things. I prefer tea to coffee, and I love talking about films. My colleagues hate rainy days, but I don't mind them!

In my free time, I enjoy reading and cooking. Cooking is my favourite hobby. At the weekend, I prefer staying at home and relaxing.

What about you? What do you like doing after work? Write soon!

Best wishes,
