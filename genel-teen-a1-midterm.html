Midterm Sınavı: Ünite 1-10 (Ara Sınav)

Read the rules, then start the exam. (Kuralları oku, sonra sınavı başlat.)

Sınav Hakkında

Bu ara sınav, Ünite 1-10'da öğrendiğin her şeyi ölçer: be (am/is/are), özne zamirleri ve a/an; okul hayatı ve Present Simple; aile, iyelik sıfatları ve çoğullar; boş zaman ve spor/hobiler; there is/are, yer edatları ve some/any; can/can't; Present Continuous ve Present Simple ile farkı; sayılabilir/sayılamayan isimler (some/any, much/many). Speaking hariç dört beceri test edilir. Toplam süre 100 dakikadır ve tek bir sayaçla ilerler — süreyi bölümler arasında kendin yönetirsin.

Listening — Read the Conversation (10 soru)

Suggested time: 15 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 15 dakika.) Two classmates talk about school and free time. (İki sınıf arkadaşı okul ve boş zaman hakkında konuşuyor.)

There is no audio in this exam. Read the printed conversation below carefully, then answer the questions. (Bu sınavda ses yoktur. Aşağıdaki basılı diyaloğu dikkatle oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Ela & Can · Before English Class

Ela: Hi Can! How are you today?

Can: Hi Ela! I'm fine, thanks, but I'm a bit tired. We have a Maths test tomorrow.

Ela: Oh, I know. I study Maths every evening this week. It's difficult for me.

Can: Me too. But after the test I'm free. On Saturday I always play basketball with my brother.

Ela: Nice! I don't play basketball, but I love swimming. There is a new pool near my house.

Can: Cool! Can you swim well?

Ela: Yes, I can. I swim every weekend. My little sister can't swim yet, so I help her.

Can: That's kind. What about your family? Are they at home on Sundays?

Ela: Usually, yes. On Sundays we visit my grandmother. She has a big garden with a lot of flowers.

Can: That sounds lovely. Listen — the bell is ringing! Our English lesson is starting now.

Ela: Oh no, we're late! Let's go. See you at lunch, Can.

Reading (2 metin · 20 soru)

Suggested time: 25 minutes. (Önerilen süre: 25 dakika.) Read both texts, then answer the questions. (İki metni oku, soruları cevapla.)

Text 1 · A Day at Green Valley School

Green Valley School is a big school in Bursa, and the students are always busy. The school opens at eight o'clock, and the first lesson starts at half past eight.

Mert is thirteen years old. He usually gets up at seven, has a quick breakfast and walks to school with his sister. He never takes the bus because the school is near his house. On Mondays he always has a Science lesson at nine o'clock, so he checks his bag for his books before it.

Elif is Mert's best friend. She is very good at sport. She can swim, and she can play basketball too. At lunchtime she often plays basketball in the playground with Cem and Deniz. They are in her class, and they sometimes talk about homework, but they usually talk about games.

Mr. Yıldız is the English teacher. He has lessons every day, so he is always busy. He doesn't have free time on Thursdays because he helps the drama club that day. He sometimes stays at school until five o'clock.

On Fridays, the class has art in the afternoon. The students paint and draw, and then they go home early because the weekend starts. Elif often meets her friends on Friday evenings, but Mert usually stays at home and plays video games.

Text 2 · The New School Library

Green Valley School has a new library, and the students love it. The room is big and full of light because there are eight large windows.

Next to the door, there is a small desk. Behind the desk, there is a friendly librarian, Miss Ayça. She helps everybody find books. Opposite the desk, there are two soft armchairs for reading, and between the armchairs there is a low table with some magazines on it.

There are thirty desks in the main room. Mert's desk is next to a window, and Elif's desk is behind his. Under every desk, there is a small box for pens and pencils. There aren't any old computers in the library — every computer is new and fast.

The reading corner is opposite the computers. There is a big blue carpet on the floor, and there are a lot of cushions on it. On the wall, there is a big clock and some posters of famous writers.

The library is everybody's favourite place. There is some water in a cooler near the door, but there isn't any food — Miss Ayça says food isn't good for the books! Next to the window, there are some plants. Elif waters them every morning.

There is only one problem: there aren't any quiet rooms for group work, so the library is often very busy.

Writing (2 görev)

Suggested time: 35 minutes — Task 1: 10 min, Task 2: 25 min. (Önerilen süre: 35 dakika — Görev 1: 10 dk, Görev 2: 25 dk.) Öğretmenin değerlendirecek; örnek cevap yoktur.

Task 1 · Email: My New Classroom (60-80 words)

Your school has a new classroom (or library). Write a short email to a friend in another city. Describe the room. (Okulunun yeni bir sınıfı (ya da kütüphanesi) var. Başka şehirdeki bir arkadaşına kısa bir e-posta yaz; odayı anlat.)

  • Use there is / there are. (there is/are kullan)
  • Use at least 2 place prepositions: in, on, under, next to, between, behind, in front of, opposite. (en az 2 yer edatı)
  • Use some / any. (some/any kullan)
  • 60-80 words. (60-80 kelime)
E-POSTA YAPISI
  • Açılış: "Hi Deniz," / "Dear Deniz," + kısa bir giriş cümlesi.
  • Gövde: odayı anlat — there is/are + edatlar + some/any.
  • Kapanış: "See you soon," / "Bye for now," + adın.
0 kelime

Task 2 · Paragraph: My School Week (120-150 words)

Write a paragraph about your typical school week: who you are, your school, your daily routines, and what you do in your free time. (Tipik okul haftanı anlatan bir paragraf yaz: kim olduğun, okulun, günlük rutinlerin ve boş zamanında neler yaptığın.)

  • Use be (am/is/are) AND have/has. (be ve have birlikte)
  • Use Present Simple for routines — dikkat: he/she için -s/-es. (rutinler için Present Simple)
  • Use can for one ability. (bir yeteneğin için can kullan)
  • Use at least 3 frequency adverbs: always, usually, often, sometimes, never. (en az 3 sıklık zarfı)
  • Use at least 3 connectors: and, but, or, so, because. (en az 3 bağlaç)
  • 120-150 words. (120-150 kelime)
