Ünite 5 · Gelecek: will · Bölüm 1/6

Grammar: The Future with will

Read the rules, study the examples, then do the exercises. (Kuralları oku, örnekleri incele, sonra alıştırmaları yap.)

Neden will?

Bir önceki ünitede geleceği be going to ile anlattın — planlar ve kanıtlı tahminler. Now we add the other big future word: will. When you guess about the future, promise something, or decide to do something at the moment of speaking, you need will. will, gelecekle ilgili tahminlerimizi, o an verdiğimiz ani kararları ve söz verirken kullandığımız kalıbı taşır.

  • I think it will rain tomorrow.Bence yarın yağmur yağacak. (tahmin)
  • The phone is ringing — I 'll get it!Telefon çalıyor — ben bakarım! (anlık karar)
  • Don't worry, I won't be late.Merak etme, geç kalmayacağım. (söz)

Bu bölümde önce will / won't yapısını kuracağız. Then we will study its two main uses (predictions and spontaneous decisions), compare will with be going to, and add useful words like I think and maybe. Adım adım gidelim.

1. Yapı: will / won't (Form)

İyi haber: will bütün öznelerde aynıdır. There is no "he wills" or "she willes" — özne ne olursa olsun will değişmez ve arkasından her zaman yalın fiil (base form) gelir.

TürYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
Olumluözne + will + yalın fiilShe will call you.Seni arayacak.
Kısa hâliözne + 'll + yalın fiilShe'll call you.Seni arayacak.
Olumsuzözne + won't + yalın fiilShe won't call you.Seni aramayacak.
SoruWill + özne + yalın fiilWill she call you?Seni arayacak mı?
Altın kural 1
  • will'den sonra fiil her zaman yalın hâldedir, -s ya da -ing almaz: "He will come." ✓ — "He will comes / will coming." ✗
  • won't = will not. Konuşmada ve yazıda won't çok yaygındır: "I won't forget."

2. Kısa Cevaplar (Short Answers)

Bir will sorusuna kısa cevap vermek çok kolaydır, because you only need will or won't — fiili tekrar etmezsin. Özne ne olursa olsun kalıp aynıdır.

SoruOlumluOlumsuz
Will it rain tomorrow?Yes, it will.No, it won't.
Will you help me?Yes, I will.No, I won't.
Will they win the match?Yes, they will.No, they won't.
Not
  • Olumlu kısa cevapta 'll kullanma: "Yes, I will." ✓ — "Yes, I'll." ✗ (cümle sonunda kısaltma olmaz).

3. Birinci Kullanım: Tahminler (Predictions)

will'in en yaygın işi gelecekle ilgili tahmin yapmaktır. A prediction is a guess about the future — bir düşünce, bir his, bir beklenti. Elimizde kesin bir kanıt olmasa da, "bence böyle olacak" deriz. Bu yüzden will çoğu zaman I think, I believe, I'm sure, maybe, probably gibi kelimelerle birlikte gelir.

  • I think our team will win on Saturday.Bence takımımız cumartesi kazanacak.
  • In the future, cars will drive themselves.Gelecekte arabalar kendi kendine gidecek.
  • Maybe it won't be sunny at the weekend.Belki hafta sonu güneşli olmaz.
  • Don't worry — everything will be fine.Merak etme — her şey yoluna girecek.
I think + will
  • Türkçede "yağmayacağını düşünüyorum" deriz, ama İngilizcede olumsuzluk genellikle will'e geçer: "I don't think it will rain." (I think it won't rain'den daha doğal.)
  • probably will'den önce, won't'tan sonra gelir: "It will probably rain." / "It probably won't rain."

4. İkinci Kullanım: Anlık Kararlar (Spontaneous Decisions)

will'in ikinci işi, tam o anda verdiğin kararları anlatmaktır. You did not plan it before; you decide now, while you are speaking. İşte tam bu an için will (genellikle 'll) kullanırız.

DurumAnlık karar (will)Türkçesi
Telefon çalıyorI'll answer it.Ben bakarım.
SusadınI'll have a glass of water.Bir bardak su alayım.
Arkadaşın yorgunDon't worry, I'll help you.Merak etme, sana yardım ederim.
Kapı çalındıThat's the door — I'll get it.Kapı çaldı — ben açarım.
Altın kural 2
  • Karar konuşma anında alınıyorsa → will. Karar önceden alındıysa → be going to (Ünite 4).
  • Söz verme, teklif etme ve ricaya "evet" deme de anlık karardır: "I 'll pay." (teklif), "I 'll do it now." (söz)

5. will mi, be going to mu? (will vs be going to)

Bu, A2'nin en önemli farklarından biridir. Both talk about the future, but the speaker's mind is different. Kısaca: plan ve kanıt varsa be going to; tahmin ve anlık karar varsa will.

Anlambe going towill
Önceden yapılmış planI'm going to visit Rome next month.—
Anlık karar—It's cold. I'll close the window.
Kanıtlı tahmin (görüyorum)Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.—
Genel tahmin / fikir—I think it will rain tomorrow.
Altın kural 3
  • Karar / plan zamanı soruyu belirler: önceden karar → going to; şimdi karar → will.
  • Gözünle bir kanıt görüyorsan (kara bulutlar, hızlanan araba) → going to. Sadece kafandaki bir his/tahmin ise → will.

6. Zaman İfadeleri (Future Time Expressions)

will genellikle bir gelecek zaman ifadesiyle birlikte gelir. These words point to the future. En sık kullanılanlar şunlardır:

İfadeAnlamıÖrnek
tomorrowyarınI will call you tomorrow.
next week / month / yeargelecek hafta / ay / yılShe will start next week.
tonightbu geceWe will finish it tonight.
in the futuregelecekteRobots will help us in the future.
in 2050 / in ten years2050'de / on yıl içindeCities will change in 2050.
Not
  • next'ten önce "the" ya da "in" kullanma: "next week" ✓ — "the next week / in next week" ✗.

7. Yaygın Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

Türkçe konuşanların will'de en sık yaptığı hatalar aşağıda. Kendi cümlelerini yazarken bu listeyle kontrol et:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
She will comes tomorrow.She will come tomorrow.will'den sonra yalın fiil (-s yok).
He wills help you.He will help you.will özneye göre değişmez.
I will to call you.I will call you.will'den sonra "to" gelmez.
Will you to come?Will you come?Soruda da "to" yok.
I not will go.I will not (won't) go.Olumsuz: will + not = won't.
The phone is ringing. I'm going to answer it.The phone is ringing. I'll answer it.Anlık karar → will, going to değil.
I think it won't to rain.I don't think it will rain.Olumsuzluk think'e geçer; "to" yok.

8. Uygulama: Yağmurlu Bir Öğleden Sonra (Model Dialogue)

Şimdi her şeyi bir arada görelim. Two friends talk about the weather and their plans after school. Koyu yazılan will / won't yapılarına dikkat ederek iki kez oku:

  • — Look at the sky. I think it will rain this afternoon.Gökyüzüne bak. Bence bu öğleden sonra yağmur yağacak.
  • — Maybe. I won't take my umbrella, though — it's too heavy.Belki. Yine de şemsiyemi almayacağım — çok ağır.
  • — Don't worry, I 'll lend you mine.Merak etme, benimkini sana veririm.
  • — Thanks! Will you be at basketball practice at three?Sağ ol! Saat üçteki basketbol antrenmanında olacak mısın?
  • — Yes, I will. I won't be late this time, I promise.Evet, olacağım. Bu sefer geç kalmayacağım, söz.
  • — Great. I think the coach will like our new plan.Harika. Bence antrenör yeni planımızı beğenecek.
  • — I hope so. If not, we 'll change it together.Umarım. Beğenmezse birlikte değiştiririz.
Kendini test et
  • Diyalogda kaç tane won't var? (Cevap: 2)
  • "I'll lend you mine." cümlesi tahmin mi, anlık karar mı? (Cevap: anlık karar / teklif)
  • "I think it will rain" cümlesi neden will, neden be going to değil? (Bölüm 5'e bak.)

Exercises (Alıştırmalar)

Choose the correct answer. (Doğru cevabı seç.) — 10 soru, her soru anında değerlendirilir.

Speaking: Vocabulary & Practice

Learn the words, then use them in your own predictions with will. (Kelimeleri öğren, sonra kendi will'li tahmin cümlelerinde kullan.)

tomorrow (adv)
the day after today (yarın)
Syn: the next day (ertesi gün) · Ant: yesterday (dün)
"I think it will rain tomorrow."
future (n)
the time that will come (gelecek)
Syn: years ahead (ileriki yıllar) · Ant: past (geçmiş)
"In the future, cars will fly."
predict (v)
say what will happen later (tahmin etmek)
Syn: guess (tahmin et) · Ant: know for sure (kesin bilmek)
"Nobody can predict the weather."
maybe (adv)
perhaps; not sure (belki)
Syn: perhaps (belki) · Ant: definitely (kesinlikle)
"Maybe we will win the game."
probably (adv)
very likely, almost sure (muhtemelen)
Syn: most likely (büyük ihtimalle) · Ant: probably not (muhtemelen değil)
"She will probably be late."
promise (v)
say you will surely do something (söz vermek)
Syn: give your word (söz ver) · Ant: refuse (reddet)
"I promise I won't forget."
change (v)
become different (değişmek)
Syn: transform (dönüşmek) · Ant: stay the same (aynı kalmak)
"Life will change in ten years."
improve (v)
get better (gelişmek, iyileşmek)
Syn: get better (iyileşmek) · Ant: get worse (kötüleşmek)
"Your English will improve soon."
hope (v)
want something good to happen (ummak)
Syn: wish for (dilemek) · Ant: fear (korkmak)
"I hope you will pass the exam."
sure (adj)
certain about something (emin)
Syn: certain (emin) · Ant: unsure (emin değil)
"I'm sure they will help us."
soon (adv)
in a short time from now (yakında)
Syn: before long (yakında) · Ant: later on (ileride)
"The bus will arrive soon."
worry (v)
feel afraid something bad will happen (endişelenmek)
Syn: be anxious (kaygılanmak) · Ant: relax (rahatlamak)
"Don't worry, it will be fine."
weather (n)
sun, rain, wind and so on (hava durumu)
Syn: conditions (koşullar) · Ant: —
"The weather will be sunny tomorrow."
plan (n)
an idea about what you will do (plan)
Syn: arrangement (düzenleme) · Ant: surprise (sürpriz)
"What are your plans for the future?"

Reading: Our New Channel

Read the text, then answer the questions. (Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

İyi okuyucular metne hazırlıklı girer. Before you read, think about these three questions (cevapları yazmana gerek yok):

  • What do you think YOUR life will be like in ten years? (Sence senin hayatın on yıl sonra nasıl olacak?)
  • The story is about the future. Which word will you see a lot? (Metin gelecekten bahsediyor. Hangi kelimeyi çok göreceksin?)
  • Look at the title. Are the people hopeful or worried? Guess! (Başlığa bak. İnsanlar umutlu mu, endişeli mi? Tahmin et!)
Nasıl okumalı?
  • 1. tur: Metni durmadan, hızlıca oku. Konuyu yakala; her kelimeyi anlamak zorunda değilsin.
  • 2. tur: Soruları oku, sonra cevapları metinde ara. will / won't yapılarına ve tahminlere dikkat et.

Words to spot (Metinde geçen, Speaking bölümünden tanıdığın kelimeler): future · maybe · change · promise · sure · soon · plan

Yeni kanal: ilk video, yeni kulüp, büyük planlar.
Our New Channel

Next month, Deniz and his friends will post their first video on their new channel, and everyone in the group is excited. Last week, Deniz called a meeting after school. 'This channel will change our weekends,' he said. 'I'm sure it will be a success.'

The four friends are in the same class at school, and they have known each other since primary school. They are fifteen years old, and they love video games, music, and football. Every day after lessons, they meet in the library or in the park near their homes. Sometimes they play games on their phones, and sometimes they just talk about new films and songs. Last month, they had a new idea. 'Why don't we make our own channel?' Elif asked. 'We can film our games and show them to other students.' Everyone liked the plan, so they started to work on it that same week.

Elif is the project leader. She thinks the launch will go well, but she is also a little nervous. 'The first video is ready,' she said, 'so we will post it soon. I think many young people will like it, because it is simple and free.'

The team works hard, but the work is also fun for them. On Monday and Wednesday, they film short clips in Kaan's room, because he has the fastest computer in the group. On Friday afternoon, they sit together and edit the videos. Deniz writes simple notes for every clip, and Elif adds music and bright colours. 'We are only students,' she said, 'so we don't have an expensive camera. But we have good ideas, and ideas are more important than money.' When a video is ready, they watch it three or four times before they post it, because they want it to be clear.

A normal weekend is very busy for the group. On Saturday morning, they wake up early and meet at nine o'clock. First, they plan the new video and choose a game. Then they film for two or three hours, and they laugh a lot when someone makes a funny mistake. After lunch, they take a short break and eat sandwiches in the garden. In the afternoon, they edit the video and check the sound. 'When we finish, we feel tired but happy,' Deniz said. 'A good weekend is a weekend with friends and a new video.'

Kaan, the group's best gamer, is more careful. 'Maybe some viewers won't understand the game at first,' he said. 'So we will make a short video. When people watch it, they will learn how to play in five minutes.'

Kaan loves football almost as much as he loves games. After school, he often plays on the school team, and then he comes home late and tired. 'When I feel tired, games help me relax,' he said. 'And when I play online with my friends, the evening feels shorter and better.' He wants the channel to help other tired students too. 'If a boy or a girl has a hard day at school,' he said, 'they can watch our funny videos and smile a little. For me, that is a very good feeling, and it is the real reason for our channel.'

The team has big plans. Next year, they will open a gaming club at school, and they will invite ten more friends. 'If the first video gets many likes, we will make a bigger one,' Deniz promised. 'We won't stop here.'

Their families like the project too, and they help in small ways. Deniz's mother makes tea and snacks when the team works at his house, and Elif's father helps them when there is a problem with the computer. 'Homework comes first,' Deniz's mother always says, 'but a good hobby is important for young people.' The friends agree with her. They finish their homework before they film, so their teachers stay happy, and their parents stay happy too. School and the channel can live together, they think, if they plan their time well and don't forget their books.

The team also dreams about the future. Deniz wants a thousand people to follow the channel by next summer. Elif wants to make videos about music and school life, not only about games. Kaan wants to film the school football matches and show the best goals of the season. 'We are all different,' Deniz said, 'but our channel can have space for all of us. When we work together, we are much stronger.' They know the first steps will be slow, but they are ready to try, because every big channel was small at the start.

Of course, the plan is not always easy. Sometimes a video is too long, and sometimes the sound is bad, so they must record it again. One time, they lost a whole file, and everyone was sad for a day. But the friends never stop for long. 'If we make a mistake, we will learn from it,' Elif said, 'and next time the video will be better.' They believe that patience is the key. When people see their new videos, the team hopes, they will understand that four young students can build something good together.

Elif smiled. 'The future will be busy,' she said, 'but I think it will be exciting too. Before the launch we will practise hard, and after it we will celebrate together.' Nobody knows exactly what will happen, but one thing is sure: the channel will not stay small for long.

Writing: Predictions About the Future

Write and submit your paragraph; your teacher will review it. (Paragrafını yaz ve gönder; öğretmenin değerlendirecek.)

Bağlaçlar Fikirleri Bağlar (Connectors)

Cümleleri alt alta dizmek bir yazı oluşturmaz. What turns a list of sentences into a real text is connectors (bağlaçlar): cümleleri birbirine bağlar, fikirler arasındaki ilişkiyi gösterirler. Geleceği anlatırken özellikle if (koşul) ve when (zaman) çok işine yarar. These are perfect for making predictions.

BağlaçAnlamıNe işe yarar?Örnek
andveEkleme yaparRobots will help us and save our time.
butamaZıtlık gösterirLife will be easier but busier.
sobu yüzdenSonuç bildirirCities will grow, so we will need more parks.
becauseçünküSebep bildirirI will study hard because I want a good job.
ifeğerKoşul bildirirIf we work together, we will succeed.
when-diğindeZaman bildirirWhen I finish school, I will travel.
before / after-den önce / sonraSıra bildirirAfter I graduate, I will find a job.

Farkı gör — aynı bilgiler, iki farklı yazım:

Kopuk cümleler ✗Bağlaçlı akıcı yazı ✓
I will finish school. I will study medicine. It will be hard. I will not give up. I will become a doctor. When I finish school, I will study medicine. It will be hard, but I won't give up, because I really want to become a doctor.
Altın kural 4
  • if ve when ile başlayan gelecek cümlesinde koşul kısmı geniş zamandır, ana cümle will'dir: "If it rains, I will stay home." ✓ — "If it will rain..." ✗
  • Yazında en az 3 bağlaç kullan; cümle cümle yazma, fikirleri bağla.

Paragraf Yapısı (Paragraph Structure)

Bir tahmin paragrafı üç bölümden oluşur. This skeleton is the same every time — içini değiştirirsin, yapıyı değiştirmezsin:

BölümNe yazılır?Örnek
1. Giriş
(Opening)		Konu cümlesi: neyin geleceğinden bahsedeceksinI think life will change a lot in the future.
2. Gövde
(Body)		Tahminlerin, sırayla. Bağlaçlarla ve I think / maybe ile bağla.Maybe cars will fly, and robots will do our jobs...
3. Kapanış
(Closing)		Son bir tahmin / genel hisI'm not sure, but I hope the future will be good.

Task (Görev)

Write your predictions about the future. (Gelecek hakkındaki tahminlerini yaz.) Use will and won't. Konu senin seçimin: yaşamın on yıl sonra, teknoloji, şehrin, ya da kendi geleceğin.

NELER OLMALI (WHAT TO INCLUDE)

  • which future you write about (your life / technology / your city)
  • three or four predictions with will
  • at least one thing that won't happen
  • one prediction with I think or maybe
  • how you feel about this future (hopeful / worried)
  • en az 3 bağlaç: and, but, so, because, if, when, before, after
  • Speaking bölümünden en az 4 kelime (future, predict, maybe, probably, change, improve, sure, soon...)

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (EXAMPLE OPENERS)

"I think the future will ..." "In ten years, technology will ..." "Maybe people won't ... because ..."

Your Paragraph (100-130 words / kelime)

Sample Answer (~125 words) I think life will change a lot in the next twenty years. Technology will improve very fast, so many jobs will be different. Maybe cars will drive themselves, and people won't need to learn how to drive. I'm sure that computers will do a lot of our work, but I don't think robots will replace teachers, because children need real people.
