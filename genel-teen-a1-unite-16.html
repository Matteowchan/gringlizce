Ünite 16 · Free Time · Bölüm 1/7

Grammar: like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing

Boş zamanını ve hobilerini anlat: neyi seversin, neyden hoşlanmazsın. (Talk about hobbies with like / love / hate / enjoy.)

Selam, ben Gri. Bu ünitede hobilerimizden bahsediyoruz: like, love, hate, enjoy. Bunlardan sonra fiile -ing ekleriz: "I like playing football.", "She loves reading.", "They hate getting up early." He / She için fiile -s eklemeyi de unutma: "He enjoys gaming." Hadi başlayalım!

Dinleme (Listening)

Kısa bir video dinle ve izle. (Listen and watch a short video.)

Hadi başlayalım!

Ne Demek? (like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing)

Bir hobiden ya da aktiviteden bahsederken like (hoşlanmak), love (çok sevmek), hate (nefret etmek) ve enjoy (keyif almak) kullanırız. Bu fiillerden sonra ikinci fiile -ing ekleriz:

  • I like playing football.Futbol oynamayı severim. (like + playing)
  • She loves reading comics.Çizgi roman okumayı çok sever. (love + reading)
  • They hate getting up early.Erken kalkmaktan nefret ederler. (hate + getting)
  • We enjoy watching videos.Video izlemekten keyif alırız. (enjoy + watching)
Kolay yol
  • Kalıp: özne + like/love/hate/enjoy + fiil-ing.
  • İkinci fiil daima -ing alır: "I like swim" ✗ → "I like swimming" ✓.

1. love > like > hate (Ne Kadar Seviyorsun?)

Bu fiiller bir aktiviteyi ne kadar sevdiğini gösterir. Genç hobilerinle (music, sport, gaming) birlikte kullan:

FiilAnlamÖrnek
loveçok sevmekI love listening to music.
enjoykeyif almakWe enjoy playing video games.
likehoşlanmakShe likes drawing in her room.
hatenefret etmekThey hate doing homework.
Kolay yol
  • Hepsinden sonra -ing gelir: love/enjoy/like/hate + fiil-ing.
  • "I love football" ✓ (isim) ama fiil kullanınca "I love playing football" ✓.

2. -ing Nasıl Yazılır? (Spelling)

Fiile -ing eklerken üç kural vardır. Bu tabloyu bir kontrol listesi gibi kullan:

KuralFiil-ing hâli
normal: sadece -ing ekleplay, read, watchplaying, reading, watching
sonu -e ile biterse: e düşermake, dance, takemaking, dancing, taking
kısa sesli + tek sessiz: sessiz iki kezswim, run, getswimming, running, getting
Kolay yol
  • "dance" → dancing (e düşer), "swim" → swimming (m iki kez).
  • "makeing" ✗ → "making" ✓. "swiming" ✗ → "swimming" ✓.

3. He / She + -s (Present Simple)

Present Simple'da özne he, she, it ise fiile -s ekleriz. Bu, like/love/hate/enjoy için de geçerlidir:

ÖzneFiilÖrnek
I / you / we / theylike / loveThey love dancing.
he / she / itlikes / lovesHe enjoys gaming online.
Kolay yol
  • he/she/it → fiile -s: love → loves, enjoy → enjoys.
  • "She love reading" ✗ → "She loves reading" ✓.

4. Olumsuz & Soru (don't/doesn't & Do/Does)

Olumsuz için don't / doesn't, soru için Do / Does kullanırız. Ana fiil yalın kalır, hobi fiili yine -ing alır:

YapıÖrnekTürkçe
olumsuz (I/you/we/they)I don't like running.Koşmayı sevmem.
olumsuz (he/she/it)He doesn't like football.Futbolu sevmez.
soruDo you enjoy singing?Şarkı söylemekten keyif alır mısın?
soru (he/she)Does she like dancing?Dans etmeyi sever mi?
Kolay yol
  • he/she/it → doesn't / Does; diğerleri → don't / Do.
  • "Does she likes?" ✗ → "Does she like?" ✓ (Does'tan sonra fiil yalın).

5. Sık Yapılan Hatalar (Common Mistakes)

-ing ve -s'i karıştırmak kolaydır. Bu tabloyu bir kontrol listesi gibi kullan:

Yanlış ✗Doğru ✓Neden?
I like play games.I like playing games.like'tan sonra -ing.
She love reading.She loves reading.she → -s.
He don't like sport.He doesn't like sport.he → doesn't.
I like swiming.I like swimming.kısa sesli: m iki kez.
Does she likes it?Does she like it?Does'tan sonra yalın fiil.
Altın kural
  • Kalıp: like/love/hate/enjoy + fiil-ing. he/she/it ise fiile -s (loves), olumsuzda doesn't, soruda Does. Does/doesn't'tan sonra fiil yalın kalır.

6. Birlikte Görelim (Two Friends Chat)

İki genç hobilerini konuşuyor. Koyu yazılan like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing biçimlerine dikkat et ve iki kez oku:

  • — What do you like doing in your free time?Boş zamanında ne yapmayı seversin?
  • — I love listening to music, and I also enjoy playing the guitar.Müzik dinlemeyi çok severim, gitar çalmaktan da keyif alırım.
  • — Cool! Do you like gaming too?Havalı! Oyun oynamayı da sever misin?
  • — Not really. My brother loves gaming, but I hate sitting at a screen all day.Pek değil. Kardeşim oyunu çok sever ama ben ekran başında oturmaktan nefret ederim.
  • — What about sport? Does your friend Su like dancing?Peki spor? Arkadaşın Su dans etmeyi sever mi?
  • — Yes! She enjoys dancing every night, and she doesn't like quiet weekends.Evet! Her gece dans etmekten keyif alır ve sakin hafta sonlarını sevmez.
Sık yapılan hatalar
  • "I like listen to music" ✗ → "I like listening" ✓ (like → -ing).
  • "She enjoy dancing" ✗ → "She enjoys dancing" ✓ (she → -s).
  • "He don't like it" ✗ → "He doesn't like it" ✓ (he → doesn't).

Alıştırma (Practice)

Doğru cevabı seç. — 10 soru, her soru hemen kontrol edilir.

Your Turn (Senin Sıran)

Talk about your hobbies and free time. Write 5 sentences with like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing. (Hobilerini ve boş zamanını anlat. like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing ile 5 cümle yaz.)

Kurallar
  • Her fiilden sonra -ing kullan (I love playing, She enjoys reading).
  • En az bir olumsuz (don't / doesn't like) ve bir he/she cümlesi (-s ile) kullan.

Cümle başlatıcıları (istersen bunlarla başla):

"In my free time I love ..." "I really enjoy ..." "My best friend likes ..." "I don't like ..." "On weekends I hate ..."
Sample Answer 1. In my free time I love listening to music and playing the guitar.
2. I really enjoy taking photos of my friends and my cat.
3. My best friend Su likes dancing, and she practises new videos every night.
4. I don't like getting up early, so I hate doing sport in the morning.
5. On Saturdays I enjoy going swimming with my brother.

Reading: Free Time After School

Metni oku, sonra soruları cevapla. (Read the text, then answer.)

Before You Read (Okumadan Önce)

Okumadan önce şu yapıları hatırla — Speaking bölümünden tanıyorsun. Metinde bunları ara:

like + -ing · love + -ing · hate + -ing · enjoy + -ing · doesn't like · Present Simple -s

İpucu
  • Metin bir grup gencin okuldan sonra ne yapmayı sevdiğini anlatıyor: müzik, spor, oyun, fotoğraf. like/love/hate/enjoy + -ing biçimlerine dikkat et.
  • Önce hızlıca oku, sonra soruları oku ve cevapları metinde ara. Cevaplar hep metnin içindedir.
Free Time After School

Hi! I am Ceren and I am fifteen. After school my friends and I do very different things. I love music, so I listen to songs every day. My best friend Kaan loves sport. He plays football with his team on Saturdays, and he enjoys watching matches on TV too.

I have a lot of free time after school. My school finishes at three o'clock. Then I go home and I do my homework. After my homework, I listen to music in my room. I love pop music, and I also like rock music. My phone is always with me, and I make new playlists every week. Sometimes I sing, but I am not very good! Music makes me happy after a long school day.

Kaan is my best friend, and he is fifteen too. He loves football very much. He plays in the school team, and the team is very good this year. On Saturdays, Kaan gets up early and he goes to the football field near his house. He plays for two hours with his friends. He likes running fast, and he enjoys scoring goals. In the evening, he is often tired, but he is very happy. Football is his big hobby.

My sister Elif likes drawing. She draws every evening in her room, and she also enjoys taking photos with her phone. She hates getting up early, so at the weekend she sleeps late. I understand her, because I hate mornings too!

Elif is my little sister, and she is thirteen. She is very good at drawing. She draws animals, people, and flowers in a big notebook. Her favourite colour is blue, so many of her pictures are blue. She likes taking photos too. She takes photos of the sky, the trees, and our cat. At the weekend, Elif does not get up early. She stays in her warm bed, and she sleeps for a long time. She says the weekend is for resting.

Our friend Deniz loves video games. He plays games online with people from other cities, and he really enjoys it. His mum doesn't like his long hours on the computer, but Deniz says gaming is his hobby. He doesn't like football at all.

Deniz is a boy in my class, and he is very friendly. He loves video games, and he plays every day after school. He has many online friends, and they play together in the evening. Deniz talks to them with his headphones, and he laughs a lot. He is very happy when he wins a game. His mum wants him to go outside more, but Deniz likes his computer. He does not like sport, and he does not play football with us.

Some students in my class love reading. They read books at lunchtime, and they enjoy talking about stories. Others prefer dancing or singing. My cousin Su loves dancing, and she practises new videos from social media every night.

My class is big, and everybody likes different things. Two boys love singing, and they are in the school music club. One girl loves reading books about space and stars. My friend Mert enjoys cooking, and he makes small cakes for his family. Su is my cousin, and she is a very good dancer. She learns new dances from short videos, and then she teaches them to me. We dance together in her room after school, and it is a lot of fun.

I don't like doing the same thing every day. On Mondays I go swimming, on Wednesdays I enjoy cooking with my mum, and on Fridays I meet my friends in the park. We chat, we laugh, and sometimes we play basketball.

My week is always busy, and every day is a little different. On Tuesdays I read comics, and on Thursdays I watch films with my family. I like new games and new ideas, so I try something different every day. Sometimes my friends come to my house, and we play games and eat snacks together. We talk about school, music, and films. My mum makes tea for us, and we laugh a lot. Free time with friends is the best part of my week.

Free time is important for teenagers. It helps us relax after a long day of lessons. Some people like quiet hobbies, like reading or drawing at home. Other people love active hobbies, like sport or dancing with friends. Many of us also enjoy sharing our hobbies online, and we like watching short videos on our phones. Everybody is different, and that is a good thing. What about you? What do you like doing after school? Do you enjoy sport, music, or games? Tell me about your favourite hobby!

Writing: My Hobbies

Boş zamanını ve hobilerini anlat ve gönder; öğretmenin okuyacak. (Describe your hobbies; your teacher will read it.)

Küçük Bağlaçlar (Small Words: and, but, because, so)

İki fikri birbirine bağlamak için küçük bağlaçlar kullanırız. Cümlelerini bunlarla birleştir:

BağlaçAnlamıÖrnek
andveI love music and I enjoy dancing.
butamaI like sport, but I hate running.
becauseçünküI love reading because stories are exciting.
sobu yüzdenI don't like mornings, so I get up late.
İpucu
  • Yazında en az 1 bağlaç kullanmayı dene: and, but, because ya da so.

Task (Görev)

Describe your free time and hobbies: what you love doing, what you don't like, and what your friends enjoy. (Boş zamanını ve hobilerini anlat: ne yapmayı çok seversin, neyi sevmezsin ve arkadaşların neyden keyif alır.) 4-6 cümle yeterli.

NELER OLMALI (What to include)

  • one sentence with love + -ing (I love ...ing)
  • one sentence with enjoy + -ing (I enjoy ...ing)
  • one he/she sentence with -s (My friend likes/loves ...ing)
  • one negative (I don't like / He doesn't like ...ing)
  • one connector: and / but / because / so

ÖRNEK BAŞLANGIÇLAR (Example openers)

"In my free time I love ..." "I enjoy ..., but ..." "My best friend likes ..."

My Writing (40-60 words / kelime)

0 kelime

Değerlendirme öğretmenin tarafından yapılacak. (Your teacher will read your writing.)

Sample Answer (~55 words) In my free time I love listening to music, and I really enjoy playing the guitar. My best friend Kaan loves football, so he plays every Saturday with his team. I like drawing too, but I don't like getting up early at the weekend. My sister enjoys taking photos, because she finds it very relaxing.
